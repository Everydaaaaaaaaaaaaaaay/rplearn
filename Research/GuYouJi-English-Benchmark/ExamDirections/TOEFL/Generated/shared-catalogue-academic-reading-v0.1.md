# Read an Academic Passage：Shared Catalogue原创

参照 toefl2026.practice1.module2.photography 的完整四单选＋一句四位置插入；Westhaven Museum及其流程为作者虚构，不复用源未核历史断言。头像仅要求旁。

## 学生区

Read the passage and answer questions 1—5. Question 5 asks you to choose one of the four marked locations in the passage.

<!-- passage:start -->
The Westhaven Museum once kept separate paper lists for each storeroom. To locate an object, an employee had to check the relevant list in person. Comparing information held in different rooms was slow, particularly when a record had been updated in one place but not another. [A]

The museum created a shared digital catalogue that staff could search from any office. Each record included an object's name, its storage location and the date it was last examined. This system made retrieval more convenient because several employees could consult the same record without moving between storerooms. [B]

However, the catalogue did not automatically make every record accurate. A wrongly typed date remained wrong even if everyone could see it immediately. The museum therefore required staff to compare each new entry with the object's physical label. A second employee then checked the examination date against the written inspection report. [C]

The change improved access to information while creating a clear procedure for checking it. Employees could search quickly and see who had reviewed each record. The example illustrates that storing information in a shared system and verifying that information are separate tasks. A useful catalogue needs both. [D]
<!-- passage:end -->

1. The word “retrieval” in the passage is closest in meaning to
   A. protection of historical objects.
   B. finding recorded information.
   C. creation of a new collection.
   D. delivery to another storeroom.
2. Why was comparing information in the old system slow?
   A. The museum had stopped examining objects.
   B. Employees were unable to read paper records.
   C. Every object had to be moved to another room.
   D. Staff had to consult separate lists in different places.
3. Why does a second employee check the examination date?
   A. To compare the new entry with another record.
   B. To search all the museum's storerooms in person.
   C. To prevent colleagues from accessing the catalogue.
   D. To plan the dates of public exhibitions.
4. The passage suggests that a shared digital catalogue
   A. eliminates the need for human review.
   B. improves access while still requiring accuracy checks.
   C. is useful only for changing examination dates.
   D. guarantees that every physical label is correct.
5. Where would the following sentence best fit?
   These two checks help prevent a typing error from becoming an accepted description of an object.
   A. Location A.
   B. Location B.
   C. Location C.
   D. Location D.

## 作者区

参考 B/D/A/B/C。第5是选正文位置，并非第五题四个自由解释；其待插句全文和[A]—[D]标记不可删。

| 题 | A作者理由 | B作者理由 | C作者理由 | D作者理由 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 全文是找信息，不是保护物件的词义。 | consult/search records使信息取回更便利，支持。 | 没有新collection制作含义。 | 少跨room不是搬运物件定义。 |
| 2 | 只说旧记录分开，不说停止检查。 | 没有不识字/读不懂原因。 | 去看list不等搬所有objects。 | 逐处查不同列表/更新不一致造成慢，支持。 |
| 3 | 与written inspection report比对日期，支持。 | 日期核查不要求走全部room。 | checking date不限制同事查询。 | examination date不是未来展览安排。 |
| 4 | 第三段明确录入错仍错、两人工核查。 | 检索便利与核准确独立需要并存，支持。 | 记录还含name/location，不是只改dates。 | 与实体标签比对不保证标签本身永远正确。 |
| 5 | 前文只有旧列表，尚无两检查先行词。 | 前文只述数字信息字段/检索方式，无两核查动作。 | 紧接compare entry with physical label与second employee checked date两动作，These two checks明确回指，支持。 | 最后段的储存/验证是两类任务，不是刚列两具体checks；结尾再放会越过其先行词/功能段。 |
