# Read in Daily Life：Equipment Loan原创

结构参照 toefl2026.practice1.module1.party_email 三题四选，新规则及人物皆为虚构。明确设备与自带物件，不借source摄影师与cooks雇员推断；头像仅要求旁。

## 学生区

Read the email and choose the best answer to each question.

<!-- email:start -->
Subject: Equipment loan for your class

Dear Dr. Marin,

Your request to borrow recording equipment for your class has been approved. Please collect the case from Room 14 in the Media Building on October 21, between 10:00 a.m. and noon. The reception desk in the main library cannot issue this equipment.

The case contains two microphones, a recorder and spare batteries. You will need to bring your own headphones, as these are not included. A technician will show you how to connect the equipment when you collect it. Written instructions are also inside the case.

Please return everything to Room 14 by 5:00 p.m. on October 24. If your plans change, contact us before the collection date so that another class can use the equipment. There is no charge for this loan.

Best regards,
Mina Patel
<!-- email:end -->

1. What is the main purpose of the email?
   A. To invite Dr. Marin to teach a recording course.
   B. To confirm arrangements for borrowing equipment.
   C. To announce a new library reception desk.
   D. To request payment for damaged equipment.
2. When can Dr. Marin collect the equipment?
   A. On October 24, between 10:00 a.m. and noon.
   B. On October 21, after 5:00 p.m.
   C. On October 24, before 5:00 p.m.
   D. On October 21, between 10:00 a.m. and noon.
3. What should Dr. Marin bring?
   A. Headphones.
   B. Spare batteries.
   C. A recorder.
   D. Written instructions.

## 作者区

参考 B/D/A。完整subject、称谓、三正文段及签名均输入；没有题目依赖未提供附件。

| 题 | A作者理由 | B作者理由 | C作者理由 | D作者理由 |

| 1 | 确认借用，不是邀请任教。 | request approved与领取/物件/归还安排支持。 | 提到前台不能发设备，不是新前台公告。 | 明示loan无收费，也未说损坏。 |
