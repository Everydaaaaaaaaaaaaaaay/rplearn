# Class Garden：五T/F原创

参照 primary4.xdf.lower.room_tf 单篇五T/F结构，新文本给明确反证；头像仅在要求旁，无角色故事。

## 学生区

阅读短文，句子与短文相符写T，有信息证明不相符写F。

<!-- passage:start -->
My name is Nina. I am ten years old. Our class has a small garden near the playground. There are twelve red flowers and six yellow flowers in it. There are no white flowers. On Mondays, I water the flowers with my friend Joe after class. Joe carries the water, and I put it on the flowers. We do not work in the garden on Fridays. Our teacher waters the flowers on that day. I like our garden very much.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. Nina is ten years old.
2. There are white flowers in the class garden.
3. Nina and Joe work in the garden on Mondays.
4. Nina waters the flowers in the garden on Fridays.
5. Joe carries the water on Mondays.

## 作者区

参考 T/F/T/F/T。

| 题 | 支持或反证 | 其他判断为什么不支持 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 T | Nina自述I am ten years old。 | F与明示年龄相反。 |
| 2 F | 明示There are no white flowers。 | T被直接存在否定推翻，不靠列举红黄猜无白。 |
| 3 T | Monday after class与朋友Joe浇花，work指此活动。 | F与明示共同活动相反。 |
| 4 F | 明示We do not work...on Fridays，老师该日浇花。 | 不是因只写Monday就推其余日不做；有明确Friday否定。 |
| 5 T | Joe carries the water，指前句周一共同任务。 | F混淆Joe搬水与Nina浇花的角色。 |

作者只借任务结构，不采用源床靠窗推不靠桌。首稿、十种判断作者说明；没有独立教师、学生、模型或商用验收。数量词非独立数学任务，支持无需把12和6相加。
