# Inside the Book：五题三选原创

来源结构参照 primary5.yogor2024.frog；只借单篇/五题三选与人物观点变化的任务结构，以下为新文本。头像仅放在要求旁，无角色开场。

## 学生区

阅读短文，每题选一个最合适的答案。

<!-- passage:start -->
Eva wants to learn to draw birds. On Saturday, she goes to the library with her friend Max. She sees a book with a bright blue cover. There is a beautiful bird on it.

“This book can teach me to draw,” Eva says. She opens it. Every page has a photo of a bird, but there are no drawing steps. Eva likes the photos, but she still does not know how to start her own picture.

Max brings another book. Its cover is brown, and there is no picture on it. Inside, each lesson shows a small drawing and the next step. First, Eva draws a circle. Then she adds a head, wings and a tail. Now she has a bird on her paper.

Eva borrows the brown book. “I should look inside a book before I choose it,” she says. “A pretty cover does not tell me everything.”
<!-- passage:end -->

1. What does Eva want to learn?
   A. To take photos of birds.
   B. To draw birds.
   C. To make book covers.
2. Why does Eva first choose the blue book?
   A. She likes the bird on its cover.
   B. Max tells her to choose it.
   C. She has read its drawing lessons.
3. What does the brown book show?
   A. Only photos of birds.
   B. A bird on its cover.
   C. Drawing steps inside.
4. Which book does Eva borrow?
   A. The blue book.
   B. The brown book.
   C. Both books.
5. What does Eva learn about choosing a book?
   A. A brown cover is always the best.
   B. A book with photos is never useful.
   C. She should check what is inside.

## 作者区

参考 B/A/C/B/C。不是“蓝书普遍劣质”，结论只围绕Eva需要绘画步骤；照片仍被Eva喜欢。原型中的直接引语是正文内容。

| 题 | A作者理由 | B作者理由 | C作者理由 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 看到照片不等目标拍照。 | 首句明示学画鸟，支持。 | 封面是吸引物，不是制作目标。 |
| 2 | 先见漂亮封面鸟图，随即称能教画，支持。 | Max后推荐的是棕书，没有先推荐蓝书。 | 打开才发现无步骤，不是已读绘画课。 |
| 3 | 蓝书是每页照片，棕书有画及步骤。 | 棕书封面明示无图。 | 每课显示小画与下一步，支持。 |
| 4 | 蓝书被查看，但借阅对象另有明示。 | 结尾直接借棕书，支持。 | 本文借阅句单独指定棕书，无借两本依据。 |
| 5 | 学到检查内容，不是按棕色一律选择。 | 照片对这次画法需要不足，不等任何用途均无用。 | 结尾先看内容再选及封面不说明全部，支持。 |
