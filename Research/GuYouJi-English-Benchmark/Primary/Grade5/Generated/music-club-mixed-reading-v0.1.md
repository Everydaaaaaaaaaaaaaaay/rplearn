# Music Club：混合阅读原创

结构参照 primary5.yogor2024.mixed：同篇两题三选与三题完整回答，五响应；Ken/Zoe为新人物，国别直接明示，不从城市作外部推断。头像仅在答题要求旁。

## 学生区

阅读短文。1—2题各选一个答案；3—5题用完整的英文句子回答。

<!-- passage:start -->
Ken is eleven years old. His friend Zoe is eleven, too. Zoe is from Australia. They go to the same school and are in its music club. Ken plays the piano, and Zoe plays the drums. They enjoy making music together.

Every Tuesday, they do their homework together after school. Then they go to the music room. Zoe plays the drums there while Ken plays the piano. On other school days, they do their homework at home.

Zoe likes drawing pictures in the evening. Ken likes swimming on Friday evenings. On the other evenings, he reads stories at home. They have different hobbies, but they are good friends.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. How old is Zoe?
   A. Ten.
   B. Eleven.
   C. Twelve.
2. Where is Zoe from?
   A. Australia.
   B. Canada.
   C. China.
3. Do Ken and Zoe do their homework together on Tuesdays?
4. What does Zoe do in the music room?
5. What does Ken like doing on Friday evenings?

## 作者区

参考：1 B；2 A；3 Yes, they do.；4 She plays the drums.；5 He likes swimming on Friday evenings. / He likes swimming.

| 题 | A作者理由 | B作者理由 | C作者理由 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 文本明示双方十一岁，不是十岁。 | Ken十一且Zoe也是十一，支持。 | 没有十二岁人物年龄依据。 |
| 2 | 直接明示Australia，支持。 | 文本无加拿大国别信息。 | 文本无中国国别信息。 |

| 开放题 | 最低内容要求 | 允许的完整句表达示例 | 不支持的内容 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 肯定双方周二一起做作业。 | Yes, they do. / They do their homework together on Tuesdays. | No或仅说其他上学日各自在家而忽略周二。 |
| 4 | Zoe在音乐室打鼓。 | She plays the drums. / Zoe plays the drums in the music room. | 把Ken弹钢琴移给Zoe；把晚间画画当音乐室活动。 |
| 5 | Ken周五晚上喜欢游泳。 | He likes swimming. / Ken likes to swim on Friday evenings. | 把其他晚上的阅读替换周五；把Zoe画画移给Ken。 |

示例不是穷尽字符串键。内容对错、语言可理解性、完整句形式分别记录：例如3的“Yes”、4的“drums”、5的“swimming”有对应内容但未满足完整句要求；She play the drums有内容且主谓形式错误，记录语法问题，不自动按字符串全部判错。如何扣分须教研另定，不宣称源rubric已给。疑似多功能回答需人工裁决。
