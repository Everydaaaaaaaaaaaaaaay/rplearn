## 学生区

阅读材料，从每题A—D中选择最佳答案。

### Film Archive Guides

Greenbank Film Archive is looking for volunteer guides for its autumn exhibition. The display brings together objects used in making short films and accounts written by the people who made them. We need guides who can help visitors understand how an object connects with a person's story. This is the main purpose of the role, even when a visitor also needs directions or a printed leaflet.

### Hours and Support

Guides work one afternoon each week, from two to five. This is an unpaid position, but the archive provides a meal after each shift. Training takes place before the exhibition opens. Volunteers do not need to own filming equipment or have professional experience in film production.

### Requirements and Advantages

Applicants must be available for the weekly shift, listen patiently to questions and explain information clearly in English. Experience of helping at a public exhibition would be an advantage, but it is not a requirement. New volunteers with the required availability and communication skills can also apply. Sending an application does not guarantee a place.

### Applications

To apply, write a short message explaining your availability and send it to the archive before 15 August. Selected applicants will be invited to an introductory meeting. Do not bring valuable equipment to this meeting; the archive supplies the materials needed for the role.

1. What is the main purpose of this text?
   A. To advertise equipment for making short films.
   B. To invite experienced directors to submit films.
   C. To recruit volunteers for an exhibition.
   D. To announce that all applicants have been selected.
2. What is the guides' primary responsibility?
   A. Helping visitors understand objects and their stories.
   B. Selling professional filming equipment to visitors.
   C. Providing meals for the people who made the films.
   D. Directing every visitor to an introductory meeting.
3. Which experience is described as an advantage rather than a requirement?
   A. Owning a personal camera.
   B. Helping at a public exhibition.
   C. Producing a professional film.
   D. Attending an afternoon film course.
