# 初一六空三选完形：The First Recording

依据[初一扩展规格0.3](../Specifications/grade7-v0.3.md)与[西城2025六空叙事目录](grade7-xicheng2025-public-groups-v0.1.json)的完整形式新写。场景、人物、通知内容和渐进练习均为虚构，未复用原雷雨/计时游戏故事；六空不与四空词库拼成一篇十空。角色头像可置答题要求旁，无角色开场故事。

## 学生区

阅读短文，从每空所给的A、B、C三个选项中选出最合适的一项。

### The First Recording

Our class needed someone to read a short notice at Friday's school meeting. Noah knew all the words, but speaking in front of a crowd made him nervous. During practice on Tuesday, he stood by the ___1___ instead of joining the others near the front of the room. I went over to him. He was still standing beside that same door, holding a copy of the notice.

"I can read it at home," he told me, "but here I keep thinking that everyone will laugh if I make a mistake."

I did not ask him to read the whole notice immediately. Instead, I suggested taking smaller ___2___. We could begin with one sentence, listen to it together, and then decide what to do next. He would not have to read at the meeting unless he wanted to.

I put a recorder on a table and explained that we could delete the recording after listening. No one else would hear it yet. After thinking for a moment, Noah ___3___ to record the first sentence. He pressed the red button and read the line. His voice was quiet, but every word could be heard. He listened to the recording twice, then said, "That sounds more like me than I thought."

For the next sentence, he wanted me to sit beside him. His hands began to shake again because he feared getting a word wrong. Though he was still ___4___, he chose to try. When he stopped at a difficult word, I waited. I spoke only ___5___ he asked for help, and then he continued from that word.

We worked through the other two sentences in the same way. At the end, the recorder held all four sentences of the notice. Noah had ___6___ the whole text, and he was smiling. He still wanted more practice before making a decision about Friday. For now, hearing his own complete recording was enough.

1. A. window　B. chair　C. door
2. A. questions　B. steps　C. songs
3. A. agreed　B. refused　C. forgot
4. A. bored　B. proud　C. worried
5. A. before　B. after　C. until
6. A. finished　B. forgotten　C. lost

## 作者区

作者向量C/B/A/C/B/A，完整六空回填及18候选核读。第一句、次句、其余两句合四句，最后完整录音是文内事件，不是录音实测或学生参加实验。Noah最后仍未决定周五公开读，不能将成功录一遍扩成已消除紧张或必须上台。

| 空 | 预期项与具体证据 | 另两项为何不是当前最佳 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 C door | 后文that same door回指原站位，地点同时与near the front对照 | window/chair都是可能的教室物体，但不是本文明示的同一door |
| 2 B steps | one sentence→listen→decide next，随后逐句练习，支持smaller steps | questions并非分解通知的方式；songs不是本任务材料，不能因声音录制就当唱歌 |
| 3 A agreed | 考虑后按红键并读首句，是同意开始录制的行为 | refused与即刻开始相反；forgot不解释有意开始这一事件，不能只看可接to record的形式 |
| 4 C worried | 手抖且because he feared getting a word wrong明确紧张仍在 | proud可能与其他时点自豪共存，但该转折空针对当前fear，bored无无聊线索；不说人在英语或现实中不能同时有多种情绪 |
| 5 B after | I waited，先等Noah请求，再帮助，再继续，限定仅请求后开口 | before倒置先后；until把请求设为说话终止点，与等待后才给帮助不符；before/until并非普遍非法连接词 |
| 6 A finished | 全四句已录入、the whole text，完整流程收束 | forgotten/lost与已按文本录完且录音仍在的关系不符，不由smiling单独推出完成 |

完整回填见作者检查的摘要和六替换记录；本稿不是从十空截六空，没有附四空词库或隐藏额外空。源内插图词注和天气词义不移植到新题；此原创不需要原图，新文字已提供地点/顺序与转折证据。实际保存修订0，仍待非作者语言自然度、最佳性、目标学生难度/用时和商用/模型验收。
