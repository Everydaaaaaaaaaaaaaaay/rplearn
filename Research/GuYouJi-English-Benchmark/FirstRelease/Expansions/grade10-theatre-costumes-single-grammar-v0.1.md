# 高一单篇十空语法：Theatre Costumes

参照g10.wenzhou2025.grammar的完整单篇十空/7给词＋3无提示，[规格0.3](../Specifications/grade10-v0.3.md)。新写虚构剧院整理经历，保留多词形式和合法关系词分支；不复制教材韩静首日文本或其64more排他解释。

## 学生区

阅读短文。无提示的空格填一个适当单词；有提示词的空格用括号中词的正确形式填空，可使用多个词。

<!-- passage:start -->
Last Saturday, I visited Hillview Theatre to help prepare an exhibition of several (1) ____ (costume). Mina, a volunteer who also works as (2) ____ artist, showed us how to handle the old cloth (3) ____ (careful). She wanted each visitor to connect the objects with the people (4) ____ had worn them on stage.

Before writing our labels, we kept (5) ____ (read) the notes attached to each object. When a description was unclear, we asked Mina instead of guessing. We agreed (6) ____ (put) the labels below the objects, where visitors could read them without moving anything. Two display cases must (7) ____ (lock) by staff before the building closes; volunteers do not have the keys.

By the end of the afternoon, I was more interested (8) ____ the stories than in the bright colours. I found every explanation helpful and considered this one of my most (9) ____ (use) visits to a theatre. I now look forward to (10) ____ (return) when the exhibition opens.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 作者区

| 空 | 作者参考响应 | 完整回填依据 | 替代检查 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | costumes | several直接修饰可数costume，复数costumes。 | costume单数不与several配；costuming指活动而非several可展示衣服。 |
| 2 | an | 职业artist首次泛指，元音读音前an。 | a读音不合；the特指身份未给。参考冠词按文内职业解，不宣称所有可能读法已穷尽。 |
| 3 | carefully | 副词carefully修饰handle的方式。 | careful形容词不作该方式修饰；carefulness名词无介词不能接该位置。 |
| 4 | who / that | 限制性关系从句people作had worn主语，who/that均一词。 | whose需要所属名词，which不按此人的普通关系指代；不可用标准who拒that。 |
| 5 | reading | keep doing，reading主动读笔记。 | read原形/过去式不作keep后此补语，being read隐含被读对象与we不合。 |
| 6 | to put | agree to do，to put两词，未受无提示一词规则。 | put/putting不能直接作此agree补语；可用agree on putting但题面没on且那增加另词，非同一给词正确形式。 |
| 7 | be locked | must后原形助动词be＋locked，cases被staff锁。 | lock缺被动，locked缺be，to be locked不接must；不是因出现by总强制被动，实际主语/施受关系成立。 |
| 8 | in | interested in所述故事。 | on/at不能表达此普通兴趣搭配。 |
| 9 | useful | use派生useful修饰visits，前面every explanation helpful限定正面效用。 | usefully副词/usage名词不作该前置形容词；used visits不表达访馆用途，useless与明确帮助评价冲突。 |
| 10 | returning | look forward to的to是介词，returning。 | to return再加to会成to to return，return原形不合。 |

完整回填（第4的who/that两条分别实际读取）：

Last Saturday, I visited Hillview Theatre to help prepare an exhibition of several costumes. Mina, a volunteer who also works as an artist, showed us how to handle the old cloth carefully. She wanted each visitor to connect the objects with the people who had worn them on stage.

Before writing our labels, we kept reading the notes attached to each object. When a description was unclear, we asked Mina instead of guessing. We agreed to put the labels below the objects, where visitors could read them without moving anything. Two display cases must be locked by staff before the building closes; volunteers do not have the keys.

By the end of the afternoon, I was more interested in the stories than in the bright colours. I found every explanation helpful and considered this one of my most useful visits to a theatre. I now look forward to returning when the exhibition opens.

第4换that不改变people指代及谓语，两个分支都保留。第6to put/第7be locked是合法两词给词响应；无提示2/4/8各一词。参考集合仍作者准备而非穷尽独立评分键；其他真实回应要核语义和形式，不能由本表缺列直接拒绝。第9的one of没有考最高级形式，most已给；不使用出现one of就强制对根词造最高级的错误规则。
