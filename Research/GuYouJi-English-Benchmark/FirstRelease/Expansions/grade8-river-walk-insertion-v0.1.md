## 学生区

阅读日记，从A—E中选出合适的句子补全文章。每个选项最多使用一次，其中有一个多余选项。

### Saturday, May 10

Yesterday our class took a walk by the river. We met at the school gate at eight in the morning. Everyone had to bring one thing for lunch. **[1]** I carried them carefully so they would not get wet.

When we reached the river, we saw a wooden bridge. A sign said it was closed for repairs. **[2]** It took us ten more minutes, but the path was safe. From there we could still see the bridge across the water.

At lunchtime, we sat under a large tree. I shared my sandwiches with Mei. After eating, she searched her bag for her water bottle. **[3]** I pointed to it, and she laughed. She had put it down before lunch and forgotten about it.

Before we left, our teacher asked us to look at the place where we had eaten. We picked up our bags and checked the grass. There were no bottles, papers or food left. **[4]** Next time, I want to visit the river again and keep it clean too.

A. So we used a path along the river instead of crossing it.
B. I felt glad that we had left the place as clean as we found it.
C. She could not find it, although it was beside her on the grass.
D. I made two sandwiches and put them in a bag.
E. We ate our lunch on the bridge while people repaired it.

## 作者区

作者参考：1 D，2 A，3 C，4 B，E多余。每空1分为本原创评分。四句依次回填后，them回指sandwiches、It回指绕行、it回指water bottle、末句keep it clean承接离开时干净场地；整体时间链为早上准备→绕封桥→午餐找瓶→检查离场。
