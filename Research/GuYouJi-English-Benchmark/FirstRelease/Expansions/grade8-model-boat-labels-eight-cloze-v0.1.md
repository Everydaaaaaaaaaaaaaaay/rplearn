完整参考回填：

Uncle Ben was preparing his model boats for a small fair. On Saturday morning, he could hardly hide his worry. Some name cards were missing, and he was afraid that visitors would see the wrong names. I wanted to cheer him up, so I offered to help with the cards.

At first I thought I could finish quickly, but the boats looked similar. The job was more difficult than I had expected. I did not know which name belonged to each boat. Rather than make up names, I decided to ask my uncle. He showed me an old notebook with drawings and descriptions of his models.

We used the notebook to sort the cards into groups. When a card and a drawing matched, we placed that card beside the right boat. A smile spread across my uncle's face, and he laughed at one of his old drawings. We had not found every missing name, so we still needed more time to finish. However, we now had a clear way to continue, and we agreed to work on the remaining cards after lunch.
