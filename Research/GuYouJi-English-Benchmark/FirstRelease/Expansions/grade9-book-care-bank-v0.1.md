The school book club takes (1) ______ five books on each visit to a small reading room. It never brings fewer than five. Before a visit, the volunteers check every book. When a cover is loose, they repair it (2) ______ throwing the book away.

After (3) ______ all the repairs, the volunteers try using the books and prepare them for the visit. They practise the steps in the picture. It is easier to (4) ______ the order of these steps when you try them yourself. A new volunteer can then help without being told what to do each time.

Some books need special care. For these books, the club writes instructions that are (5) ______ enough for younger pupils to follow. The volunteers read the instructions with the pupils and answer their questions. Keeping a book useful is part of the club's work, as well as bringing books to the room.

## 作者全组分配、回填与开放响应

| 空 | 词库项 | 作者可成立响应 | 条件与其他选择检查 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | at least | at least | 从不少于五本支持至少五本；不是至多。其他词库项不能同时表达给定数量及后句限制。 |
| 2 | instead of | instead of | 修补替代丢弃，后接throwing；at least不组成替代关系，其余动词/形容词不能连接repair it和该动名词。 |
| 3 | finish | finishing / having finished | after后主动动名词、已完成分支都能带all the repairs，后续试用/准备相容；finish原形或finished独立形式不合此after补语。两响应为同一词库项的形式，不计用了两项。更广时体分支未独立穷尽。 |
| 4 | remember | remember | easier to＋原形记住次序，自己实践给记忆支持；不能把clear the order当一般正确记忆表达，finish已被前面的完整修补关系分配，且finish the order在此不是自然的步骤记忆含义。 |
| 5 | clear | clear | instructions are…enough for pupils to follow，清楚说明便于理解；clearly是方式副词不作此形容词补足，其他词库项的全组一次分配亦不成立。 |

完整回填at least / instead of / finishing或having finished / remember / clear，两种第3响应各回填全文。五项一次分配、两个两词短语、给词复合动词形式、图示次序与主体均核查；不把词汇第一节的“每空一词”套来。允许复合动词形式是本原创明示规则，源原键没有此分支，不能冒称源已独立认证having finished。
