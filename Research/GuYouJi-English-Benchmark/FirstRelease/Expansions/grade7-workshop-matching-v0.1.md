# 初一活动匹配：After-school Workshops

来源模式：g7.xicheng2025.matchingA；依[初一草案0.3](../Specifications/grade7-v0.3.md)。四完整活动卡、三人物需求、一多余选项；新题明确每个选项最多用一次。纯文字卡片为原创展示选择，没有复用源插图；日期、时间、地点保留，但本组三题只判断活动需求。

## 学生区

阅读四项活动介绍，为三名同学选择最合适的活动。每个选项最多使用一次，其中有一个多余选项。

### A · Story Circle

Listen to a short English story and talk about its characters with other students. You can also tell the group which part you like best. Our teacher will help everyone join the discussion.

Time: 3:30 PM, March 12 · Place: Library

### B · School Map Lab

Help new students find their way around our school. Draw a map with the classrooms, garden and main gate. You will learn to add clear names and simple directions to your map.

Time: 3:30 PM, March 13 · Place: Room 205

### C · Paper Workshop

Learn to fold paper into small boxes. Choose colourful paper and make a box for pencils or other small things. You can give your finished box to a friend.

Time: 3:30 PM, March 14 · Place: Art Room

### D · Bird Notes

Watch real birds in the school garden with a teacher. Write down what they do and where you see them. Bring a notebook. We will watch quietly and leave the birds alone.

Time: 3:30 PM, March 15 · Place: School Garden

1. Mina: I want to hear an English story and share my ideas about the people in it.
2. Leo: I want to draw something that helps new students find places in our school.
3. Nora: I want to watch real birds outside and write notes about their actions.
