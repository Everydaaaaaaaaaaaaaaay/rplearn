## 学生题面

阅读短文和表格，从每题四个选项中选出最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
A school repair club helps pupils use their pencil cases and small wooden toys again. The following fictional records show the time spent on one item and the number of items repaired during each period. The time column gives a range of minutes for one item, not the total working time of the club.
<!-- passage:end -->

<!-- table:start -->
| Period | Minutes for one item | Items repaired |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January–February | 6–8 | 20 |
| March–April | 5–7 | 24 |
| May–June | 4–6 | 30 |
| July–August | 8–10 | 18 |
| September–October | 5–8 | 26 |
| November–December | 4–7 | 28 |
<!-- table:end -->

<!-- passage:start -->
The club gives new volunteers help before asking them to work alone. In May, for example, Mei first watched Lian repair a pencil case. She then practised on another one with his help. When she understood the steps, she explained them to a younger pupil. This allowed her to check what she had learned and helped someone else at the same time.

An item is counted as repaired only when its owner can use it again. Students also write short instructions when an item needs special care. At the end of each period, the club discusses what went well and which steps still need practice. The records help the group plan its work, but the club also listens to the pupils who bring items in.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. How many items did the club repair during July–August?
   A. 8. B. 10. C. 18. D. 30.
2. Why does the writer mention Mei's experience?
   A. To show why new toys cost more in May.
   B. To give an example of helping a new volunteer learn.
   C. To explain why pupils should work alone from the start.
   D. To describe a competition between Mei and Lian.
3. What is the best title for the whole text?
   A. A Race to Repair the Most Items
   B. Why Every Toy Needs Special Care
   C. Six Ways to Buy a New Pencil Case
   D. How a School Repair Club Works

## 作者全组解答

| 题 | 作者答案 | 定位与全部选项检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | C | July–August行最后一列18件；A/B是该行每件所用分钟范围的端点，不能当件数；D是May–June件数，错行。全文已明示范围属于每件时间，不把表格误读成总工时。 |
| 2 | B | 第二段先总述帮助新志愿者，再以Mei观察/有人帮助练习/讲给小学生的完整过程作例证。A无价格/买新玩具内容；C与先帮助再独立相反；D没有比赛，Lian是帮助者。 |
| 3 | D | 表格工作记录、志愿者学习及完成标准/服务反馈一起说明俱乐部怎样工作。A没有竞赛或只追件数；B仅特需保养且Every过强；C买新物品不是修复任务，六行期间不是六个购买方法。 |

完整学生材料包括全部三段、六行表格、三题和十二选项。向量C/B/D，全组复解；人物、月份、数量单位和示例作用一致。每题1分是原创工程分制，不冒称源每题2分或真人rubric。
