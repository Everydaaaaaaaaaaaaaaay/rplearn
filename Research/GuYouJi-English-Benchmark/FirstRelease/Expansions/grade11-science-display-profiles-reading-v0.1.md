# 高二四简介三题四选：Four Student Displays

参照g11.spring2024.readingA四简介、三题四选及场馆/日期/成对共同特征；[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade11-v0.2.md)。原源23从画廊展出推借用仍待裁决，本原创两卡明确学生制作。所有场所/日期/作品虚构，不提供现实预约。

## 学生区

阅读材料，从每题A—D中选择最佳答案。

### Shadow Stage

North Hall, 18 May to 1 July 2030. This display explores how moving shapes change the pictures on a wall. Visitors can stand between a lamp and a screen, adding their own shadows to the scenes. The equipment stays in the hall, and staff explain how to join in without touching it. No recordings of visitors are kept.

### Future Kitchens

West Gallery, 1 October to 20 November 2030. Students present possible arrangements for shared kitchens through drawings and movable paper models. Visitors can suggest changes by placing a comment card beside a model. These are proposals, not finished kitchens available to rent. The display opens after the other three have closed.

### Sound Cards

South Annex, 3 May to 25 May 2030. A school science club created the card sets used in this display. Each set links a written description of a sound with a place inside the school. Visitors match the cards to prepared recordings using headphones; they cannot add their own recordings. The students explain why some descriptions were revised after the first trial.

### Turning Tales

East Room, 11 May to 2 June 2030. Drama students built the objects in this collection to tell different versions of the same short story. Visitors turn the objects to reveal another character's account, then discuss which details change. These are students' own constructions rather than objects borrowed from a professional theatre. A printed guide is provided for visitors who prefer not to join the discussion.

1. Where can visitors become part of the pictures on display?
   A. South Annex.
   B. West Gallery.
   C. North Hall.
   D. East Room.
2. Which display is open in October 2030?
   A. Future Kitchens.
   B. Shadow Stage.
   C. Turning Tales.
   D. Sound Cards.
3. What do Sound Cards and Turning Tales have in common?
   A. They both collect new recordings from visitors.
   B. They both remain open throughout the summer.
   C. They both borrow objects from a professional theatre.
   D. They both use materials made by school students.
