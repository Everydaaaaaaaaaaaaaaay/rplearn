### More Than Printed Pages

At the college print room, students often arrive with a deadline approaching and a file that refuses to print. They can usually leave with the pages they need, but the machines are only part of the service. **[1]**

Rosa Chen checks the file before starting a print job, asking the student which pages and copies are needed. When the job is ready, she makes sure the pages are in the right order. **[2]** As the student starts checking the pile, Rosa waits rather than immediately calling the next person. A queue may form outside, but she does not want someone to leave with an avoidable problem.

Rosa travels by bus to reach the campus before the first classes begin at eight. **[3]** "I do that because students with morning classes need time to collect their work, especially when there is a problem with a file," she says. She sees that extra time as part of the service, rather than a favour students should have to ask for.

The effect of her patience is visible in the way visitors describe the room. Mina Liu, a first-year history student, sometimes arrives after struggling with a file on her own. **[4]** "Rosa doesn't pretend every fault is easy to fix," Mina explains. "She tells me what she can do and lets me ask questions. That makes the problem feel manageable again."

"I still enjoy seeing someone walk out with the work they came for," Rosa says. **[5]** She hopes to train new colleagues to give students the same attention, even when there are more orders than they expected. For her, explaining a limitation honestly is as much a part of helping as pressing the print button.

A. On days before major deadlines, she opens the room a little earlier.
B. She keeps a family photograph on the shelf beside the door.
C. She then hands them to the student and asks whether anything is missing.
D. After twelve years here, she has no plans to leave the service.
E. The room closes at six each evening.
F. A visit to the room helps her feel calmer before returning to class.
G. This small room also gives anxious visitors someone willing to listen.
