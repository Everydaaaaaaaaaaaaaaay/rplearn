### Making a Group Plan Work

A group can work hard and still follow two different plans. One member prepares a poster while another expects a talk. How can they avoid this confusion? __1__

#### Make the goal clear.

Before dividing jobs, decide what the finished project needs to do. When offering an idea, remind yourself: "__2__" Then ask whether it would help the group reach that goal. An attractive idea may not fit this project.

#### Check where people are.

Knowing who has a job does not tell you how it is going. Some members have finished; others are waiting for materials. __3__ These brief reports should include both finished work and delays. The group can then see who needs help.

#### Leave room for changes.

A plan may no longer fit the time or materials available. __4__ During this reserved time, compare the old plan with the new limits. Change a decision for a clear reason, rather than change everything at every meeting.

#### __5__

People may remember the same discussion differently. Keep a shared note of what was agreed, who will do each job and when to check it again. Make sure everyone can find the latest version, instead of following an older plan.

A useful plan connects the goal, current progress and next actions. It gives the group something to check and improve.

A. Here are some ways to make the whole plan clearer.
B. If one member finishes early, the whole project is complete.
C. Set aside a little time to discuss new problems together.
D. We need to agree on what the project must achieve.
E. Write down what has been agreed.
F. A short update from everyone can show what help is needed.
G. No changes should be made after the first meeting.
