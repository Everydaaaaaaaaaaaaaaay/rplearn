<!-- PASSAGE_START -->
Every Friday, our class runs a small lost-property desk. We put objects on the table and keep (1) ___ about where we found them. If a student can describe a missing object, we (2) ___ it after checking the student's name. Some bottles are (3) ___ of water, so we keep them away from books. If you cannot find your object, please (4) ___ for us to check the cupboard. We do not give an object to a student just because the student likes it.
<!-- PASSAGE_END -->

## 作者区

作者参考：1 notes，2 return，3 full，4 wait，即A/B/C/D。每空1分为本题选择。

| 空 | 本篇依据 | 其余词排除 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | keep notes about地点记录；后面where从句说明记录内容 | keep return/wait不是此处记录搭配；full也不能组成keep full about位置记录 |
| 2 | 有物主描述与核对姓名后return it，指归还失物 | we notes主谓不符；full不是此校园归还动作；wait it不能表达此动作 |
| 3 | be full of water说明需远离书本的水瓶 | notes/return/wait不构成此容量状态；不得以水多为理由替换词库外filled |
| 4 | wait for us to check表示等待核查 | notes/return/full均不能在本语境组成该等待结构；return for us不是请人等核查的意思 |

全组回填：

Every Friday, our class runs a small lost-property desk. We put objects on the table and keep **notes** about where we found them. If a student can describe a missing object, we **return** it after checking the student's name. Some bottles are **full** of water, so we keep them away from books. If you cannot find your object, please **wait** for us to check the cupboard. We do not give an object to a student just because the student likes it.

四词各用一次且原形，单空/全组的事件和指代一致；we学生值班组、them桌上失物、it当前物品、us核查人员。开放英语中可有其他词，但本题评分受有限词库约束，不将其当四个无提示空或自由表达评分。

全文为虚构校园情境，不作为现实失物管理制度。实际保存修订次数0；没有独立老师/学生/商业或生产结论。
