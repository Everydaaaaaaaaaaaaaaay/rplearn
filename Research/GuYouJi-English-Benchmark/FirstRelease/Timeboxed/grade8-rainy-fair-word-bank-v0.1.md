## 学生题面

阅读短文，从词库中选出恰当的单词并用其适当形式填空，使短文通顺、意义完整。每空仅填一词，每词仅用一次。

On the morning of the school fair, Lila got up (1) ________ than usual. Heavy rain had kept her awake, and she was worried about the posters her class had left outside. She called two classmates, and they agreed to meet before the other students arrived. Together, they went to check (2) ________ little stall in the playground.

The three friends (3) ________ by moving the wet boxes under a roof. Lila opened each one (4) ________ so that the paper inside would not tear. In the last box, she (5) ________ a bag of dry string. They used it to hang the posters where visitors could still see them. Lila checked the knots not once but (6) ________ before letting go.

Their teacher arrived while they were working. She said that moving the posters had been a good (7) ________ and brought them some hot water. The kindness of their classmates filled the little stall with (8) ________. When the first visitor stopped to look at a poster, all three friends (9) ________.

By lunchtime, the sun had come out and the boxes had (10) ________. The fair was finally busy. Lila was glad they had come early instead of waiting for somebody else to solve the problem.
