## 学生题面

阅读语篇，从共享候选中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项，使意思通顺。选项中有一项为多余项。

My friends often use their phones to keep notes, but I like a small notebook. (1) ________ Those forty pages are easy to carry in my school bag. Why do I like it so much?

(2) ________ For example, I write down which books I need to return. Then I can check the list before leaving home. Second, it gives me a place for ideas. Sometimes a funny name comes to me on the bus. I write it down before I forget it.

(3) ________ A quick picture can show the shape of a flower better than a long sentence. Some of my pages have maps on them too.

(4) ________ When I read an old page, I remember what I was doing that day. A shopping list may bring back a happy afternoon with my father.

My notebook is nearly full now. (5) ________ But I will keep this old one on my shelf. Its pages do not look perfect, yet they tell my own story.

C. I do not need to use only words.

D. Last, it keeps small parts of my past.

F. Every page must be neat and perfect.
