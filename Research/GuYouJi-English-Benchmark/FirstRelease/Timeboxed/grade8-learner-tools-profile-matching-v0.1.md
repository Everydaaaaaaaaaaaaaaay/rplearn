## 学生题面

阅读四个工具介绍，根据人物需求选择最适合的工具。有一个多余选项。

Four imaginary tools for English learners are described below.

### 共享候选

A. StoryTrack offers short stories in several levels. You can read one page at a time and tap an unfamiliar word for a simple meaning. There are questions after each story, but this tool does not provide spoken conversations or correct your writing.

B. SpeakPair gives you everyday situations to talk about. You record a short answer, listen to it and try again. Its main aim is to get you speaking. It is not a spelling checker, and the activities are not a collection of storybooks.

C. ListenReplay contains short recorded dialogues. You can slow a dialogue down, replay one part and then check the written script. It is useful when you want to hear where one word ends and the next begins, rather than practise writing a long text.

D. WriteFix helps you look again at your own written sentences. It marks possible spelling mistakes and explains them with examples. You decide whether to make each change. It does not offer audio stories or let you practise a conversation with a partner.
