## 学生题面

从每题所给的四个选项中选出最符合语境和语法的一个答案。

1. —The visit we were looking forward to has been cancelled.
—________! I really wanted to go.
   A. What a pity
   B. With pleasure
   C. Help yourself
   D. Congratulations

2. Before every workshop, the technician performs the same ________ check of the tools.
   A. routine
   B. fictional
   C. retired
   D. remote

3. The weight of the blue model is equal to ________ of the red one.
   A. those
   B. it
   C. that
   D. what

4. She was clearly ________ at the repeated interruptions and asked everyone to listen for a moment.
   A. strict
   B. sensitive
   C. normal
   D. annoyed

5. As a student, he ________ spend his lunch breaks in the library; it was his daily routine.
   A. had better
   B. might
   C. would
   D. should

6. The pattern is the same ________ the one we used last year.
   A. what
   B. when
   C. as
   D. where

7. The project has just been approved. The old noticeboard ________ next Monday.
   A. is to be replaced
   B. was replacing
   C. has just replaced
   D. replaces

8. I ________ the final page when the lights suddenly went out last night.
   A. am reading
   B. have read
   C. will read
   D. was reading

9. To match the five-pointed outline, the badge was made ________ a star.
   A. in the company of
   B. in the shape of
   C. in the hope of
   D. in the name of

10. The board includes a diagram ________ how the parts fit together.
   A. showing
   B. shows
   C. having shown by
   D. shown

11. The project has reached the stage ________ every decision has to be recorded.
   A. whom
   B. which
   C. where
   D. what

—________. Let’s get those first.
   A. That’s a good point
   B. Help yourself
   C. You’re welcome
   D. Congratulations

   A. approve
   B. meet
   C. resolve
   D. prove

   A. choosing
   B. to choose
   C. having chosen
   D. chosen

   A. run out of
   B. give in to
   C. walk out on
   D. live up to
