## 学生题面

根据句意、首字母及汉语提示，用单词的正确形式完成句子。在作答栏写完整单词，包括已给出的首字母。

1. The figures in the final report must be a________ (准确的), so please check them again.

2. The club hopes to r________ (减少) the amount of paper used in its weekly notices.

3. Each team member is r________ (负责的) for checking one part of the display.

4. Living away from home helped her become more i________ (独立的) in making everyday decisions.

5. The visit is on our s________ (日程安排), but the starting time has not been confirmed.

6. Keeping a clear record is e________ (必不可少的) when several people share the same equipment.

7. The designer offered a p________ (切实可行的) way to repair the stand using tools we already had.

8. We cannot accept more than forty visitors. Forty is the m________ (最大限度).

9. The teacher demonstrated a new m________ (方法) for organising the interview notes.

10. Completing the first exhibition together was an important a________ (成就) for the new club.

## 作者核对

### 参考表达与逐题依据

| 题 | 参考（可能含变体） | 依据与常见错误边界 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | accurate | be后用形容词accurate；accuracy是名词，accurately为副词。 |
| 2 | reduce | to后接原形reduce，减少amount不是把所有纸移除；reduction不合动词位置。 |
| 3 | responsible | be responsible for为承担责任的搭配；responsibility不能直接作此形容词表语。 |
| 4 | independent | more后用形容词independent，independence是名词；此句说日常决策能力。 |
| 5 | schedule | on our schedule表示已列入日程；拼写schedule，非只填写缺失后缀。 |
| 6 | essential | is后用essential形容词，essence不是同一语法形式。 |
| 7 | practical | practical修饰way并由现成工具的可实施性支撑；practically不修饰名词way。 |
| 8 | maximum | 定冠词后作上限名词maximum；minimum意为下限，与cannot accept more than不合。 |
| 9 | method | a new后接单数method，for说明方法用途；不同语义的manner或means须按完整语境复核，参考集合未认证穷尽。 |
| 10 | achievement / accomplishment | 两词均为a开头可数成就名词，an important后单数，均与完成首次展览相容。 |
