## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入一个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。

At the winter exhibition, a team of (1) ________ (patience) students showed visitors a paper model of their town. The empty hall (2) ________ (be) full of people that afternoon. The students explained (3) ________ (clear) how they had made each part, from the tiny windows to the bridge over the river.

“We didn't buy (4) ________ (something) from an online model shop,” said Amy, one of the students. “We asked our neighbours for old boxes and clean paper. One neighbour even gave us a box full (5) ________ buttons. We used them as round seats in the little park.”

(6) ________ some visitors preferred the colourful shops, Amy's favourite part was a small grey building at the edge of the model. It was the old station. Her grandfather had drawn it from memory. Amy checked his drawing against a photograph, and then she built (7) ________ station from a food box. She added a clock above the entrance.

Working with other students made the job (8) ________ (easy) than doing everything alone. They divided the work, but met every Friday to check that the streets fitted together. Their teacher said that a model could tell the (9) ________ (town) story as well as show its buildings.

“Next term, we expect more students (10) ________ (share) their family memories,” Amy said. “Those stories can help us decide what to add.”

## 作者核对

### 逐响应作者参考

| 响应 | 作者参考 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | patient | 修饰students需形容词patient，patience为名词 |
| 2 | was | that afternoon叙述过去，单数hall配was |
| 3 | clearly | 修饰explained用clearly |
| 4 | anything | 否定didn’t下不定代词anything |
| 5 | of | full of固定搭配 |
| 6 | Although / Though / While | 主从句形成偏好对照，Although/Though或对比While成立，句首大写 |
| 7 | the | 前句已介绍the old station，后文特指同一车站用the |
| 8 | easier | than触发比较级easier |
| 9 | town’s / town's | town与story所属关系，用town’s，接受直/弯撇号 |
| 10 | to share | expect sb to do，用to share两词；给词槽不能套无给词一词限制 |

### 完整形式与边界

以长郡普通校卷十空完整模式补本格，原relay模拟来源不升级。给词1/2/3/4/8/9/10、无给词5/6/7；第10空两词to share合法，第9空所属撇号两形接受；源具体事实、音乐流量和DNA类比不继承到新叙事。只建立形式参照，未独立校准难度。
