Nora wanted to repair a torn bag instead of buying a new one. On Saturday, she visited (1) ________ small repair club near her home. There, old objects (2) ________ by volunteers and their owners together. A broken zip or a loose handle could become a chance to learn.

At first, Nora expected someone to do all the work for her. Mrs Lin, a volunteer, gave her a needle and some thread in order (3) ________ her how to begin. “If you (4) ________ the first few stitches carefully, the next ones will be easier,” she said. Mrs Lin not only showed her the steps (5) ________ also let her practise at her own speed.

Soon, a younger boy arrived with two toy (6) ________. One had lost a wheel. Nora watched him search a box of spare parts while she continued working on her bag. Neither of them finished quickly. However, they both smiled when (7) ________ objects could be used again.

Nora has kept the bag ever since. She says it now means (8) ________ to her than a new one would. When a friend asked (9) ________ she still carried an old bag, she told him about the patient volunteer and the stitches she had made herself. She also suggested (10) ________ the club together the following Saturday.
