## 学生题面

从每题所给的四个选项中选出最符合语境和语法的一个答案。有两个空的题，每个选项是一组完整搭配，只选择一项同时填入两空。

1. Calling a classmate careless in front of everyone is a way to ________ that person, not to praise them.
   A. adjust
   B. justify
   C. obtain
   D. criticize

2. Lena meant to keep the plan secret, but she mentioned it ________ during lunch and immediately regretted it.
   A. at ease
   B. by accident
   C. on purpose
   D. in return

   A. let his friends down
   B. break into a smile
   C. let off steam
   D. rise to his feet

   A. admit
   B. adjust
   C. evaluate
   D. obtain

   A. temporary
   B. typical
   C. solar
   D. royal

   A. landscape

   A. hide
   B. spend

   D. owe

   A. frequent
   B. important
   C. sensitive
   D. minor

   A. ensure

   C. threaten
   D. assist

   A. consume
   B. reduce
   C. ignore
   D. produce

   A. is lasting

   A. which
