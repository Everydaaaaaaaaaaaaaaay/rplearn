## 学生题面

阅读材料，根据内容和两个给定段首续写两段，使之构成完整短文。续写词数约150词。

My first Saturday at Mrs Chen's bakery was supposed to be simple. I had spent the week learning how to fold the paper bags, clean the counter and read the handwritten labels on the cake boxes. Mrs Chen said that I did not need to work fast yet. I needed to check each order carefully and ask whenever I was unsure.

That morning, however, the bakery was much busier than usual. Rain had driven people inside, and a line stretched almost to the door. I was proud that Mrs Chen trusted me at the front counter while she finished decorating cakes in the kitchen. Whenever the bell above the door rang, I tried to greet the next customer before it stopped shaking.

Two large white boxes stood on the collection shelf. Both had blue labels. One contained a chocolate cake for a child's eighth birthday; the other held a lemon cake for a family's quiet celebration. I had checked the names when the boxes arrived, but then somebody asked about bread rolls, the telephone rang, and a small boy dropped a bag of biscuits. By the time Mr Lewis came to collect his order, I was thinking about three different things.

He gave his name, and I handed over the box nearest the edge of the shelf. He thanked me and said his family would be pleased. I watched him leave, feeling that I was finally becoming useful. Then I looked at the remaining label. Mr Lewis's name was still there.

For a moment, I hoped I had read it incorrectly. I checked again. The lemon cake was still on the shelf; the chocolate cake had gone with Mr Lewis. His family was expecting the cake that afternoon. The other customer had not arrived yet, but would certainly notice that her order was missing.

I could have moved the remaining box further back and said nothing until somebody asked. Mrs Chen was still in the kitchen. She had not seen which box I had given him. Yet the order book lay open beside the register, with the names and contact details of both customers. There was still a way to put things right, if I stopped pretending that I could manage everything alone.

### 完整形式与边界

一完整续写任务、两给定段首、两段合约150词，不拆两题或两段各150。源未明确段首计词是否排除，本文参考同时报告含段首与新增正文数量，不借其他卷口径；约数不是私造精确区间。源明示25分不等已得分析rubric。

作者关注：保留第一人称/过去叙事、两盒蛋糕和订单本的线索；第一段主动告知并合理解决调换，第二段衔接family returned与叙述者预期；不能让所有错误凭空消失或由客户主动不明就里地解决。结尾行动体现改变即可，不必喊口号。

I took a breath and told Mrs Chen what I had done. She listened without interrupting, then opened the order book. “Let's call them before they cut it,” she said. I explained the mistake to Mr Lewis while she checked the other order. Fortunately, he was still nearby. He brought the unopened box back, and I checked both labels with Mrs Chen before handing him the lemon cake. My hands were shaking, but the two orders were finally correct.

When the family returned later that afternoon, I was still expecting angry words. Instead, Mr Lewis smiled and showed me a photograph of the celebration. His mother had enjoyed the lemon cake, and he wanted to thank us for sorting out the problem so quickly.
