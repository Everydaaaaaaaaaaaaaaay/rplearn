Mina and Tom are discussing a recording of their school play.

Mina: The audience clapped loudly, but you looked unhappy afterwards. Did something go (1) ________ during the performance?

Tom: No one missed a line. The problem came later. When I listened to the recording at home, my voice was so quiet that I could hardly hear it.

Mina: Could you (2) ________ up the volume?

Tom: We tried that, but it also made the music much louder. I think the recorder was too close (3) ________ the speaker playing the music.

Mina: Then perhaps we should test a different position (4) ________ of buying new equipment. We could put the recorder near the actors during tomorrow's rehearsal.

Tom: That sounds sensible. Have you used this recorder before?

Mina: No, (5) ________. You'll need to show me the controls.

Tom: That's fine. We can also ask Mr Lee for (6) ________. He recorded last year's play and may know a better place for it.

Mina: We shouldn't change any settings (7) ________ we have saved the current recording. Otherwise, we might lose the only copy.

Tom: Agreed. Let's save it first and make a second recording tomorrow. Then we can (8) ________ one version with the other and decide which is clearer.

Mina: Good. Clear sound is just as important (9) ________ acting when people listen to a recording without seeing the stage.

Tom: Still, let's ask the actors whether they can hear every line, rather than deciding everything by (10) ________.

Mina: Yes. Their opinions will help us notice problems we might miss.

## 作者核对

### 逐响应作者参考

| 响应 | 作者参考 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | wrong | go wrong表示出问题；后文澄清演出没错，问题在录音 |
| 2 | turn | turn up the volume提高音量，后文also louder呼应 |
| 3 | to | too close to固定距离关系 |
| 4 | instead | instead of结构表替代购买 |
| 5 | never | No, never回答是否曾使用；No前后与后句不会操作一致 |
| 6 | advice / help / guidance / suggestions / tips | ask sb for advice/help等均可；后文说明老师可能提供帮助；不穷尽合理名词 |
| 7 | until / unless | until强调保存前不改；unless在未满足保存条件时不改，也可成立；不能机械只收until |
| 8 | compare / contrast | compare/contrast A with B均可引出两版本比较 |
| 9 | as | as...as等比结构，第二as不能省 |
| 10 | ourselves | by ourselves表示仅凭两人自己；双方共同决策用第一人称复数反身代词 |

### 完整形式与边界

一篇完整双人对话十空，每空一个词含缩写，不改为十个独立句或整句输出。第6/7/8空有多条成立表达，作者已列已识别分支；开放词表不是穷尽答案，页面不自动判分。设备判断属于虚构角色的推测，不当实测声学结论。
