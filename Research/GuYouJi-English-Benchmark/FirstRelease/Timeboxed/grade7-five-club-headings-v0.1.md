## 学生题面

阅读语篇，根据每段内容，从共享候选中选出适当的小标题。

Our school is opening five new clubs. Read about them and choose a heading for each part.

### (1) ________

You don't need an expensive camera to join this club. Bring a pencil and some paper. We look closely at pictures of cats, birds and other animals, then try to draw their eyes and feet. We share our pictures at the end.

### (2) ________

Here, a small box can become a drum, and two spoons can make a simple sound. We listen to each other and play short songs together. You can also bring an instrument from home.

### (3) ________

Do you enjoy a good story? In this club, you choose a short one and tell it to a partner. Your partner can ask about the people in it. You do not have to remember every word from the book.

### (4) ________

We grow beans in small pots near a window. Each week, we check the leaves and write down what we see. Some children bring pictures of the flowers in their gardens too.

### (5) ________

Two players sit on different sides of a board. Before moving a piece, you need to think about your partner's next move. We learn new games and explain our ideas after each round.

### 共享候选

A. Make music together

B. Learn about plants

C. Tell a story aloud

D. Draw animals

E. Play thinking games
