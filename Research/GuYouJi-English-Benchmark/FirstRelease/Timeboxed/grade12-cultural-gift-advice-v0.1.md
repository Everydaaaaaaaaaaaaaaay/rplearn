## 学生题面

你的英国朋友Alex即将回国，想为喜欢园艺的母亲带一件有中国文化特色的礼物，来信征求你的建议。请回复邮件，推荐一件礼物，解释文化特色及适合他母亲的原因，并表达祝愿。

### 答题要求

1. 新增邮件正文约80词；给定称呼和落款不计入词数。
2. 推荐一件有中国文化特色的具体礼物，使推荐理由与收礼人的兴趣对应，并表达祝愿。
3. 可补充合理细节，以使推荐具体、连贯。

Dear Alex,

[在此写完整正文]

Best wishes,
Lin

## 作者核对

### 未评分参考示例

Dear Alex,

I would recommend a paper-cut picture of flowers for your mother. Paper-cutting is a traditional Chinese craft, and a flower design would connect it with her interest in gardening. Choose one mounted on a simple card, so it will be easy to carry home. You could add a short note explaining why you chose the pattern. That personal message may matter as much as the gift itself. I hope she enjoys it and that you have a pleasant journey home.

Best wishes,
Lin

### 流畅但任务错位的反例

Dear Alex,

Buying gifts is an important part of travelling. There are many shops near the station, and they often stay open until late in the evening. You should compare prices carefully and check how much space is left in your suitcase. Remember to keep your passport and ticket somewhere safe. If the weather is cold, wear a warm coat on the way to the airport. I think travelling abroad is a useful experience because it helps us understand different ways of life.

Best wishes,
Lin

反例不能因接近字数、句子通顺就当高质量：购物和旅行建议代替具体文化礼物推荐，没有解释适合母亲的原因，也缺针对收礼人与返程的祝愿。
