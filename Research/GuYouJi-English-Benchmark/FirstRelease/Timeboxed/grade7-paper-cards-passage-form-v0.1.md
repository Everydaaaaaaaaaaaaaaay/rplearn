## 学生题面

Eva likes making things with paper. She thinks it is (1) ________ (use) to learn a simple skill that does not need a screen. Her grandfather often helps (2) ________ (she) after dinner. With his help, Eva learns (3) ________ (quick), but she still makes mistakes.

Last spring, she (4) ________ (start) making cards for her friends. At first, she used too much glue, and some cards would not open. Her grandfather did not make the cards for her. He only asked, “What can you change next time?” Eva tried thinner paper and less glue. Soon she could make (5) ________ (colour) cards with small flowers inside.

(6) ________ finishing her first good card, Eva put it beside a family photo. The next day, she (7) ________ (show) it to her art teacher. The teacher liked the flowers and asked her to bring more cards to class.

Eva was happy, (8) ________ she taught two friends how to make them. Now her (9) ________ (card) are not always perfect, but each one is different. One of her friends has taken (10) ________ interest in paper art too.

## 作者核对

### 逐响应作者参考

| 响应 | 作者参考 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | useful | it is useful to do结构需形容词 |
| 2 | her | 动词helps后的宾格her |
| 3 | quickly | 修饰learns用副词quickly |
| 4 | started | Last spring明确过去，started |
| 5 | colourful / colorful | 修饰cards用colourful；同样接受美式colorful |
| 6 | After | finishing之前用After表示完成之后，句首需大写 |
| 7 | showed | The next day沿用过去故事，showed |
| 8 | so / and | so表受到鼓励后的后续行动；and并列高兴与教朋友也成立。不能把语篇偏好的因果解释当唯一合法连接。 |
| 9 | cards | her后受each one等信息支持复数作品cards |
| 10 | an | take an interest in固定表达，interest元音起首 |
