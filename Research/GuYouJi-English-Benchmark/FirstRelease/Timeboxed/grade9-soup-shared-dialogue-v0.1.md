## 学生题面

根据对话内容，选出适当的共享选项补全对话。

A: I promised to cook dinner, but I don't know what to make.

B: (1) ________

A: A vegetable soup? That's a good choice. What should I do first?

B: (2) ________

A: Done. Shall I turn on the cooker now?

B: (3) ________

A: All right. I've cut them up and put them in the pot with some water. Can I start heating it?

B: (4) ________

A: It's boiling now. I'll turn down the heat. What happens next?

B: (5) ________
