Judy enjoys singing. Her younger brother, Ben, sometimes comes to listen after his own class finishes. He is not a member of the music club. Eric plays the drums and helps put the instruments away. The four students like different kinds of music, but they enjoy making a song together.

## 作者核对

### 逐响应作者参考

| 响应 | 作者参考 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Every Wednesday. / On Wednesdays. | 明确每周三；不把at home或after class的别处信息混入。 |
| 2 | They are too loud. / Because they are too loud. | 问Max不打鼓的原因，正文直接说明；最多5词。 |
| 3 | Judy’s younger brother. / Judy's younger brother. | 问人物关系；Ben是Judy弟弟，不是四位固定成员之一。 |
