## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据内容判断句子正误。正确选A，错误选B。

When Leo arrived at the community hall for his first chess club meeting, he went straight to Room 3. He had copied that number from an old poster. The room was empty, and he began to think that the meeting had been cancelled.

A volunteer noticed him looking at the door and asked what he was trying to find. After hearing his answer, she explained that the club had moved to Room 8 the previous month. She walked there with him and introduced him to the club leader.

The meeting was already under way, but Leo was still able to join a beginners' game. Before he left, the leader gave him a new timetable. Leo put it in his bag and thanked the volunteer. On his next visit, he planned to check the timetable instead of relying on the old poster.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Leo chose Room 3 because he had seen its number on an old poster.
   A. 正确
   B. 错误

2. The empty room showed that the chess club meeting had been cancelled.
   A. 正确
   B. 错误

3. The volunteer told Leo that the club was now meeting in Room 8.
   A. 正确
   B. 错误

4. Leo missed the whole meeting and was unable to play chess.
   A. 正确
   B. 错误

5. Leo decided to use the new timetable on his next visit.
   A. 正确
   B. 错误
