## 学生题面

从每题所给的四个选项中选出最符合语境和语法的一个答案。有两个空的题，每个选项是一组完整搭配，只选择一项同时填入两空。

1. —We have finished checking every file, and it is getting late.
—Then ________. We can start the next task tomorrow.
   A. it couldn’t be better
   B. let’s call it a day
   C. you’ve got me there
   D. don’t mention it

2. We should judge the proposal by ________ it achieves, rather than by its attractive title.
   A. that
   B. why
   C. what
   D. whether

3. Explaining an idea in your own words can reveal your ________ of it.
   A. comprehension
   B. compensation
   C. compassion
   D. consumption

4. The cabinet was locked, and only Sam had the key. With the notebook now on the desk, we conclude that he ________ it out.
   A. might take
   B. must have taken
   C. should have taken
   D. could take

5. The notes ________ from the first trial, most of ________ were damaged, are still readable.
   A. dating; them
   B. dating; which
   C. dated; whom
   D. dated; them

6. Dust had ________ on the unused shelves during the long closure.
   A. accumulated
   B. anticipated
   C. assessed
   D. analyzed

7. As a precaution, save a copy on the local drive ________ the internet connection fails.
   A. in case
   B. so that
   C. even though
   D. now that

8. Maya’s action was intentional rather than automatic; she placed the label upside down ________.
   A. deliberately
   B. automatically
   C. ultimately
   D. instinctively

9. The briefing ________ by the project leaders next Tuesday will introduce the new borrowing rules.
   A. to hold
   B. to be held
   C. to be holding
   D. having held

10. The procedure follows long-established routines without any new approach. It is strictly ________.
   A. conventional
   B. innovative
   C. contemporary
   D. progressive

11. None of our usual solutions worked, so we need to ________ by looking at the problem in a completely new way.
   A. pave the way
   B. shun the spotlight
   C. keep it at bay
   D. think outside the box

12. After leaving the football team, Mei ________ pottery and attended her first class.
   A. held up
   B. took up
   C. made up
   D. turned up

13. We should thank the volunteers, without ________ help the event would not have been possible.
   A. where
   B. which
   C. whom
   D. whose

14. The instructions must remain clear. We must not improve speed ________ clarity.
   A. at the mercy of
   B. by means of
   C. at the expense of
   D. in favour of

15. —Let’s keep the two records separate; they measure different things.
—________. Combining them would confuse everyone.
   A. Not at all
   B. That makes sense
   C. You’re welcome
   D. Don’t mention it
