## 作者核对

### 参考表达与逐题依据

| 题 | 参考（可能含变体） | 依据与常见错误边界 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | These old photographs are being preserved with digital equipment by the school. / These old photos are being preserved by the school using digital equipment. | 旧照片作承受者，are being preserved体现正在进行的被动；The school preserves虽然近义却不满足指定结构。 |
| 2 | Although her leg was injured, Nora still finished the race, which made her family proud. / Although Nora had injured her leg, she still finished the race, which made her family proud. | injure的恰当形式与让步关系保留，which从句指完成比赛这件事；仅用and made改写没有满足定语从句要求。 |
| 3 | Many beginners find it challenging to explain this process clearly. / Many beginners find explaining this process clearly a challenge. | it＋形容词补语＋真正宾语，或动名词宾语＋名词补语均满足find结构；challenge可转challenging，不能find it is challenging冒充该宾补结构。 |
| 4 | I still remember the afternoon when your encouragement swept away my doubts. / I still remember that afternoon, when your encouragement swept my doubts away. | when引导修饰afternoon的定语从句，swept away表达打消疑虑；时点与鼓励行为相连，不能仅并列两句而漏规定结构。 |
| 5 | Teachers are concerned that the loss of practice time may affect students' progress. / The teachers are concerned that a loss of practice time might affect the students' progress. | be concerned与loss都须保留，may/might表达可能性；绝对断言一定毁掉进步改变原意，lost不能代替规定的名词loss。 |
