Jon was waiting on Platform Two when he (1) ________ a cloth bag under a bench. Most passengers had already left. He walked (2) ________ the platform to the information desk, but nobody there knew who owned it. A worker asked him to wait while she checked the station's lost-property list.

Looking again, Jon saw a small label hidden (3) ________ the handle. Its edge was (4) ________, but a name and a phone number were still readable. He placed the bag on the desk as (5) ________ as he could, without opening it. He had meant to buy a sandwich before his train arrived; (6) ________, he stayed to explain where he had found the bag.

The worker called the number. A woman answered (7) ________ and said that the bag contained her sketchbook. She was still in the station, searching the shops. A few minutes later, she hurried towards the desk. The space between two waiting groups was (8) ________, so Jon moved his suitcase to let her through.

The woman checked the outside of the bag. Although the label was damaged, the rest (9) ________ clean and dry. “I thought I'd lost all my drawings,” she said with (10) ________. She thanked Jon and the worker. Only then did Jon check the departure board. There was still time for his sandwich.
