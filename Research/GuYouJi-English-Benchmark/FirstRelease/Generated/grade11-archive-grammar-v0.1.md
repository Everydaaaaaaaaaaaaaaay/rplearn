# 高二原创单篇语法候选：居民资料档案

虚构项目，参照[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade11-v0.2.md)及g11.2025.grammar单篇10空；源机器人故事/宣传不复写。阅读短文：无给词空填一个适当单词，给词空用该词的正确形式，给词响应可以多词。

<!-- passage:start -->
(1) ____ (establish) in 2018, the Brook Community Archive collects memories of a fictional riverside neighbourhood. Its volunteers organise three (2) ____ (activity): interviewing residents, sorting photographs and preparing displays. The archive began as (3) ____ small collection in a former shop, but it now occupies two rooms in the community centre.

Many of the records (4) ____ (make) by former residents in the 1980s contain names that younger neighbours do not recognise. Rather than guessing who the people were, the volunteers attach questions to the records and invite families to help. They also ask for permission before (5) ____ (publish) a photograph online. A picture that can be viewed in the centre is not automatically cleared for public use.

The team, (6) ____ members include students and retired workers, meets every Saturday. When families provide corrections, the volunteers explain (7) ____ (clear) what has changed and retain the earlier notes. This practice makes the history of each decision visible. To make the material (8) ____ (access) to visitors who cannot read small print, the team provides large-text copies and recorded descriptions.

The number of records in the archive (9) ____ (increase) steadily since the project began. Nevertheless, the volunteers say that a larger collection is not necessarily a more accurate one. Their aim is (10) ____ (preserve) residents' accounts while keeping uncertainties visible, so that future readers can distinguish a remembered story from a confirmed detail.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 作者回填与合法响应检查

| 空 | 作者允许响应 | 句法/语境与替代检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Established | 句首分词修饰Archive，档案项目被成立，非Establishing主动；Having established缺主动宾语且施受不合。 |
| 2 | activities | three直接限定可数activities；初稿three kinds of activity也可成立，已删kinds of，避免用种类数强行推出名词复数。 |
| 3 | a / one | 首次引入small collection，单数可数，a为常见不定冠词；one也可强调最初仅一小集合，实际回填语义成立。an读音不合，the特指依据未提供；允许集合仍非独立认证穷尽。 |
| 4 | made | records被居民在1980年代制作，过去分词后置；making主动不合，being made与明确过去制作时段不合；were made会与主句contain成为无连接双谓语。 |
| 5 | publishing | before作介词后动名词，隐含主体volunteers；publish原形不合，published与主动出版不合。 |
| 6 | whose | 非限定关系从句中限定members所属team；which不能直接限定members，that不引该非限定从句。 |
| 7 | clearly | 副词修饰explain；clear形容词不修饰此谓语方式，clarity名词不合。 |
| 8 | accessible | make＋宾语＋形容词补语，accessible to visitors；access名词/动词不表达该补语，accessibly不能作为material补语。 |
| 9 | has increased / has been increasing | number单数＋since至今；现在完成和持续完成均与steadily相容，实际回填两分支，不只收预写has increased。 |
| 10 | to preserve | aim is to…说明目标，不受无给词一词规则；preserving也可作表语描述活动目标，作者主动回填后认为语义成立，另允许preserving。 |

向量Established/activities/{a,one}/made/publishing/whose/clearly/accessible/{has increased, has been increasing}/{to preserve, preserving}。无给词仅3、6，均一词；给词9可三词，不能误套初三全部一词规则。回填核查主语number、过去分词施受及两个开放变体分支。
