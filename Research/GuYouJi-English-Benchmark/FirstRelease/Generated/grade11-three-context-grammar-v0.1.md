# 高二原创三篇语法：拍摄、访客卡与学习指南

虚构学习活动，参照[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade11-v0.2.md)及g11.2024.grammar的三篇3/3/4、合计10空。保持全文和各篇条件，不合成一篇；源目标集合是参考，未强制同次逐点复制。

阅读三个独立短文。无给词的空只填一词；给词空用所给词的适当形式，必要时可多词。第4、6、7空无给词，其余为给词空。

### A
<!-- passage:A:start -->
Last summer, Mira decided (1) ______ (film) the plants in a small garden. She improved her work by (2) ______ (compare) new pictures with earlier ones. By the time she joined the school media team in September, she (3) ______ (learn) how to keep a record of each picture's place and date. This earlier experience helped her explain changes that a single picture could not show.
<!-- passage:A:end -->

### B
<!-- passage:B:start -->
(4) ______ the visitors liked the first set of information cards, the team still wanted to know whether the cards worked for people using them without help. Three (5) ______ (volunteer) watched the next visitors and wrote down their questions. The revised cards, (6) ______ showed clearer names and directions, were then tried with another group. Positive comments and practical use were considered separately.
<!-- passage:B:end -->

### C
<!-- passage:C:start -->
The reading club became interested (7) ______ making a guide for new members. The writers asked beginners which explanations they found useful, so that the final guide would be more (8) ______ (help) than the first version. (9) ______ (write) in plain language, the completed guide had already been checked by two club members before it was printed. At nine yesterday morning, copies (10) ______ (place) on a table by helpers, and the first visitors read them after the doors opened.
<!-- passage:C:end -->

## 作者回填、合理变体与未决评分范围

| 空 | 作者预期/可成立的响应 | 条件与替代检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | to film | decide to do表示决定的行动；filming不能直接接此decided，to filming不合不定式结构。 |
| 2 | comparing | by后动作的动名词；compare/to compare不合此介词宾语，compared不是所问主动行为。 |
| 3 | had learned / had learnt | by September加入前的既有经验、how内容宾语，支持过去完成且两拼写变体；simple learned/learnt的叙事可接受性另留教师裁决，不能仅因主键是完成时就自动判错。 |
| 4 | Although / Though / While | liked与still wanted practical test形成让步/对照；While也可能兼时间读法，不因作者想测让步就排除。Whereas可成立的对照读法另留裁决；However无法直接连接所给前置从句。 |
| 5 | volunteers | Three及后续过去主动行为指三个人，名词复数；volunteer单数不合数量。 |
| 6 | which | 逗号包围的非限制从句、cards非人复数、showed缺主语；that不按标准非限制用法放行，who不是本篇卡片身份。 |
| 7 | in | interested in making固定关系；at/on/to不合本句给定构式。 |
| 8 | helpful | useful/direct feedback及more…than说明帮助新成员的程度；helpless虽可派生，却与提供有用说明的目标不符，helpfully不能作be后形容词补足语。 |
| 9 | Written / Having been written | guide是write的承受对象，普通过去分词/完成被动分词均能表达已完成的语言写法；Being written的状态/过程解读是否被completed与before printed排除仍待裁决，不静默承认或判错。 |
| 10 | were placed / had been placed | copies复数、by helpers被动；过去事件和回看已摆好可有两时态读法。were being placed的9点进行解读也可能与之后开门相容，另留裁决；was placed与复数不符。 |

三篇3/3/4，篇内空位分别1—3、4—6、7—10；不把第10的were placed按无给词一词规则拒绝。作者把确定的结构错误、可成立响应、尚需裁决的合理候选分开，没有把列出的集合说成穷尽评分键。
