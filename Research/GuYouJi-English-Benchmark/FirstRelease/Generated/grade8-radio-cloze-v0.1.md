# 初二原创完形候选：校园广播

虚构情境，参照[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade8-v0.2.md)与完整锚g8.2025.cloze的10空四选结构。本文不复制源家庭故事；作者检查不代替教师或学生试做。

阅读短文，从每题A、B、C、D四个选项中选出填入空白处的最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
When our school started a student radio show, I offered to read the weekly notices. I enjoyed writing, but speaking into a microphone made me (1) ____. My hands shook during the first practice.

Before Friday's show, I wrote a notice about a drawing lesson. The room number was 204. I checked it twice, so I thought I was completely (2) ____. But when the red light came on, I read too quickly and said "240" instead.

After the show, Mia came to me with a puzzled look. "I couldn't find the lesson," she said. I looked at my paper. The number on it was right, but the number I had said was (3) ____.

At first, I wanted to blame the old microphone. Then I remembered how clearly everyone could hear the music. The problem was not the (4) ____. It was my hurried reading. I felt sorry because my (5) ____ had sent Mia to another room.

I (6) ____ my mistake to the teacher and asked to make a correction. In the next notice, I gave the right number and read it (7) ____, leaving a short pause between each digit. Mia listened and repeated "204" back to me.

Now I practise with a partner before each show. My partner tells me which words are hard to follow, and I change the way I read them. I have become more (8) ____ because I know how to prepare. I also try to be (9) ____ when someone asks me to repeat a notice. Good radio work isn't just about having a clear voice. It's about making sure the (10) ____ reaches the people who need it.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. A. proud B. sleepy C. nervous D. hungry
2. A. alone B. ready C. silent D. late
3. A. wrong B. useful C. clear D. missing
4. A. timetable B. drawing C. classroom D. equipment
5. A. question B. mistake C. advice D. invitation
6. A. lent B. sold C. admitted D. carried
7. A. slowly B. secretly C. angrily D. suddenly
8. A. frightened B. ashamed C. bored D. confident
9. A. careless B. patient C. absent D. noisy
10. A. furniture B. money C. information D. traffic
