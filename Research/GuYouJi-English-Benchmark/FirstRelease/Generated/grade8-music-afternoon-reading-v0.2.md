# 初二原创成长叙事：At the Welcome Desk

虚构社区音乐活动，依据[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade8-v0.2.md)和完整配套g8.2025fengtai.readingB的成长叙事/三题四选模式。当前午餐说明阅读不代表本体裁，新增独立故事家族，不把旧题换标题计新样本。

阅读短文，从每题所给的四个选项中选出最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
Last month, after my friend Sara described volunteering at a community music afternoon as easy, I agreed to help there. When I was asked to work at the welcome desk, I began to worry because I knew very little about music. What if a visitor asked a question I could not answer?

The night before the event, I studied the programme and tried to remember every musician's name. I even wrote a long welcome speech, which I said as quickly as I could when the first visitors arrived. One man waited until I had finished and then asked where his daughter should leave her violin case. My speech had not answered his question at all.

Sara, who noticed what had happened, did not tell me to learn more names. Instead, she suggested asking each visitor what they needed before giving directions. She also showed me a list of helpers who could answer different questions. I did not have to know everything, but I had to know whom to ask.

Soon a woman came to the desk carrying two bags. I began to point towards the concert room, but she explained that she was bringing materials for the children's activity upstairs. This time, after listening to her whole question, I called the activity helper, who came down to meet her. The woman thanked us for making her first visit easier.

Later, when several families arrived together and a line formed outside the door, I felt nervous again. However, I asked Sara to give out programmes while I helped people with their questions. We worked as a team instead of trying to do every task at the same time.

At the end of the afternoon, the organiser thanked all the volunteers. I had not learned every musician's name, and I still needed help with some questions. Yet I no longer thought that being useful meant having every answer ready. It meant paying attention, finding the right person to help, and speaking up when I needed support. I asked whether I could return for the next event.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. How did the writer first feel about working at the welcome desk?

   A. Proud.
   B. Bored.
   C. Worried.
   D. Angry.

2. What helped the woman with two bags reach the right helper?

   A. Listening before calling the activity helper.
   B. Repeating the welcome speech slowly.
   C. Leaving her bags in the concert room.
   D. Meeting all the musicians in person.

3. What did the writer learn from the volunteer experience?

   A. Learning every name is necessary before helping.
   B. Most visitors need the same welcome speech.
   C. Good volunteers should manage every task alone.
   D. Listening and seeking support can be useful.

## 作者逐题依据与干扰项检查

| 题 | 正解依据 | 各干扰项与范围核对 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 C | 第一段began to worry及担心无法回答，明确初始担忧 | A没有初始自豪表现，末段变化不能换到初始；B备稿/担心问题不是无聊；D没有责怪他人或愤怒描写。 |
| 2 A | 第四段听完整问题后联系activity helper，对方下楼接来访者 | B长欢迎词为第二段男性访客前的旧做法，并未帮此女性找到接应者；C她的材料给楼上儿童活动，concert room只是叙述者未听完时原拟的方向，文中未让她把包留在那里；D没有会见全部音乐家的事件，也不响应她要找儿童活动的需求。 |
| 3 D | 末段明示paying attention/finding the right person/speaking up，前面的询问与分工支撑这一反思 | A末段尚未记全名字仍能帮忙，否定必要条件；B男性放琴盒、女性找活动，长欢迎词并不响应这些不同需要；C排队时主动请Sara分发programme，全文支持协作而非单人包揽。 |

作者完整解题C/A/D，逐项检查说话者/人物、首次尝试/后续改变、原拟路线/实际接应和整体成长。第2只问这一女性得到帮助的过程，不扩大为所有访客都由同一方式解决；第3不把掌握音乐知识写成毫无作用，而是排除必须拥有每个答案的过强条件。三题分别考初始原因、具体行动和整体意义，不要求读者了解音乐专业知识。

实际修订1，完整0.1前稿另存。逐组件比较后，把六对短句重写为时间/原因/关系从句，减少碎片化；同时压缩过长选项，第1改成初始情绪四选，第2/3保留原问题操作和正确事件依据。全文及三题再次解为C/A/D，作者核查不是独立盲评。源体裁/问题操作与语言负荷并列检查，不能仅因正文接近一个源样本就认定初二难度成立。独立教师、学生先修/难度/用时与商业/生产准入未知。
