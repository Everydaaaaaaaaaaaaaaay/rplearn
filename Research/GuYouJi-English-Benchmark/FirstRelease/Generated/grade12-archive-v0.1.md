# 高三原创阅读候选：Community Archive Projects

参照[高三规格](../Specifications/grade12-v0.1.md)与g12.2023.readingA的四简介/3题四选、需求匹配/细节/共性结构。虚构社区档案活动，不引用真实机构承诺。原文、问题与条件组合原创；这是较直接的高三阅读任务候选，不能代表完整备考难度。

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从每题所给的四个选项中选出最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
A community archive is seeking volunteers for four projects. Choose a project that suits your interests and availability. Applicants may join only one project this month, as each team needs time to review its members' work.

**Street Voices**

Volunteers interview long-term residents about shops that have disappeared. The team meets at the community centre on Saturday mornings for three hours. Recording experience is useful, but beginners can receive training. When two residents remember an event differently, volunteers must keep both accounts rather than turn one into an agreed fact. Recordings may be published only after the speakers approve them.

**Photo Notes**

Working from home, volunteers add descriptions to scanned photographs. The task requires two hours in total, which may be spread across the week. No previous experience is needed; a short guide is provided. Volunteers should distinguish dates printed on photographs from dates estimated from clothing or buildings. Estimates must be labelled. Descriptions should be returned by Sunday evening.

**River Records**

This team walks along the river for three hours on Sunday afternoons, comparing current landmarks with old maps. Volunteers must complete safety training before the first walk. Where a map's location cannot be identified confidently, the uncertainty must remain in the record. The team needs observations, not attractive guesses about where a building once stood.

**Letter Transcripts**

Volunteers work from home for at least four hours each week. Previous experience in reading historical handwriting is required. The task is to type letters exactly as written, without improving their grammar. An unreadable word should be marked as unclear instead of replaced with a likely one. Original spellings are retained, even when a modern reader might regard them as mistakes.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. Which project best suits someone with no experience who can offer only two hours this week?

   A. Street Voices.
   B. River Records.
   C. Photo Notes.
   D. Letter Transcripts.

2. What is required before a Street Voices recording can be published?

   A. Both residents must remember every event in the same way.
   B. The speakers must agree to its publication.
   C. The interview must take place in a shop that has disappeared.
   D. The volunteer must have previous recording experience.

3. What principle is shared by the four projects?

   A. Volunteers should make every record easy by removing differences.
   B. Volunteers must replace uncertain information with a likely answer.
   C. Only information that has a known date should enter the archive.
   D. The limits of what is known should be visible in the records.

## 答案与实际作者审核

答案：C/B/D。

| 题 | 正解与条件检查 | 干扰项排除 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Photo Notes无经验也可、总共2小时；同时满足两个条件 | A可培训但需3小时，不只按beginner选它；B3小时且安全培训；D在家不等于时间够，至少4小时且需既有经验 |
| 2 | Street Voices最后句只有speakers approve后可出版，B是语义改述 | A原明确记忆不同须保留两种；C采访消失商店相关居民，不是要求在已消失店中采访；D经验只是useful，初学可训不是必需 |
| 3 | 街访不同记忆保留、照片估日期标记、地图不能确信位置留不确定、信件不可读词标不明；D涵盖四种限制 | A删差异违街访；B猜替代违地图/信件；C照片明确允许估日期并标注，不因日期未知一概拒存 |

真实修正：初稿Photo Notes只说“每次两小时”，与每周总时长需求不匹配，改为two hours in total及可分散。初稿Letter Transcripts只说“经验优先”，无法明确排除零经验候选，最终依虚构项目需求写required。第3题初稿概括“保证档案完全准确”，四项只要求保留分歧/估计/不明，不能保证完全准确，改为知识边界可见。

复解时只用题面条件，不补申请人具体哪天有空。第2C是字面误解而不是要用户猜实体店现状。第3要整合四卡，不把所有未知都判错误。完成者给出的日期/活动约束均属于虚构项目；独立复核、目标学生表现和较难说明/论证对照仍待完成。
