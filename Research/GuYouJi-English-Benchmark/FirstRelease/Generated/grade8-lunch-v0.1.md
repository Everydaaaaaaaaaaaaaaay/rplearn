# 初二原创阅读候选：A Lunchroom Notebook

普通初二阅读首轮候选，虚构学校与观察活动。参照[初二规格](../Specifications/grade8-v0.1.md)及g8.2025.readingC的过程、比较和限定结论结构，使用新的材料与问题。作者审核后候选，非学生标定题。

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从每题所给的四个选项中选出最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
When students at Brook School complained about food waste, the lunch club wanted to do something. Some members suggested putting up colourful posters. Nora thought they should first find out what was happening. For one week, the club counted the trays returned with unfinished rice and wrote the numbers in a notebook.

The notes showed that many students left rice on their trays. However, the club did not ask why they had left it. Nora spoke to several students. Some said the usual serving was too large, although they liked the rice. Others said they did not enjoy eating rice that day. The same unfinished food could have different reasons.

The next week, students could choose a smaller serving and ask for more later. The menu stayed the same as in the first week. Club members counted unfinished trays at the same time each day. Fewer trays came back with rice on them. Nora was pleased, but she reminded the club that a clean tray did not show how much food a student had eaten. Someone who took less rice might simply have had less to leave.

The club decided to keep the choice of serving sizes and continue its notes for another month. It would also ask whether students had enough to eat. The posters went up too, but the club could not tell how much they had helped. At the next meeting, Nora brought the notebook instead of announcing that the problem was solved.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. Why did Nora want the club to count unfinished trays first?

   A. To learn whether students liked the posters.
   B. To learn about the situation before making a change.
   C. To learn why each student had left rice.
   D. To find out how much rice each student had eaten.

2. What stayed the same during the two weeks?

   A. The choices of serving size.
   B. The number of unfinished trays.
   C. The food offered on the menu.
   D. The reasons students gave for leaving rice.

3. What did Nora think about the cleaner trays?

   A. They proved every student had eaten more rice.
   B. They showed the posters had solved the problem.
   C. They meant all students now liked the rice.
   D. They did not tell the club how much students had eaten.

4. Why did the club plan to ask whether students had enough to eat?

   A. To check that less unfinished food did not hide another problem.
   B. To measure how much students remembered the posters.
   C. To persuade all students to choose the smaller serving.
   D. To compare the rice with a different lunch menu.

## 答案与实际作者检查

答案：1 B；2 C；3 D；4 A。

| 题 | 正解依据 | 各干扰项的排除理由 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 第一段find out what was happening及先计数后改动，支持先了解现状 | A海报尚未贴出且计数不测喜好；C第二段明确首次未问原因，之后才访谈；D盘数不测每人实际吃掉的量，第三段再次限定 |
| 2 | 第三段明确menu stayed the same；计数时点也保持，但选项不另设该真项 | A第二周新增小份；B第二周更少；D未作两周原因相同的比较，不能由同菜推出 |
| 3 | 第三段clean tray不能显示吃掉多少，随后举取少可能剩少 | A把剩余下降改成摄入增加；B海报贡献明确未知；C第一周有人当天不喜欢，未说全部改变口味 |
| 4 | 第四段继续记录并问是否吃够，连第三段少取少剩的替代解释，支持排查减少剩余是否伴随吃不足 | B吃够问题不测记住海报多少；C让学生选份量，不劝全体都选小份；D两周菜单保持相同，没有换菜单比较 |

真实修正：初稿第2项比较时间只问“第二周保留了什么”，容易把持续记录理解为另一正确方向；最终锁定“两周保持不变”并确保四项只一个有明确同一性依据。第4项原拟“证明每个人都吃得更健康”，不能由本材料测量推出，已改为检查是否吃够；正确选项保留与吃够问题的跨句联系。第1/4题早期干扰项含厨师职业/海报颜色，太离题，改为同一观察活动中的测量对象、访谈目的或干预范围混淆。以上为作者修改，不冒充独立解题。

逐项尝试其他选项：第2项D的原因可能现实中相似，但文章没有第二周原因比较；题面是文内理解，不能由可能性取代证据。第4项只说检查潜在问题，不把它写成已经发生饥饿。篇章不宣称真实学校实验或因果验证。难度为作者暂定中等，真实年级适配/用时未知；第二来源和独立复核仍待完成。
