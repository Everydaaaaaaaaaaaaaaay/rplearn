# 初三原创阅读候选：图书修复

参考[初三规格](../Specifications/grade9-v0.1.md)和g9.2026.readingC的问题—方法—限制—行动说明结构，文章为虚构学校活动，不复写源塑料技术/医学主张。学生题面不预显标题，保留整体标题题与加粗指代词。

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从每题所给的四个选项中选出最佳选项。

<!-- passage:start -->
At Meadow School, some library books had loose covers or torn pages. Their information was still useful, but students found them difficult to read. Instead of throwing every damaged book away, the library started a small repair club. Its members soon discovered that different problems needed different kinds of help.

For a book with a loose cover and all its pages, the club could make a new cover under the librarian's guidance. The new cover held the pages together again. A book with missing pages was a different matter. The librarian first tried to find the missing pages or another complete copy. Without the missing information, a neat cover alone could not make the book ready for readers. Such a book stayed off the shelves until the problem was dealt with.

Repairing books allowed existing copies to be borrowed again. **It** also gave club members a chance to learn practical skills. However, the work took time. Sometimes a book was too badly damaged to repair. The librarian then decided whether to replace it. The club kept notes about what had been done, so the next member would not have to start by guessing.

The school supported the club in several ways. A teacher helped members practise on old notebooks before they worked on library books. Families gave the club materials for new covers. Students who did not repair books checked that returned copies had no loose pages before they went back on the shelves. Once a month, the librarian reviewed the books still waiting for help. The aim was to keep useful books available, while making sure that their pages could be read in the correct order.
<!-- passage:end -->

1. Why did the library start the repair club?

   A. All the books contained information that was out of date.
   B. Some useful books were physically difficult to read.
   C. Students wanted to replace the library with a workshop.
   D. The library had stopped lending books to students.

2. Which book could become usable just by getting a new cover?

   A. A book with an important section missing.
   B. A book too badly damaged to repair.
   C. A complete book whose cover had become loose.
   D. A book whose missing information had not been found.

3. What does the bold word “It” in Paragraph 3 refer to?

   A. Repairing books.
   B. Borrowing books.
   C. Replacing books.
   D. Keeping notes.

4. What does the last paragraph mainly describe?

   A. How families chose books to borrow.
   B. Why the library stopped using old notebooks.
   C. Which books contained the most useful information.
   D. How different people supported the repair work.

5. Which would be the best title for the text?

   A. A New Cover Solves Every Book Problem
   B. Giving Useful Books Another Chance
   C. Replacing All the Old Library Books
   D. Finding Missing Pages: The Club's Only Task

## 答案与实际作者审核

答案：B/C/A/D/B。

| 题 | 正解依据 | 干扰项排除依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 首段useful信息与loose/torn导致难读，B同时保留两条件 | A与still useful不符；C没有学生要撤图书馆；D改为停借全馆，文中只对未修好的个别书暂下架 |

| 4 | 教师训练、家庭材料、学生检查、管理员月复查共同为支持修复 | A只取家庭并换成借书选择；B旧本用来训练而非停用；C没有按内容价值排序，不能由末句aim改成本段主题 |

真实修正：初稿最后段只有教师一句与“应当保护书”结论，段落重点题容易在支持措施/价值判断间摇摆；补成四种实际支持行为，明确本段功能。初稿同时在题面上方显示英文候选标题，整体标题题可照抄，最终移除学生题面的标题。第2题在“新封皮”前增加all its pages条件，避免将缺页书也视为完成。

复核限制：第1题D把个别未修书下架扩大为全馆停借，是范围干扰；不把缺少全馆停借的信息说成外部事实否定。第3题强调活动指代，保持标记。作者暂判基础至中等说明文，未测学生表现；新编修复活动不宣称真实案例或完整技术指导。第二来源、更多体裁和真人复核仍待完成。
