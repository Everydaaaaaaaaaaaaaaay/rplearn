# 高三原创单篇语法候选：便携演示台

虚构工作坊，参照[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade12-v0.2.md)及g12.2025anshan.grammar单篇10空。无给词空填一个适当单词，给词空使用正确形式，可多词。

<!-- passage:start -->
(1) ____ (design) for a fictional travelling science club, a portable demonstration table can be folded into a box. Nora, a student volunteer, developed the first model after visiting two school (2) ____ (workshop). She wanted visiting teams to carry less equipment without losing space for small experiments.

The club meets in a hall (3) ____ the storage cupboards are already full. Finding enough room for another large table (4) ____ (be) therefore a continuing problem. Nora suggested a folding model, but she did not assume that a smaller package would automatically be easier to use.

During an early trial, one volunteer moved the folded box through a narrow doorway while another checked the handles. The pair worked (5) ____ (careful), stopping whenever the box caught on the frame. Their notes showed that the handle positions mattered as much as the size of the box. Nora made the next model easier to carry by (6) ____ (adjust) the handles.

She later asked three different teams to test it. A measurement (7) ____ (record) during only one attempt was not treated as proof of the table's performance in every school. The club compared the teams' notes and took differences in the rooms they had used (8) ____ account. Some doorways were narrow; others had steps.

Since the trial began, Nora (9) ____ (collect) both favourable comments and reports of difficulties. She was pleased (10) ____ (finish) the first trial before the club's public demonstration, but she said that an encouraging trial was a reason to continue checking, not a guarantee that the table would suit every setting.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 作者回填与合法变体

| 空 | 允许响应 | 依据与替代检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Designed | 句首过去分词，table被设计；Designing与table主动设计不合；Having designed缺主动宾语。 |
| 2 | workshops | two限定具体工作坊复数，workshop单数不合。 |
| 3 | where | hall地点、从句主谓宾完整；which/that不能作无介词地点状语，一词限制排除in which。 |
| 4 | is | 动名词短语Finding…作单数主语，continuing problem现在状况；are不一致，was与当前持续陈述不合。 |
| 5 | carefully | 修饰worked方式；careful形容词不合，care名词/动词不合副词位置。 |
| 6 | adjusting | by后动名词带直接宾语the handles；adjust原形不合介词补语，adjustment后不能直接接the handles。初稿thus making/to make的结果/目的界限没有裁决清楚，已改成明确介词补语后重解。 |
| 7 | recorded | measurement被记录的后置分词；recording主动不合；was recorded与主句was not treated无连接。 |
| 8 | into | take differences into account固定搭配，其他介词不组成此account整体；初稿differences ___ the rooms可between/among/in/across等，已改变句子以收紧目标，不把合理变体判错。 |
| 9 | has collected / has been collecting | since至今，Nora单数；可现有累积或持续收集。过去时不表达本段持续至今视角。 |
| 10 | to finish / to have finished | pleased＋不定式；过去完成试验可用一般不定式表达成功动作，完成不定式强调先完成；两者实际回填，不要求给词只有一词。 |

向量Designed/workshops/where/is/carefully/adjusting/recorded/into/{has collected,has been collecting}/{to finish,to have finished}。多分支目前为同作者语境判断，生产不能把此列表当已认证穷尽集合。第6实际改为by adjusting，不用不确定的thus to make分支作为正确答案；第8以take…into account收紧原介词关系多解。
