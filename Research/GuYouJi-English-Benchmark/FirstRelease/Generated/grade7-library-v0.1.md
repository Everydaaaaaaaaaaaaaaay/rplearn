# 初一原创阅读候选：A better school library

原创虚构情境。规格依据：[初一0.1](../Specifications/grade7-v0.1.md)；结构参考g7.2021.readingB，人物、事件、文章、问题和选项为新写。作者检查已记录，独立复核与学生标定待做。

请阅读短文，从每题所给的A、B、C三个选项中选出最佳答案。

Last month, our school library had two problems. Some students talked loudly at lunchtime, and new students often could not find the books they wanted. Three students decided to help. Here is what they did.

**Jon**

I made a map of the library. It showed where students could find stories, science books and books about art. At first, I put the map behind the front desk. However, students could not see it easily there. So I moved it to the wall beside the door. Now students can look at it when they come in.

**Mina**

I wanted to read stories to younger students, but I felt nervous at first. My hands were cold, and I spoke so quietly that they could hardly hear me. I practised with a friend after school. A week later, I could read clearly. The younger students asked me to come back the next Friday.

**Leo**

I made a sign asking everyone to speak quietly. I also talked to students who were making too much noise. I spoke to them in a friendly way, and most of them listened. We kept our map and sign in the library. We also showed two new volunteers how to use them.

1. Why did the three students decide to help?

   A. To make the library easier to use.
   B. To buy more books for the library.
   C. To keep the library open after school.

2. Why did Jon move his map?

   A. It showed the wrong places for the books.
   B. Students could not see it easily behind the desk.
   C. Mina wanted to put her books behind the desk.

3. What does “nervous” mean in Mina's story?

   A. Excited and ready to celebrate.
   B. Angry because someone was noisy.
   C. Worried about doing something.

4. What did Leo do about the noise?

   A. He asked the younger students to leave.
   B. He made a sign and spoke to students kindly.
   C. He moved all the books to another room.

5. What can we learn from the last two sentences?

   A. Only the three students were allowed to help.
   B. The map and sign would be thrown away next term.
   C. Other volunteers would be able to continue the work.

## 答案与作者检查

答案：1 A；2 B；3 C；4 B；5 C。

| 题 | 答案依据 | 干扰项检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 开篇两问题及三人的解决措施，都指向改善使用体验 | B未买书；C未改变开放时间 |
| 2 | Jon明确说旧位置难看见，因此移到门旁墙上 | A不是位置标错；C将人物与行动混合，文中没有该要求 |
| 3 | 手冷、声音小、需要练习提供担心表现的语境，后文清楚朗读构成变化 | A不是庆祝；B将Leo解决的噪声归给Mina |
| 4 | Leo先做安静标志，再友好提醒吵闹学生 | A混淆朗读对象与被提醒者，并新增赶走；C没有搬书 |
| 5 | 保留材料并教新志愿者使用，目的是延续工作 | A与新志愿者参与冲突；B与保留材料冲突 |

检查结果：五题各有一个最佳选项，依据均在本文；不需要外部知识。范围/人物/时点和题文一致；上下文词义未提前释义；正确选项长度没有统一最长规律。第5题是跨两句的短跨度推断，不由“新志愿者”推断他们已经完成工作。

实际修正：原末句直接说明延续工作的目的，使第5题只是照句改述；已删去该目的从句，让第5题结合保留材料和教新志愿者两句获得结论，仍保留明确依据。
