# 初三原创短文填空候选：Our Weather Notebook

依据[初三规格0.1](../Specifications/grade9-v0.1.md)和g9.2026.grammar，单篇10空、语境/给词、所有响应限一词。虚构学校观察活动，不引用真实研究结果，不把记录天数等当学生或模型表现数据。

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据语境或括号内单词的提示，在每个空格内填入一个恰当的词。每空只填一个词，要求意义准确、形式正确，使短文完整连贯。

<!-- passage:start -->
Our school weather club (1) ______ (begin) last term. At first, we only wrote down whether each day was sunny or rainy. Then our science teacher showed us a way to compare the days more carefully.

We are proud (2) ______ our notebook, but we know it cannot tell us everything about the weather. As (3) ______ result, we write notes about how each record was made. We keep both the numbers (4) ______ these notes. If a reading looks strange, we check it instead of quietly changing it.

(5) ______ (Lucky), a few older students have offered to help. All the younger club (6) ______ (member) can ask them how to use the tools. The notebook is kept in a dry box that is (7) ______ (close) after each meeting.

Friday is one of the (8) ______ (busy) days for our club. We enjoy (9) ______ (share) our weekly notes with other classes. If they have questions, we consider (10) ______ (they) questions and answer as clearly as we can. Sometimes our answer is that we do not know yet. The notebook helps us ask better questions, not pretend that we have all the answers.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 允许答案与实际作者检查

| 空 | 允许响应 | 约束与替代检查 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | began | last term的已发生开始事件，begin过去式；begin/begins不保持该过去时点；had begun是多词，不符合本任务每空一词 |
| 2 | of | be proud of固定搭配，所自豪对象notebook；for/about不属于本给定搭配 |
| 3 | a | as a result固定词组；the/an不符合该形式 |
| 4 | and | both the numbers and these notes；or/but不构成此两项并列结构 |
| 5 | Luckily | 句首副词修饰老生愿帮这一幸运情况；Lucky形容词缺所修饰名词，Luck名词不符合本句独立副词功能 |
| 6 | members | All the younger club members的all约束可数成员复数；member单数不符合all，members'缺所限定名词 |
| 7 | closed | is closed被关上/保持关闭，after each meeting；close表示近等不同意义，不符合收箱结束活动语境；closing缺被动含义并变动作状态 |
| 8 | busiest | one of the＋最高级＋复数days；busy/busier不符合；most busy多词亦不合一词规则 |
| 9 | sharing | enjoy＋动名词，share正确形式；share/ shared不能作本结构宾语；to share既多词又不符合enjoy结构 |
| 10 | their | questions前需要所有格限定词；they主格、them宾格、theirs独立所有格不能按标准书面语直接限定后面的questions |

实际填答：began / of / a / and / Luckily / members / closed / busiest / sharing / their。填回全文，过去的成立与目前记录/储存/分享常规相容；每空一词，不把高中合法多词模式带入。第7接受状态/被动两种理解，都对应同一个closed。

实际修正：已保存写入的首稿。第6原The younger club member也可作单数主语，原“无冠词”的排除理由错误，改All the younger...强制复数。第10原we answer them有questions先行词，theirs也可表达“他们的问题”，不能据原键them排除；改为questions前的所有格空，重解收their。学习难度/用时及独立复核未知，结构匹配不证明全国初三总体标准已标定。
