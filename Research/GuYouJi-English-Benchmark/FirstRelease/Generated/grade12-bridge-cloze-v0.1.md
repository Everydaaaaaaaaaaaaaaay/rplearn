# 高三原创15空完形候选：桥边的展览

虚构项目叙事，参照[规格0.2](../Specifications/grade12-v0.2.md)与g12.2025anshan.cloze的15空四选、长期行动与回馈结构。阅读短文，选择各空的最佳选项。

For years, Mara had photographed the old footbridge near her village. She liked the way its shape changed with the seasons. When a local exhibition invited residents to contribute, she decided to (1) ____ a series of her pictures.

At the exhibition meeting, a younger organiser suggested displaying only the most beautiful view. Mara initially (2) ____. A single polished photograph would be easier to arrange. But an older resident asked where the temporary crossing had been during a winter repair. Mara searched her collection and found no (3) ____ of it. She had avoided photographing anything that spoiled the familiar view.

The question made her reconsider the purpose of the exhibition. Was she presenting a bridge, or only the bridge she (4) ____ to remember? She returned to the river with a notebook and began asking people about their journeys across it.

Their accounts did not always (5) ____. One resident remembered the repair starting in November; another insisted it was December. Instead of choosing the more confident speaker, Mara (6) ____ both accounts and looked for dated notices. A notice showed that work had been announced in November but the crossing had closed in December. The two people had remembered different (7) ____ of the same event.

She also found a photograph in a neighbour's drawer. It showed the temporary crossing, but a child's face was clearly visible. The neighbour permitted Mara to examine the picture, not to (8) ____ it. She respected that limit and used a drawing of the crossing in the exhibition instead.

Preparing the display took longer than she had (9) ____. Each new conversation raised questions about something she had once regarded as obvious. Yet the extra work gave the photographs a richer (10) ____. They were no longer simply attractive views; they were connected to people's daily lives.

At the opening, visitors paused beside a panel explaining the two repair dates. Some added their own memories. Mara could not (11) ____ every detail, but she could show where each claim came from and what remained uncertain.

The organiser later asked what she had learned. "A good record needs more than a steady camera," Mara replied. "It needs (12) ____ to notice what your favourite view leaves out." She continued photographing the river, now paying attention to ordinary crossings as well as special light. Her project had (13) ____ from a collection of personal favourites into a shared investigation. For Mara, its value lay less in producing a (14) ____ picture than in giving others a reason to (15) ____ their own stories.

| 1 | D | 展览邀请投稿，submit作品；destroy/hide与投稿相反，lend借出非提交展品的主要意图。 |
