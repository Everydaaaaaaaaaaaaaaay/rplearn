# 大学英语六级：共享计算资源的十空词库选择

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A—O中为每空选一个最合适的词，填写字母。词形不变；每项最多使用一次，五项不用。以下为虚构大学项目情境。

<!-- passage:start -->
A fictional university is testing a new policy for its shared computing service. (1) ____, every research team received the same number of hours, regardless of the size of its experiments. This arrangement was easy to explain, but requests soon exceeded the total processing (2) ____ of the machines. The staff could not create extra computing power simply by changing the timetable.

The revised policy allows staff to (3) ____ more time to projects that can justify a temporary increase. Teams must describe the calculation they intend to run, rather than merely ask for the largest possible allowance. Deadlines are deliberately (4) ____ : users can move them within an agreed period instead of being assigned one fixed date. This choice helps researchers adapt their plans when an experiment produces unexpected results.

To (5) ____ demand, technicians record the length of each job and inspect waiting times every morning. They test the recording system repeatedly, accepting it as (6) ____ only when its readings agree with an independent timer within a stated tolerance. The new records make patterns easier to identify. (7) ____ , accurate information alone does not settle which competing project deserves priority; that still requires a decision under the published policy.

No team was required to take part in the optional follow-up. A (8) ____ trial, conducted after the first one and lasting only ten days, explored another problem: duplicate requests. To prevent a team from receiving two allowances under different names, staff now (9) ____ access to one verified account per team. Users may also (10) ____ a request if they wish to cancel it completely before processing begins. The project report treats these procedures as local experiments, not as evidence that one allocation policy would suit every institution.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：E（initially）

开头对照旧的平均分配与后文revised policy，initially限定最初阶段。nevertheless需要前项转折，本句尚未引入可转折的先前主张，不能承担“最初”的篇章功能。

### 2：C（capacity）

processing capacity表示机器总处理能力，与下一句cannot create extra computing power呼应。participant是参与者，不是处理能力；其他词不能在total processing之后表示这一资源总量。

### 3：B（allocate）

allocate more time to projects为向项目分配更多时间，符合临时增加的定义。restrict是限制而非增加；monitor/overlook不表达分配；withdraw more time to projects不搭配。

### 4：D（flexible）

下一句明确可以在约定期间移动截止日期且没有单一固定日期，flexible最合适。mandatory只说强制性，不解释可移动；permanent与这里按研究计划调整不合；adverse表示不利，不能概括后文这种弹性安排；reliable描述可靠性，不描述可移动性。

### 5：G（monitor）

record length及inspect waiting times是监测需求的操作，monitor对应。allocate/restrict demand分别指分配/限制需求，未体现此处记录观察；overlook是忽视，与持续检查相反；withdraw demand是撤回要求，不是跟踪需求。

### 6：L（reliable）

重复与独立计时器在容差内一致，是本段对记录可靠性的明确检验，reliable对应。flexible为可改变，不由准确读数推得；mandatory/permanent为强制/永久，均无此依据；adverse为不利而非读数一致。

### 7：H（nevertheless）

前一句说记录使规律更清楚，后句强调准确数据仍不能决定优先权，是让步转折nevertheless。initially只标初期，不能表达此处“准确但不自动裁决”的论证关系，其余选项不作此句间副词。

### 8：N（subsequent）

after the first one和only ten days限定后一轮短期试验，subsequent表示后续。mandatory与optional且No team was required冲突；permanent与十天结束冲突；adverse要求an且不表这一时序。flexible/reliable已由第4/6空语境及每项最多一次约束占用，不可在第8空重用；不能只凭词性声称所有形容词都不合法。

### 9：M（restrict）

restrict access to one verified account表示限定每队仅一账号，直接实现prevent duplicate allowances。allocate access to one account只表示分配入口而不表达限制数量；monitor/overlook/withdraw分别为监测/忽视/撤销，不能表达允许一个但排除重复账号。

### 10：O（withdraw）

wish to cancel it completely及before processing限定完全撤回尚未处理请求，withdraw对应。restrict会缩减范围而非完全取消；monitor为观察，allocate为分配，overlook为忽视，都不构成申请人正式取消请求这一行动。

完整向量E/C/B/D/G/L/H/N/M/O，十个不同词库项，无词形变化；A/F/I/J/K五项不用。必须按整段语境及完整一次分配比较候选，不把每空拆成独立文章。页面各空重复显示同一15项只为选择控件，不是150个独立词项。

形式复用已核官方2016六级样卷第26—35题，一篇10空/15词、每项最多一次；不是新增来源、年度真卷或六级产品人群/CEFR标定。给词原形不变，不对自由补全用AI判分。内容为原创虚构共享计算项目，与源在线旅行评价材料分开，不是现实科研资源政策建议。作者依据及全部同类干扰反证不等独立教师、学生、商用、生产验证。
