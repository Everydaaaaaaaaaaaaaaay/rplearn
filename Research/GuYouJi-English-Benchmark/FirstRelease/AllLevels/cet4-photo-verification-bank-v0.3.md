# 大学英语四级：校园图片核实的十空词库选择

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A—O中为每空选一个最合适的词，填写字母。词形不变，每项最多使用一次，五项不用。本文为虚构校园报道案例，不需要额外图片。

<!-- passage:start -->
An unusual photograph was shared (1) ____ across Westford College's student network. It seemed to show water covering the library floor, and several class groups passed it along. The student newspaper considered publishing a warning. However, its editor wanted (2) ____ before describing the picture as a real incident. The reporters decided to (3) ____ information from the person who had taken it.

One reporter was (4) ____ for contacting the photographer; this was her assigned duty, not the editor's. Her job was to (5) ____ what the picture actually showed from what the comments claimed. She used a (6) ____ method that required no special equipment: she asked to see the original, uncropped photograph. Its edges revealed a table beneath a small model of the library. The water belonged to an art project, not a flooded building.

The editor wanted a (7) ____ between reporting quickly and checking facts. The team chose to (8) ____ the alarming caption with a short explanation of how the image had been misunderstood. It kept the picture so that readers could compare the original claim with the corrected account.

To (9) ____ students to check surprising images, the newspaper published a friendly guide rather than blaming people who had shared the mistake. The reporters also saved their notes so that future colleagues would not (10) ____ time repeating the same investigation.

## 作者核对

### 1：O（widely）

across the student network及several class groups passed it along表传播范围，widely对应。accurately只表准确程度，不能概括多群体传播；其他候选在此不构成方式/范围副词。

### 2：F（evidence）

editor wanted something before treating picture as real，需要evidence而非单凭图片印象。waste/balance虽可作名词，均不表证明事件真实的依据；后文核原图进一步确认这一证据功能。

### 3：D（collect）

from the person who had taken it指定向拍摄者取得资料，collect information搭配及动作相合。separate为分离已有信息，此处尚在获取；replace需旧资料与替换对象；balance不表达向来源收集信息。

### 4：K（responsible）

assigned duty且排除editor明确责任归属，be responsible for符合。fragile表示脆弱/易损，不表承担分派的任务；practical/frequent/immediate/traditional均不表达谁负责联系。

### 5：L（separate）

separate what the picture showed from what comments claimed表示区分图像内容和他人声称。collect/replace不能表达这一X from Y辨别关系；balance是权衡而非分清来源不同的两个说法；encourage不符合此结构。

### 6：I（practical）

no special equipment及ask for original说明方法凭现有条件能实行，practical最合适。fragile指脆弱/易损而不表这种可行性；traditional无历史传统依据；frequent/immediate只表频率/时间；responsible不表实施条件。旧available可表示可用，与practical构成不必要的近义竞争，本版已撤下。

### 7：C（balance）

a balance between A and B表示在迅速报道与核事实之间取得平衡。evidence/waste不表达两个目标之间求协调；不能只因balance也能作动词就在本名词槽排除它。

### 8：J（replace）

replace X with Y表示用核正说明替换惊慌标题。collect/separate不表用新标题替代旧标题；balance X with Y是权衡，不会实施后文caption被改正的结果；encourage不以caption作此被劝行动者。

### 9：E（encourage）

friendly guide的目的在促使学生核图，encourage students to check符合。collect/replace/separate/balance不形成这一对人的劝导不定式结构；本句没有要求改变词形。

### 10：N（waste）

saved notes避免后来同事重复同次调查，是不浪费时间，waste time doing符合。collect/replace/balance/encourage不能在此表达因重复调查而白费时间；既有记录可复用的因果由本段明示。
