# 大学英语四级：午餐盒试行的十空词库选择

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A—O中为每空选一个最合适的词，填写字母。词形不变，每项最多使用一次，五项不用。本文为虚构校园案例。

<!-- passage:start -->
When Westford College planned a reusable lunch-box scheme, the canteen staff first had to (1) ____ information about how students ate. They asked each class where its members usually had lunch and how many boxes they threw away. Reducing packaging (2) ____ was the main aim, but a hopeful prediction would not be enough. The team needed (3) ____ from actual use before buying a large supply.

A small trial followed. Staff checked the scales against known weights so that they could measure discarded material (4) ____. They rejected a washing plan that required machines the college did not own. Instead, they chose a (5) ____ arrangement that could work with ordinary sinks and shelves. The trial would not (6) ____ throwaway packaging with reusable boxes throughout the college immediately; only two classes joined at first.

To (7) ____ students to bring boxes back, the team posted friendly reminders near classroom doors. It also sought a (8) ____ between allowing enough return time and keeping enough boxes for the next lunch. Each borrower was (9) ____ for rinsing a box after use; the agreement assigned that duty to users, not canteen workers.

Staff then had to (10) ____ cracked boxes from intact ones, placing the two groups in different trays. They recorded losses as well as successful returns. A final decision would consider these difficulties alongside the amount of packaging avoided.
<!-- passage:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：D（collect）

ask each class取得吃饭和丢盒信息，是collect information。separate只表示分开已有资料，未表达首次获取；balance强调权衡，不能代此收集动作；encourage/replace不与information在此构成所述采集行为。

### 2：N（waste）

前文boxes they threw away与reducing packaging指减少包装废物，waste对应。evidence是本试验要取得而非减少的证据；balance为平衡，不能指被丢弃包装；其他词不构成这一包装废物名词槽。

### 3：F（evidence）

from actual use与前句hopeful prediction对照，需要实际证据evidence。waste虽来自使用但并不等用来支持采购决策的依据，且第2空已受包装丢弃语境约束；balance不能由from actual use在此表达实际证据。

### 4：A（accurately）

用已知重量校验秤是为准确测量，accurately对应。widely表示广泛，不表示已知重量校准带来的准确性；其余选项在此不能充当测量方式副词。

### 5：I（practical）

required machines the college did not own与后文ordinary sinks and shelves形成可行性对比，practical概括可实行安排。available只说可获得，不解释设备约束；traditional不能从普通水槽推出历史传统；frequent/immediate分别说频率/立即，均不回答能否凭现有设备实行；responsible形容安排不表该可行性关系。

### 6：J（replace）

replace X with Y是以可重复盒替代一次性包装。collect/separate为收集/分开而非替代；balance X with Y是权衡两者而非把旧包装换成新盒；encourage不以throwaway packaging作此被鼓励的行动者。

### 7：E（encourage）

friendly reminders以促使归还为目的，encourage students to bring boxes back符合结构和意图。collect/separate/replace不能以students to bring作此动词宾补；balance也不构成这一不定式劝导结构。

### 8：C（balance）

a balance between A and B表示在归还宽限与可用库存之间求平衡。evidence/waste不是在这两项之间求取的权衡；balance虽兼动词，此处由a和between限定为名词，不按单一词性标签判断全篇。

### 9：K（responsible）

后文agreement assigned that duty to users明确责任归属，be responsible for表示负责。available只说可参与而不表示承担义务；practical是可行而非担责；frequent/immediate/traditional不能表达指定谁洗盒。

### 10：L（separate）

两组放different trays明确要把破损与完好盒分开，separate X from Y对应。collect X from Y是从某来源收集，但intact ones不是破损盒的来源；replace不能用from表达以另一组替代；balance/encourage不表分类分置。

完整向量D/N/F/A/I/J/E/C/K/L，B/G/H/M/O五项不用。词形不变，每项最多使用一次；完整十空回填并结合前后信息/搭配/全局分配比较，不把题干截成六空。正文不是现实节能研究，普通设备可行性和责任属于本案例明示条件。
