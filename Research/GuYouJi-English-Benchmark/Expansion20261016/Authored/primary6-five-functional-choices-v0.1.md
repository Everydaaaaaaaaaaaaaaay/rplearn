# 情境功能选择：primary6-five-functional-choices

## 学生题面

根据中文情境选择最合适的一句英语。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. 你想知道去邮局的路，先向路人问路。
   A. How much is the stamp?
   B. Could you tell me the way to the post office?
   C. What time do you finish work?

2. 你想知道朋友昨天做了什么。
   A. What did you do yesterday?
   B. What are you doing now?
   C. What will you do tomorrow?

3. 你没有听清对方的话，请他再说一遍。
   A. You are welcome.
   B. Never mind.
   C. Could you say that again, please?

4. 朋友建议周六一起骑车，你同意这个建议。
   A. That sounds like a good idea.
   B. I am sorry to hear that.
   C. Where is your bike?

5. 你想知道这本书是谁的。
   A. What is the book about?
   B. Whose book is this?
   C. How many books do you have?

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：B

B询问去邮局的路线；A问价格，C问下班时间。

### 2：A

yesterday要求过去问句A；B当前、C未来。

### 3：C

C请求重复；A回应感谢、B宽慰不表达重复请求。

### 4：A

A接受建议；B遗憾、C问地点均不表达同意。

### 5：B

B问所属；A内容，C数量。
