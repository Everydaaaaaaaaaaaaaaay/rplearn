# 小五：多信息卡正误判断

## 学生题面

阅读全部四张社团卡，判断每句话是否与信息相符，选T或F。

| A. Paper Models | B. Rhythm Room |
| --- | --- |
| For pupils who enjoy making things. | For pupils who enjoy music. |
| Tuesday, 15:30—16:15 | Monday and Thursday, 15:30—16:15 |
| Room 2 | Music Room |
| Build small paper houses and bridges. Work with a partner. | Learn short songs. Try simple rhythms on drums. |

| C. Nature Notes | D. Puzzle Partners |
| --- | --- |
| For pupils who enjoy observing plants. | For pupils who enjoy solving puzzles. |
| Wednesday, 15:30—16:15 | Friday, 15:30—16:15 |
| School Garden | Library |
| Draw leaves and keep a simple plant diary. | Solve number and word puzzles in pairs. |

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Pupils in Paper Models make small things from paper.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

2. Rhythm Room meets only on Friday.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

3. Nature Notes takes place in the school garden.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

4. Puzzle Partners asks pupils to work alone.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

5. All four cards give information about after-school clubs.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：T

A卡明确纸房子和桥。

### 2：F

B卡为周一及周四，非只周五。

### 3：T

C卡地点明确School Garden。

### 4：F

D卡明确in pairs，非独自。

### 5：T

四卡均有适合兴趣、放学后15:30时段、校内地点及活动，为社团信息卡。
