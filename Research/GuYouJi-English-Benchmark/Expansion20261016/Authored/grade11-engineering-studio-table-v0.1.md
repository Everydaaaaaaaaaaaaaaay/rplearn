## 学生题面

阅读全部项目说明与表格，从每题四个选项中选择最佳答案。

The following programme is fictional.

## Riverside Engineering Studio

Riverside Technical Centre invites students to a summer studio on designing useful objects for public spaces. Teams will examine how a noticeboard, a bicycle stand or a movable screen is used, then develop and test a small model. This is a design programme; no real structures will be installed during the course.

### Who can apply?

Applicants must be in school Year 10 or Year 11 and must have a parent or guardian's permission. They must be available for every compulsory session below. Previous design experience is welcome but is not required. A student who cannot attend either feedback workshop should choose a later programme instead.

### Application

Send an online form and a short statement explaining a public-space problem you would like to explore. Attach a recommendation from an adult who is not a member of your family. The recommendation should describe how you work with other people; it does not need to assess your artistic ability.

### Compulsory attendance

| Activity | Schedule | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core studio | 6—17 July | 28 hours in total |
| First feedback workshop | 20 July, 14:00—17:00 | 3 hours |
| Final feedback workshop | 27 July, 14:00—17:00 | 3 hours |

The two feedback workshops are additional to the 28 core hours.

### What participants receive

Professional designers will discuss each team's choices and suggest ways to test a model. Participants can revise their designs after the first feedback workshop and explain those revisions at the final one. Finishing the programme does not provide a professional qualification or guarantee entry to another course.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A同时满足年级、许可、全勤；B缺最终必修、C年级超界、D缺许可，经验不能替代许可。没有从未说明的条件默认满足。

### 2：C

28核心+两次各3小时=34，表注明确另加；A漏两次、B漏一次、D无文本依据。

### 3：B

正文承诺专业反馈及首轮后修订；A明确不安装真实结构，C/D明确不提供或保证。
