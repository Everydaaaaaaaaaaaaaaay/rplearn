# 独立句单选：primary6-ten-sentence-choices

## 学生题面

从每题三个选项中选择合适的一项。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. There are two ____ on the desk.
   A. box
   B. boxes
   C. boxs

2. We visited the museum ____ Sunday.
   A. on
   B. at
   C. in

3. Look! The children ____ a kite now.
   A. fly
   B. flew
   C. are flying

4. Mina is twelve. Her sister is ten. Mina is ____ than her sister.
   A. old
   B. older
   C. oldest

5. Yesterday I ____ to the park with Dad.
   A. go
   B. went
   C. going

6. This ruler is mine. That one is ____.
   A. her
   B. she
   C. hers

7. I am thirsty. I would like ____ water.
   A. some
   B. many
   C. a

8. Tom cannot find his pen. Please help ____.
   A. he
   B. him
   C. his

9. There ____ a small lake near our school.
   A. is
   B. are
   C. be

10. We must ____ quietly in the reading room.
   A. talking
   B. talks
   C. talk

## 作者核对

### 1：B

two要求复数boxes；A单数，C复数拼写错。

### 2：A

具体星期用on；at/in不合此日名搭配。

### 3：C

Look与now标当前正在做，复数主语are flying；其余时体不合。

### 4：B

than与给定年龄比较要求older；old未比较，oldest最高级。

### 5：B

Yesterday明确过去，went为go过去式；going缺助动词，go不合过去时间。

### 6：C

独立表语表示所属用hers；her需名词，she是人称主格。

### 7：A

water不可数，肯定请求some water；many配可数复数，a配单数可数。

### 8：B

help后宾格him；he主格、his所属不适用。

### 9：A

a small lake为单数，there is；are复数，be非限定。

### 10：C

must后动词原形talk；另两项为非原形。
