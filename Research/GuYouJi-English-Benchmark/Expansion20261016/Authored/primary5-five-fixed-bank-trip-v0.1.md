# 小五：共享词库填空

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A至E中选出合适的原词并填字母，词形不变。按每空上下文选择，不额外添加一次限制。

Every Friday, Rosa visits her aunt. She waits for the train at a small (1) ____. She likes to arrive (2) ____ because she does not want to miss it. Before getting on, she buys a (3) ____ at the desk.

At nine, the (4) ____ comes in. Rosa finds her seat and puts her bag under it. She sits beside the (5) ____ so that she can look outside. Her aunt meets her at the next stop. Their weekend together is beginning.

共享词库：

A. early
B. train
C. ticket
D. station
E. window

## 作者核对

### 1：D

在station候车，其他项不表交通场所。

### 2：A

early作arrive的时间方式修饰；其他候选不构成此句时间关系。

### 3：C

buy a ticket为购票，其他项不能构成交通登车前柜台购买的该对象。

### 4：B

明确comes in与此前waiting for train衔接，选train；station固定不驶入。

### 5：E

look outside支持坐在window旁；其他候选不提供这项看外部景色的关系。
