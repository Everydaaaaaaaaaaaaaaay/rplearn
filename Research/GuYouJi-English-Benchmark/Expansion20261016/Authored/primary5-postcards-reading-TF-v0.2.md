# 小五：阅读正误判断

## 学生题面

读短文，选T（与短文相符）或F（与短文不符）。

On Friday, Leo's class wrote postcards to a class on Green Island. Their teacher, Ms Lin, put the finished cards in a large envelope. She planned to post it after school. Leo offered to carry it to the office, where a member of staff would collect the school mail.

At the office door, Leo found a card on the floor. It was Mia's postcard. He returned to the classroom with it before giving the envelope to the office. Mia thanked him and added her card to the others. The class did not need a new envelope. Leo then took the complete set to the office, and Ms Lin posted it that afternoon.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The class wrote postcards on Friday.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

2. Ms Lin planned to post the cards before school.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

3. Leo found Mia’s card near the office door.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

4. The class used a new envelope after finding the card.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

5. Ms Lin posted the complete set that afternoon.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：T

首句明确Friday。

### 2：F

原文after school，与before school相反。

### 3：T

office door处发现卡，后句确认Mia。

### 4：F

明确did not need a new envelope。

### 5：T

结句给出that afternoon与complete set。
