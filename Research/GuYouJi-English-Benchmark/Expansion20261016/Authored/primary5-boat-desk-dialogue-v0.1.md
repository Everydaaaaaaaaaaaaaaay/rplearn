# 共享候选对话补全：primary5-boat-desk-dialogue

## 学生题面

从A至F中选择合适的句子补全五空对话。有一句不用。

Mara: Hello. I would like some tickets for the boat trip.

Helper: (1) ____

Mara: Two adult tickets, please.

Helper: (2) ____

Mara: At three o'clock, if possible.

Helper: (3) ____

Mara: Half past two is fine. Where should we wait?

Helper: (4) ____

Mara: Can we bring our dog?

Helper: (5) ____

Mara: I see. We will leave him at home with Grandma. Thank you.

共享句子：

A. Please wait beside the blue flag.
B. What time would you like to leave?
C. The trip takes twenty minutes.
D. How many tickets would you like?
E. Sorry, animals are not allowed on the boat.
F. That trip is full, but there are places at half past two.

## 作者核对

### 1：D

D询问人数，后答Two adult tickets；B问时间，A问等候位置，其他不承接人数。

### 2：B

B问出发时间，后答three；D人数、A位置及C时长均不接此答。

### 3：F

F对三点无票提出两点半，后句明确接受half past two；C只给时长，其余不解释改时。

### 4：A

A答等待位置blue flag；其余不提供地点。

### 5：E

E说明不能带动物，后答把狗留家；其余不回应携犬请求。
