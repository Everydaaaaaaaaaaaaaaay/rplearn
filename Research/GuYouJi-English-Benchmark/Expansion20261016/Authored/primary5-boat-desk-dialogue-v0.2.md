# 小五：共享候选对话补全

## 学生题面

从A至E选择合适的句子补全完整五空对话。按上下文独立选择；原参照未明确每项只用一次。

Mara: Hello. I would like some tickets for the boat trip.

Helper: (1) ____

Mara: Two adult tickets, please.

Helper: (2) ____

Mara: At three o'clock, if possible.

Helper: (3) ____

Mara: Half past two is fine. Where should we wait?

Helper: (4) ____

Mara: Can we bring our dog?

Helper: (5) ____

Mara: I see. We will leave him at home with Grandma. Thank you.

共享句子：

A. Please wait beside the blue flag.
B. What time would you like to leave?
C. How many tickets would you like?
D. Sorry, animals are not allowed on the boat.
E. That trip is full, but there are places at half past two.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

C问票数，后答Two adult tickets；其余问时间或给地点、禁犬、改班次，均不匹配该答。

### 2：B

B问出发时刻，后答three；C人数不匹配，A/D/E是别的回答。

### 3：E

E说三点满额而两点半有位，后句明确接受half past two；其他选项不包含该改时关系。

### 4：A

A给blue flag候船地点，其他选项不回答Where。

### 5：D

D说不能携犬，后句明确狗留家；其余不解释这项安排。
