# 情境功能选择：primary2-five-small-functions

## 学生题面

选择能完成这段小对话的一项。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. — What are those? — ____
   A. It is a kite.
   B. They are kites.
   C. I am fine.

2. — What colour is your bag? — ____
   A. It is blue.
   B. It is a bag.
   C. They are blue.

3. — Do you like apples? — No. ____
   A. I like apples.
   B. I like bananas.
   C. Yes, I do.

4. — What can the bird do? — ____
   A. It can fly.
   B. It is yellow.
   C. It is a bird.

5. — Would you like some water? — ____
   A. I can jump.
   B. They are cups.
   C. Yes, please.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：B

those问复数物品，用They are；A单数，C回答状态。

### 2：A

问单数bag的颜色；A同时符合，B不答颜色，C主语数不符。

### 3：B

No后改说喜欢香蕉与拒绝苹果相容；A/C与明确否定相冲突。

### 4：A

can do问能力，A回答飞；B颜色、C物类不回答问题。

### 5：C

C接受饮水邀请；A能力、B物品识别均不回答邀请。
