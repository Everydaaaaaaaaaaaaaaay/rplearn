# 单选完形：primary5-free-toy-fair-cloze

## 学生题面

读短文，为八个空各选一项。每空三个选项；没有已答例题。

Last Saturday, Nora took a box of toy cars to the school fair. The cars had belonged to her brother, (1) ____ he no longer played with them. Nora wanted to give them to younger children. Before the fair, she put a small price card on the box: “Free.”

At first, nobody took a car. “Perhaps they cannot see your card,” her friend said. Nora moved the card to the front. A little boy stopped and (2) ____ at a blue car. “How much is it?” he asked. Nora showed him the sign and explained that he could take it without (3) ____.

The boy smiled, but he did not leave at once. He wanted to (4) ____ something useful in return. He noticed that the box was nearly empty and offered to carry it to Nora's classroom later. Nora thanked him for his (5) ____ offer.

By noon, all the cars had new owners. Nora and the boy carried the box (6) ____ because it was light. They put it on a shelf for another fair. Nora had expected to lose a few old toys that day. Instead, she had (7) ____ a new friend. She decided to keep the same clear sign for the (8) ____ fair.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

旧归属与不再玩形成转折，but；or不表此转折，because会把所属说成不玩的结果。

### 2：B

at a blue car支持looked at；slept at不能表达关注车，listen at缺声音对象且搭配不合。

### 3：C

Free与不需要付款对应paying；其他动作不是价格问题。

### 4：C

do something useful为做有用的事；break/forget不表达回报帮助。

### 5：A

主动帮忙的offer是kind；其余负面形容与情节冲突。

### 6：B

because light解释搬得轻松easily；heavily反向，quietly未由重量关系支持。

### 7：C

make a new friend为交朋友；close/wear不能以friend作此结果宾语。

### 8：A

决定保留供未来另次fair，对应next；last过去、broken破损均不合未来使用。
