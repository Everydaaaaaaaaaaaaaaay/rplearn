# 语法选择语篇：grade7-yard-map-grammar-choice

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从每空四个选项中选择最合适的一项。

Last Tuesday, our class made a map of the school yard. Ms Reed gave us (1) ____ large sheet of paper and asked us to mark the paths. We worked in pairs. My partner, June, carried a ruler, and I brought two (2) ____.

We started beside the gate. June (3) ____ the distance to the first tree while I wrote it down. It was longer (4) ____ we had expected. We could not measure it with one short ruler, (5) ____ we used a long string instead.

Before we drew the lines, Ms Reed asked, “Whose notes are these?” “They are (6) ____,” we answered. She reminded us to put both our names on the page. Then she showed us how (7) ____ the lengths smaller for the map.

By the end of the lesson, every pair had a map. June pointed to ours and said, “Look! The path by the wall is the (8) ____ one.” It was shorter than all the other paths we had measured. Tomorrow we (9) ____ the maps with another class. If we find a mistake, we (10) ____ it before putting our work on the wall.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

large sheet首次引入单数可数且large辅音起首，用a；an音不合、the无特指、零冠词不合单数。

### 2：B

two后可数名词复数pencils；其余不是该复数物品。

### 3：C

Last Tuesday固定过去事件，需要measured；其他为当前或未来。

### 4：C

longer后比较连接than；as不是此比较级结构，其余无比较功能。

### 5：B

短尺不够所以改长线为结果so；but转折、or选择、although让步不合句间因果。

### 6：D

回答notes所属且后无名词，用ours；our需名词，we/us人称非所属。

### 7：B

showed us how to make为疑问词不定式；其余在此不能形成完整从句。

### 8：C

the与后文shorter than all说明最高级shortest；shorter比较两个，shortly副词。

### 9：D

we后需要限定谓语；D will share完整且与Tomorrow相合。sharing缺助动词，shares与we人称数不符，been shared也缺限定助动词。

### 10：C

条件发生后主句we will correct完整；corrects与we数不合，correcting缺助动词，to correcting非限定谓语。没有靠未来语境排除可能的习惯性原形。
