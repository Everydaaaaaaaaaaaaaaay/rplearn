# 小二：日常小对话 · 2

## 学生题面

选择能完成这段小对话的一项。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. — What are these? — ____
   A. It is a cup.
   B. They are cups.
   C. I like cups.

2. — May I have a ruler, please? — ____
   A. Here you are.
   B. I am fine.
   C. It is blue.

3. — Do you like tea? — ____, I do.
   A. No
   B. Not
   C. Yes

4. — What is your name? — ____
   A. I am six.
   B. My name is Tim.
   C. I am at school.

5. — What can you do? — ____
   A. I can jump.
   B. I am happy.
   C. It is a ball.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：B

these问复数识别；B给出对应复数物类。A单数，C偏好。

### 2：A

A递交所请求物；B状态，C颜色不完成请求。

### 3：C

后半I do肯定，须Yes；No/Not均与该回应结构不合。

### 4：B

B回答姓名；A年龄，C位置。

### 5：A

A回答能力；B情绪，C物品识别。
