# 小二：日常小对话 · 1

## 学生题面

选择能完成这段小对话的一项。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. — How old are you? — ____
   A. I am seven.
   B. I am a boy.
   C. I am at home.

2. — What colour is the cup? — ____
   A. It is big.
   B. It is yellow.
   C. It is a cup.

3. — Can you swim? — No, ____.
   A. I can
   B. I am
   C. I cannot

4. — Where is my book? — ____
   A. It is on the desk.
   B. It is red.
   C. It is a book.

5. — Thank you for the pencil. — ____
   A. I am eight.
   B. You are welcome.
   C. It is yellow.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A回答年龄；B是身份，C是位置。

### 2：B

B回答颜色；A大小，C物类。

### 3：C

No对应cannot；A肯定能力，B的am不能代替can。

### 4：A

A说明位置；B颜色，C只重复物类。

### 5：B

B礼貌回应感谢；A年龄、C颜色不是回应感谢。
