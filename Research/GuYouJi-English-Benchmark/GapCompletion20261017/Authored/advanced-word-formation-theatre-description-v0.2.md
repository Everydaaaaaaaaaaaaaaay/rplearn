# 高级：给词构形 · 听见舞台

## 学生题面

阅读短文，用每空括号中的提示词构成一个合适的单词。用大写字母填写；例0已答，不计作答。英美合法拼写均接受，本机对照忽略输入大小写。

A small theatre spent several weeks on the (0) PREPARATION (PREPARE) of its first audio-described performance. The aim was to describe visible action during pauses in the dialogue, without speaking over the actors.

A local arts (1) ____ (ORGANISE) lent the theatre a set of headsets. Before the first rehearsal, the team checked the (2) ____ (AVAILABLE) of every device. Each character needed an immediately (3) ____ (RECOGNISE) label, so the script used names rather than vague references such as “the person on the left”. The describer had to make a (4) ____ (JUDGE) about which gestures mattered to the plot. Reading every stage direction aloud was not an option.

The first rehearsal exposed an (5) ____ (EXPECT) problem which nobody had anticipated: a noisy scene change left no quiet interval for a crucial description. The director moved that information to an earlier pause. The describer then spoke (6) ____ (CLEAR) enough for each word to be heard. After the performance, the team invited three written (7) ____ (SUGGEST) from each test listener. They were asked for one thing to keep and two things to change. Making the production accessible had required close (8) ____ (COLLABORATE) between people who normally worked in separate rooms.

## 作者核对

### 1：ORGANISATION / ORGANIZATION

a local arts后单数机构名词；两种英美拼写指同一机构。

### 2：AVAILABILITY

the...of every device要求可用性名词；available是形容词。

### 3：RECOGNISABLE / RECOGNIZABLE

label前形容词，immediately修饰“可识别的”；两种拼写同义。

### 4：JUDGEMENT / JUDGMENT

make a...about引入一次判断；两种名词拼写均接受。

### 5：UNEXPECTED

nobody had anticipated明确没有预料，要求UNEXPECTED；EXPECTED与该后文冲突。

### 6：CLEARLY

spoke后方式副词，enough后置；clear为形容词。

### 7：SUGGESTIONS

three明确要求可数复数SUGGESTIONS，后句一项保留加两项改变也对应三条建议。

### 8：COLLABORATION

close...between作名词搭配，指合作；动词原形不能作该宾语。
