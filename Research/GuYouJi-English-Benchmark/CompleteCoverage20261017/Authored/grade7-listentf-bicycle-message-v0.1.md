## 作者核对

### 完整原创对话脚本

L: Ted, is your bicycle ready?

T: Not yet, Luke. The man is putting on a new wheel. He says I can collect it at half past four.

N: But the shop closes at five. Can you get here in time?

T: I have music practice until twenty past four, so I cannot walk here quickly enough.

L: I can collect it for you. My lesson finishes at four.

N: Good idea. Ted, write down the number on your paper ticket for Luke.

T: Thanks, both of you. I will leave the ticket with Luke now.
