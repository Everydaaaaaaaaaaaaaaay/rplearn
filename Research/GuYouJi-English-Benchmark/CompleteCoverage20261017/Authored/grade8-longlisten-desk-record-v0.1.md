## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Nina, the front of our classroom is ready, but the desks are still in rows.

W: We need them in groups of four for tomorrow's quiz. The teacher asked us to move them after lunch.

M: I will move them. Can you write the team names on these cards?

W: Yes. We are both in the Blue Team, aren't we?

M: Yes. I will put the blue card on the table nearest the door.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: How was your visit to your grandfather, Adam?

M: Great. We found a box of photos from his first job. He repaired buses then.

W: Were there photos of the buses?

M: Yes, and of the people he worked with. I wanted to scan them, but the machine at home was broken.

W: You can use the one in the school library.

M: Good idea. He said I could borrow the photos until Friday. I will bring them in on Wednesday.

W: Ask him to write the names on a separate sheet. It will help you remember who everyone is.
