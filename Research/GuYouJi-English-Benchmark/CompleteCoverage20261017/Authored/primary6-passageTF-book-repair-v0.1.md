## 作者核对

### 完整原创脚本

N: I am Nora. I found an old storybook in a box at home on Sunday. It belonged to my mother when she was a child. Some pages were loose, but none were missing. I wanted to use ordinary sticky tape to fix them. My mother said that might damage the paper later, so we took the book to a free repair session at the library. A volunteer showed us how to attach the loose pages with special paper strips. We did the work ourselves while she helped. The book is now back on our shelf at home. I am glad we repaired it instead of throwing it away.
