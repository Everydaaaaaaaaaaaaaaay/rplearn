## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: What do you want to buy, Leo? A T-shirt?

M: My T-shirt is fine. I need trousers for the school show. My sister is buying a skirt.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Is your birthday on June the twenty-second, Ruby?

W: No. That is our class party. My birthday is on June the twelfth. My cousin’s is on July the twenty-second.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Do you walk to school, Jack?

M: I used to, but now I ride my bike. The bus is too crowded in the morning.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: When are you at school, Amy?

W: From eight in the morning until three in the afternoon. I eat lunch at school too.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Do you start your homework after dinner, Dan?

M: First I take a walk with Mum. Then I do my homework. I only watch TV on Saturdays.
