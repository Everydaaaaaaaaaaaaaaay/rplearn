## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: We could save money by taking the early bus to the field centre. It gets there at seven.

W: But the building does not open until half past eight. I would rather pay a little more for the later service than wait outside with all the equipment.

M: Fair point. The later one arrives at eight twenty. Should we buy tickets today?

W: Yes. There are only eighteen seats, and the driver does not sell standing tickets.

M: I will reserve two. Can we leave the equipment at the centre after the survey?

W: Only the waterproof boxes. We need to bring the cameras back because another group has booked them for Monday. I have written that on the return checklist.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: The stall next to ours will sell bread, so we should change our plan.

M: Could we sell soup instead? It would go well with their bread.

W: That would be useful, but we have no safe way to keep soup hot outdoors. What about packs of seeds from the school garden? We already have envelopes and labels.

M: Good. We need to explain that some seeds are for pots and others need a large bed.

W: Exactly. Put that on the front, not just the plant name. People should know before they buy.

M: What will we do with the money?

W: First pay back the cost of the display boards. Anything left will buy new tools for the garden. Let us make a sign so everyone knows.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: Thank you for helping with the town sound map. We are collecting short recordings that show how familiar places sound at different times. A recording does not have to be loud or unusual. The quiet closing of a shop can be just as useful as a busy station.

Please make two recordings at your chosen place, one in the morning and one later in the day. Keep each one under a minute. Begin by saying the place and the time, but do not include names or private conversations. If somebody can be clearly heard speaking, choose another moment or ask the project leader whether the recording can be used.

Send your files by Sunday evening, together with one sentence explaining what changed between the two visits. We will not choose the loudest recordings as winners. There is no competition. The aim is to create a shared picture of the town through sound, and every usable contribution will help.
