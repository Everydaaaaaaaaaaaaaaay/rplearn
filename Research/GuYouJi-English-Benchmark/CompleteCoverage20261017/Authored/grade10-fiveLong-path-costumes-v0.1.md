## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The stepping stones look slippery after the rain. Shall we cancel the garden visit?

M: We can use the gravel path instead. It is a little longer, but it goes to the same gate.

W: That sounds better. I will tell the group to follow that route.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Have you found all the costumes for the play?

W: Almost. We have enough coats, but we need two more hats. I asked the theatre shop, but hiring them is expensive.

M: Could we make them from cardboard?

W: I tried that yesterday. They looked good under the stage lights, so I will make the second one this evening.

M: I can help. What should I bring?

W: Some strong tape. We have plenty of paint already.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Your cake is much smaller than last year's. Did you change the recipe?

M: No. I used the same ingredients, but made only half the amount. There were too many leftovers last time.

W: That makes sense. Does it still have nuts?

M: Yes. I will put a clear label beside it. There is a separate fruit plate for anyone who cannot eat nuts.

W: I can write the label while you cut the cake.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: The walking club wants a map with every footpath on it.

W: That could be difficult to read. Most visitors only need the route between the station and the lake.

M: So you think we should simplify it?

W: Yes. Keep the main route, the toilets and the bus stops. We can provide a link to a detailed map for people who want more.

M: Good. I will remove the minor paths and enlarge the labels.

W: Then print one copy first, so we can test whether the names are readable.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: This Friday our class will run a second-hand puzzle stall outside the library. We are collecting complete puzzles only. Before donating one, please count the pieces and put them in a sealed bag inside the box. If pieces are missing, keep that puzzle at home; a picture of the finished puzzle cannot replace missing pieces. Bring donations to Room Six by Wednesday afternoon. Volunteers will price the puzzles on Thursday. Most will cost between two and five pounds. The stall opens at twelve thirty and closes at two. The money will buy new cushions for the library reading corner. Please do not arrive early expecting to buy the best puzzles before the stall opens: helpers will be arranging the tables, and no sales will be made before twelve thirty. Any unsold puzzles will be offered to the after-school club, with the owner's permission.
