## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

W: Did you go to the park by bus, Ben?

M: No. Dad drove me there. We came home by bus because he went to work.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

M: Are you joining the football club, Amy?

W: Not this term. I have a music lesson on Tuesdays, when the football club meets. I will join the art club on Fridays.

M: Do you need to bring paint?

W: No, the club has paint. I only need an old shirt.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

W: Where shall we meet for the museum trip, Jack?

M: At the school gate at nine. The bus leaves at a quarter past nine.

W: Should we bring our lunches?

M: Yes. The museum cafe is closed this week. We can eat in the garden if it is dry.

W: I will bring a sandwich and some water.
