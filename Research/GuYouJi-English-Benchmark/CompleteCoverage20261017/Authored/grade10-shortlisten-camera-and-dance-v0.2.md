## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

M: Could I borrow your camera for the harbour trip on Friday?

W: Of course. It has been in the cupboard for months, though. Plug it in tonight, or you may find the battery empty when you get there.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

W: Does your dance class start at ten past three every day?

M: From Monday to Thursday, yes. On Friday it begins twenty minutes earlier because our teacher has to catch a train.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

M: I like this jacket, but the sleeves are rather short. Could I try a larger size?

W: Certainly. The fitting room is behind you. I will bring the next size in blue and grey.

### 对话4完整原创脚本

W: Will this hurt? I have never had a tooth taken out before.

M: You may feel some pressure, but the area is numb now. Keep your head still and raise your hand if you need me to stop.

### 对话5完整原创脚本

M: I am sorry. The last two tickets for tonight were sold a few minutes ago.

W: Oh, what a pity. My brother is only here for one evening, and I had been looking forward to taking him to the show.
