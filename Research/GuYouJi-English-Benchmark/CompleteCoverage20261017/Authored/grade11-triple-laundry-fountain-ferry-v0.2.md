# 高二三篇式：衣物标签、饮水台与渡船模型

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，根据短文内容填空。在未给提示词的空白处仅填写1个适当的单词，在给出提示词的空白处用括号内所给词的正确形式填空。

### A

After (1) ____ (sort) the clothes into two piles, I checked the labels before using the washing machine. I found it difficult (2) ____ (read) the instructions on an old woollen jumper because the printing had almost disappeared. None of the printed instructions (3) ____ (be) in English. My aunt translated them for me, and we decided to wash that jumper by hand. The rest could go into the machine together.

### B

The new drinking fountain in our square looks decorative, (4) ____ it also serves a practical purpose. It has three separate (5) ____ (outlet), at different heights. One is low enough for a dog to reach; the middle one fills a reusable bottle. The highest outlet, (6) ____ can be operated with an elbow, leaves the user's hands free. A small drain carries away water that misses the container.

### C

The accuracy of a ferry model depends (7) ____ careful measurements rather than the number of tiny details added to it. At a club meeting I saw an (8) ____ (impress) model made from plain cardboard. Its lower deck had been (9) ____ (build) before the maker discovered an error in the width. He showed us the correction instead of hiding it. This imperfect model has since (10) ____ (become) the club's most useful teaching example.

## 作者核对

### 1：sorting / having sorted

after后普通或完成动名词均可，分类先于查标签。

### 2：to read

find it difficult to read是不定式结构。

### 3：was / were

none of复数名词可接单数或复数谓语；本段过去叙事，两者都收。

### 4：but / yet / and

but/yet表达装饰外的实用对照，and添加另一功能，均不违背语境，不能只收but。

### 5：outlets

three separate后可数复数。

### 6：which

逗号后的非限制性从句缺主语，指最高出水口，用which。

### 7：on / upon

depend on/upon均为依赖某条件，均限1词。

### 8：impressive

修饰model且前有an，impressive表示令人印象深刻；不将其他未列派生词直接判错。

### 9：built

had been后被动过去分词，甲板被建造。

### 10：become

has之后过去分词become，与原形同形。
