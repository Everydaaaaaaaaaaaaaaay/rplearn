## 作者核对

### 完整原创脚本

N: I am Daniel. On Saturday I went to a pottery class with my friend Leo. We nearly arrived late because we waited at the wrong bus stop. Luckily, the next bus came quickly. Our teacher showed us how to make a small bowl. I had never worked with clay before, and my first bowl fell over. The teacher gave me some more clay, and I tried again. Leo made a cup instead of a bowl. We could not take our work home that day because it still needed to go into the kiln. We will collect it next weekend. I plan to give my bowl to my grandmother.
