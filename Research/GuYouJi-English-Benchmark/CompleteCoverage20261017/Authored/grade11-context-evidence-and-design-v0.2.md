# 高二语境词汇：证据与设计

## 学生题面

根据各句首字母与汉语注释完成单词拼写。填写完整单词。

以下各句彼此独立。首字母已印出的空，请在输入框填写完整单词（包括该首字母）。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The designer made a r____（粗略的） sketch before drawing the final plan.

2. The old photograph provides v____（视觉的） evidence of the bridge before it was rebuilt.

3. The two routes are e____（同样地） long; both are exactly eight kilometres.

4. The laboratory locked the samples in a s____（安全的） cabinet to prevent unauthorised access.

5. An a____（年度的） inspection takes place once every year.

6. The researcher kept a d____（详细的） record of every change, including its exact time.

7. The team had to r____（修改） the plan after the doorway was found to be too narrow.

8. The booklet includes a brief s____（总结） of the main findings at the end.

9. The visitors waited p____（耐心地） while the technician repaired the projector.

10. The sample was completely d____（干燥的）, with no moisture left after heating.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：rough

rough sketch固定搭配初稿。

### 2：visual

visual evidence看得见的证据。

### 3：equally

equally修饰long，等长。

### 4：secure / safe

secure/safe cabinet均合安全存放语境。

### 5：annual

once every year定义annual。

### 6：detailed

detailed record详细记录。

### 7：revise

revise plan修改方案。

### 8：summary

名词summary。

### 9：patiently

副词patiently修饰waited。

### 10：dry / dried

was completely dry描述状态，was completely dried可读为已完成的被动干燥过程；after heating与no moisture同时支持两种。
