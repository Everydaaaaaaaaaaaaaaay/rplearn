# 高一逐空限词：露天咖啡馆的小铃

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据短文内容填空；给词空使用所给词的正确形式，不额外限1词。第5、6、7、9空各填1词；第10空已明确标注“填2词”，须恰好2词。

During my first weekend job at an open-air café, I made (1) ____ (care) notes about which counter handled each kind of order. The tables stood beside a busy riverside path, and visitors often found it difficult (2) ____ (hear) their names when an order was ready. The manager had just (3) ____ (give) us a small handbell to try instead of calling every name across the crowd.

Before the café opened, we watched him (4) ____ (demonstrate) how he wanted it to be used. One ring meant that a fresh set of orders was ready to collect. The order numbers would appear on a board beside the counter. We thought the arrangement was clear. It was not (5) ____ the first customer asked where to go that we realised the board could not be seen from the entrance.

We moved the board and put the bell, (6) ____ handle was painted blue, directly beside it. This made the link between the sound and the order numbers easier to notice. The printed menu, on (7) ____ other hand, needed to stay beside the tables, where people chose what to buy. Putting every notice in one place would have solved one problem by creating another.

I rewrote the short collection instructions as (8) ____ (clear) as I could. We asked a new colleague to follow them without help, and she immediately found a word that could refer to either counter. After changing it, we paid attention (9) ____ the questions customers still asked. By Sunday afternoon, we were spending more time answering individual questions (10) ____ (填2词) shouting the same announcement over and over again.

## 作者核对

### 1：careful

careful作notes的定语；不是修饰动词的carefully。

### 2：to hear

find it difficult to hear，不定式补足形式。

### 3：given

had just之后用过去分词given。

### 4：demonstrate / demonstrating

watch+宾语可接不带to不定式或现在分词，分别从完整动作/正在演示的视角描述同次演示，两者均合语境。

### 5：until / till

not until/till...that均可，首次顾客提问才发现问题。

### 6：whose

whose handle表示铃与手柄的所属，非限制性关系从句。

### 7：the

on the other hand固定连接结构。

### 8：clearly

as...as中的正级副词修饰rewrote。

### 9：to

pay attention to固定搭配。

### 10：rather than / instead of

两个两词连接语均可连接answering与shouting两种工作活动，语境比较采用前者而非后者。不是1词rather或3词in place of。
