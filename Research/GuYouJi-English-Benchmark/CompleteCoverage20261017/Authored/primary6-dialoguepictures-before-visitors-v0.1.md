M: Have you finished the message for the visitors?

W: Not quite. I am writing the last line on this paper, then I will leave it at the desk.

### 对话B完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue B.

M: You are wearing a cook's hat. Are you making lunch?

W: Yes. I am stirring the soup in the pot. The visitors will eat after their tour.

### 对话C完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue C.

M: How are you getting to the meeting point?

W: On my bicycle. I am riding there now, so I do not miss the start.

### 对话D完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue D.

M: What are you holding open in front of you?

W: A book about the town. I am reading a page before I show the visitors around.

### 对话E完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue E.

M: Where are you taking that large box?

W: I am carrying it to the storeroom. These things must be put away before everyone arrives.

### 对话F完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue F.

M: Are you filling the pot at that sink?

W: No. I am washing my hands. I have just finished moving the dusty chairs.
