### 全组回填

Ben washes his white football socks with a new red T-shirt. Mum tells him to wash the shirt alone, but he does not follow her advice. Now the socks have pale red marks. The marks are pink!

Ben has a match tomorrow, but the shops are already closed. His brother offers an old pair. There is a hole in one sock. Grandma fixes it. She is good at sewing.

"Are these for me?" Ben asks. "Yes, they are yours now," his brother says. Ben thanks both of them: first his brother, then Grandma.

On Saturday, Ben wears the socks and plays well. Later, he reads every washing label before turning on the machine. The reading comes first. He doesn't want the same mistake again.
