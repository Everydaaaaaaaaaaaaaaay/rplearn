## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Have you got a pen in your hand, Holly?

W: It is an open book. My pen and eraser are in the pencil case.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Can you see the woman with long dark hair?

M: Yes. She is standing near the two boys. One boy has glasses, and the other has a green shirt.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Please write your name here. Is it Nina Hill?

W: It is Nina Wood. Hill is my teacher’s family name, and Nina is my first name.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: Is Ben your cousin, Oscar?

M: No, he is my classmate. We sit next to each other. My cousin is in a different school.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Tell me about your toy shelf, Emma.

W: There are five dolls and three little cars. I keep my two books on the shelf below it.
