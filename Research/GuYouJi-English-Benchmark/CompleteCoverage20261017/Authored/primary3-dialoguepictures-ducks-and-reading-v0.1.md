### 对话A完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue A.

M: What is this?

W: It is an open book.

### 对话B完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue B.

M: What colour is your ball?

W: It is green.

### 对话C完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue C.

M: Can I have a cup, please?

W: Yes. Here is a blue cup.

### 对话D完整原创脚本

N: Dialogue D.

M: Are there two ducks?
