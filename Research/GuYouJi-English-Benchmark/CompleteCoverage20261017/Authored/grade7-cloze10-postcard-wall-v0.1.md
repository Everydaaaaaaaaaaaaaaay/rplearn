# 初一十空四选：教室里的明信片墙

## 学生题面

阅读下面完整短文，从每题四个选项中为第1—10空各选最合适的一项。

There is a postcard wall in our classroom. The cards come from our families and friends. They show different places, but we do not put them up by (1) ____. A blue card can be next to a red one. We put cards from the same (2) ____ together, so it is easy to find a place.

Each Friday, one student chooses a card and (3) ____ us about it. The card may show a busy street or a quiet village. We can ask questions, but we must (4) ____ until the speaker finishes. Sometimes the student does not know the answer. That is all right. We can (5) ____ it up in a book later.

You do not have to travel far to (6) ____ a card. One of my cards shows the park beside my home. I took a photograph and made the card (7) ____. There is no name on the front, so I write my name on the (8) ____.

At the end of each month, we choose three cards to keep in a class book. We (9) ____ the others to their owners. The wall changes, and there is always something (10) ____ to see.

### 答案与逐项依据

1. B。A：不按重量分组并非本句蓝红相邻所说明的特点，颜色对照直接指向colour；B：蓝卡可挨红卡，说明不按颜色排列；C：未给按大小分组的对照，此处以蓝红颜色明确举例；D：自制寄送卡并无售价分组依据

2. D。A：没有按寄出月份分组，后半句找地点对应地区；B：全属同一班，没有多班分类；C：卡来自不同家庭，但目的是按地点找卡，非家庭归属；D：同地区卡放一起，方便找地点

3. C。A：不是为我们购买卡片内容，buy us about结构不成立；B：carry us about不是向同学介绍卡片内容；C：tells us about sth告诉我们卡片情景；D：wait us about不表示介绍卡片

4. B。A：未说要忘掉问题，后文还会查找；B：until speaker finishes说明要等待对方讲完；C：大家还要提问讨论，不是先离开；D：规则要求等讲完，不是喊叫打断

5. D。A：give it up表示放弃，与后来查书相反；B：pick it up可表示捡起物品，不能由in a book表达查答案；C：wake it up用于唤醒，不表示查找答案；D：look it up查找未知答案

6. C。A：卡片不需靠旅行破坏；B：不是讨论旅行多远才会弄丢卡片；C：不必远行也能带本地景物卡来；D：作者自制并带来，不是要从别人借卡

7. B。A：不是卡片或公园自行制作自己；B：I took a photograph and made表示本人自制；C：主语I不对应herself；D：主语非复数they，不对应themselves

8. A。A：front没有名字，就在背面写名；B：不在地板上写寄送者名；C：没有往窗上写名的安排；D：写在卡上，而非公园的树上

9. A。A：选择三张留下，其他归还原主人；B：没有烧毁同学卡片的活动；C：不收藏其余卡，明确交还而非隐藏；D：卡属同学，未获义卖或出售安排

10. A。A：墙上卡每月变化，有新内容；B：有卡可看，empty与something to see不合；C：卡本来无声，不表示更换后产生的新内容；D：没有故意展示破卡的规则

### 全组回填

There is a postcard wall in our classroom. The cards come from our families and friends. They show different places, but we do not put them up by colour. A blue card can be next to a red one. We put cards from the same area together, so it is easy to find a place.

Each Friday, one student chooses a card and tells us about it. The card may show a busy street or a quiet village. We can ask questions, but we must wait until the speaker finishes. Sometimes the student does not know the answer. That is all right. We can look it up in a book later.

You do not have to travel far to bring a card. One of my cards shows the park beside my home. I took a photograph and made the card myself. There is no name on the front, so I write my name on the back.

At the end of each month, we choose three cards to keep in a class book. We return the others to their owners. The wall changes, and there is always something new to see.
