# 高二：剧场道具间

## 学生题面

(1) ____ (open) in 1998, the prop cupboard behind our town theatre holds far more than spare hats. It is (2) ____ place where objects acquire new identities: a wooden box becomes a treasure chest, while a kitchen chair becomes a throne. Last month two (3) ____ (student) were invited to help prepare a display about this hidden part of theatre life.

Rather than arranging the objects by price, the pair chose (4) ____ (show) how the same item had appeared in different plays. A small card beside each object explains (5) ____ it was used for. The most popular object is an umbrella (6) ____ handle was repaired with bright blue tape. Visitors can compare a photograph of its first appearance with one taken ten years later.

The students described each change as (7) ____ (accurate) as they could. They also asked the theatre's (8) ____ (manage) for permission to include a broken crown. She agreed, because the damage revealed how many times it had been worn. Since the cupboard was rearranged in May, several local actors (9) ____ (offer) to contribute objects of their own. Each new contribution arrives with a short note, so visitors know not only what an object is, (10) ____ also why somebody decided to keep it.

## 作者核对

### 1：Opened / Having been opened

被动分词和完成被动分词均与1998年开设早于现状相容；不得用主动Having opened缺宾语来排除完成被动。

### 2：a / one

a place引入场所；one place强调其中一个场所，后文不排他，均可。

### 3：students

two要求复数。

### 4：to show

choose to show合本句选择展示的动作；其他未列非直接判错。

### 5：what

explains what it was used for，for缺宾语，what填入内容关系。

### 6：whose

umbrella whose handle，所属关系限定词。

### 7：accurately

as...as正级副词修饰described，不排出其他空的程度变体。

### 8：manager

the theatre的人员为manager，后文She对应可数单数人。

### 9：have offered / have been offering

since时间框架允许完成及完成进行，主语several actors复数。

### 10：but

but与前面的not only及后面的also组成常用并列结构，是本空已核接受形式。原解析仅援引常用配对，尚不足以证明and在本句必错，故撤销其显式排除，未列形式待复核；此举不将and列为已核正确，也不放宽一词限制。
