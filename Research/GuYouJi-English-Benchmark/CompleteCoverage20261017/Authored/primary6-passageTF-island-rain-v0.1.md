## 作者核对

### 完整原创脚本

N: My name is Hannah. This is my second visit to Green Island. Last year I came with my school, but this time I am here with my parents and my brother. It rained all morning, so we stayed inside and played cards. The rain has stopped now. This afternoon we plan to cycle to the lighthouse. Tomorrow morning we are going out in a small boat with a local guide. My brother hopes to see seals, but the guide says we may not see any. Even with the rain, I am enjoying the holiday. I would love to come back again.
