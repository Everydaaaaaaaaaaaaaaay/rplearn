## 作者核对



### 完整原创脚本

M: Shall we visit the boat museum on Sunday, Lily?

W: It is closed on Sundays. Let us go on Saturday instead.

M: Good idea. Is it the large building beside the bridge?

W: No, that is the library. The museum is in an old house near the river.

M: What can we see there?

W: Small wooden boats. Some are only as long as your hand. We can watch a person making one.

M: How will we get there?

W: By bus. It takes twenty minutes from our school.

M: Then I will bring my camera.

W: Ask before taking photos. Some parts of the room are not open for photographs.
