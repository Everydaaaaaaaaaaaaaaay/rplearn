### A篇（第1—5空）

Last Sunday, Dad and I made a wooden frame for my drawing. I was (1) ____ when the glass would not fit. We had measured the drawing but forgotten the space inside the frame. Dad did not throw anything away. He took a ruler and (2) ____ the glass again. It was two centimetres too wide. We could not make the glass (3) ____ with our tools, so we made a new, larger frame (4) ____. The first frame was still useful: my little sister put her smaller drawing in it. In the end, we had two pictures on the wall, and nobody's work was (5) ____.

### B篇（第6—15空）

I thought joining our street's repair group would be simple. I had fixed my bicycle and expected to be (6) ____ immediately. Yet on my first visit, I could not name several tools.

Jo, a volunteer, noticed me by the door. Instead of doing everything for me, she asked which part I (7) ____. I pointed to a wheel drawing. She put matching tools beside it and let me try. Her help was a (8) ____, not a replacement for my effort.

The following week, a visitor brought a chair with a loose leg. I wanted to begin, but Jo asked me to listen (9) ____. One screw, the visitor explained, had fallen out. That detail (10) ____ our plan: tightening the remaining screws would not be enough.

The missing screw was in the visitor's bag. (11) ____ he left, we asked him to sit on the repaired chair and check it.

My knowledge is still (12) ____, but I no longer hide questions. Last week, I showed a newcomer where the tools were, passing (13) ____ Jo's patience. Being useful does not always mean knowing the answer (14) ____. Sometimes it starts with being ready to (15) ____.

### 答案与逐项依据

1. C。A：发现装不下是新问题，不是解除原有担忧；B：前文忘记内空间，不能说已为装不下做好准备；C：已以为按画尺寸做好，却发现玻璃装不下，是意外

2. A。A：用尺重新核玻璃宽度，随后得到两厘米差；B：用尺取得宽度差，不是重新给玻璃上漆；C：ruler量长度，不是称玻璃重量

3. B。A：亮度与两厘米宽度差无关；B：玻璃过宽且工具不能裁窄，因此扩大相框；C：玻璃重量不是导致装不下的宽度问题

4. C。A：肯定句末neither不表示以新方案代替；B：肯定句中不能用either代too，且原裁玻璃方案没有完成；C：不能改窄玻璃，改做大框替代

5. A。A：旧框给妹妹、新框给我，两个成果都有用途，没有白费；B：墙上两幅已完成，nobody finished与结局矛盾；C：没有把作品退回的事件，不能概括材料都派上用途

6. A。A：过去修过自己的车，预期能马上帮上忙，与后文不会工具形成落差；B：主动加入服务组不支持希望别人看不见自己；C：expected与已有修车经验呼应，confused是实际落差不是预期

7. B。A：没有藏工具或图，不能解释随后指图尝试；B：后文指向认识的轮子图，从已理解部分搭起任务；C：询问图上哪部分理解，不是在确认工具所有权

8. B。A：还需要作者自己尝试，不是帮其完成的最终成果；B：让其认图选工具自己尝试，是帮助起步；C：展示工具与认图不是保密规则

9. C。A：Jo要求听取细节，非少听；B：Jo和访客同在，不是要求独自听，重点先听后动手；C：开始动手前先听访客，后获遗漏螺丝信息

10. C。A：这信息说明原方案不够，不是证明原方案可行；B：方案尚需补件并维修，细节本身未完成计划；C：新增螺丝已丢信息，说明原只拧紧的方案须改

11. C。A：离开不是检查座椅的否定条件；B：尚未离开就检查，非虽已离开却叫他坐下；C：离开前坐下检查，才知道修好可离开

12. B。A：仍有不懂的词和问题，非知识完备；B：仍记生词且常提问，说明知识仍有限；C：已能引导新成员取工具，不是毫无用处

13. A。A：pass on patience表示把得到的耐心传给新成员；B：pass away意为去世或消失，不表示传递耐心；C：pass out可昏倒或分发实体物，不与patience构成这里传承

14. A。A：对应起初immediately预期及允许先提问倾听；B：knowing the answer incorrectly不表示起初过高的即时知晓预期；C：没有保密答案情节，非默默知道

15. B。A：这是互助维修，不是与访客比赛；B：最后呼应Jo让作者先听访客，以及理解陌生者需求；C：准备离去不能概括留下耐心帮助的中心

### 全组回填

### A篇（第1—5空）

Last Sunday, Dad and I made a wooden frame for my drawing. I was surprised when the glass would not fit. We had measured the drawing but forgotten the space inside the frame. Dad did not throw anything away. He took a ruler and measured the glass again. It was two centimetres too wide. We could not make the glass narrower with our tools, so we made a new, larger frame instead. The first frame was still useful: my little sister put her smaller drawing in it. In the end, we had two pictures on the wall, and nobody's work was wasted.

### B篇（第6—15空）

I thought joining our street's repair group would be simple. I had fixed my bicycle and expected to be useful immediately. Yet on my first visit, I could not name several tools.

Jo, a volunteer, noticed me by the door. Instead of doing everything for me, she asked which part I understood. I pointed to a wheel drawing. She put matching tools beside it and let me try. Her help was a starting point, not a replacement for my effort.

The following week, a visitor brought a chair with a loose leg. I wanted to begin, but Jo asked me to listen first. One screw, the visitor explained, had fallen out. That detail changed our plan: tightening the remaining screws would not be enough.

The missing screw was in the visitor's bag. Before he left, we asked him to sit on the repaired chair and check it.

My knowledge is still limited, but I no longer hide questions. Last week, I showed a newcomer where the tools were, passing on Jo's patience. Being useful does not always mean knowing the answer immediately. Sometimes it starts with being ready to listen.
