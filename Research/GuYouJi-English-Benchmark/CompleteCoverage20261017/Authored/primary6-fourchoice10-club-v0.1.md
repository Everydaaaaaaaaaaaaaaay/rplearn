# 小六：社团与器材

## 学生题面

完成全部十题，每题从A、B、C、D选一个最佳答案。第5题有两个空，需选择同时适合两空的一组词，仍只选一个字母。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. A friend says, "Thank you for returning my notebook." You reply, "____"
   A. You are welcome.
   B. Excuse me.
   C. I am sorry.
   D. See you tomorrow.

2. To find which page explains how to make the paper boat, look at the book's ____.
   A. cover colour
   B. price
   C. author photo
   D. contents

3. The wooden stool is ____ than the metal chair.
   A. light
   B. lightest
   C. lighter
   D. more lighter

4. The hall opens at two. We arrived at 1:50, ten minutes ____.
   A. lately
   B. early
   C. nearly
   D. softly

5. I was tuning the guitar when the bell rang. Ask about the activity: ____ were you doing ____ the bell rang?
   A. What, when
   B. Where, what
   C. When, what
   D. What, who

6. The music lesson begins ____ three o'clock on Tuesdays.
   A. in
   B. on
   C. between
   D. at

7. The three students ____ the stage when I entered yesterday.
   A. was cleaning
   B. were cleaning
   C. is cleaning
   D. am cleaning

8. This rope is much ____ than the thin string.
   A. thick
   B. thickest
   C. thicker
   D. more thicker

9. The lamp is bright ____ for us to read the small labels.
   A. enough
   B. much
   C. many
   D. most

10. This is ____ drum in our cupboard.
   A. smallest
   B. smaller
   C. the smallest
   D. small

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A：回应对方的感谢。；B：请求引起注意，不回应感谢。；C：道歉，题面未有过错。；D：告别，未回应感谢。

### 2：D

A：封面颜色不提供内容页码。；B：价格不提供内容页码。；C：作者照片不提供内容页码。；D：目录提供内容和页码。

### 3：C

A：light为原级。；B：lightest为最高级。；C：than前用比较级lighter。；D：more与lighter重复。

### 4：B

A：lately表示近来。；B：比开门提前十分钟。；C：nearly不能在这里作时间差补语。；D：softly表示轻柔的方式。

### 5：A

A：What问活动，when引出响铃时刻。；B：Where不问活动，what不连接此从句。；C：When不问所做活动，第二空what不合此从句。；D：who不能连接the bell rang作时间从句。

### 6：D

A：in不用于此钟点。；B：on不用于此钟点。；C：between需要成对端点，不直接用于此单点。；D：钟点前用at。

### 7：B

A：was不与复数students一致。；B：复数students与过去时were cleaning一致。；C：is不与复数主语及过去语境一致。；D：am只与I搭配。

### 8：C

A：thick是原级。；B：thickest是最高级。；C：比较级thicker与much及than相配。；D：more与thicker重复。

### 9：A

A：bright enough to read表达亮度足够。；B：much不放在bright后构成此结构。；C：many不构成此形容词结构。；D：most不构成此形容词结构。

### 10：C

A：单数可数名词前缺限定词。；B：smaller前缺限定词。；C：the smallest作定语修饰drum。；D：small前缺限定词。
