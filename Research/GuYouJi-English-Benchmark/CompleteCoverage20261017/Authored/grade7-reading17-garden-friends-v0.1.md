# 初一五组十七题：花园伙伴与科学老师

## 学生题面

阅读A—E五组中的全部九份文字材料，回答第1至第17题；每题从A、B、C、D四个选项中选出最合适的一项。各题2分，共34分。人物与事件均为虚构。

### A组（第1—3题）

#### A1

I am Finn. This is my toy bus. I keep **it** in a clear box when I finish playing. The bus is red.

#### A2

Hi, I am Hope. My purple lunch box is in my schoolbag. The bag is blue.

#### A3

I am Ian. Blue is my favourite colour. I have a blue cap for sunny days.

#### A4

Hello, I am Jess. I like blue best, too. My new gloves are blue, but my coat is grey.

### B组（第4—7题）

#### B1

Dear Kai,

Our school garden will be open to visitors next Saturday. I would like you to come and see our plants. I help at the garden club every Tuesday. There are now flowers beside the old wall. Please bring your sketchbook if you want to draw them.

Zoe

#### B2

Dear Zoe,

Thank you for inviting me. I can come on Saturday morning with my aunt. She will bring her camera, and I will bring my sketchbook. I especially want to see the yellow flowers you wrote about. We will meet you at the school gate at ten.

Kai

### C组（第8—11题）

#### C1

Our school has a small garden room. There are three wooden benches for students and a grey desk for Mr Stone. We leave our workbooks on the benches while we look at the plants. Mr Stone keeps the garden tools in a cupboard.

Today, Anna found a green cloth bag near the door. Mr Stone placed it in a box on his desk. Inside are a blue cap and two yellow gloves. No name is written on the bag. Its owner can ask Mr Stone for it at break time. Students may also **borrow** garden tools from him, but must bring them back after using them.

### D组（第12—14题）

#### D1

My name is Theo. I am twelve and I study in Class Three. I like drawing plants, but my favourite activity is growing them. I have two small pots at home. My cousin enjoys drawing the flowers when they open.

Tuesday is my favourite day because our garden club meets after school. We check the plants and write down what has changed. On Sundays, I usually take a walk with my cousin. I enjoy our walks, but I like the club meetings even more. I always have something new to tell Mum afterwards.

### E组（第15—17题）

#### E1

Meet our new science teacher, Ms Bell. She is thirty-six years old. Green is her favourite colour. She goes to the nature club on Fridays. On Sunday afternoons, she reads adventure stories at home.

Ms Bell makes us look carefully before we answer questions. She brings simple objects to class and asks us to describe them. She listens when we disagree and helps us explain our ideas. She does not take the school choir. Mr Fox teaches the choir. We look forward to learning more with our new teacher.
