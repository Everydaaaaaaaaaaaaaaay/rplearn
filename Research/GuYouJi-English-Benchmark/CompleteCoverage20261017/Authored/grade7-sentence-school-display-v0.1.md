# 初一句中词形：学校展板

## 学生题面

在每句空白处填入一个适当的单词，或括号内单词的正确形式。无给词空为一个单词；给词空按正确形式，不另加一词上限。

以下十五句彼此独立，保留给词与无给词位置。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. A pair means two things, not one ____ three.

2. The green rulers belong to us. They are ____ (we) rulers.

3. Look! There ____ (be) a single bird on the sign.

4. The cat is hiding ____ the table; only its tail comes out from beneath it.

5. The child lost two baby ____ (tooth) this month.

6. The scarf belongs to Jack alone. This is ____ (Jack) scarf.

7. We have four clean ____ (brush) ready for painting.

8. Please do not ____ (touch) the wet paint.

9. —____ gloves are these? —They belong to my sister.

10. The visitors took a lesson in ____ (China) cooking.

11. She read the warning ____ (careful) before opening the tin.

12. We saw ____ owl near the school; the bird flew away immediately.

13. Our teacher gave ____ (we) a new task.

14. Would you like ____ (come) to the display with us tomorrow?

15. The room was quiet ____ every child was reading silently.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：or

not one or three列排除数量。

### 2：our

名词前物主限定词our。

### 3：is

当前观察单数存在句is。

### 4：under / beneath

under/beneath均在桌子下，后文beneath印证位置。

### 5：teeth

two后tooth复数teeth。

### 6：Jack's

唯一所有者Jack的所有格。

### 7：brushes

four后brush复数brushes。

### 8：touch

do not后原形touch。

### 9：Whose

问答所有权，Whose。

### 10：Chinese

Chinese修饰cooking。

### 11：carefully

修饰read的方式副词；未给比较上下文不列比较式，但未知不直接错。

### 12：an

owl元音起首首次引入an；the在共享知识语境可成立但未列待复核。

### 13：us

give后作宾语us。

### 14：to come / to be coming

like后普通/进行不定式对未来出席均合法。

### 15：because / as / since

三种原因连词均合完整因果语境。
