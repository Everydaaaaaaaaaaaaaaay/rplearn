## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: My name is Tom. Our class will give a puppet show on Wednesday. We wanted to use the school hall, but another class needs it that day. Our show will be in Room Six. We have made six puppets from old socks. I will move the rabbit, and my friend Emma will move the fox. The story is about two animals looking for a new home after a storm. My sister helped us make small trees from paper. The show starts at two thirty and lasts twenty minutes. Please come ten minutes early. You may bring a younger brother or sister. There is no ticket to buy.
