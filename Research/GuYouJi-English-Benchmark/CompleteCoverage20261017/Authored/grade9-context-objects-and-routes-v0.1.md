# 初三语境词汇：物品与路线

## 学生题面

根据各句首字母及句意写出完整单词。首字母也须输入。

以下各句彼此独立。首字母已印出的空，请在输入框填写完整单词（包括该首字母）。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The road sign gives the d____ to the station as two kilometres, not the time needed to walk there.

2. The box weighed less after the bricks were taken out; it was much l____ than before.

3. The knife is not sharp enough to cut the string; it is too b____.

4. This bottle holds one l____ of water, which is one thousand millilitres.

5. The painting is a c____ of the original; the real one is kept in the museum.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：distance

公里是距离distance，不是耗时。

### 2：lighter

重量比较lighter。

### 3：blunt

blunt钝与not sharp同义。

### 4：litre / liter

英美拼写litre/liter同量；不按语义相似无限收。

### 5：copy

copy与original/real对照。
