## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: May I clean the board for you?

W: Thank you. You can use this cloth.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Does your uncle live in the mountains, Rose?

W: No. His home is near the sea.

M: Are you going to swim there this Saturday?

W: Not this time. He will take me out in his small boat.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: I would like to buy a hat. How much is this big red one?

W: It is ten pounds. We also have a small red hat and a big green one.

M: I will buy the big red hat, please.
