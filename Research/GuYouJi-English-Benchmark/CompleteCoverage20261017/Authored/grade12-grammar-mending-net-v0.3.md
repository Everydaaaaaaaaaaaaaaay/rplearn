# 高三：渔网修补课

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

On a small harbour island, a retired fisher started teaching visitors to mend nets. At the beginning of each session, she asked them to notice (1) ____ a damaged section differed from the surrounding mesh. A quick glance was rarely enough. The class began with a (2) ____ (practice) demonstration, using a net large enough for everyone to see. Beside it lay a neatly (3) ____ (fold) example of a finished repair.

Each visitor received two wooden (4) ____ (needle) and a length of coloured cord. The colour made new stitches easier to distinguish from older ones. On the table was a card on (5) ____ the order of the first three stitches was drawn. After (6) ____ (check) the tension of each row, the visitors were ready to start the next one. The aim was not to finish quickly, (7) ____ to make each new section strong enough to hold its shape.

By the time the island's summer festival began, the class (8) ____ (produce) a large display net assembled from individual practice pieces. Some sections were imperfect, and the teacher left them that way so visitors could compare different results. Since the festival, several participants (9) ____ (return) for further lessons. The teacher is delighted (10) ____ (welcome) them back. A skill that once seemed useful only on fishing boats has become a way for people of different ages to spend time together, talking while their hands learn a new rhythm.

## 作者核对

### 1：how

notice how...differed承接观察损坏与周边差异的方式。

### 2：practical

a practical demonstration实操展示，形容词practical；名词practice的合成用法待复核而非自动错。

### 3：folded

副词neatly修饰过去分词定语，example为被折叠物。

### 4：needles

two要求复数。

### 5：which

on which引导定语从句，介词后which为宾语。

### 6：checking / having checked

after后动名词及完成式均表示检查先于开始下一行，保留两种合法体形式。

### 7：but / only / just

but构成not to X, but to Y的目的对照；only或just也可限定后一to不定式为真正目的，本段目标是保证新织部分足够牢固。三种一词形式均接受；这是本题语境的作者复核，不据此宣布所有not结构可互换。原and的显式排除缺乏充分论证，移至未列形式待复核，未认定已核实正确。

### 8：had produced / had been producing

by the time festival began前已完成，过去完成。 过去完成进行式也能表示截至该过去时点已经持续进行的活动；不以原预期仅列完成式排除。

### 9：have returned / have been returning

since festival后复数participants用完成/完成进行。

### 10：to welcome / to have welcomed

delighted后普通和完成不定式均可表已发生的迎回。
