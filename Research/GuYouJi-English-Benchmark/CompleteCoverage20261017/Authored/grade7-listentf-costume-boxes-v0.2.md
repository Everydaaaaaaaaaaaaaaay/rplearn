## 作者核对

### 完整原创对话脚本

N: Toby, do we have all the clothes for the play?

T: Yes, Nina. There are two boxes here. The blue box has the hats, and the red one has the coats.

L: I can carry both boxes to the hall.

N: Thank you, Leo, but the red one is heavy. Let us carry that one together.

T: I will take the blue box. Are we going through the garden?

N: No. It is raining now. We can use the inside stairs.

L: Fine. Let us move the boxes before the other pupils arrive.
