# 初一五组十七题：手作伙伴与新老师

## 学生题面

阅读A—E五组中的全部九份文字材料，回答第1至第17题；每题从A、B、C、D四个选项中选出最合适的一项。各题2分，共34分。人物与事件均为虚构。

### A组（第1—3题）

#### A1

I am Amy. This is my little green notebook. I write animal names in **it**. The first page is about ducks.

#### A2

Hi, I am Ben. My blue pencil case is on my desk. I keep two black pens inside.

#### A3

I am Cara. My yellow bag has a picture of a rabbit. Yellow is my favourite colour.

#### A4

Hello, I am Dan. I like yellow, too. My new ruler is yellow, but my schoolbag is brown.

### B组（第4—7题）

#### B1

Dear Tom,

My name is Ella. I am in Class Two at River School. I like making paper animals. I go to the craft club every Wednesday. My favourite colour is purple. Would you like to be my pen friend? I hope we can share our ideas.

Ella

#### B2

Dear Ella,

Thank you for your letter. I am happy to be your pen friend. My favourite colour is green. I draw animals at the art club on Mondays. I do not make paper animals yet. Perhaps you can show me an easy one.

Tom

### C组（第8—11题）

#### C1

This is our school art room. There are four large tables for students and one small white desk for Mr Reed. He keeps paper on that desk. Students use the large tables for drawing and model making.

This morning, Lucy found a red pencil case under a chair. Mr Reed put it in the drawer of his small desk. There are three pencils and a blue rubber inside. There is no name on it. If it belongs to you, please **collect** it from Mr Reed after lunch. It will stay in the drawer until its owner comes.

### D组（第12—14题）

#### D1

My name is Oscar. I am twelve and I am in Class Four. I like drawing, but my favourite activity is making models. I make small boats from paper at home. My sister enjoys drawing more than making things.

Saturday is my favourite day. In the morning, I go to the model club with my cousin. After lunch, we work on a boat together. On Sunday, I usually help Dad in the garden. I enjoy that too, but I always look forward to Saturdays most.

### E组（第15—17题）

#### E1

Let me introduce our new music teacher, Ms Green. She is thirty-one years old. She likes blue and often carries a blue music bag. On Tuesdays, she plays the piano at a community centre. She enjoys walking on Sundays and takes photographs of trees.

Ms Green teaches us songs, but she also asks us to listen to one another. She is patient when we find a song difficult. She does not run the school football club; Mr Hill does that. We are glad to have her at our school.
