### 答案与逐项依据

1. C。A：since引起点或因果，不能表达这里到早了；B：门开后不必因未开门等十分钟；C：还等十分钟门才开，说明开门前到；D：arrived until doors opened不表此处先到再等

3. B。A：地图未给天气路线，先走哪处看距离；B：根据地图线路判断先后位置；C：纸地图不是按价格选择行程，后句依据距离；D：地图没有可听的噪声让他们跟随

4. C。A：后文next才去公交厅，不把电车放最后；B：是当日活动，不是昨天先去；C：电车离入口近，因此先参观；D：后来确进了电车，非从不去

5. B。A：馆内不在水中，不能游进去；B：go inside进入停放的电车；C：孩子不是飞进展车；D：参观进入，不是做饭

### 全组回填

Dad took us to a transport museum. Arriving before it opened, we waited ten minutes. Through a window, I saw a red bus.

Inside, we received a free map. My sister wanted to see the tram (有轨电车), but I wanted the bus hall. We followed the route on the map. The tram was closer to the entrance, so we went there first.

The tram was not moving, and we could go inside. A bell hung near the wooden seats. My sister rang it once. The guide said, “One time for each visitor.”

Next, we visited the bus hall. Dad showed us a photo of his uncle working as a bus driver, sitting behind the wheel. I asked many questions about him.

We made paper tickets for free. I wrote “River Street” on mine. My sister chose “Hill Park”. We put them in a bag to keep the rain off.

After lunch in the museum cafe, we went back to the tram. I sat by the window and pretended to be a passenger going to River Street. Dad checked my paper ticket. My sister laughed and asked where the tram would stop next.

At four, we had more to see, so Dad promised to bring us back. I kept my ticket with the museum map. They cost nothing, but they helped me remember a happy day.
