## 作者核对

### 错误1

Last month明确过去叙事，用opened。

### 错误2

two限定可数名词复数pupils。

### 错误3

asked us to write，不定式to加在write前；未预标位置。

### 错误4

did not后主动态动词原形write。

### 错误5

双重比较more easier可删more；也可将easier改easy，构成more easy，列为同一错误的替代修法。

### 错误6

was后用happy描述失主的状态，不能用副词happily作此表语。

### 错误7

classmates后可加who/that引出过去帮助过他的同学；也可把helped改helping作主动分词后置修饰。三修法同属一个错误，不重复计入。

### 错误8

girl指人，限定性定语从句主语可用who/that。

### 错误9

had后的主动完成式用returned。

### 错误10

a useful后此处表示一次经历，用单数experience。

### 一种完整修订

Last month, our class opened a lost-property desk beside the school office. On the first morning, two pupils brought a scarf and a pair of gloves. Our teacher asked us to write a short description of each item. We kept the descriptions in a folder, but we did not write anyone's name on the noticeboard. Matching the gloves was easier than finding the scarf's owner, who was very happy when we finally called her. A younger child thanked the classmates who helped him look for his gloves. The scarf belonged to a girl who had returned to the music room to look for it. At the end of the week, everyone agreed that our small desk had been a useful experience.
