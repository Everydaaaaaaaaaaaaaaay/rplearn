Last (1) ____（星期六）, I helped my (2) ____（叔叔） deliver a large cake to a community hall. It was the (3) ____（第三） cake he had made that morning, but the only one with three layers. He put it in a tall box so that the top would not touch the lid.

It was raining when we left, so I carried an (4) ____（雨伞）. We used the side door near the hall's (5) ____（厨房）. The path was narrow, and we walked (6) ____（慢慢地）. Inside, a woman showed us the cake table. There were five paper (9) ____（花） on the wall behind it, but no other decorations. My uncle lifted off the box while I held the lid. The cake had arrived safely. After another ten minutes, the hall doors (10) ____（终于） opened. We stood back as the first guests came in and noticed the cake.

## 作者核对

### 1：Saturday

日名Saturday。

### 2：uncle

中文叔叔与my uncle吻合。

### 3：third

第三third修饰cake。

### 4：umbrella

冠词an后单数umbrella。

### 5：kitchen

厨房kitchen。

### 6：slowly

副词slowly修饰walked。

### 7：ourselves

主语we配反身复数ourselves。

### 8：because / since / as

都能表明搬得慢的原因；中文因为与语境均支持。

### 9：flowers

five后复数flowers。

### 10：finally / eventually

两副词均可表示经历等待后的最终开门。
