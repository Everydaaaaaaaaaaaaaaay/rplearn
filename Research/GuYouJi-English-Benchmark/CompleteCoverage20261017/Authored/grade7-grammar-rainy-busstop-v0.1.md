# 初一语法：雨中的车站

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

I lost two (1) ____ (glove) on the bus last Friday. Our teacher had taken (2) ____ (we) to a sports centre, and we were tired on the way home. (3) ____ (Lucky), my friend noticed the gloves on the seat before the bus drove away. He jumped back inside to get them, but his water bottle (4) ____ (fall) out of his bag as he stepped down again.

We picked it up together. I felt much (5) ____ (happy) than I had a minute earlier because I had my gloves back. Then the rain began. Our teacher had (6) ____ umbrella, but it was not large enough for everyone. We talked about (7) ____ (go) into the shop nearby, although we had no money left to spend.

We waited (8) ____ the next bus under the shop's roof. The owner came out and told us we could stay there. A very (9) ____ (friend) driver stopped for our group when he saw the teacher wave. As soon (10) ____ we got inside, I put the gloves deep in my bag. This time, I checked the seat before getting off.

1. 第1空（glove）

2. 第2空（we）

3. 第3空（Lucky）

4. 第4空（fall）

5. 第5空（happy）

6. 第6空（无提示，填1词）

7. 第7空（go）

8. 第8空（无提示，填1词）

9. 第9空（friend）

10. 第10空（无提示，填1词）

## 作者核对

### 1：gloves

two后复数gloves。

### 2：us

taken后宾格us。

### 3：Luckily

句首评价整个事件用副词Luckily。

### 4：fell

last Friday当次叙事中掉落用fell。

### 5：happier

much...than明确比较级happier。

### 6：an

umbrella元音起始，首次引入一把伞用an。

### 7：going

about后接动名词going。

### 8：for

wait for the next bus固定搭配。

### 9：friendly

very修饰形容词friendly，描述司机友善。

### 10：as

as soon as引导刚进入就收好手套。
