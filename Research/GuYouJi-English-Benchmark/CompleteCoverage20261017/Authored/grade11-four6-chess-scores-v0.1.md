# 高二六选四：教练藏起了比分

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A—F六个句子中为四个空白各选出最合适的一句。每个句子只能使用一次，其中有两项多余。

At the beginning of a training weekend, a chess coach pinned the results of every practice game beside the door. Before long, players were checking the list between moves. Some chose opponents mainly because a win against them seemed likely. Others became reluctant to discuss a game they had lost, even when it contained an interesting decision.

(1) ____ The coach noticed that conversations began with a player's total rather than with a question about a move. The table was useful for recording results, but it was becoming a poor substitute for examining how those results had been reached. She took it down for the next session without announcing that winning no longer mattered.

Players still completed their games under the usual time limits. They did not, however, report the result to the whole group immediately afterwards. (2) ____ These positions became the starting points for an afternoon discussion. The coach asked one player to explain the choice that had been made and the other to propose a serious alternative. Neither could dismiss the other's argument merely by pointing to the final result.

(3) ____ One player, whose attack had failed near the end, showed that an earlier exchange had given him a promising position. His opponent agreed that the later mistake did not make that earlier choice foolish. The group could examine both decisions separately, rather than allowing the winner's name to settle the discussion of every move in the game.

At the end of the weekend, the coach put the results back beside the door. (4) ____ A score could show who had won; it could not by itself explain which idea had deserved closer attention. The players had not been asked to stop competing. They had been asked to bring something more useful than a total to a conversation about how to improve.

### 共享候选

A. The numbers were beginning to replace the positions on the board as the focus of attention.

B. The point was to change what the score was allowed to explain.

D. Instead, each pair submitted one position about which they still disagreed.

E. The coach abolished every time limit so that no game would have a result.

F. A convincing explanation could therefore come from someone who had lost the game.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

逐项核对：A：承接查排名/选易胜对手，后句由总分而非棋步发问具体说明注意力转移；B：最终解释原则尚未通过新训练活动展开，此空更直接承接初始异常；C：后文每对双方均须解释，未禁止胜者参加；D：新作业尚未引入，下一段才说明代替报告结果；E：后文明确usual time limits，未取消限时；F：尚无新的讨论方法供therefore推导败者也有好解释。不靠把别空正确项先划掉来解释本空。

### 2：D

逐项核对：A：注意力问题前段已分析，不能引入后句These positions的交付来源；B：抽象目的没有引入These positions的具体棋局局面；C：与每对双方均解释不同选择矛盾；D：Instead接不立刻汇报结果，one position给These positions先行词；E：usual time limits仍保留且比赛有输赢，直接矛盾；F：只是推论败者可解释，不引入每对提交的positions。不靠把别空正确项先划掉来解释本空。

### 3：F

逐项核对：A：问题已经诊断，本段转向新讨论方法允许的评价；B：结尾原则尚需具体败者案例支持，此处F更精确引出One player；C：后文败者与胜者一起讨论，没有禁止获胜者；D：提交位置的方法已执行，本段介绍其效果与具体例子；E：限时始终保留，不能解释后面的早期与晚期决策区分；F：因此败者也能提出有说服力解释，后句攻击最终失败但早期选择合理为实例。不靠把别空正确项先划掉来解释本空。

### 4：B

逐项核对：A：回到最初问题但不解释为什么放回成绩表；B：承接放回成绩表，精确限定分数能说明什么与后句A score could...could not；C：放回表没有取消讨论或排除胜者，且与最后继续竞争相反；D：成对提交任务已完成，此处是全周末原则归纳；E：放回结果表说明仍记录结果，未取消计时或输赢；F：败者例子上段已完成，末段需要概括成绩作用而非仅重述例子。不靠把别空正确项先划掉来解释本空。
