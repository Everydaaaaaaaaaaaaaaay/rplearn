She had come out early and was delighted to (3) ____ the parcel there. I no longer needed to walk to her house. What seemed a delay had actually (4) ____ me time. Plans sometimes change, and a different route can bring an (5) ____ meeting.

### B篇（第6—15空）

I began arranging them by title. The librarian stopped me gently. "A title doesn't always tell you the (7) ____." The Last Train was a story, not a transport guide. She showed me how the label numbers (8) ____ to the shelf signs.

I worked so slowly that I felt I was holding everyone (9) ____. Another volunteer said accuracy mattered more than speed. He checked a few books with me, then stepped back. His help made me more (10) ____, rather than more dependent.

Later, a child wanted a book about insects. She hoped to (11) ____ the kinds she had seen in her garden. We found a picture guide, not the adventure story I had first considered.

Order was not just for tidy shelves: it made information (12) ____ to find. I wrote down the system to (13) ____ it next week. Now I explain the labels to newcomers (14) ____ asking them to hurry. Understanding a rule helps more than simply being told to (15) ____ it.

### 答案与逐项依据

1. C。A：没有路线宽度信息，时间对比不能推出宽度；B：没有声音信息，不能用时长判断噪声；C：五分钟比另一条十五分钟短，对应最短路线

2. A。A：十点开门比阿姨九点离开迟，等开门无法赶在离家前交包裹；B：当时尚未等且等会错过，不能说等待已成功；C：有另一条路线可走，等不利于按时交付，非必需

3. C。A：阿姨尚未收到，无退还我物品情节；B：阿姨是在站旁从我手上收，不是把包裹邮寄出去；C：我带包裹给阿姨，她在车站收取

4. B。A：不用再走到家，作者总结省时，非反耗时；B：站旁交付省去剩下到阿姨家的路程，反省时；C：没提供两倍耗时，而且免去剩余路程

5. A。A：原计划到阿姨家交付，途中相遇并非预期；B：阿姨delighted，非不友好相遇；C：已经实际相遇，不是不可能的相遇

6. A。A：原以为容易，实际看不懂规则，以however标转折；B：不懂规则不是工作容易的必然结果，非因此；C：前后是预期与困难反差，不是相似例

7. C。A：是公共馆分类标签，非以个人书主区别；B：未提售书价格，分类按主题而非售价；C：列Last Train其实故事非交通，说明标题不保证主题分类

8. B。A：数字不对书架标志表示反对；B：标签数字与书架标志对应，relate to符合；C：数字不会听书架标志

9. A。A：hold everyone back指拖慢大家，与slowly相呼应；B：馆内整理不表示把所有人留在馆外；C：hold everyone upside down是把人倒提，与整理慢无关

10. B。A：能自行工作不是变得看不见；B：先核几本再放手，明确rather than dependent；C：accuracy更重要，协助不是使其粗心

11. C。A：见到的实际种类不是要创造新种类；B：没有要把昆虫从图片抹去的要求；C：想知道庭院里见到的各类昆虫，图鉴便于辨认

12. C。A：已成功找到图鉴，非无法找资料；B：此处概括秩序改善检索，不是作者初学速度；C：理解分类后可找到合适材料，信息更易检索

13. B。A：未有出售馆内分类制度的情节；B：记下分类系统供下周使用；C：写下是保留以便下次用，不是摧毁

14. A。A：先解释标签后要求效率，before后接动名词表示先后；B：unless后不能只用asking构成此处条件从句；C：between需要对应两个边界，不能单接asking构成此处先解释后催快的时间关系

15. B。A：新成员整理书不需要出版规则；B：follow a rule遵守规则，与理解规则之所以然相对；C：说明规则是为帮助合理遵守，不是教破坏规则

### 全组回填

### A篇（第1—5空）

I left home early to take a parcel to Aunt May. The path through the garden was usually the shortest route, only five minutes instead of fifteen. That morning, however, the gate was locked. A notice said it would open at ten, an hour after my aunt had to leave. Waiting was therefore pointless. I turned towards the longer route. Two minutes along it, I met my aunt at a bus stop. She had come out early and was delighted to receive the parcel there. I no longer needed to walk to her house. What seemed a delay had actually saved me time. Plans sometimes change, and a different route can bring an unexpected meeting.

### B篇（第6—15空）

On my first morning as a library volunteer, I put returned books in order. I had imagined an easy job. The shelves, however, followed rules I did not know. Books with similar titles were sometimes far apart.

I began arranging them by title. The librarian stopped me gently. "A title doesn't always tell you the subject." The Last Train was a story, not a transport guide. She showed me how the label numbers related to the shelf signs.

I worked so slowly that I felt I was holding everyone back. Another volunteer said accuracy mattered more than speed. He checked a few books with me, then stepped back. His help made me more independent, rather than more dependent.

Later, a child wanted a book about insects. She hoped to identify the kinds she had seen in her garden. We found a picture guide, not the adventure story I had first considered.

Order was not just for tidy shelves: it made information easier to find. I wrote down the system to use it next week. Now I explain the labels to newcomers before asking them to hurry. Understanding a rule helps more than simply being told to follow it.
