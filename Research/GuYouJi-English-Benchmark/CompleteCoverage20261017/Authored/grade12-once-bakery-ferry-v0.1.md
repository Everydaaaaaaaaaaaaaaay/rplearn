1. How many bread rolls are still being baked?
   A. Eight.
   B. Twelve.
   C. Twenty.

2. Where are the maps now?
   A. In the top drawer.
   B. On a shelf by the office door.
   C. Beside the spare keys in a box.

3. What do the speakers decide to do?
   A. Stop listening to the recording.
   B. Open the window despite the noise.
   C. Use the fan with the window closed.

4. Why is the woman unable to take the ferry today?
   A. She has arrived after its departure.
   B. Strong wind has stopped the service.
   C. Her ticket is valid only next week.

5. Why does the woman decline the invitation?
   A. She has a prior family commitment.
   B. She dislikes walking near the sea.
   C. She expects bad weather that day.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: I ordered twenty bread rolls for eleven o’clock. Are they ready?

W: Twelve are in this bag. The other eight are still in the oven, but they’ll be out in ten minutes.

M: That’s all right. I’m a little early anyway.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: I can’t find the walking maps. Aren’t they usually in the top drawer?

M: They were, but we moved them to the shelf beside the office door yesterday. The drawer now holds spare keys.

W: Thanks. I’ll look on the shelf.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: It’s getting warm in here. Shall I open the window?

W: The traffic noise would make it hard to hear the recording. Could we use the fan instead?

M: Good idea. I’ll switch it on and leave the window shut.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: I’ve reached the harbour, but there’s a notice saying today’s ferry has been cancelled.

M: Yes, the wind is too strong. Your ticket will be valid tomorrow if the weather improves.

W: I’ll check the forecast before making another trip down here.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Would you like to join us on the coastal walk this Saturday? The weather should be lovely.

W: I’d love to, but I’ve promised to help my brother move into his new flat. Could you tell me when you plan the next walk?

M: Of course. I’ll let you know.
