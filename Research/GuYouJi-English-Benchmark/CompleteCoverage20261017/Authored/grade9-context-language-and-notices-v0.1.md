# 初三语境词汇：文字与通知

## 学生题面

根据各句首字母及句意写出完整单词。首字母也须输入。

以下各句彼此独立。首字母已印出的空，请在输入框填写完整单词（包括该首字母）。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Please put these names in a____ order, from A to Z.

2. The notice says the pool is c____ for repairs, so nobody may swim there today.

3. A word with the opposite meaning is an a____; for example, hot is one for cold.

4. She could not hear the announcement because the loudspeaker was s____; it made no sound at all.

5. The missing page is page ten: page nine is followed d____ by page eleven in this copy.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：alphabetical

from A to Z定义alphabetical order。

### 2：closed

closed for repairs不能入泳。

### 3：antonym

术语定义antonym，不依赖整句语义评分。

### 4：silent

silent与no sound共同限制；未知同义形式仍复核。

### 5：directly

directly表示中间没有第十页，与followed by完整结构。
