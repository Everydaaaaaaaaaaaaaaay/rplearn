A school drama group prepared captions for a short video of its latest performance. The first difference (1) ____ the two draft versions was easy to spot: one used a separate line for every pause, while the other joined several speeches together. Before either version was shown, the actors checked (2) ____ (they) own lines against the script. Each change of speaker (3) ____ (need) a clear label; otherwise, a viewer could attribute a remark to the wrong character.

Long captions made the screen look (4) ____ (crowd). Simply shortening every line was not a satisfactory answer, because a brief word could still carry an important part of the story. The group tested both versions using (5) ____ same scene and the same screen. Their teacher reminded them that it was essential (6) ____ (keep) the conditions consistent if they wanted to make a useful comparison. One revised caption was so clear (7) ____ the viewers identified the speaker without looking away from the action.

Two (8) ____ (child) from a younger class watched the trial. All three (9) ____ (suggest) they offered concerned moments when a caption vanished before a character finished speaking. The group extended those captions and tested the scene again. This time, the viewers followed the exchange more (10) ____ (accurate). The team kept the original drafts as well as the revision, so the changes could be explained rather than merely claimed.

## 作者核对

### 1：between

the difference between the two versions的固定关系，两个版本之间的差异。

### 2：their

own lines前用物主限定词their，与actors对应。

### 3：needs / needed

现行字幕原则可用needs；置于过去项目叙事内也可用needed。两种时态视角均与全文相容，不强行唯一。

### 4：crowded

look后的表语形容词，crowded描述字幕挤满屏幕。

### 5：the

the same为固定限定结构，同一场景、同一屏幕。

### 6：to keep

it was essential to keep...不定式作后置的实际主语，指测试时保持条件一致的要求。

### 7：that

so+形容词+that引出具体结果，从句有完整主语谓语。

### 8：children

two要求child不规则复数children。

### 9：suggestions

all three限定三个可数建议，需复数名词suggestions。

### 10：accurately

more已给，副词accurately修饰followed，不能双加more。
