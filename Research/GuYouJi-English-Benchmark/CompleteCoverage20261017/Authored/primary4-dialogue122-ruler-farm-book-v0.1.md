### 材料1完整原创脚本

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Where does your uncle live, Amy?

W: He lives on a farm near our town.

M: Are you going to ride a bike there this weekend?

W: No. I am going to help him collect eggs.

M: Can I borrow this book and read it at home?

W: Yes. Please show me your library card. Would you like the small green book?

M: No. I would like the big green one, please.
