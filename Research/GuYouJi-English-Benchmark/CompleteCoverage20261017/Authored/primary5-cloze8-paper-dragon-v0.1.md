# 小五：教室里的纸龙

## 学生题面

阅读短文，为每一空选择最合适的一项。每空独立选择，不设每项只能用一次。

There is a class show on Friday. We want to make a paper dragon for (1) ____ classroom. I am (2) ____ the paper with scissors (剪刀), and May is drawing the face. She has only a red pen. So the dragon has (3) ____ eyes. We join the pieces (4) ____ glue. It has two wings, one on each side. We make (5) ____ feet as well, one below each wing. “Carry it (6) ____,” May says. “The glue is still wet.” The dragon is too big for me to carry alone. I ask May to (7) ____ me. We want to hang it up now, (8) ____ we have to leave it on a table until the glue is dry. It must be dry before we can hang it up.

### 全组回填

There is a class show on Friday. We want to make a paper dragon for our classroom. I am cutting the paper with scissors (剪刀), and May is drawing the face. She has only a red pen. So the dragon has red eyes. We join the pieces with glue. It has two wings, one on each side. We make two feet as well, one below each wing. “Carry it slowly,” May says. “The glue is still wet.” The dragon is too big for me to carry alone. I ask May to help me. We want to hang it up now, but we have to leave it on a table until the glue is dry. It must be dry before we can hang it up.
