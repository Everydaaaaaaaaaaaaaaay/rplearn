Our class planned an (1) ____ (usual) group photograph for the end of term. Instead of standing in rows outside a classroom, we wanted everyone to appear doing a small part of an ordinary school day. The photographer asked us (2) ____ (stand) around the courtyard while she looked for a position that would include the whole group. The sun had already (3) ____ (rise) above the roof, and the shadows were shorter than they had been during our earlier trial.

She took several (4) ____ (photo), but we were still discussing who ought to be in them. A classmate pointed towards the caretaker, (5) ____ work kept the courtyard ready for us every morning. We had included the newly painted benches while leaving out the person who looked after them. I asked if she would like to join us. She smiled and said that she was proud (6) ____ the work, although she had never expected it to put her in a class picture.

As (7) ____ result of that conversation, we invited a few other staff members who often helped our class. We made sure each person could decide whether to take part. The courtyard became crowded, and our first carefully planned arrangement had to change. When the photographer asked us to move again, we replied as (8) ____ (polite) as we could and tried to leave enough space for everyone.

The photographer then showed us a camera (9) ____ could take a picture after a short delay. She put it on a stand, checked the frame and came over to join the group. We could now take turns (10) ____ (填2词) leaving one person permanently outside the picture. The final photograph was less tidy than our first plan, but it recorded a fuller version of the school day we wanted to remember.

## 作者核对

### 1：unusual

an后需元音起始形容词，且后文强调不同常规排队照；unusual合文意。

### 2：to stand

ask somebody to stand。

### 3：risen

had already之后过去分词risen。

### 4：photos

several要求可数复数photos。

### 5：whose

whose work表示caretaker的工作。

### 6：of

be proud of，后接工作名词。

### 7：a

as a result固定结构。

### 8：politely

as...as中的正级副词修饰replied。

### 9：which / that

camera后限定关系从句缺主语，which/that均可。

### 10：rather than / instead of

take turns与leaving...对照，rather than/instead of均可表达轮流而不是始终遗漏一人；各恰好2词。
