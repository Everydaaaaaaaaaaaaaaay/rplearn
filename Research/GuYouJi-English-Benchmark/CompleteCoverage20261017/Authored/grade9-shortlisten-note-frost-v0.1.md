## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: You always remember what to buy. Do you keep a list on your phone?

M: I used to, but now I stick a note on the fridge. I look at it before leaving home.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Is that silver watch yours, Kate?

W: No. It is my brother's. Dad gave it to him, and he let me wear it for the concert.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: The grass is white this morning. Will it snow again tomorrow?

M: No, that is frost, not snow. The forecast says tomorrow will be sunny, with rain returning on Friday.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I cannot upload my project. The school website will not accept the file.

W: It may be too large. Try making the photographs smaller before you send it again.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: I am writing a talk about how we save water at home. Can I describe your rain barrel, Dad?

M: Of course. Explain that we use its water for the garden, instead of turning on the outside tap.
