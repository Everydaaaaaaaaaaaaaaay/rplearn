# 小六：车站与行李

## 学生题面

完成全部十题：从每题括号内的两个词或词组中选取合适的一项，原样填入空白，不填字母或给词变形。两项均合法时，填写其中任意一项即可；不要将两项一起填写。候选只供本题使用。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The driver is ____ the last door now. (checking / check)

2. All passengers must ____ their own bags. (carry / carries)

3. Last Saturday we ____ how to read a railway map. (learnt / learned)

4. Dad did not ____ a taxi to the station. (took / take)

5. This case is ____ for the narrow luggage shelf. (small enough / enough small)

6. The waiting room opens ____ six in the morning. (on / at)

7. I cannot lift the box alone. Can you help ____? (I / me)

8. The children ____ behind the yellow line when I saw them. (were standing / stands)

9. Leah enjoys ____ pictures of old stations. (draw / drawing)

10. Please hold the glass jar ____. (firmly / firm)

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：checking

checking：is checking是现在进行结构。；check：is后不能直接接原形check构成这里的谓语。

### 2：carry

carry：情态动词must后接原形。；carries：must后不能用第三人称单数carries。

### 3：learnt / learned

learnt：learnt是learn的合法过去式。；learned：learned同样是合法过去式，不限定一种地区拼写。

### 4：take

took：did not后不能接过去式took。；take：did not后用动词原形take。

### 5：small enough

small enough：形容词small后接enough。；enough small：enough不置于small前。

### 6：at

on：钟点前用at，不用on。；at：at six表示六点。

### 7：me

I：I是主格，不能作help的宾语。；me：me是宾格，可作help的宾语。

### 8：were standing

were standing：复数children与were standing一致，过去观察语境成立。；stands：stands为第三人称单数，不与children一致。

### 9：drawing

draw：enjoy后不直接接动词原形。；drawing：enjoy后接动名词drawing。

### 10：firmly

firmly：firmly为副词，修饰握住动作。；firm：firm为形容词，不能作这里的方式副词。

### 完整合法回填

The driver is checking the last door now.

Leah enjoys drawing pictures of old stations.
Please hold the glass jar firmly.
