### 完整原创脚本

N: Last Sunday, I had five jobs to do at home. First, I carried a heavy box into the storeroom. Next, I swept the floor to get rid of the dust. Then I watered the plant by the window because its soil was dry. After that, I washed my hands under the tap. Finally, I cooked some soup for lunch. I was hungry after all that work.
