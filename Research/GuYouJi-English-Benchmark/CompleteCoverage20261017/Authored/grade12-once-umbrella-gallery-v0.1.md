1. What does the woman offer to do?
   A. Sell the man an umbrella.
   B. Lend the man her umbrella.
   C. Drive the man to his house.

2. What will the woman do?
   A. Visit without a guided tour.
   B. Cancel her visit to the gallery.
   C. Book a place on the full tour.

3. What is the man doing?
   A. Checking a student’s written homework.
   B. Taking notes during a public lecture.
   C. Recording material for a programme.

4. What does the man suggest?
   A. Choosing the cheaper camera despite its bag.
   B. Paying extra for a camera with a new bag.
   C. Giving up photography until prices fall.

5. Where are the speakers most probably?
   A. At a cinema entrance.
   B. At a railway station.
   C. At a motorway service area.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: The rain has started, and I left my umbrella at home. I suppose I’ll have to buy another one.

W: There’s no need. You can borrow mine from the office. I’m staying here until the rain stops.

M: Thanks. I’ll bring it back tomorrow morning.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: I’d like to visit the gallery on Tuesday, but the website says the student tour is full.

M: That tour is full, but you can still visit the rooms on your own. Only the guided group needs advance booking.

W: Then I’ll go without joining the tour.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Could you read the last sentence again? There was a noise from the corridor just as you finished.

W: Of course. Should I start the whole interview again?

M: No, just that sentence. I can join the clean recording to the earlier part when I edit the programme.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: The second-hand camera takes clear pictures, but the carrying bag is scratched. Should I buy a new camera instead?

M: The bag isn’t what takes the pictures. If the camera works well, I wouldn’t pay twice as much just for a neat bag.

W: That makes sense.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: The board says our train leaves in five minutes. Are we at the right platform?

W: Yes, platform six. I’ve just checked with the staff by the ticket barriers. We only need to walk past those two carriages.

M: Good. Let’s hurry before the doors close.
