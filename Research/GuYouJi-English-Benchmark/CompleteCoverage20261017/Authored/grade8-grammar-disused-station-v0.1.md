# 初二语法：旧车站的周末

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

Our town has a small station which no longer serves trains. A group of (1) ____ (volunteer) opens it to visitors on Saturdays. Last month, I (2) ____ (spend) a morning there with my grandfather. He remembered buying tickets from the little window when he was young. I had only seen the building from a passing bus, so I was curious about what was inside.

The entrance was (3) ____ (wide) than it looked from the road. We walked through it and found an exhibition of old suitcases. Grandfather stopped beside the (4) ____ (heavy) case in the display; all the weights were printed on the labels. He could not believe people had carried it without wheels. A volunteer explained that cases used to be lifted by railway workers as well as their owners.

We then looked (5) ____ a wall of photographs showing the station in winter. Grandfather recognised a shop in one of them. He pointed it out, (6) ____ I could not find it at first because its sign was so small. We moved closer and read the name together. It was the same shop (7) ____ which he had bought his first bicycle.

Before (8) ____ (leave), we bought two postcards from the little ticket window. The volunteer wrapped them for (9) ____ (we). Grandfather promised (10) ____ (bring) some of his own photographs next time. On the bus home, he told me stories about journeys I had never heard of before.

## 作者核对

### 1：volunteers

a group of后可数人群复数volunteers。

### 2：spent

Last month具体当次经历用spent。

### 3：wider

than明确比较级wider。

### 4：heaviest

the...in the display与全部标重构成展品最高级heaviest。

### 5：at

look at photographs。

### 6：but / yet

识别者指出而我起初仍找不到，but/yet均表达对比；未列连接词仍待复核。

### 7：from

buy a bicycle from a shop，介词前置from which。

### 8：leaving

before后动名词leaving，未给所有变式作排他证明。

### 9：us

for后宾格us。

### 10：to bring

promise to bring不定式。
