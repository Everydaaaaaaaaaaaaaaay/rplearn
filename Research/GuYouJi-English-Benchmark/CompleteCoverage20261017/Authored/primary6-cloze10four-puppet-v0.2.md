## 作者核对

### 全组回填

Last Friday, our class put on a puppet play. The stage was in the school hall, so I carried a blue cloth bag to the hall for the show. Inside it were two puppets, a fox and a duck. Their heads were made of wood, so they were quite heavy.

Before the show, I opened the bag and found only the fox. Where was the duck? I looked under the chairs, but the floor was empty. Then Mia pointed to the bag. There was a small hole in its bottom. The duck might have fallen out on the way.

We walked back along the same path. Near the school gate, a younger boy was holding our duck. He had picked it up beside the gate. We thanked him and hurried back.

The audience was still waiting. At the same moment, Mia held the fox while I held the duck. We were ready at last. After the play, I put both puppets on a table and sewed the hole. Next time I will check the bag before leaving home. I do not want to lose another actor!
