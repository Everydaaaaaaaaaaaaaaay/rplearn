On (1) ____（星期二）, our class took a model street to the town (2) ____（图书馆）. The model had (3) ____（十二） small houses and a tiny river. Each house could be lifted off so that visitors could see inside. It was a display, rather than a toy for children to play with.

The librarian's (4) ____（女儿） met us at the door. She cleared a long table while we carried the boxes in. We moved the smallest pieces (5) ____（仔细地） because their walls were thin. I had made the baker's shop by (6) ____（我自己） at home, and I was nervous about carrying it through the narrow doorway.

We checked the street (7) ____（两次） before opening the doors. My shop was the (8) ____（第九） building from the left, beside a small (9) ____（木制的） bridge. Later, visitors asked how we had made the windows. I showed them a spare wall and explained the steps. I felt (10) ____（自豪的） when they stopped to look closely at the details.

## 作者核对

### 1：Tuesday

明确日名星期二。

### 2：library

地点名词library对应图书馆。

### 3：twelve

twelve后houses，基数十二。

### 4：daughter

所有者的女儿，单数daughter。

### 5：carefully / cautiously

两个方式副词均合小心移动脆弱模型；中文仔细地在这里可含谨慎。

### 6：myself

主语I的反身代词myself。

### 7：twice

两次为频率副词twice。

### 8：ninth

序数第九ninth。

### 9：wooden

wooden修饰bridge表示木制。

### 10：proud

feel proud表示为完成作品自豪。
