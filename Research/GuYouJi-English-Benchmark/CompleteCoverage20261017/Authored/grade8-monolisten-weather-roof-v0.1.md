### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Hello, I am Maya, and I would like to tell you about our school weather project. There are twelve students in our team. We started in October, after our geography teacher showed us a notebook kept by a former pupil. It contained weather records from twenty years ago. We wanted to compare them with today's weather. At first we measured the temperature outside the library, but the reading changed whenever someone opened its warm doors. We now use a shaded corner of the roof garden. Two students go there each school day at one o'clock. They read the thermometer and write down the amount of rain. On Fridays, our whole team enters the week's results into a computer. We publish a simple chart once a month on the school website. Our next step is to add wind measurements. A parent has offered to help us build the equipment during the holiday, so we do not have to buy an expensive set.
