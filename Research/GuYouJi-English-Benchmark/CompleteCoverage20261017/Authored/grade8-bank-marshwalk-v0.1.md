Our science club arranged a walk through the wetland near town. We wrote (1) ____ names on a list and met at the school gate. Everyone had (2) ____ a bottle of water and a small notebook. Our teacher arrived even (3) ____ than usual because she wanted to check the route before we set off.

She asked us to speak (4) ____ near the birds. Several signs explained the (5) ____ between the paths open to visitors and those kept closed for wildlife. I wanted to go straight to the lake, (6) ____ the teacher said the higher path would let us see more of the area first.

We walked (7) ____ a narrow wooden bridge. One side of it was wet, and I stepped into a shallow puddle. Both my (8) ____ were soon damp, but my spare socks were safely wrapped in my bag. The teacher had (9) ____ us to bring them during the previous lesson, and I was glad I had listened.

This was the (10) ____ visit I had made to the wetland. On the first visit, I had hurried past the signs. This time, I noticed how the different paths fitted together. We sat on a bench by the lake and drew the route before walking back to the bus stop.

## 作者核对

### 1：our

names前物主限定词our。 对应词库项：we。

### 2：brought

had后过去分词brought。 对应词库项：bring。

### 3：earlier

even...than比较级earlier。 对应词库项：early。

### 4：quietly

speak quietly保护附近鸟类；未核平形quiet不列错，其他形式待复核。 对应词库项：quiet。

### 5：differences

the differences between两类道路差异，派生复数名词。 对应词库项：different。

### 6：but

原计划直奔湖边与改走高路的转折。 对应词库项：but。

### 7：across

walk across a bridge。 对应词库项：across。

### 8：feet

both my feet不规则复数。 对应词库项：foot。

### 9：taught

had后不规则过去分词taught。 对应词库项：teach。

### 10：second

第一次与本次形成第二次visit。 对应词库项：two。
