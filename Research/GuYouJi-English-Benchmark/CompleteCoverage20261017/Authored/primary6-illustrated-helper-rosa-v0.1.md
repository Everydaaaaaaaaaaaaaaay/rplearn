N: Rosa is helping at the city farm this week. She is one point six five metres tall. She will look after the ducks this morning. The fish are looked after by another helper, and the cat stays in the office. The helpers wear blue coats when they work outside, and Rosa has one too. She lives on Park Street, a short walk from the farm. She sometimes cycles to the farm with her brother. On other days her mother brings her by car. Rosa does not take the bus there. This afternoon she will help put clean straw in the animal shelters.
