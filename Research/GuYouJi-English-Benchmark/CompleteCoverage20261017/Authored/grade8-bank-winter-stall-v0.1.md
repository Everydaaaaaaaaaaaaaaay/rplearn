The afternoon became much (3) ____ than the morning. We moved the table away from the open door and wrapped the glass jars (4) ____ so that they would not break. Nobody hurried while doing that job, even when a queue began to form. It was our (5) ____ fair as a class, so we were still finding out how to organise things.

We stayed late (6) ____ the last visitors had only just arrived. Several people had (7) ____ cards and decorations which they wanted to show us before leaving. The younger (8) ____ put their cards beside the jars, and their parents helped them write the prices.

At the end, we had no (9) ____ about where to put the money: we had promised it all to the school breakfast project. We counted it with the teacher and waited (10) ____ her to write the total on the board. The classroom was untidy, but everyone wanted to do the fair again next year.

## 作者核对

### 1：their

old toys前物主限定词their。 对应词库项：they。

### 2：sold

fair过去叙事中卖出sold。 对应词库项：sell。

### 3：colder

much...than比较级colder。 对应词库项：cold。

### 4：carefully

不赶时间而关注玻璃完整，carefully修饰wrapped。 对应词库项：careful。

### 5：first

首次班级集市用first。 对应词库项：one。

### 6：because

晚来的访客解释继续留守。 对应词库项：because。

### 7：made

had后过去分词made，叙述访客已做好卡片。 对应词库项：make。

### 8：children

younger children与parents对应复数人群。 对应词库项：child。

### 9：choice

have no choice about...派生名词choice。 对应词库项：choose。

### 10：for

wait for her to write，介词for。 对应词库项：for。
