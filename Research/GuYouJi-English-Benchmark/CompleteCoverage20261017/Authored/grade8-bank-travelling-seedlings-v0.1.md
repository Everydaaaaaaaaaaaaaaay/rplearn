### 共享词库

surprise | he | without | far | lose | three | friend | find | gentle | because

My uncle wanted to take some young plants to (1) ____ old friend in another town. He had (2) ____ the address and thought he would have to cancel the visit. Then he remembered that it might be written on a card in his desk.

The town was much (3) ____ away than I expected. We put the plants in a box and covered them with paper. Uncle held the smallest one (4) ____ while I placed a rolled towel around its pot. We did not close the top of the box (5) ____ the leaves were taller than the sides.

Uncle's friend was a very (6) ____ person. He met us at the bus station and carried the box to his house. As soon as we arrived, Uncle (7) ____ the card in his coat pocket. He had put it there when he went to look for the desk key, then forgotten about it.

I knew that it was the (8) ____ house on the street because the first two had blue doors. We went in (9) ____ knocking because Uncle's friend was right beside us. His garden was much larger than ours, with a covered area for young plants. I was (10) ____ to see how many different shapes of leaf there were. We left our box beside the path and spent the afternoon looking around.

## 作者核对

### 1：his

old friend前物主限定词his。 对应词库项：he。

### 2：lost

had lost先前弄丢地址的完成叙述。 对应词库项：lose。

### 3：farther / further

物理距离比较farther/further均可，一词限制均满足。 对应词库项：far。

### 4：gently

拿着幼苗动作轻，用副词gently。 对应词库项：gentle。

### 5：because

叶子高于箱壁解释不盖顶。 对应词库项：because。

### 6：friendly

very friendly person形容词friendly。 对应词库项：friend。

### 7：found

当次叙事中在衣袋找到用found。 对应词库项：find。

### 8：third

前两房蓝门，本房第三，third。 对应词库项：three。

### 9：without

without knocking：主人在旁因此未敲门。 对应词库项：without。

### 10：surprised

I was surprised to see表达人的感受。 对应词库项：surprise。
