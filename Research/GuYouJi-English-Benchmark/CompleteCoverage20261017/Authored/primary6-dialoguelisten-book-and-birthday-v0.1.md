1. What did the woman buy?

   C. A book about trains.

2. What will Leo do before swimming?

   C. Finish a music lesson.

3. Which bag will Leo take?
   A. The red one.
   B. The blue one.
   C. The green one.

4. What will the children make?
   A. A cake.
   B. A birthday card.
   C. A paper boat.

## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

M: I thought your sister wanted a toy train for her birthday.

W: She did, but now she wants a book about trains. I bought one yesterday.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

W: The library closes at five today, Leo. Can you return my book after school?

M: I finish at four. I can take it back before my swimming lesson at half past four.

W: Thank you. The book is in the blue bag, not the red one.

M: All right. I will take the blue bag.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

M: Are we making a cake for Mum this afternoon?

W: Dad has already made one. Let us make a birthday card instead.

M: I can draw the flowers. Can you write the words?

W: Sure. We should finish it before Mum comes home at six.

M: I will get the paper and pencils now.
