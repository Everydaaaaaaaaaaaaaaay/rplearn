## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: How much are these apples?

W: Four yuan a bag. There are three apples in each bag.

M: I will take two bags, please.

W: Here you are. That is eight yuan.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Are the visitors coming to our school on Friday?

W: Yes, but they will arrive at eleven, not ten.

M: Will we show them the garden first?

W: The music room comes first. They want to hear our school band.

M: What will we do after lunch?

W: We will take them to the garden. Remember to bring your camera.

M: I cannot. My brother is using it.

W: Then bring your notebook. You can write down the visitors' names for our class report.
