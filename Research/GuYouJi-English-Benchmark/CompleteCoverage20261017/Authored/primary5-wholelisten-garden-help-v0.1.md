## 作者核对



### 完整原创脚本

W: Can you help at the school garden on Monday, Sam?

M: Yes. Where should I meet you?

W: At the front gate at half past nine. We will walk to the garden together.

M: Should I bring some plants?

W: The teacher has the plants. Bring your old gloves. We will put the plants into large pots.

M: Do we need to carry water from home?

W: No, there is water at the garden. Bring a bottle for yourself, though.

M: What if it rains?

W: We will work in the covered area beside the hall. We do not need to cancel the activity.
