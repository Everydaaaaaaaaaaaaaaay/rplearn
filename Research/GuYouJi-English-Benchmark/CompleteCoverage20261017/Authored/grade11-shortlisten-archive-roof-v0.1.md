1. What did the man pay for?
   A. Entering the archive.
   B. Joining an exhibition.
   C. Obtaining image copies.

2. Why did the woman keep most tiles?
   A. New ones were unavailable.
   B. The old ones were still usable.
   C. She wanted to avoid climbing the roof.

3. What disappointed the man?
   A. The lecture topic was unfamiliar.
   B. There were too few examples.
   C. There was too little time for questions.

4. How does the woman view the disagreements?
   A. They can improve the plans.
   B. They make teamwork pointless.
   C. They show nobody is prepared.

5. What does the man suggest?
   A. Printing five hundred immediately.
   B. Printing a smaller initial batch.
   C. Cancelling the printed programme.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The archive says the photographs are free to view. Why did you pay twelve pounds?

M: I did not pay to see them. I ordered high-resolution copies for the exhibition. The charge was for making those files.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: You finished the roof repair quickly. Did you replace all the tiles?

W: Only the broken ones. Most were sound, so throwing them away would have been wasteful.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: I expected the lecture to be difficult, but the speaker used everyday examples.

M: Yes, though I wish she had left more time for questions. That was the part I was most looking forward to.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Is your new team getting along?

W: We disagree often, but people explain why. It takes longer to decide things, yet the final plans are usually better than my first ideas.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Shall we print five hundred copies of the programme?

M: We only sold two hundred tickets last year. Let us start with three hundred and print more if bookings rise.
