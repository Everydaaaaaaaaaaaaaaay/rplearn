## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Are you looking for trousers, Zoe?

W: No, I have some. I need a skirt for the concert. I will wear it with my blue T-shirt.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Is your birthday on September the thirteenth, Evan?

M: No, that is my friend’s. My birthday is on October the thirteenth. Our music club starts on September the third.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Do you still ride your bike to school, Sara?

W: Not now. I take the bus because the new school is far away. It is too far to walk.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: What time do you arrive at school, Tom?

M: At eight. I leave at four in the afternoon. We have lunch at school, so I stay there all day.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Do you take a walk after dinner, Lily?

W: Not straight away. I watch a short TV programme first. Then I finish my homework, and we walk after that.
