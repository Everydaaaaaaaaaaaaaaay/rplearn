## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: My name is Oliver. Last spring I started a travelling chess stall with my aunt. She had taught me the game when I was nine. We noticed that children at the Sunday market often had to wait while their parents shopped. So we took three folding tables there and invited them to play. At first we asked each player for a pound, but many children did not have any money. Now everyone can play free. A local shop gives us old chess sets, and we repair them. We keep all the pieces in numbered cloth bags, because the cardboard boxes got wet in the rain. The stall runs every second Sunday, from ten until twelve. Beginners learn with my aunt, while I play short games with more experienced children. Next month we will add a fourth table. We are not planning a competition: we simply want more children to have a turn.
