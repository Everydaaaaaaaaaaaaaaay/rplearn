## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Hi, Nina. Are we going to practise basketball at ten tomorrow?

W: The court is busy at ten. Let us meet there at half past nine.

M: Fine. I will bring the basketball.

W: Good. I have two bottles of water for us.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Excuse me. Did anyone find a wallet this morning? It is green.

W: Yes. A boy in Class Four brought one to the school office.

M: That sounds like mine. Where did he find it?

W: Under a bench in the playground.

M: Can I collect it now?

W: Yes. Please tell the office teacher what is inside it.

M: There is a small photo of my dog inside.
