## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The posters came out pale. Should I order darker ink?

M: The ink is fine. The paper is absorbing it too quickly. Try a smoother sheet before changing the colour.

W: I will test that on one poster first.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: The orchard owners agreed to our survey, but not during the apple harvest.

W: I expected that. Their workers will be too busy. Could we visit in November instead of September?

M: Yes. The manager said the second week would be best.

W: We should change our research question too. We cannot count visitors if the orchard is closed to the public then.

M: We can interview the owners about how the harvest went. That would still give us useful evidence.

W: Agreed. I will revise the question list.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Your grandfather's old radio looks good in the display. Does it still work?

M: Yes, but I decided not to switch it on during the exhibition. It picks up too much interference in that room.

W: Then how will visitors hear what radio sounded like at the time?

M: I have a recording of him describing his favourite programmes. We can play that through a small modern speaker.

W: Have you checked that he is comfortable with people hearing it?

M: Yes. He helped me choose the extract and asked me to remove one personal story.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I have worked out the cost of the school magazine: eight hundred pounds for four hundred copies.

W: That is more than we expected. Could we print fewer copies?

M: The printer said three hundred would cost six hundred and sixty pounds, because setting up the machine costs the same.

W: We have three hundred paid orders already. Perhaps print three hundred and keep the digital version available for everyone else.

M: I worry that late buyers will miss out.

W: We can ask them to reserve a copy by Friday before confirming the final number. There is no need to guess.

M: Good. I will send the reservation form today. Should we cut the colour pages?

W: No. Those pages show the art projects. We agreed they mattered more than the glossy cover, so use a plain cover instead.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: This is an update on the old footpath behind the school. The council has agreed to repair the steps, but work cannot begin until a survey checks the retaining wall. Until then, the path will remain closed. Please do not move the temporary barriers. The alternative route through Mill Street adds about ten minutes to the walk. The school is not asking pupils to arrive earlier; the first morning activity will start ten minutes later while the closure continues. Students who use the public bus should follow the normal timetable, because its route is unchanged. We expect another update on Wednesday, but Wednesday is not a promised reopening date. Families will receive a message once the wall has been checked and a safe date has been confirmed.
