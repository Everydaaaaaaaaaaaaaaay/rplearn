# 小五：给新同学的路线卡

## 学生题面

阅读短文，为每一空选择最合适的一项。每空独立选择，不设每项只能用一次。

A new pupil comes (1) ____ our classroom on Monday. His name is Ben. At lunch time, he asks where the (2) ____ is. “I want to borrow a book,” he says. I write a route (路线) card for him. The book room is (3) ____ the music room and the art room. Both rooms are on the ground floor. The card says, “Turn (4) ____ at the stairs, not right or back.” The walk is (5) ____: it takes only one minute. I draw two arrows and colour (6) ____ green. Ben (7) ____ at the card carefully. He can follow it easily (8) ____ the words and arrows show every turn. At the door, he waves and thanks me.

### 全组回填

A new pupil comes into our classroom on Monday. His name is Ben. At lunch time, he asks where the library is. “I want to borrow a book,” he says. I write a route (路线) card for him. The book room is between the music room and the art room. Both rooms are on the ground floor. The card says, “Turn left at the stairs, not right or back.” The walk is short: it takes only one minute. I draw two arrows and colour them green. Ben looks at the card carefully. He can follow it easily because the words and arrows show every turn. At the door, he waves and thanks me.
