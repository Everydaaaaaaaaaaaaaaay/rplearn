On (1) ____（星期五）, my (2) ____（祖母） wanted to play chess in the (3) ____（花园）. A small table usually stood beside the back door, but it was too low for her chair. We went to the shed to look for another one. The (4) ____（第八） table in the row was just the right height.

It was quite (5) ____（重的）, so my brother helped me carry it. We moved it (6) ____（仔细地） through the doorway, keeping both sides level. I wiped the top by (7) ____（我自己） while Grandmother brought out the chess pieces. One black piece was missing, and we used a button in its place.

The first game lasted (8) ____（二十） minutes. My brother watched us, but he did not give advice (9) ____（在……期间） the game. At the end, Grandmother pointed to the winning move (10) ____（高兴地）. I had lost, but the table had worked well. We left it under the roof by the door so that we could use it again the next day.

## 作者核对

### 1：Friday

日名Friday。

### 2：grandmother

祖母grandmother。

### 3：garden

花园garden。

### 4：eighth

第八eighth。

### 5：heavy

形容词heavy与重的、两人搬吻合。

### 6：carefully / cautiously

小心移动避免晃动，可carefully或cautiously。

### 7：myself

I反身myself。

### 8：twenty

twenty后minutes。

### 9：during

during后the game名词短语。

### 10：happily / joyfully

两副词均表示开心地向孙辈展示胜利。
