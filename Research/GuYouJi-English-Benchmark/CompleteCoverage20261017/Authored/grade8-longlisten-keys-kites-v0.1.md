## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Good evening. I am staying in Room Twenty-One, but my key does not work.

M: I am sorry. Let me check it at the desk. Is your name Mrs Green?

W: Yes. I have tried the key twice.

M: The card is damaged. I will give you a new one. Please return the old card to me now.

W: Here it is. Thank you.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Why are you putting the kite away, Sara? The wind is still strong.

W: The string broke twice yesterday. I need a stronger one before I fly it again.

M: There is a shop by the bridge. I bought some string there last month.

W: Is it open on Sunday?

M: Yes, until four. It is only half past two now. We can walk there.

W: Good. I will take the kite with me, so the shopkeeper can see what I need.

M: I will come too. I need some glue for my model boat.
