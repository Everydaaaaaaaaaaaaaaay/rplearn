On Sunday morning, Grandma baked small cakes for her neighbours. She put six cakes in each of two paper (1) ____. One box had a red lid, and the other had a green lid. She asked me to take (2) ____ to two different houses.

The red box was for Mr Lee, who lived (3) ____ the bank and the post office. The green one was for Mrs Hill at the end of the street. I wrote their names on cards, but I left the cards on the kitchen (4) ____.

Halfway down the street, I noticed my mistake. I could not remember which colour belonged to which person. I did not (5) ____ the right address for each box, so I went back and (6) ____ Grandma. She showed me the cards.

This time I tied each card to the right box (7) ____ a piece of string. Mr Lee was at home and thanked me. Mrs Hill was out, (8) ____ her daughter took the other box. When I got home, Grandma asked whether I had delivered all (9) ____ cakes. I counted six and six in my head. '(10) ____!' I said.

## 作者核对

### 全组回填

On Sunday morning, Grandma baked small cakes for her neighbours. She put six cakes in each of two paper boxes. One box had a red lid, and the other had a green lid. She asked me to take them to two different houses.

The red box was for Mr Lee, who lived between the bank and the post office. The green one was for Mrs Hill at the end of the street. I wrote their names on cards, but I left the cards on the kitchen table.

Halfway down the street, I noticed my mistake. I could not remember which colour belonged to which person. I did not know the right address for each box, so I went back and asked Grandma. She showed me the cards.

This time I tied each card to the right box with a piece of string. Mr Lee was at home and thanked me. Mrs Hill was out, but her daughter took the other box. When I got home, Grandma asked whether I had delivered all twelve cakes. I counted six and six in my head. 'Yes!' I said.
