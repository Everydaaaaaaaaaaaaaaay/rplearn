## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Would you like some tea, Emma?

W: No, thank you. I would like a glass of water.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Is your new classroom on the first floor?

M: No, it is on the second floor, next to the music room.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: What do we need for lunch, Mum?

W: We have rice and fish. Please buy some noodles at the shop.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: What colour are your football shoes, Jack?

M: They are orange. My running shoes are blue, and my old shoes are black.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Does your father go to work by bus?

W: No. His office is near our home, so he walks there.
