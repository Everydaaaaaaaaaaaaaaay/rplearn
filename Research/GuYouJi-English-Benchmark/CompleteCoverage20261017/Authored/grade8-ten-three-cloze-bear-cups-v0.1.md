# 初二十空三选：熊与那只蓝杯子

## 学生题面

阅读下面完整寓言，从每题三个选项中为第1—10空各选最合适的一项。每题1.5分，共15分。

In a town where animals could talk, Bear ran a little tea room. He owned twelve cups. Eleven were white, but one was blue. Every evening, he counted them before closing. One night, he counted only (1) ____ white cups beside the blue one. A cup was missing.

Bear remembered seeing Crow near the shelf. Without asking anyone a question, he hurried outside and (2) ____ Crow of taking it. Crow said he had only carried clean plates into the kitchen. Bear did not believe him.

Mouse had been washing up. She asked Bear to show her exactly how he had counted. Bear pointed to the cups on the shelf. Mouse then asked, "Did you look anywhere (3) ____?"

Bear shook his head. Mouse led him to the kitchen. The missing cup was behind a bowl, waiting to be washed. Bear felt (4) ____. He had turned a guess into a claim against his neighbour.

He went to Crow and said, "I was wrong. You didn't take anything. I'm sorry." Crow accepted the (5) ____, but said he had felt hurt. Bear listened without trying to (6) ____ his mistake.

The following evening, a spoon was missing. This time, Bear did not look for someone to blame. He first checked the tables and the washing area. A visitor was still (7) ____ the spoon to stir her tea.

Bear waited until she finished. Afterwards, he made two lists: things on the shelf and things still in use. Keeping the lists (8) ____ made the count clear.

Mouse asked if he would ever make another mistake. "Of course," said Bear. "But I'll ask questions (9) ____ making claims." Crow smiled and joined them for tea. The blue cup stood between the white ones, a reminder that seeing only (10) ____ of the picture was not enough.

### 答案与逐项依据

1. B。A：十一白加蓝已全十二，不会缺一；B：原十一白一蓝，少一白则看见十只白杯；C：原只有十一白，不可能缺杯后有十二白

2. B。A：warn sb of taking不是这里指控某人偷拿的搭配；B：accuse sb of doing指无核查就指控取杯；C：remind sb of taking指提醒做过的事，不是无证据的指控

3. A。A：anywhere else表示还看没看其他地方；B：anywhere almost不构成其他地点表达；C：anywhere never不构成其他地点表达

4. C。A：没有嫉妒对象，情绪来自自己的误责；B：指控错误不是胜利；C：发现杯在厨房、误责邻居，感到羞愧

5. B。A：没说付钱，Crow接受的是口头歉意；B：前句认错道歉，Crow接受道歉；C：还未邀请喝茶，接受的是前句道歉

6. A。A：Bear不辩护自己的错而听受伤感受；B：他正在认错改错，不是拒绝纠正错误；C：他已明确说I was wrong，非不肯承认

7. A。A：访客用勺搅茶，所以勺还不在架上；B：搅茶是公开用途，不是藏勺；C：访客喝茶，不是在售卖勺子

8. A。A：两份单分开，区分架上与在用物，避免混计；B：藏单不利大家核点，文中要计数清楚；C：架上与在用是不同清单，不应写成相同

9. C。A：unless后不能单接making claims作此处条件从句；B：between making claims需要两个边界，不表达先核后断言；C：先问清再断言，before明确顺序

10. C。A：看全不是问题，结局总结应是只看部分不足；B：seeing only none of不构成此处只见一部分的表达；C：第一次只看架上部分，没查厨房或在用，信息不全

### 全组回填

In a town where animals could talk, Bear ran a little tea room. He owned twelve cups. Eleven were white, but one was blue. Every evening, he counted them before closing. One night, he counted only ten white cups beside the blue one. A cup was missing.

Bear remembered seeing Crow near the shelf. Without asking anyone a question, he hurried outside and accused Crow of taking it. Crow said he had only carried clean plates into the kitchen. Bear did not believe him.

Mouse had been washing up. She asked Bear to show her exactly how he had counted. Bear pointed to the cups on the shelf. Mouse then asked, "Did you look anywhere else?"

Bear shook his head. Mouse led him to the kitchen. The missing cup was behind a bowl, waiting to be washed. Bear felt ashamed. He had turned a guess into a claim against his neighbour.

He went to Crow and said, "I was wrong. You didn't take anything. I'm sorry." Crow accepted the apology, but said he had felt hurt. Bear listened without trying to defend his mistake.

The following evening, a spoon was missing. This time, Bear did not look for someone to blame. He first checked the tables and the washing area. A visitor was still using the spoon to stir her tea.

Bear waited until she finished. Afterwards, he made two lists: things on the shelf and things still in use. Keeping the lists separate made the count clear.

Mouse asked if he would ever make another mistake. "Of course," said Bear. "But I'll ask questions before making claims." Crow smiled and joined them for tea. The blue cup stood between the white ones, a reminder that seeing only part of the picture was not enough.
