# 初一语法：星期六的汤

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

Last Saturday, my brother and I (1) ____ (help) Grandma make lunch. We washed three (2) ____ (tomato) and put them beside the sink. Grandma gave us a small knife and showed us how to keep (3) ____ (we) fingers away from its edge. My brother worked (4) ____ (careful), so I watched him before trying it myself.

At first, my pieces were large and uneven. My brother said his pieces looked (5) ____ (good) than mine, but Grandma just smiled. It was (6) ____ first time I had prepared vegetables for a whole family. After (7) ____ (cut) the last tomato, I put all the pieces into a bowl. We were both proud (8) ____ our work, even though the kitchen table was wet.

While the soup was cooking, Grandma talked about the (9) ____ (important) of cleaning as we worked. She said a cook needed space, not just good food. We washed the bowl and dried the table before lunch. Both my mother (10) ____ my grandmother tried the soup. I thought it tasted especially good because we had made it together.

7. 第7空（cut）

8. 第8空（无提示，填1词）

## 作者核对

### 1：helped

Last Saturday叙述当次帮忙用过去式。

### 2：tomatoes

three后可数名词复数tomatoes。

### 3：our

名词fingers前用物主限定词our。

### 4：carefully

修饰worked用副词carefully。

### 5：better

than明确比较，good不规则比较级better。

### 6：the

the first time指第一次。

### 7：cutting / having cut

after后可用动名词cutting；完成式having cut强调已切完同样合叙事。

### 8：of

be proud of固定介词搭配。

### 9：importance

the importance of需要派生名词importance。

### 10：and

both...and配对连接两个品尝者。
