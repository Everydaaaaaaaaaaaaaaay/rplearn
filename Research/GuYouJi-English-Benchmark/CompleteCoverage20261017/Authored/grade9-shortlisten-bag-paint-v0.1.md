1. Why does the boy prefer a pencil for notes?
   A. It helps him avoid distractions.
   B. His phone is broken.
   C. Pencils are easier to carry.

2. Which backpack belongs to Zoe?
   A. The one with a red strap.
   B. The one with a green strap.
   C. The one with a yellow strap.

3. Why is the school yard closed?
   A. It is covered in snow.
   B. The lines are being painted.
   C. A concert is taking place.

4. What does the girl suggest?
   A. Ask everyone to wear a badge.
   B. Avoid meeting new people.
   C. Use the name when replying.

5. What did the boy enjoy most?
   A. Choosing the music.
   B. Carrying the camera.
   C. Rewriting the ending.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Why do you carry a pencil instead of using your phone for notes?

M: The phone distracts me. When I write with a pencil, I do not stop to check messages.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Did you leave your backpack in the music room, Zoe?

W: That is not mine. Mine has a yellow strap. The one with the red strap is Liam's, and the green one belongs to Sam.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Will the school yard be open this afternoon?

M: No, workers are painting the lines. It will open again tomorrow morning, unless it rains tonight.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I keep forgetting names when people introduce themselves.

W: Try using the person's name once in your reply. It helps me connect the name with the face.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Did you enjoy making the class film?

M: Very much. I liked choosing the music most. Carrying the camera was tiring, and we had to rewrite the ending twice.
