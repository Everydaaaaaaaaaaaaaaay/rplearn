1. How did Amy feel during most of the film?
   A. Bored.
   B. Frightened.
   C. Amused.

2. How much will the man pay for the gloves?
   A. Twelve pounds.
   B. Eighteen pounds.
   C. Thirty pounds.

3. Who helped the woman find the path?
   A. A bus driver.
   B. A woman with a dog.
   C. A school friend.

4. What is the boy's hobby now?
   A. Making model planes.
   B. Collecting stamps.
   C. Taking bird photos.

5. Which box will the woman carry?
   A. The small one.
   B. The heavy one.
   C. The long one.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Was the film frightening, Amy?

W: The beginning was a little scary, but the two brothers were so funny that I laughed through most of it.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: These gloves cost eighteen pounds. That pair costs twelve.

M: I will take the twelve-pound pair. They fit better, and I can spend the rest on a book.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Did you enjoy the mountain walk?

W: Yes, but we nearly lost the path. Luckily a woman walking her dog showed us the way down.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: Do you still make model planes at weekends?

M: Not now. I used to enjoy that, but these days I take photos of birds in the park.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Can you help me carry these boxes?

W: Of course. Put the small one on the chair. I will take the heavy one, and you can carry the long one.
