### A组（第1—3题）

#### A1

Brook Club celebrates its Readers’ Day on the second Sunday in September 2028. The club at Pine Hill chooses September 20. These local events belong to our story, and neither club must follow the other’s calendar. At Brook, families bring a favourite page to read aloud together.

The helpers prepare seats and place books where young readers can reach them. They arrive before the families and stay to put everything away. Some visitors know the stories well but have never noticed the people setting up the room. This year the children will make a display to thank those helpers. A short message about a useful act is welcome; buying a large present is not necessary.

### B组（第4—6题）

#### B1

Our school is small, with six classes beside a village field. We have grass to play on and no fence around it. Mr Reed, our maths teacher, makes lessons lively with games about lines and shapes. Once we made a square by holding a long piece of string. We call him Uncle Reed because he listens to our ideas.

Our English teacher is Ms Bell. She brings songs and little stories into the lessons. **They** give us chances to meet a new word more than once, and help us remember it. We can sometimes choose which story to hear again. Mr Cole teaches PE and likes the trees at the edge of the field.

This picture shows the four of us. Mr Cole is sitting on a low branch. Mr Reed is the man in dark sunglasses. Ms Bell and I stand under the tree. I am the shortest person and am wearing a little cap. Nobody in the picture is holding a ball.

### C组（第7—10题）

#### C1

In our school story, a language group follows a routine for remembering sounds. A new member listens to a short word twice and tries to keep its sound in mind. Speaking comes next. The teacher does not begin by handing out a page to copy. She wants the children to know what they are trying to say before they say it.

The group meets for a few short games each week. Members hear the words, choose suitable pictures and use the words again. Repeated practice across different days is the important part of this routine. One long look at a list is not enough for the group’s activity. The story does not claim that every child remembers in exactly the same way.

Amir and Zoe heard two languages at home when they were small children, before primary school. Their club friends mostly hear the new language in school activities. Other lessons fill much of the timetable, leaving less time to practise it. That is why the teacher includes small opportunities to listen and speak again whenever the club meets.

### D组（第11—15题）

Read the three poems below.

#### D1

Your picture book is closed at last;
The little lamp is glowing low.
I draw the curtain on the day;
Our busy feet have ceased to go.

Your small toy boat lies on its side;
Your tiny hand rests near my own.
The moon is keeping watch outside;
You need not drift to sleep alone.

#### D2

Dear Mother, when you feed me first,
I hear your humming by the door.
You brush the dust out of my fur.

At night I kiss your gentle hand;
It is the last thing I will kiss.
Beside you is my favourite place.

#### D3

I sweep the steps with careful hands,
Then count the plates we need for tea.
I put them where the table stands.

I help to fold the washing too,
And bring clean towels for us three.
There is a little I can do.
