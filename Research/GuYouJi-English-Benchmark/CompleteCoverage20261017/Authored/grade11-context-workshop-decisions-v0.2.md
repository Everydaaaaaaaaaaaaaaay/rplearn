# 高二语境词汇：工坊与决策

## 学生题面

根据各句首字母与汉语注释完成单词拼写。填写完整单词。

以下各句彼此独立。首字母已印出的空，请在输入框填写完整单词（包括该首字母）。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The new timetable was only a t____（暂时的） arrangement until the damaged room reopened.

2. The carpenter measured the w____（宽度） of the doorway before moving the cabinet.

3. Two people gave c____（相互矛盾的） accounts of the accident, so the cause was still uncertain.

4. The workshop has a fixed b____（预算） for materials and cannot spend more this month.

5. The guide asked each visitor for p____（许可） before taking a photograph of them.

6. The repair manual contains clear i____（说明） on how to replace the wheel.

7. There was only a s____（轻微的） difference in the two measurements, less than one millimetre.

8. The metal handle will e____（膨胀） when heated in this demonstration.

9. The damaged part was removed and r____（替换） with a new one.

10. Before publishing the chart, she checked that the numbers were a____（准确的）.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：temporary

temporary与until reopened时间边界吻合。

### 2：width

门洞宽度名词width。

### 3：conflicting / contradictory

两形容词均为相互矛盾，accounts语义相容。

### 4：budget

资金计划上限budget。

### 5：permission

ask for permission before doing。

### 6：instructions

操作说明常用复数instructions；instruction有不可数用法，不能仅凭预期复数直接拒绝，未列形式待复核。

### 7：slight / small

slight/small都可描述小于一毫米的轻微差异。

### 8：expand

will后原形expand，词义膨胀。

### 9：replaced

was removed and replaced平行被动。

### 10：accurate

numbers accurate，形容词准确。

逐空按完整句/语篇回填，未知形式待复核、不作语义相似度自动判分。人物与事件虚构，旧稿暂停不解除，独立教研、学生、商用和生产未验证。
