# 小六：梦想、工具与台阶

## 学生题面

用括号中所给单词的适当形式填空，完成全部五句。每空不限填一词；已核实的合法变体同等接受，其他表达可能显示待复核。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Handle the glass jars ____, so that you do not break them. (careful)

2. I have often ____ about flying over our town. (dream)

3. This tool can open tins as well as cut string. For these two jobs it is ____ than the tool that only cuts string. (useful)

4. Ms Park teaches our science class. She is our science ____. (teach)

5. Ben was ____ onto the bus when his ticket fell from his hand. (step)

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：carefully

carefully：副词carefully修饰handle。；careful：此处需副词修饰动作，不能直接用形容词。；未列出的形式尚未穷尽核实，显示待复核，不据此断言错误。

### 2：dreamt / dreamed

dreamt：have后用过去分词dreamt，词典列明。；dreamed：dreamed同为过去分词，不因另一拼写而判错。；dream：have后需要过去分词。；dreaming：have后不能直接接-ing构成此完成时。；未列出的形式尚未穷尽核实，显示待复核，不据此断言错误。

### 3：more useful

more useful：两项任务都可完成而另一只能完成一项，语境支持more useful。；useful：原级不直接接此than比较。；most useful：最高级不合than比较。；未列出的形式尚未穷尽核实，显示待复核，不据此断言错误。

### 4：teacher

teacher：表示教科学的人，用单数teacher。；teach：our science后需人物名词。；teachers：一个Ms Park对应单数，不是复数。；未列出的形式尚未穷尽核实，显示待复核，不据此断言错误。

### 5：stepping

stepping：was后进行时用stepping，双写p。；step：was后原形不成此主动进行时。；steping：漏双写p，不是规范词形。；未列出的形式尚未穷尽核实，显示待复核，不据此断言错误。

### 完整回填

Handle the glass jars carefully, so that you do not break them.
I have often dreamt about flying over our town.
This tool can open tins as well as cut string. For these two jobs it is more useful than the tool that only cuts string.
Ms Park teaches our science class. She is our science teacher.
Ben was stepping onto the bus when his ticket fell from his hand.
