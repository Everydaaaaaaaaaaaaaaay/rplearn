## 作者核对

### 错误1

one明确要求可数名词单数box。旧稿a box of seed中的seed可作集合/物质名词，不能把它稳妥视为语法错误，故改为one boxes这一清楚的数一致错误；不是将seed的合法用法判错。

### 错误2

修饰read，用副词carefully。

### 错误3

suggested后动词作宾语用sorting。

### 错误4

did not后用原形match。

### 错误5

gardener后who/that引出限制性定语从句；working为主动分词定语。均符合联系该园丁的语境，视为同一错误。

### 错误6

hour的h不发音，单数前用an。

### 错误7

would后主动原形arrive。

### 错误8

wait不能直接接them；可换成及物await，也可在them前加for，保持等待种子的意思。不同位置和操作同属一处错误。

### 错误9

When everything was ready和thanked确定过去叙事，用gave。

### 错误10

表示建议的advice不可数，some useful advice。

### 一种完整修订

Our school received one box of seeds from a local garden club last spring. Before opening the box, I read the instructions carefully. The club suggested sorting the packets by planting month, so we made four trays. One packet was empty, and its label did not match the list inside the box. I sent a message to the gardener who worked at the club, and she replied within an hour. She explained that the missing seeds would arrive the next day. We decided to await them before putting the packets on the shelf. When everything was ready, our teacher gave us some useful advice and thanked us for the work.
