# 高二五给词式：一座桥的新名字

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据短文内容填空；给词空使用所给词的正确形式，不额外限1词。其余无给词空各填1词。

The footbridge, (1) ____ (build) in 1974, had never had an official name. Most residents simply called it the back bridge, although the entrance they once used from the main road had long since disappeared. When a pupil asked why the bridge had no sign, the town council invited suggestions. One remembered a visiting engineer (2) ____ drawings had helped local builders finish the crossing. Older residents had referred to her simply (3) ____ the woman with the blue folder.

Several (4) ____ (proposal) were submitted, including names based on the river, the nearby gardens and the engineer's work. The committee tried to consider each one as (5) ____ (serious) as possible. It prepared a short booklet in (6) ____ the reasons for every suggestion were explained. The aim was to let residents compare the names before choosing, rather than to give an advantage to the person who had sent a suggestion first.

The committee decided (7) ____ (invite) people to contribute memories of using the bridge. Some recalled taking a younger brother to school; others remembered the year when repairs forced them to walk a longer route. The discussion did not turn every memory into a fact about the bridge's construction, but it showed how much everyday experience had gathered around an ordinary crossing.

No new sign was ordered (8) ____ the public meeting had taken place. Residents eventually chose the engineer's name. The footbridge became the first local structure (9) ____ (name) after someone who had never lived in the town. What mattered most to the committee was not the new word on the sign, (10) ____ the way a question from one pupil had led people of different ages to talk to one another.

## 作者核对

### 1：built / having been built

非限制性被动分词可用built或完成被动having been built，1974年建成早于后续命名。

### 2：whose

whose drawings说明工程师与图纸的所属。

### 3：as

refer to somebody as...表称呼。

### 4：proposals

several要求复数proposals。

### 5：seriously

as...as中正级副词修饰consider。

### 6：which

in which，介词后的关系代词。

### 7：to invite

decide to invite动词不定式。

### 8：until / till

否定主句到公众会议后才订牌，until/till均合。

### 9：named / to be named

the first structure后，named分词定语与to be named被动不定式均合；给词不额外限1词，三词形式也接受。不增添who/which从句。

### 10：but

not...but...对照标志上的名称与交流过程。

全文人物、场所和事件均虚构，来源只参照完整具体形式。已列合法变体同等接受，不设三答案硬上限；未列词形待复核，不直接判错。给词的多词形式限于已核的动词时态/语态、不定式等词形表达，不因含有词根便自动接收新增关系从句。去首尾空白、合并内部空白、忽略大小写，不按词根匹配。无给词空超过1词不符本组规则。作者核对不证明答案穷尽、年级难度等值或独立质量验收，后端固定六题未接完整组。
