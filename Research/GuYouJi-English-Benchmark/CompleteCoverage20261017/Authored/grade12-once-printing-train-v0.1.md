1. Why does the man want the electronic file?
   A. To change the report’s conclusions.
   B. To make the text easier to read.
   C. To print copies for a large meeting.

2. When will the woman now leave?
   A. At six o’clock.
   B. At seven o’clock.
   C. At eight o’clock.

3. How does the man feel about the booking?
   A. He regrets making it without that information.
   B. He is delighted that the pool will be quieter.
   C. He is indifferent to the pool being closed.

4. What is the woman planning to do?
   A. Apply for a job driving a bus.
   B. Prepare a new bus timetable.
   C. Interview an experienced local driver.

5. What does the man ask the woman to do?
   A. Book the smaller room.
   B. Cancel the larger room’s booking.
   C. Invite more people to the meeting.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: I’ve brought you a printed copy of the report. I thought that would be easier to read.

M: Thank you, but could you send the file as well? The print is rather small, and I can enlarge it on my screen.

W: Certainly. I’ll email it now.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: I thought you were leaving on the seven o’clock train. Why are you still here?

W: That service has been cancelled. They’ve put me on the eight o’clock one instead. There’s no extra charge.

M: At least you haven’t had to buy a more expensive ticket.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: The hotel has just told us the swimming pool will be closed during our stay.

M: If only we’d known that before we paid. The pool was the main reason I chose that hotel.

W: Shall I ask whether we can change the booking?

M: Yes, please.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Have you chosen a subject for your local radio project?

W: Yes. There’s a bus driver who has worked on the same route for thirty years. I’ve arranged to ask her how the neighbourhood has changed.

M: That should give you a story beyond the timetable.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: I can reserve the small meeting room for you, but the larger one is already booked.

M: There’ll only be six of us, so the small room will be fine. Please reserve it for Thursday afternoon.

W: All right. I’ll send you the confirmation shortly.
