## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Is that rattling sound coming from the window?

W: I thought so, but it stops whenever I hold the lampshade. One of its clips must be loose.

M: I will tighten it after I switch the lamp off.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: I found a letter from my grandfather in this book. There is no date on it.

M: Does the envelope have a stamp?

W: No envelope, but he mentions that my mother is about to start school. She started in September nineteen eighty-two.

M: Then the letter must be from around that time.

W: Yes. I will keep it with the family photographs, rather than leave it in the book.

M: You could scan it too, so everyone in the family can read it.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Could you cover my shift at the bike repair stand on Saturday?

W: In the morning, yes, but I have a class at one.

M: That is enough. I need to collect some donated bikes at ten, and I will be back by noon.

W: Do I have to repair anything difficult?

M: No. Just write down what people need. We will do the actual repairs when I return.

W: All right. I will bring a notebook.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: The choir sounded different today. Were there fewer singers?

M: The same number came, but we stood in a circle instead of rows.

W: Why did you change that?

M: In rows, the back singers could hardly hear the front. In a circle, everyone could hear the whole group.

W: Will you stand like that for the concert?

M: Probably not; the audience needs to see us. But it is useful for practice.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: Hello, everyone. I am the coordinator of our neighbourhood book box. It has been beside the bakery for six months. At first we only accepted novels, but many children asked for picture books, so we have added a lower shelf for them. You may borrow one book at a time, without signing a form. Please return it to the same box when you have finished. Books are not for sale. A problem recently was that people left large piles of magazines on the ground. They became wet before volunteers could collect them. From now on, please contact us before donating more than five books, and do not leave magazines at the box. We will open a second box near the swimming pool next month. Anyone who can help check that box on Monday evenings should speak to me after this meeting. We already have enough helpers for Saturdays.
