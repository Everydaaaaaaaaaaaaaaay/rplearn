# 初三语法：候船亭里的故事

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

A storm stopped the evening ferry, and the waiting room filled with (1) ____ (passenger). Among them was a woman carrying a large collection of (2) ____ (draw). She placed the folder on a dry bench and spoke (3) ____ (soft) to a child who was frightened by the wind. The child stopped crying when she opened the folder.

I had never (4) ____ (see) anything like those pictures. They showed the same island in different seasons, but there were no buildings in any of them. The woman said she had made the pictures (5) ____ (she). As a child, she had lived on the island; now she was returning to show them to an old friend. We listened (6) ____ her explanation while the rain struck the roof.

The child asked whether the island really had no houses. The woman smiled and pointed at an empty space beside a tree. She had left the houses out, not because they were unimportant, (7) ____ because she wanted to draw the shapes of the land. After (8) ____ (hear) this, the child tried to draw the waiting room without its walls. His first picture was much (9) ____ (simple) than hers, but we recognised the benches and the clock.

We were still looking at his picture when the ferry staff announced that the boat would not leave until morning. Instead (10) ____ packing away at once, the woman turned to a fresh page. She asked the child what they should draw next, and he chose the rain on the windows.

## 作者核对

### 1：passengers

filled with泛指多名乘客用复数。

### 2：drawings

a collection of后为图画名词复数drawings。

### 3：softly

修饰spoke需softly。

### 4：seen

had never seen过去分词。

### 5：herself

made the pictures herself强调亲自创作，且保留同一女性指代。

### 6：to

listen to an explanation。

### 7：but

not because...but because对照真正理由。

### 8：hearing / having heard

after hearing及after having heard均先听解释再尝试画，完成式不因多词排除。

### 9：simpler / more simple

simpler与more simple两比较形式均可，much与than明确比较。

### 10：of

instead of doing固定结构。
