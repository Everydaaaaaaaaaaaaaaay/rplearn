## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: I cannot hear the doorbell from the back room. Should we buy a louder one?

W: Before spending any money, let us move the receiver to the hallway. The cupboard may be blocking the sound.

M: Good idea. I will try that this evening.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: I am making posters for the film club, but the room number has changed again.

M: It is Room Fourteen now. The big screen in Room Nine is being repaired.

W: I will correct that. Do we still start at half past four?

M: Yes. The film lasts an hour, and we will discuss it for twenty minutes afterwards.

W: Then I will put five fifty as the finishing time. Can you check the poster before I print it?

M: Certainly. Send me a copy tonight.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: I saw you leaving the market with an empty basket. Were the vegetables sold out?

W: No. I had left my wallet at home. I did not realise until I tried to pay.

M: Couldn't you use your phone?

W: The battery was flat. The stallholder offered to keep my vegetables until noon, which was kind.

M: I am driving past your house. I can take you there and back.

W: Thank you. I have plenty of time; it is only ten thirty now.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: You have three photographs of the old bridge. Which will you put in the display?

M: I like the one at sunset, but it hides the repair work. The photograph from the riverbank shows the new stone clearly.

W: Then use that one. Visitors need to understand what changed.

M: I agree. I will keep the sunset picture for the cover of our club magazine.

W: Remember to ask the photographer before printing either picture.

M: She has already agreed, as long as we include her name.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: Welcome to the school poster festival. You will find the posters in the covered playground, rather than the hall. The hall is being used for a music rehearsal. There are twenty-four entries this year, but only twelve display boards. Two posters therefore share each board. The small number in the corner belongs to the poster, not to the board, so check it carefully when voting. Every visitor may vote for one poster. Please write its number on the paper slip and put the slip in the red box by the entrance. Voting closes at a quarter past three. Our art teacher will announce the winner at half past three. After that, please leave all the posters in place until four, so the school photographer can take a picture of the complete display. You may collect your own work from four to four thirty.
