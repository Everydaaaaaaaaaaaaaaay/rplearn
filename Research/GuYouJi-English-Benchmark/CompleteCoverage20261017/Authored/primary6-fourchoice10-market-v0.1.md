# 小六：集市与购物袋

## 学生题面

完成全部十题，每题从A、B、C、D选一个最佳答案。第5题有两个空，需选择同时适合两空的一组词，仍只选一个字母。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. You accidentally spill water on another person's bag. Say, "____"
   A. You are welcome.
   B. I am sorry.
   C. Good luck.
   D. Well done.

2. To see how much the fruit costs per kilogram, read the ____.
   A. bus map
   B. story title
   C. weather chart
   D. price label

3. This shopping bag is ____ than the one I carried yesterday.
   A. heavier
   B. heavy
   C. heaviest
   D. more heavier

4. The stall opens at nine. We arrived at 8:40, twenty minutes ____.
   A. nearly
   B. recently
   C. early
   D. loudly

5. I was choosing apples when the rain started. Ask about the activity: ____ were you doing ____ the rain started?
   A. Where, what
   B. When, what
   C. What, who
   D. What, when

6. The fruit shop opens ____ eight every Sunday.
   A. in
   B. at
   C. on
   D. behind

7. The three sellers ____ the boxes when I arrived yesterday.
   A. were moving
   B. was moving
   C. is moving
   D. am moving

8. The green melon is much ____ than the small pear.
   A. big
   B. biggest
   C. bigger
   D. more bigger

9. This bag is light ____ for me to carry with one hand.
   A. much
   B. enough
   C. many
   D. most

10. This is ____ basket on the shelf.
   A. cheapest
   B. cheaper
   C. cheap
   D. the cheapest

## 作者核对

### 1：B

A：回应感谢，不是道歉。；B：为刚发生的过失道歉。；C：祝好运，不是道歉。；D：称赞成就，不是道歉。

### 2：D

A：公交图不说明水果单价。；B：故事标题不说明水果单价。；C：天气图不说明水果单价。；D：价签说明每千克的价格。

### 3：A

A：than前用比较级heavier。；B：heavy为原级。；C：heaviest为最高级。；D：more与heavier重复比较。

### 4：C

A：nearly不作此minutes后的时间差补语。；B：recently表示最近，不表达提前二十分钟。；C：比开摊提前二十分钟。；D：loudly表示大声的方式。

### 5：D

A：Where不询问活动，what不能连接此从句。；B：When不问活动，第二空what不适合此从句。；C：who不能在the rain主语前作时间连词。；D：What询问选择苹果这项活动，when连接下雨时刻。

### 6：B

A：in不用于此钟点。；B：钟点前用at。；C：on不用于此钟点。；D：behind表示空间位置，不是此时间关系。

### 7：A

A：复数sellers和过去语境与were moving一致。；B：was不与复数主语一致。；C：is不与复数主语及过去语境一致。；D：am只与I一致。

### 8：C

A：big是原级。；B：biggest为最高级。；C：much与比较级bigger及than相配。；D：more与bigger重复。

### 9：B

A：much不在light后构成此结构。；B：light enough to carry表示轻到足以提起。；C：many不用于此结构。；D：most不用于此结构。

### 10：D

A：cheapest前缺限定词。；B：cheaper前缺限定词。；C：cheap前缺限定词。；D：the cheapest作定语修饰单数basket。
