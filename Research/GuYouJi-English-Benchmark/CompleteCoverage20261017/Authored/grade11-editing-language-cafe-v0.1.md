## 作者核对

### 错误1

Last autumn的过去活动，主句用suggested；that从句let是建议内容，不混成同一谓语。

### 错误2

two baskets复数，of picture cards说明篮中内容。

### 错误3

responsible for后用动名词explaining。

### 错误4

asked me to repeat，to放在repeat前。

### 错误5

修饰answered动作，very patiently。

### 错误6

Instead of后动名词hurrying。

### 错误7

meaning属于question，whose作从句中meaning的所属限定词。

### 错误8

wait for somebody to do，for放在the visitors前；不能把await机械移接同一宾补结构。

### 错误9

several要求复数，people为常用形式，persons作为较正式的人数复数也合语境，均接受。

### 错误10

promised to后的主动不定式用原形bring。

### 一种完整修订

Last autumn, I volunteered at a Saturday language cafe where neighbours practised everyday conversations. At the beginning, our coordinator suggested that we let visitors choose their own topics. She placed two baskets of picture cards on a table near the entrance. I was responsible for explaining the activity, but I spoke too fast during the first round. A visitor asked me to repeat the instructions, and I answered her very patiently. Instead of hurrying through the next round, I demonstrated one exchange with another volunteer. The exchange used a simple question whose meaning was clear from the picture. Afterwards, I waited for the visitors to choose a card and then invited them to work in pairs. By lunchtime, several people had asked when the cafe would meet again, which made our coordinator happy. I returned home with a notebook full of suggestions and promised to bring clearer examples the following week.
