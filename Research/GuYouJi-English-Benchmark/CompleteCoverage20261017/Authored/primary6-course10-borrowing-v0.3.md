自有课文配套（可参照；不是受控延时记忆测验）

A. The club room

Our club room opens after lunch on Tuesdays. Members may borrow a ball for one day. The borrowing desk is at the front of the room. The balls stand in a wide basket. Before taking a ball, members write their names in the blue book. They return the ball to the same place the next day.

B. A visit to the theatre

Last Saturday, my sister and I went to a puppet show. I kept my ticket in a coat pocket. The theatre was across the river. We took the second bus after lunch. From the window, we could see the water and a line of small boats. The bus stopped near the theatre door.

C. The meeting place

A guide met us at the theatre. Walk for one minute from the bus stop. Then you will see a yellow door. Remember to bring your notebook. The guide wanted us to draw one puppet after the show. My sister brought a pencil for each of us.

D. Back at home

At home, I drew the fox puppet at my desk. The upstairs room was quiet that evening. I could hear the clock, but nobody was talking. My school bag was empty after I took out all the books. I put the drawing in it so I could show my friends the next day.

## 作者核对

### 1：borrow

borrow：配套A说明成员借球一天，原词borrow与may后原形对应。；依据：配套1原句 Members may borrow a ball for one day. 未列形式待复核，不宣称合法表达穷尽。
