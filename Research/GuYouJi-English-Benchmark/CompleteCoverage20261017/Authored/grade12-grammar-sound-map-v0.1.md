# 高三：没有画面的声音地图

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

A local museum commissioned a sound map of a market street that was about to be rebuilt. The team wanted to discover (1) ____ the street had changed over time, using recordings rather than a sequence of photographs. Their first visit produced a (2) ____ (use) reminder that sound could be misleading: the loudest noise came from building work on a neighbouring road, not from the market itself.

The team returned with a carefully (3) ____ (organise) schedule. They recorded several short (4) ____ (conversation) with traders before the stalls opened. Each participant signed a form on (5) ____ the intended use of the recording was explained. The interviewers avoided (6) ____ (interrupt) when somebody paused to remember a detail. The purpose was not to create a perfectly smooth performance, (7) ____ to preserve the way people actually spoke about their working lives.

By the time the exhibition opened, the museum (8) ____ (collect) recordings made in four different months. Visitors could listen to the same corner at dawn, at midday and after closing time. Since the first weekend, several former traders (9) ____ (write) to the museum with memories of sounds that no microphone had captured. The curator is pleased (10) ____ (hear) from them. Their letters cannot replace the recordings, but they can explain a call, a bell or a rhythm that a listener might otherwise dismiss as background noise.

## 作者核对

### 1：how

changed over time与下文比较月份指变化方式，how最直接；未列其他嵌入问词仍待复核。

### 2：useful

a...reminder需有用的形容词useful，后文解释获得的提醒。

### 3：organised / organized

副词carefully修饰过去分词作schedule定语，两种拼写均收。

### 4：conversations

several短对话可数复数。

### 5：which

on which中which为介词宾语。

### 6：interrupting / having interrupted

avoid动名词及完成动名词列为可接受形式，未列其他形式不直接判错。

### 7：but

not...but...两目标对照。

### 8：had collected

by the time opened前已收集，过去完成。

### 9：have written / have been writing

since后复数traders允许完成及完成进行。

### 10：to hear / to have heard

pleased后不定式普通与完成均能对应已收到来信的欣喜。
