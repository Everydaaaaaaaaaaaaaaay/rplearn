   B. She is boring.

   C. Inside the pencil case.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The green scarf is lovely. Are you buying it for your sister?

M: No, she wants a hat. This scarf is for my grandmother. Her birthday is on Sunday.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: The train leaves at ten fifteen. Shall we meet here at ten?

W: That is too late. Let us meet at nine forty-five so we have time to buy the tickets.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Is your new science teacher strict?

M: Not really. She often tells funny stories to explain things. Even difficult lessons make us laugh.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Have you returned my ruler? I need it for maths.

W: I put it under your notebook, beside the pencil case. It is not in the schoolbag.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Will the football match still be outside tomorrow?

M: No. The field is too wet after the rain. We will play basketball in the hall instead.
