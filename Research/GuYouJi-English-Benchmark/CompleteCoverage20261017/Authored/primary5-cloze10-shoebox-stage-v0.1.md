### 全组回填

Nina and Leo take the shoes out of a shoebox. Now it is empty. They turn it into a puppet theatre. The stage is low, so Leo puts it on a table with four legs.

Nina's paper bird has a broken wing. Leo gives her some tape. "Let's repair it." She sticks the wing back on and puts trees behind the puppets. It is their forest.

The show begins. Leo speaks loudly, but Nina speaks quietly. "We can't hear the bird," someone says. Nina uses a louder voice.

Later, Leo opens the rubbish bin. "Don't throw it away!" Nina says. "We can use it again." They keep their theatre for another story. They will write it tomorrow, the day after today.
