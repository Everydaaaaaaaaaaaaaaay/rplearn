### 全组回填

It is six in the morning. My little brother is still sleeping in bed, but I am awake. I go downstairs with Dad. He makes bread in our bakery (面包房). I put the fresh loaves into paper bags. They come straight from the oven (烤箱), so they are still warm. A small bell rings above the door when our first customer comes in. We must finish putting the bags on the shelf before we can sell them. The shop is open now, and the street begins to get busy.
