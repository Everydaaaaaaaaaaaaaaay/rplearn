A. At the bookshop

Nina and her father visited a new bookshop on Friday. The maps were behind the counter. A shop assistant showed them a small town map. Follow the red line to the art shop. This was the first instruction printed on it. Nina bought a pencil at the bookshop before they went home.

B. The Saturday plan

The next morning, Nina invited her cousin to join her. There were twelve pictures on the town map. They decided to visit three of the places. Nina said, "We must arrive before the doors close at four." Nina wrote that reminder on a small card. The card fit inside her coat pocket.

C. A change of plan

Their first stop was the old museum beside the lake. They wanted to see a model ship. Nina later wrote in her diary, "We stayed inside because the rain was heavy." From a window, they watched people opening umbrellas. When the rain stopped, they continued their walk to the art shop.

D. Checking the route

The walk from the lake took a quarter of an hour. The cousins stopped once to check the map. The art shop was on the east side of the square. They found it beside a bakery. Nina chose a postcard with a picture of the model ship on it.
