# 高一：面包店、画笔与列车

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

### A

My aunt runs a bakery (1) ____ opens at seven every morning. Yesterday she put three small (2) ____ (cake) in the window. They were (3) ____ (cheap) than the large cakes on the shelf. I wrote their prices as (4) ____ (clear) as I could. Before leaving, I checked that each price was beside the correct cake.

### B

Our art teacher brought an old painting to class. The colours were (5) ____ (bright) than I had expected. She told us (6) ____ look at the shadows before mixing our paints. I had always been afraid (7) ____ making mistakes, but she explained that we could let the paper dry and try again on another sheet.

### C

At the station we saw a passenger (8) ____ (push) a red bicycle towards the lift. He was the man (9) ____ ticket my brother had found on a bench. We returned it to him before the train arrived. He thanked us for (10) ____ (return) it. We were glad that a small discovery had solved his problem.

## 作者核对

### 1：which / that

限定关系从句指bakery并缺主语，which/that可互换。

### 2：cakes

three要求可数复数cakes。

### 3：cheaper

than明确要求比较级。

### 4：clearly

wrote的方式副词且as...as正级结构。

### 5：brighter

than引出形容词比较级。

### 6：to

told us to look。

### 7：of

afraid of doing固定搭配。

### 8：pushing / push

saw+宾语后可接不带to的动词原形push，也可接pushing：前者把观察到的动作或状态作为整体，后者突出当时正在进行的情景；本句未限定只能取后一视角，两者均接受。Cambridge English Grammar Today的Hear, see, etc. + object + infinitive or -ing明确列see、notice两种结构。新增关系代词的整从句及pushed是否属于本题所给词变形范围仍未核实，继续待复核；不由本题推及met等其他动词。

### 9：whose

whose ticket中whose作所属限定词，指man的票。

### 10：returning / having returned

for后动名词，两种体形式均与已归还动作相容。
