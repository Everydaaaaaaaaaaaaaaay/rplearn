# 初二语法：停电后的共同晚餐

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

On the (1) ____ (three) evening of our holiday, the lights in the holiday house went out. Two large dishes (2) ____ (be) still in the oven when we noticed the problem. My uncle checked the switch, while everyone else sat (3) ____ (quiet) around the kitchen table. Nobody wanted to spoil the meal we had spent all afternoon preparing.

There were several (4) ____ (different) between our ideas for saving dinner. My cousin wanted to order food from the village, but my aunt thought the shop would already be closed. In (5) ____, we had plenty of bread, cheese and fruit. The kitchen was so dark (6) ____ we could not see what was inside the cupboard, so we brought a camping lamp in from the car.

Both the younger children (7) ____ the adults found jobs to do. The children washed the fruit, and my uncle moved the cold dishes onto a tray. After (8) ____ (carry) everything outside, we sat around the garden table. My cousins had suggested eating there earlier in the day, so the idea was (9) ____ (they), not ours.

When the people in the next house saw the lamp, they came to ask if we needed help. We invited them (10) ____ (join) us. The meal was different from the one we had planned, but nobody went hungry. We stayed outside talking even after the lights came back on.

7. 第7空（无提示，填1词）

## 作者核对

### 1：third

the third evening序数词。

### 2：were

两盘食物作复数主语，过去状态were。

### 3：quietly

坐着这一动作的方式用副词quietly。

### 4：differences

several后可数派生名词复数differences。

### 5：fact

in fact固定短语引出其实已有足够食物。

### 6：that

so dark that结果结构。

### 7：and

both...and连接孩子与成人。

### 8：carrying / having carried

after carrying/after having carried均保留搬完再坐下的时序。

### 9：theirs

the idea was theirs用名词性物主代词，无后接名词。

### 10：to join

invite someone to do，to join为必要多词。
