## 作者核对



### 完整原创脚本

M: Are we going to watch a play at the theatre tomorrow, Anna?

W: Not a full play. We are going to watch the actors practise, and then see the room where they keep their costumes.

M: Where should our class meet?

W: Outside the school office at ten. The visit starts at half past ten.

M: Can I take photos of the costumes?

W: The theatre has asked us not to take photos in that room. You can draw a picture in your notebook instead.

M: Will we see the actors after they practise?

W: Yes. They will answer our questions for fifteen minutes.

M: I want to ask how they remember all their words.

W: Write it down now, so you do not forget to ask.
