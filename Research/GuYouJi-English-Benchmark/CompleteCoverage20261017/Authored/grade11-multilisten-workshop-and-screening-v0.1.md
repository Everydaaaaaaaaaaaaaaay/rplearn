## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: I thought the print workshop was for people who already knew how to use the machine.

M: That was the plan last month, but the tutor has changed it. Beginners can now join on Saturday morning. The afternoon session is still for experienced users.

W: Then I should choose the morning. Do I need to bring my own paper?

M: No. Paper and ink are included in the fee. Bring an old shirt, though. The ink can be difficult to wash out.

W: I can do that. Will I make a poster to take home?

M: You will try several small designs first. If there is time, you can use one on a poster. The tutor wants people to learn the process, rather than leave with one perfect piece.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: We have only twelve seats for the film club tonight. Shall we move the screening to the dining hall?

W: It has more chairs, but the sound from the kitchen would spoil the quiet scenes. Let us keep the small room and show the film again on Thursday.

M: The people on the waiting list might not like waiting.

W: We should tell them before they travel here. They can choose Thursday, or have their money returned. We must not promise everyone a seat tonight.

M: I will send a message now. What about the discussion after the film?

W: Keep it short tonight. Ask viewers to write one question on a card. We can begin Thursday's discussion with those questions, so the second group has something to respond to.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: Welcome to the school costume store. This room holds clothes from earlier productions, and our aim is to help new groups reuse them. Please work in pairs today. One person can check the label while the other records the size. Do not guess a size from the number alone: the same label may have been used by different makers. Use the measuring tape on the desk if necessary.

We used to put damaged clothes in a box by the door. That made them easy to forget, so there is now a red rail for anything needing repair. Hang such items there, with a note describing the problem. Do not try to repair them during this session; that is a separate job for next week.

Before you leave, save your list on the shared computer. You do not need to print it. The next group will use the digital list to check what is available before planning their play.
