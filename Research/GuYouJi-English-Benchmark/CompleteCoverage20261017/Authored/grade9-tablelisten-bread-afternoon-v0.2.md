### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Good morning. Here is news about the School Bread Afternoon next Wednesday. It starts at two in the afternoon and finishes at five. Small groups will make bread rolls together. The aim is to practise teamwork in the kitchen: everyone will have a different job, and no one should try to do everything alone. Before we begin, please wash your hands and put on an apron. When you move the trays, carry them carefully with both hands. Only the adult helpers will put trays into the hot ovens or take them out. The helpers will provide recipe cards, so there is no need to bring a cookery book. At the end, you can take your bread home in a paper bag. As well as enjoying the afternoon, we hope you will learn useful cooking skills that you can practise with an adult at home. Please sign up at the school office by Monday.
