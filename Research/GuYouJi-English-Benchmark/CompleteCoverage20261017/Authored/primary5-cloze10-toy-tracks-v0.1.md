### 全组回填

Ms Lin sees blue marks on the floor. They lead to an open window. "Did a bird fly in?" she asks.

Hugo finds a toy dinosaur under the art table. Unlike the toy cars, it has feet. They are blue with paint. Mina says, "I pressed its feet on paper as a stamp yesterday."

Mina brings water and a cloth. She begins cleaning the floor. Hugo moves the chairs. They work together, side by side, and soon the floor is clean.

Ms Lin puts the toy on a tray. "Clean your tools when you finish," she says. Mina writes a note for the art table: "Wash the toys. Close the paint." It will remind everyone what to do next time.
