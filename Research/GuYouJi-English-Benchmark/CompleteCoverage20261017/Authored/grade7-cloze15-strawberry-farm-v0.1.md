### 全组回填

Rain on our class trip to a farm made us disappointed. We might have to stay on the bus! Luckily, it stopped before we arrived.

A farmer gave each of us a basket for strawberries to take home. He showed us rows of plants. He asked us to walk between them, not step on them.

My friend Kate found a big strawberry and called me over. It was under leaves, so I had to look carefully. After picking six, I wanted more, but it was time for our next activity.

We washed our hands and made fruit salad. We cut strawberries, apples and bananas into pieces and mixed them without water. Kate had no spoon, so I lent her my spare one. She returned it afterwards.

After lunch, we watched the farmer feed the hens, putting food in a dish. Then we carried empty baskets to the gate for later visitors.

Before we left, I bought a little jar of strawberry jam for my grandma. I wanted her to try it on toast. On the way home, we talked about our favourite parts of the visit. Kate liked the hens, but I liked making food with my friends.

At home, I gave Grandma her jam. She asked if we had got wet. “Not after we got there!” I said. It was a busy day, and I had a lot to tell her.
