# 小六课文参照回填：小镇游行日

A. Getting ready

Our town held a small parade on Sunday. A blue flag hung above the meeting point. We packed the costumes on Saturday evening. Finally, we put the hats in a large bag. On Sunday, we ate breakfast at seven. The bread was still warm, and there was a bowl of fruit on the table.

B. Walking to the square

My brother and I walked together to the square. We left the house after the clock struck eight. The streets were still wet from the rain. We walked slowly on the wet stones. At the square, a band was getting ready to play. We waited beside a flower stall.

C. A note for the helpers

One helper could not find the costume bag. I sent a message to the group leader. She told us where to look. The bag was between two chairs near the stage. We opened it and gave everyone a hat. Then we carried the empty bag back to the stall.

D. The last turn

At the end of the parade, the band played a short song. The children quickly formed a line beside the fountain. Everyone could see the small stage. We had enough space for all twelve dancers. When the song ended, the dancers bowed and the people clapped.
