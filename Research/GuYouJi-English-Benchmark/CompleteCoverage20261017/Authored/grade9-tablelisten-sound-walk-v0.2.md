## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Here is a message about our Old Street Sound Walk this Saturday. The walk will begin at one in the afternoon and end at four. Meet outside the school gate. We will listen to familiar streets in a new way and learn about local history. A former shopkeeper will tell us what the market sounded like when she was a child. Some stops are next to people's gardens, so please speak quietly there. Do not record anyone's conversation without asking. The guides will provide maps showing all the stops. You do not need to print your own. Bring a pencil to add notes to the map. In the last part of the walk, we will practise interviewing a volunteer about one sound they remember. We hope this will build your confidence in interviewing people. You can use a school recorder; a phone is not required.
