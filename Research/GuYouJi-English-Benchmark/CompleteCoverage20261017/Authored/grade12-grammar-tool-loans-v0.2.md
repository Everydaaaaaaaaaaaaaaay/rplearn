# 高三：借用记录中的空白

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

Borrowing a tool at our neighbourhood lending room takes about half (1) ____ hour. Every borrower (2) ____ (receive) a numbered card. The volunteers write down (3) ____ (they) names and the expected return dates before handing over any equipment. The shelves hold tools of different (4) ____ (size), so a clear record matters more than a quick glance at an empty space.

Each tool belongs on a numbered hook, (5) ____ makes a missing item easy to identify. The tools must (6) ____ (return) before the date on the card. A borrower can either request a later return date (7) ____ bring the tool back on time. A change of plan can usually be arranged if the borrower calls first. With these records, the volunteers can trace a missing tool more (8) ____ (easy) than they could with their old notebook.

A drill (9) ____ (leave) outside the locked room last Friday. Nobody had signed it back in, and the volunteers could not tell whether its owner had brought it back or another person had found it. Their final (10) ____ (decide) was to contact the borrower before changing the record. The gap in the notebook was small; the uncertainty it created was not.

## 作者核对

### 1：an

half an hour为固定时间量表达，hour以元音音素起首，用an。

### 2：receives

叙述现行规则，every borrower为单数，第三人称单数receives。

### 3：their

names前用所属限定词their，回指借用者，不用主格或独立物主代词。

### 4：sizes

of different sizes表示不同尺寸，different后此处用可数复数。

### 5：which

逗号后非限制性关系从句指前句整件事，which作主语；that不能如此引导。

### 6：be returned

must后须原形，被归还为被动，be returned保留必要助动词。

### 7：or

either X or Y把申请延期与按时归还列为两种选择，or连接两个原形动词短语。

### 8：easily

more已在空前；用副词easily修饰trace，不能再填more easily形成双more。

### 9：was left

last Friday的单一已发生事件，drill为被留下物，用was left。其他时态视角未核不自动判错。

### 10：decision

their final...was中需单数名词作主语，decision指该次决定。
