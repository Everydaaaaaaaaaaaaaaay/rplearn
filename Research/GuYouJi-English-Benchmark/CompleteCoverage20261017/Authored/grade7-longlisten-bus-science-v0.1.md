## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Which bus goes to the swimming pool, Miss? Number Six?

W: Number Six goes to the park. You need Number Nine.

M: Thank you. Should I wait outside the bank?

W: No. The stop is outside the post office.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Mina, does the science club meet on Tuesday?

W: We usually meet then, but this week it is on Thursday.

M: Are we using the science room?

W: No. We will meet in the library. We are going to make small paper bridges.

M: Do I need to buy any paper?

W: The club has enough paper. Just bring some glue.

M: All right. I will put it in my bag now.
