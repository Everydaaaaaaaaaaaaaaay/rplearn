## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Good afternoon, everyone. I am Eva, your guide for the museum's evening visit. Our group will meet at the side entrance at six thirty, not at the main door. The front hall is being painted. Please bring a small torch. We will use it in the old kitchen, where the electric lights will be turned off for part of the visit. You do not need to bring food: the museum cafe will give each visitor a sandwich. Last month our evening group visited the boat room. Tonight we will spend most of our time in the kitchen, learning how families cooked a hundred years ago. You may take photographs, but please do not use a flash. At the end, we will leave through the garden gate. The visit finishes at eight. If your parents are collecting you, tell them to wait by that gate, rather than outside the ticket office.
