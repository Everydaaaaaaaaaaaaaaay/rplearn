1. When is the reading club?
   A. On Tuesday.
   B. On Thursday.
   C. On Friday.

2. What pet does Jane have?
   A. A rabbit.
   B. A dog.
   C. A cat.

3. Where is Sam’s schoolbag?
   A. On the desk.
   B. By the door.
   C. Under the chair.

4. What can Lucy’s sister do?
   A. Play the piano.
   B. Play the guitar.
   C. Play tennis.

5. How many of the pens belong to Tom?
   A. One.
   B. Two.
   C. Three.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Is the reading club on Tuesday, Ben?

M: No, it is on Thursday. Tuesday is for the chess club.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Do you have a dog, Jane?

W: No. My pet has long ears and likes carrots. It is a white rabbit.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Where is your blue schoolbag, Sam?

M: Under the chair. The green bag on the desk is my brother’s.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Can your sister play the piano, Lucy?

W: No, but she can play the guitar very well. I play the piano.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Are these three pens yours, Tom?

M: Only the black one. The two red pens are Lily’s.
