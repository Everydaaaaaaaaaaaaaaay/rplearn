# 初一句中词形：厨房与规则

## 学生题面

在每句空白处填入一个适当的单词，或括号内单词的正确形式。无给词空为一个单词；给词空按正确形式，不另加一词上限。

以下十五句彼此独立，保留给词与无给词位置。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. There are twelve months ____ a year.

2. The brown apron is mine. This is ____ (I) apron.

3. Look! There ____ (be) two plates beside the bowl.

4. The lid is lying ____ top of the cooker.

5. The farmer keeps three ____ (goose) beside the pond.

6. The recipe belongs to Anna alone. It is ____ (Anna) recipe.

7. Put the two empty ____ (glass) on this tray.

8. Can you ____ (wash) this bowl before lunch?

9. —____ coat is hanging here? —It belongs to the cook.

10. This book is about ____ (China) tea.

11. The cook moved the hot pot ____ (slow).

12. This is ____ old apron that I found in the cupboard.

13. The cook showed ____ (we) how to hold the pan.

14. Would you like ____ (help) with the school lunch tomorrow?

15. I washed my hands ____ they were covered in flour.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：in

twelve months in a year，单词in。

### 2：my

名词前物主限定词my。

### 3：are

当下复数存在句are。

### 4：on

on top of完整介词结构。

### 5：geese

three后goose的不规则复数geese；不以未核mouses变体作为参考。

### 6：Anna's

单人所有者Anna’s。

### 7：glasses

two后表示玻璃杯的复数glasses。

### 8：wash

can后原形wash。

### 9：Whose

问所有者，Whose。

### 10：Chinese

Chinese修饰tea。

### 11：slowly

修饰moved的方式副词slowly。

### 12：an / the

an引入一件旧围裙；the可限定刚找到的那件，均合定语从句语境。

### 13：us

show后宾格us。

### 14：to help / to be helping

like后普通/进行不定式均可指明天帮忙。

### 15：because / as / since

三种一词原因连词均合满手面粉导致洗手。
