### 答案与逐项依据

1. A。A：多数时间装饰小物，帘子未准备，little thought说明关注少；B：他没响应需要帮忙，非已仔细考虑整体幕布；C：投入大部分精力人偶，与equal均等考虑不合

2. B。A：certain to find表示肯定将发现，不符合此处已经看到后才意识的反应；B：到观众到场才发现没挂帘，与之前未关注相应；C：ready to find不表达意外发现没挂帘的情绪

3. B。A：实际一人无法吊杆系绳，不是玩笑；B：Badger早先叫帮搬杆，是请求援助；C：不是奖品或获奖公告

4. A。A：两人同时分工，他扶一端时同伴系另一端；B：两人协作非相互让步矛盾，while直接说明同时动作；C：除非对方系另一端才不扶不符合一起操作

5. C。A：解释自己与责怪对方不是相似处理；B：Otherwise表示否则的另一后果，与已选择诚实说明不合；C：没甩锅而选择解释自己失误，是替代可能的责怪

6. C。A：tell the difference是辨别异同，不是此处承认实情；B：tell the time指报时，未问钟点；C：诚实说明自己延误，不再嫁祸，tell truth合适

7. A。A：与是否需两人对比，是否能独立完成；B：与两人需求相对的不是再做一次；C：与两人需求相对的不是户外做

8. A。A：on time表示准时，呼应第一次迟开；B：帘子不是上架销售，不能总结演出准时；C：on purpose表示故意，不表达第二次按时开始

9. C。A：其他工作本身不嘲笑人偶装饰；B：工作不能表示人与人意见不一致；C：人偶顺利表演依赖幕布等配套，不能只顾自己的装饰

### 全组回填

In a storybook village, Rabbit and Badger planned a puppet show. Rabbit could make beautiful puppets, while Badger knew how to hang a curtain. Rabbit wanted everyone to notice the puppets, so he spent most of the morning adding tiny buttons and hats. He gave little thought to the curtain.

Badger asked for help carrying the curtain pole. "In a minute," Rabbit said. The minute became half an hour. Badger finally carried it alone, but could not hold both ends and tie the rope at the same time. He left the pole on the floor.

When the audience arrived, Rabbit was surprised to find no curtain hanging. He began to blame Badger. Then he saw the heavy pole and understood what the earlier request for help had meant.

Rabbit put down his puppets. Together, they lifted the pole. Rabbit held one end while Badger tied the other. The curtain was soon ready, but the show had started late.

Afterwards, a small squirrel praised the puppets and asked why the curtain had been missing. Rabbit could have blamed his partner. Instead, he explained his own part in the delay.

Badger was pleased that Rabbit had told the truth. They decided to prepare the next show with a list of jobs. Next to every task, they wrote whether one animal could finish it alone or whether two were needed.

For the next performance, Rabbit finished fewer tiny decorations. The puppets still looked lovely, and the curtain went up on time. From behind it, Rabbit gave Badger a quiet smile.

He had not become less interested in his own work. He had simply learned to notice what his own work depended on. When they packed up, Rabbit did not walk away with just his puppets. He stayed to help Badger take down the curtain.
