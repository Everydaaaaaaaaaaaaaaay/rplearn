# 小六：修补与画架

## 学生题面

完成全部十题：从每题括号内的两个词或词组中选取合适的一项，原样填入空白，不填字母或给词变形。两项均合法时，填写其中任意一项即可；不要将两项一起填写。候选只供本题使用。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Bert is ____ blue paint for the wooden sign. (mix / mixing)

2. Maya hopes to ____ a small frame before lunch. (make / makes)

3. Yesterday I ____ how to clean the brushes. (learned / learnt)

4. My sister did not ____ a ruler to the workshop. (bring / brought)

5. The empty shelf is ____ to hold the long frame. (wide enough / enough wide)

6. We left the wet brushes ____ a jar of water. (in / at)

7. This is Nia's drawing. Please return it to ____. (her / she)

8. The two helpers ____ the table when I arrived. (were cleaning / cleans)

9. Thank you for ____ the broken hinge. (mending / mend)

10. Please close the paint box ____. (careful / carefully)

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：mixing

mix：is后需现在分词，mix原形不能直接组成此进行结构。；mixing：is mixing构成现在进行时。

### 2：make

make：to后用动词原形make。；makes：不定式标记to后不能用第三人称单数makes。

### 3：learned / learnt

learned：learn的过去式，昨日语境成立。；learnt：Oxford同列learn的过去式learnt；与learned同等接受。

### 4：bring

bring：did not后接原形bring。；brought：did not后不能使用过去式brought。

### 5：wide enough

wide enough：enough修饰形容词放其后。；enough wide：enough不能在这里置于形容词wide前。

### 6：in

in：水罐内部用in a jar。；at：at不表达题中刷子置于水罐内的位置。

### 7：her

her：介词to后用人称代词宾格her。；she：she是主格，不能直接作介词to的宾语。

### 8：were cleaning

were cleaning：复数主语与were cleaning一致，过去到达语境成立。；cleans：cleans既为现在时又是第三人称单数，不与复数主语一致。

### 9：mending

mending：for后动词用-ing形式。；mend：for后不能直接接动词原形mend作此补语。

### 10：carefully

careful：careful为形容词，不能在此修饰close。；carefully：carefully为副词，修饰关盒动作。
