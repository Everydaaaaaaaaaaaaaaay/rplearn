# 小六：菜园与种子

## 学生题面

完成全部十题：从每题括号内的两个词或词组中选取合适的一项，原样填入空白，不填字母或给词变形。两项均合法时，填写其中任意一项即可；不要将两项一起填写。候选只供本题使用。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Rosa is ____ three seeds into each pot now. (putting / put)

2. You should ____ the dry leaves into this basket. (puts / put)

3. Last month our class ____ how to grow beans. (learned / learnt)

4. The class did not ____ any seeds last winter. (plant / planted)

5. The path is ____ for the little wheelbarrow. (wide enough / enough wide)

6. These beans came ____ our school garden. (from / at)

7. Uncle gave the empty pots to my sister and ____. (I / me)

8. The two pupils ____ the young plants when the teacher arrived. (were watering / waters)

9. The children finished ____ the seed labels before lunch. (write / writing)

10. Please count the empty pots ____. (accurately / accurate)

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：putting

putting：is putting构成现在进行时。；put：is后不能直接接原形put构成此谓语。

### 2：put

puts：情态动词should后不用puts。；put：should后用动词原形put。

### 3：learned / learnt

learned：learned是合法过去式。；learnt：learnt是Oxford列明的合法过去式，同等接受。

### 4：plant

plant：did not后用原形plant。；planted：did not后不接过去式planted。

### 5：wide enough

wide enough：enough修饰形容词wide置于其后。；enough wide：这里不能使用enough wide。

### 6：from

from：come from说明来源。；at：come at不表达这里的产地来源。

### 7：me

I：并列后仍作介词to的宾语，不能用主格I。；me：并列宾语my sister and me使用宾格me。

### 8：were watering

were watering：复数主语two pupils与were watering一致。；waters：waters为第三人称单数，与复数主语不符。

### 9：writing

write：finish后不能在此接原形write。；writing：finish后接动名词writing。

### 10：accurately

accurately：accurately为副词，修饰count。；accurate：accurate为形容词，不在此修饰count。
