## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

M: Is my report ready to send? I have checked the figures twice.

W: The figures are fine, and the writing is clear. But the title still says last year. Change that before you send it to the club secretary.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

W: I usually collect books from the library van at a quarter past nine. Is it the same on Wednesday?

M: No. On Wednesdays it arrives thirty minutes later, after its stop at the hospital.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

M: Can I move to the empty seat by the window?

W: Please stay in your seat with your belt fastened until we have taken off. I can help you change seats once we are in the air.

### 对话4完整原创脚本

W: Would you like the back as short as the sides?

M: Yes, please, but leave a little more at the front.

W: All right. Keep your head straight while I use the scissors.

### 对话5完整原创脚本

M: Did the art festival reply about your painting?

W: Yes! They have chosen it for the main hall. I can hardly wait to tell my family. I never expected it to be accepted.
