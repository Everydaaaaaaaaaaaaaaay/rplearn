# 高二五给词式：观鸟屋里的笔记本

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据短文内容填空；给词空使用所给词的正确形式，不额外限1词。其余无给词空各填1词。

Last autumn, a group of pupils (1) ____ (invite) to help organise the notebook kept in a small bird hide beside a lake. The building was known (2) ____ the reed room because tall plants hid most of it from the path. Its regular visitors included a retired teacher, (3) ____ neat handwriting appeared on many pages, and several people who wrote only a date and a few words before moving on.

The pupils selected four (4) ____ (entry) that described the same quiet corner of the lake on different mornings. One visitor had (5) ____ (write) that almost nothing was visible through the mist. Another reported a noisy group of birds arriving shortly after sunrise. There were more differences between the accounts (6) ____ the pupils had expected. At first, they wanted to decide which description was the most accurate.

After (7) ____ (read) the pages together, however, they noticed that the writers had not all sat in the same place or stayed for the same length of time. A person close to the low window could see a patch of water hidden from someone standing at the back. The notebook did not provide enough detail to reconstruct every visit, but it did suggest what future visitors might record more carefully.

The notes could help the next reader, provided (8) ____ they distinguished a brief observation from a conclusion about the whole morning. The pupils added a few optional prompts for time, weather and viewing position. Regular visitors kept (9) ____ (return) to the hide and gradually began using them. The value of the notebook depended (10) ____ the care taken with these ordinary details, rather than on whether a page contained a long list of unusual birds.

## 作者核对

### 1：was invited / were invited

a group可按整体用was，也可在英式一致中按成员用were；均为过去被动，群体受到邀请。

### 2：as

be known as表示被称作。

### 3：whose

whose handwriting表退休教师的字迹。

### 4：entries

four要求entry复数entries。

### 5：written

had后过去分词written。

### 6：than

more differences than...比较结构。

### 7：reading / having read

after后普通或完成动名词均表示共同阅读先于注意差别。

### 8：that

provided that引出笔记有用的条件。

### 9：returning

keep doing形式，持续来访用returning。

### 10：on / upon

depend on/upon均表依赖细致记录。
