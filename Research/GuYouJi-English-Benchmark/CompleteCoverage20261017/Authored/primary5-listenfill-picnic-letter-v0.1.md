Dear Ben,

Our class had a picnic on Saturday. We met near my favourite park. Before we left, we made some sandwiches in the school kitchen. Two of our neighbours came to help. We carried the food carefully because the boxes were open. At the park, we played games and shared our lunch. We put all the rubbish in a bag. Before going home, we returned the empty boxes to school. Everyone helped to wash them.

Best,
Amy
