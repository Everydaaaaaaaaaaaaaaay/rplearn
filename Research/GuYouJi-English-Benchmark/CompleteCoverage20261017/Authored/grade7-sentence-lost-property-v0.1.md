# 初一句中词形：失物与人物

## 学生题面

在每句空白处填入一个适当的单词，或括号内单词的正确形式。无给词空为一个单词；给词空按正确形式，不另加一词上限。

以下十五句彼此独立，保留给词与无给词位置。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. There are seven days ____ a week.

2. This is ____ (I) red notebook, not yours.

3. Look at this photo: there ____ (be) exactly three children in it.

4. The ball is sitting ____ top of the cupboard.

5. A person normally has two ____ (foot).

6. The jacket belongs to Emma alone. It is ____ (Emma) jacket.

7. There are three printed ____ (notice) on this board.

8. What can you ____ (see) through the window?

9. —____ red bag is this? —It belongs to Tom.

10. We ate some ____ (China) food at the festival.

11. The box is ____ (real) heavy; I cannot lift it.

12. Maya plays ____ violin in the school orchestra.

13. The guide asked ____ (we) to wait by the door.

14. Would you like ____ (go) to the island by boat tomorrow?

15. I stayed indoors ____ I had a fever and needed rest.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：in

seven days in a week；无给词一词。

### 2：my

名词前用物主限定词my。

### 3：are

当前照片说明三个孩子，用are；其他时间形式未列不直接错。

### 4：on

on top of固定空间表达。

### 5：feet

two后不规则复数feet。

### 6：Emma's

明确单一所有者Emma，所有格Emma’s；直弯引号等价。

### 7：notices

three后可数复数notices。

### 8：see

can后动词原形see。

### 9：Whose

按答语询问所有者Whose，其他问法待语境复核。

### 10：Chinese

国别形容词Chinese修饰food。

### 11：really

副词really修饰heavy。

### 12：the / a

the violin指演奏乐器，a violin可指一把具体小提琴；都合当前句。

### 13：us

asked后的宾格us。

### 14：to go / to be going

would like后普通/进行不定式均可表达明天行程；不补给词一词限制。

### 15：because / as / since

三个一词连词均能表达发烧导致留在室内。
