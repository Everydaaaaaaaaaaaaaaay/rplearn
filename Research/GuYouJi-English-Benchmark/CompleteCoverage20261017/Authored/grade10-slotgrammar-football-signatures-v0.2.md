# 高一逐空限词：足球上的两个签名

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据短文内容填空；给词空使用所给词的正确形式，不额外限1词。第5、6、7、9空各填1词；第10空已明确标注“填2词”，须恰好2词。

The ball in our club cupboard had been signed (1) ____ (careful) by two former players. Their names were still readable, although the surface around them was badly worn. When I borrowed it for a display, I promised (2) ____ (return) it before the next training session. We had once (3) ____ (lose) a much newer ball while clearing the equipment room, so the club secretary wrote down exactly where this one was going.

At the display, one of the oldest (4) ____ (player) in the town came over to look at the signatures. He recognised both names but remembered the players joining the club in different years. He wanted a yes-or-no answer when he asked (5) ____ the two signatures had been added on the same day. I did not know. A photograph in the club album showed only one of them signing, so it could not settle the question.

The secretary called a former captain, (6) ____ brother had kept the old match programmes. Together they found a report about a reunion at which both players had taken part (7) ____ a short exhibition game. It included a short interview in which both players described signing the ball together after the match. The visitor laughed when I read it aloud. He had attended the reunion himself, but had remembered the players from their earlier seasons rather than from that one afternoon.

Beside the old ball, we placed a much (8) ____ (heavy) leather one from an earlier period. A small card described the game in (9) ____ it had been used. Visitors spent longer comparing the objects than we had expected. For the final club photograph, we decided to keep the worn signed ball (10) ____ (填2词) replacing it with a perfect new one. The marks that made it look untidy were also the reason people had stopped to ask about it.

## 作者核对

### 1：carefully

修饰had been signed的方式副词carefully。

### 2：to return

promise to return。

### 3：lost

had once之后过去分词lost。

### 4：players

one of the oldest之后用复数players。

### 5：whether / if

前文明确yes-or-no，asked后间接一般疑问用whether/if，非原因或方式提问。

### 6：whose

whose brother表示former captain与brother的所属。

### 7：in

take part in固定搭配。

### 8：heavier

much在这里直接修饰比较级，较早时期的皮球与前文旧球构成对比，用heavier；不虚构题面含than。未列more heavy等形式待复核，不自动计错。

### 9：which

in which中介词后的关系代词，先行词game。

### 10：rather than / instead of

keep...rather than/instead of replacing...，两种两词形式均符合保留旧球而不换新球的选择。
