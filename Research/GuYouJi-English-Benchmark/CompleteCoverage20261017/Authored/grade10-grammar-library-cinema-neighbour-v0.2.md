# 高一：借书、电影与邻里

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

### A

Last Friday, I worked with a volunteer (1) ____ showed me around our school library. We carried two empty (2) ____ (shelf) into the reading room. One shelf was (3) ____ (wide) than the doorway, so we turned it sideways. We moved as (4) ____ (careful) as possible because the floor had just been cleaned. By lunchtime, there was space for the new books.

### B

Our class watched a short film about the night sky. It contained (5) ____ (many) pictures than words, but each picture was important. Before the film, our teacher asked us (6) ____ listen for the names of three planets. My friend became interested (7) ____ astronomy and borrowed a book afterwards. We plan to watch the next film together.

### C

On Sunday I met a neighbour (8) ____ (carry) a heavy suitcase. She was the woman (9) ____ daughter had helped me find my lost cat. I offered to carry the case upstairs. She thanked me for (10) ____ (help) her. It only took a few minutes, but we both felt happier after our short conversation.

## 作者核对

### 1：who / that

限定关系从句指volunteer并缺主语，who/that均可。

### 2：shelves

two明确要求复数，shelf变shelves。

### 3：wider

than引出比较，wider为比较级。

### 4：carefully

as...as possible中修饰moved的正级方式副词；不能换比较级。

### 5：more

than words要求many比较级more。

### 6：to

asked us to listen，不定式补足语。

### 7：in

interested in astronomy固定搭配。

### 8：carrying

本空接受所给词的现在分词carrying。新增关系代词的who was carrying / that was carrying虽能组成句子，但现有“所给词正确形式”证据不足以支持将整从句自动接收，改为待复核；不直接判错。给词可用必要助动词/不定式等多词形式，不等于任意增加从句成分。

### 9：whose

whose daughter表达先行词woman与daughter的所属，whose作限定词。

### 10：helping / having helped

for后动名词；helping和强调已完成的having helped均合感谢此前帮助。
