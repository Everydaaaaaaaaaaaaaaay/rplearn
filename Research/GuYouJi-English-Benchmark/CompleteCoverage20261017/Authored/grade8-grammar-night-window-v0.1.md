My brother and I wanted to make a birthday card for our father. There were several old (1) ____ (photo) of our street in a family album, but none showed it at night. We thought a night photograph would look (2) ____ (interesting) than a daytime one. Father often came home late from work, so the lights in the windows were familiar to him.

We waited (3) ____ (patient) until the sky was dark enough. My brother was (4) ____ (hold) the camera while I opened the window. He told me not to change the camera settings while he was checking the photograph.

## 作者核对

### 1：photos

several后复数photos。

### 2：more interesting

than前比较级more interesting，给词不限制一词。

### 3：patiently

等待的方式用副词patiently。

### 4：holding

was后表示当时进行的holding。

### 5：in

in the sky。

### 6：so

云遮住月亮因此再等，用so；其他连词是否契合仍不作穷尽排除。

### 7：than

larger比较级接than。

### 8：following

the following morning指接着的早晨。

### 9：our

names前物主限定词our。

### 10：to give

hope to give不定式。
