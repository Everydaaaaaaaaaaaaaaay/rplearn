## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

W: Tom, is your bike outside?

M: No. My bike has a flat tyre. I walked to school today, but I will take the bus home if it rains.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

M: We need tomatoes and rice for dinner. Shall we go to the market?

W: The market is closed on Mondays. We can get both at the shop near the station.

M: Do we need milk too?

W: No. There is a full bottle in the fridge.

M: Then I will write tomatoes and rice on the list.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

W: The play starts at three, but we should arrive at half past two.

M: Why so early, Lucy?

W: I need to put on my costume. You can help me with my hat after that.

M: I thought you were watching the play.

W: No, I am in it this time. I play a shopkeeper.
