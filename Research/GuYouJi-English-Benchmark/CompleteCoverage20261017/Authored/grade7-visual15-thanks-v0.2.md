### A组（第1—3题）

#### A1

Willow Library holds its Helpers’ Day on the third Sunday in May 2027. Hill Library celebrates on May 8 instead. These are made-up local events, so the two places do not have to choose the same date. At Willow, children bring a drawing or a small card rather than an expensive gift.

The volunteers often spend their weekends finding books, repairing torn pages and helping new readers. They sometimes work quietly at the back of the library, so visitors may not notice them. A thank-you card can show that their work matters. This year the children will put the cards beside the lending desk for everyone to see.

### B组（第4—6题）

#### B1

My school stands outside the city and has only five classes. There is no fence around the grass where we play. Our maths teacher, Mr Holm, uses games to explain shapes, and we call him Uncle Holm because he is so friendly. In one game we stand at the corners of a large triangle.

Ms Lane teaches English through songs and short stories. **They** help us remember new words because we hear the words again in something enjoyable. She sometimes asks us to choose a story to hear a second time. Mr Kent, our PE teacher, enjoys spending time among the trees near the playground.

Here is a picture of the three teachers and me. Mr Kent is sitting on a low branch. Mr Holm is the man wearing dark sunglasses. Ms Lane and I are standing below the tree. I am much shorter than the adults and have a small cap on. None of us is carrying a book in this picture.

### C组（第7—10题）

#### C1

At Lake School, a language club uses a simple memory routine. New members first hear a short word, listen again and remember its sound. Only then do they try to say it. The teacher says that looking at a printed word once is not the routine. Listening, remembering and using the word are its three connected steps.

The children practise the words in short games on several different days. Each time they hear and use a familiar word again, it becomes easier to recall in the next game. They do not learn every word immediately, and the teacher does not expect everyone to work at the same speed. This is a classroom account, not a scientific test of every child’s memory.

Two members, Lin and Eva, began hearing two home languages when they were very young, before they started school. Other members meet their new language mostly at the club. Their school timetable is busy with other lessons, so they have fewer chances to hear and use it. The club therefore makes room for a little repeated practice rather than one long list to look at once.

### D组（第11—15题）

Read the three poems below.

#### D1

Rest your small hand beside the bear;
I pull the blanket to your chin.
The evening lamp makes one soft square;
The quiet night is settling in.

Your wooden train has reached its shed;
Your little shoes wait by the door.
The moon looks gently at your bed;
We’ll play tomorrow as before.

#### D2

Dear Mother, in the dawn you feed
Your furry child beside the gate.
You smooth my fur and hear my call.

When evening settles on our tree,
I touch your cheek with one small kiss.
Your face is last and dear to me.

#### D3

With a small broom I sweep the hall,
Then count the cups upon the tray.
I carry clean ones to us all.

I fold the cloth and make a space;
No one must ask me twice today.
I like to help around our place.
