## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Is that your schoolbag on the desk, Mike?

M: No. My bag is under the chair. That is my water bottle. The ball is outside.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Who are you talking about, the woman with long hair?

W: No, the boy with glasses. He is wearing a blue shirt.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Hello, I’m Eva White. Are you Leo Brown?

M: I’m Leo, but my family name is Green. Brown is my friend’s family name.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Is Amy your sister, Rosa?

W: No. Amy is my cousin. We live near each other. My sister is Lucy.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: What can you see in your brother’s picture?

M: Four cats, two small dogs and a red house. The cats are all near the house.
