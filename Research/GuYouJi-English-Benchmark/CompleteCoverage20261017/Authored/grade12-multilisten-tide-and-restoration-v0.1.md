## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: I have booked the coast walk for ten tomorrow. Should I bring the tide chart?

M: The guide has one, but bring your boots. The route stays above the water, though parts of the path will be muddy.

W: I assumed we would go down onto the beach to see the rocks.

M: That was the original plan. The group now includes people who cannot manage the steps, so the guide has chosen a level path with views of the same rocks.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: These radio parts arrived without labels. Are we supposed to guess which set they came from?

W: No. Photograph each part next to a ruler, then put it in a separate bag. We will compare the pictures with the repair records.

M: That will take longer than cleaning everything first.

W: Perhaps, but cleaning could remove pencil marks that help us identify them. We need a record of how they arrived before doing anything else.

M: If a part is broken, can we throw it away?

W: Keep it. Even a damaged piece may show how the set was put together. We are documenting these radios, not trying to make every one work again.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: I liked your interview with the bus driver, but the opening sounds as if he dislikes his job.

M: He said the early start was exhausting. That seemed a strong beginning.

W: It is strong, but later he explains that he enjoys the quiet first journey. Removing that part changes his point.

M: We only have three minutes. What could I cut instead?

W: The long description of the bus model repeats information in the caption. Keep his explanation of why the first journey matters.

M: You are right. I will also send him the edited version before we publish it.

W: Good. Ask whether the edit represents his meaning accurately, rather than whether he likes the music.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I expected the night photography class to begin with camera settings, but you asked us to leave our cameras in their bags.

W: For ten minutes. I wanted you to notice where the light came from before looking through a screen.

M: I began to see reflections that I had walked past before.

W: Exactly. A technically sharp image is not automatically an interesting one.

M: Do you think my picture of the empty ticket window works?

W: The light is good, but the bright sign at the edge draws attention away from the window. Try changing where you stand rather than immediately deleting it.

M: Should I ask someone to stand by the window to make it less empty?

W: Only if that is the story you want. The absence of a person can itself be meaningful. Do not add one merely because you think every photograph needs a face.

M: I will try a different angle first, then compare the two versions.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: This afternoon we are opening a temporary display about the old river crossing. You will see tickets, photographs and a model ferry. The model was made recently from measurements and descriptions; it is not a surviving boat from the period. We have placed that information beside it because an accurate reconstruction can still be mistaken for an original object.

The ticket collection is incomplete. Some months are represented by a single ticket, while others have none. A missing month does not show that the ferry stopped running. It tells us only that no ticket from that month is in this collection. Other records may eventually help us establish the timetable.

If your family has a relevant photograph, please tell the volunteer at the desk. You can let us make a copy while keeping the original. We would also like to record what you know about it, including any uncertainty about the date. A carefully described uncertainty is more useful than a guessed year that later visitors repeat as fact.
