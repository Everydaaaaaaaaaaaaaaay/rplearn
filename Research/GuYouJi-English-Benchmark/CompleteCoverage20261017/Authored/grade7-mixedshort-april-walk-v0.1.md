## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Do you need a new skirt, Nina?

W: No, I need a T-shirt for sports day. My trousers are still fine.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Is your birthday on April the eighth, Owen?

M: That is my sister’s birthday. Mine is on April the eighteenth, not in May.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Do you take the bus to school, Eva?

W: No. My school is near home, so I walk. My brother goes to his school by bike.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: What time do you get to school, Ben?

M: At nine in the morning. I leave at three in the afternoon, and I stay there for lunch.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Do you watch TV just after dinner, Mia?

W: I do my homework first. Then I take a walk with Dad. I watch TV at the weekend.
