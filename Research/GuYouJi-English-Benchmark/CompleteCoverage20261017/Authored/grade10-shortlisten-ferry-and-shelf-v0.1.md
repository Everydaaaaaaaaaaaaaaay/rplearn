1. What is the woman's problem?
   A. The numbers are too small to read.
   B. The journey takes too long.
   C. She has lost the timetable.

2. When will the last ferry leave today?
   A. At 6:40.
   B. At 6:55.
   C. At 7:15.

3. Where does the conversation probably take place?
   A. At a theatre ticket desk.
   B. At an airport check-in desk.
   C. At a restaurant entrance.

4. What does the man probably do?
   A. He cooks meals.
   B. He makes clothes.
   C. He makes wooden furniture.

5. How does the woman feel now?
   A. Angry.
   B. Relieved.
   C. Lonely.

## 作者核对

### 对话1完整原创脚本

M: You said you could not use the new timetable. Is the journey too long?

W: The journey is fine. It is the print that bothers me. Without my glasses, I cannot make out any of those tiny numbers.

### 对话2完整原创脚本

W: I thought the last ferry left at twenty to seven.

M: That is the usual time. Today it is leaving fifteen minutes later because the crew need to finish a check.

### 对话3完整原创脚本

M: Are there any seats left in the balcony for the next performance?

W: Yes, two in the front row. If you buy them now, you can collect the tickets at the counter beside the stage door.

### 对话4完整原创脚本

W: Will this piece of wood be wide enough for the new shelf?

M: Let me measure it. Yes, but I will cut a little off the end before fitting it between the cupboards.

### 对话5完整原创脚本

M: Here is your wallet. It was under the chair, exactly where you were sitting.

W: Thank goodness! I was beginning to think I had left it on the bus. Now I can stop worrying about all my cards.
