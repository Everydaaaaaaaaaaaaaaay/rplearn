# 初三语法：会移动的小书箱

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

The first travelling book box in our street began with a simple (1) ____ (invite). A neighbour asked everyone to bring a book they no longer needed. There were only six (2) ____ (family) at the first meeting, and nobody expected the idea to last very long. The box itself was (3) ____ (careful) built from a wooden crate which had once held fruit.

By the end of the first week, we had (4) ____ (receive) enough books to fill it. The younger children made labels by (5) ____ (they), while the adults added small wheels to the bottom. We could move it indoors when it rained. The box belonged neither to our school (6) ____ to the nearest public library. Anyone in the street could borrow from it, provided (7) ____ they returned each book when they finished reading it.

We soon found that leaving a message about a book was more useful than simply (8) ____ (say) that it was good. One child wrote that a story was funny but difficult in places. Another said its pictures helped her understand the ending. These short notes made choosing a book much (9) ____ (easy) for the next reader.

I used to think that sharing books meant keeping a list of names. Now I look forward (10) ____ finding a new note in the box. Sometimes I choose a book because of a comment written by someone whose reading taste is very different from mine.
