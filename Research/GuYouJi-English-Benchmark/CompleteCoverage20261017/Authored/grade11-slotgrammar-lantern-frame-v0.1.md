# 高二五给词式：不对称的灯笼骨架

## 学生题面

阅读短文，根据短文内容填空；给词空使用所给词的正确形式，不额外限1词。其余无给词空各填1词。

The art club hoped (1) ____ (copy) the outline of a lantern shown in an old festival programme. The photograph was small, and the students could see only one side clearly. A narrow strip on the left might have been part of the frame or merely a shadow. On the one hand, they wanted to follow the picture; on (2) ____ other, they needed a frame that could stand without support.

When the first model leaned sideways, the group could not immediately explain (3) ____ had gone wrong. They checked the lengths of the strips and found that the base was not as (4) ____ (wide) as the outline they had drawn. Fortunately, the mistake (5) ____ (discover) before any covering paper was attached. Altering a bare frame was much easier than taking apart a finished lantern, so the students could try another arrangement without losing all their work.

They kept the first frame, (6) ____ had become a useful comparison, beside the revised one. A club member placed the two on a table and asked a younger visitor which looked more like the photograph. The visitor chose the crooked frame because its shadow matched the dark strip in the picture. The students could not help (7) ____ (laugh). Their supposed failure had reproduced a detail they had been trying to remove.

It was (8) ____ reminder that copying an image required decisions about what the image actually showed. Several small (9) ____ (adjust) made the second frame stand more securely, although its outline no longer matched the photograph in every respect. The finished display included not only the new lantern, (10) ____ also both early frames and a short account of the choices behind them. Visitors could see the work as a sequence of decisions, rather than as a single object that had appeared complete.

## 作者核对

### 1：to copy

hope to copy不定式。

### 2：the

on the one hand/on the other固定对应。

### 3：what

explain what had gone wrong，what作从句主语，指框架问题。

### 4：wide

as...as要求形容词原级wide。

### 5：was discovered / had been discovered

叙事过去被动was discovered及强调早于贴纸的过去完成被动均合。

### 6：which

逗号后非限制性关系从句主语，指first frame。

### 7：laughing

could not help doing，用laughing。新增but laugh虽可另造正常结构，当前给词变形范围未核，不自动接收，未列待复核。

### 8：a / one

a reminder引入提醒；one reminder强调其中一次，也符合全文。

### 9：adjustments

several small后用派生复数名词adjustments。

### 10：but

not only...but also固定并列。

全文人物、场所和事件均虚构，来源只参照完整具体形式。已列合法变体同等接受，不设三答案硬上限；未列词形待复核，不直接判错。给词的多词形式限于已核的动词时态/语态、不定式等词形表达，不因含有词根便自动接收新增关系从句。去首尾空白、合并内部空白、忽略大小写，不按词根匹配。无给词空超过1词不符本组规则。作者核对不证明答案穷尽、年级难度等值或独立质量验收，后端固定六题未接完整组。
