# 小五：书桌上的分类牌

## 学生题面

阅读短文，为每一空选择最合适的一项。每空独立选择，不设每项只能用一次。

It is Friday afternoon. We are (1) ____ our teacher with a book sale. Sam and I (2) ____ the books to three tables. The story table is (3) ____ the window. I can stand beside the table and see the playground outside. Each table has a large sign. A girl cannot find the dog story she wants. She asks me to help her (4) ____ it. I show her the sign and take her to the right table. Soon there is a (5) ____ line of children. It goes from the table to the door! The girl says, “My little brother loves dogs.” She buys a book for (6) ____ little brother.

### 全组回填

It is Friday afternoon. We are helping our teacher with a book sale. Sam and I carry the books to three tables. The story table is near the window. I can stand beside the table and see the playground outside. Each table has a large sign. A girl cannot find the dog story she wants. She asks me to help her look for it. I show her the sign and take her to the right table. Soon there is a long line of children. It goes from the table to the door! The girl says, “My little brother loves dogs.” She buys a book for her little brother.
