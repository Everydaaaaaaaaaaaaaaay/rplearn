### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: This is a message from the school radio club. Our Story Radio workshop will be on Thursday, from four in the afternoon until six. It is open to students who have never made a radio programme before. The aim is to help you read stories clearly, so a listener can follow them without seeing your face. We will try short passages first and then work in pairs on a longer story. To avoid interruptions, keep phones outside the recording room. School recorders will be ready on the desks. If you cannot think of a story, you may use pictures from the box beside the door for ideas. Choose one and imagine what happened just before the moment in it. After the workshop, one story from each pair will be played at lunchtime on Friday. Listeners can send questions about future programmes to the club through the school website.
