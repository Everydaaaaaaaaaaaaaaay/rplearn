### 一种完整修订

A local history room let our class help reorganise a shelf of copied letters, each of which had a separate reference number. The copies were available to readers, but the twelve original letters were stored in a locked cabinet. Our job was to make a catalogue that would help visitors find the copy they needed without handling the originals. We began by arranging the copies according to their reference numbers rather than their size. One reader pointed out that a number had been incorrectly copied and asked us to check it against the old list. The volunteer who kept the original list explained that the catalogue had changed several times over the years. She gave us two previous versions so that we could follow the changes instead of guessing. After comparing them, we realised that one entry had been moved to a different section, not lost. We added a note to explain the move, but we did not remove the old reference number. The completed catalogue made it easier for visitors to trace the copies, and the volunteer thanked us for working so carefully.
