### 示例（已解，不计入五题）

How much does the cap cost?

A. Six pounds fifty.

B. Eight pounds fifteen.

C. Eight pounds fifty.

答案：C。A：旧价不是现价；B：没有十五便士；C：现价八镑五十

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Can you collect me at the gallery at five twenty?

M: I have a meeting until five thirty. Give me twenty minutes to drive there.

W: All right. I will wait by the side entrance.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: You have sketched several versions of that entrance.

W: The client wants more daylight without losing the original brickwork. I will discuss these plans with the structural engineer before presenting them.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: The course costs six hundred pounds, but you only pay one third when you book. The rest is due when it starts.

M: Fine. I will make the booking payment today.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: We had enough food and the weather was fine, so I expected to reach the summit.

W: Why did you turn back?

M: One member twisted her ankle. Carrying on would have put her at risk, so the whole team returned together.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: I read novels on my tablet because I can enlarge the text.

M: That would be useful, but I still prefer a paper book. I like leaving it open on the table without a battery running down.

### 示例完整原创脚本

M: Is this cap six pounds fifty?

W: That was the old price. It is eight pounds fifty now.
