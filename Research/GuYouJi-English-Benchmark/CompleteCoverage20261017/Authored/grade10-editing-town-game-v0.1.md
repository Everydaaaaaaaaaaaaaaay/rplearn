## 作者核对

### 错误1

游戏规则用一般现在时，Each player单数与follows一致，用starts。

### 错误2

修饰wrote，用clearly。

### 错误3

ask somebody to do，加to于check前。

### 错误4

过去测试时的小学生给建议，用gave；who knew是另一个定语从句，不作为主句谓语。

### 错误5

two修饰可数名词复数suggestions。

### 错误6

bridge为单数，当前地图关系用connects；依过去叙事时点描述连接的桥也可用connected。两种已核时态同等接受。

### 错误7

much可加强比较级shorter，more重复比较，删more。若提出其他修法，未列者待复核。

### 错误8

After介词后用playing；本题每处仅一词，不能一次写having played。

### 错误9

information不可数，too much information。

### 错误10

more easier重复比较，可删more；easier改easy也形成more easy，另列同一错误的修法。

### 一种完整修订

For our English club, Mina and I designed a board game about a small town. Each player starts at the station and follows a different route. We wrote the rules clearly and put a copy beside each board. Before the first test, our teacher asked us to check the map for missing street names. A pupil who knew the town very well gave us two useful suggestions. We listened to her and added a bridge which connects the two sides of the river. The new route was much shorter than the old one. After playing one complete game, we asked the group what they wanted to change. They all agreed that one card had too much information on it. We removed a repeated sentence from the card and tested the game again, which made the instructions easier to follow.
