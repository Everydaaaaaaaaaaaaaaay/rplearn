# 初三语法：影子演出的距离

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

Our drama club had to make a (1) ____ (decide) about its next performance. The stage was too small for the number of (2) ____ (actor) in the club, and the school could not lend us a larger room. One member suggested a shadow play. It was an (3) ____ (usual) practical idea: the screen could stand at the front while most of us worked behind it.

We had (4) ____ (choose) a story about two travellers who lost their way. The travellers blamed (5) ____ (they), rather than their map, when they finally discovered the mistake. Our first paper figures were attractive, (6) ____ they did not move well. Neither the person holding a figure (7) ____ the person reading its lines could see the whole screen. We had to take turns watching from the audience seats.

Instead of (8) ____ (argue) about whose movement was right, we marked places on the floor. One position made the figure look much (9) ____ (large) than the other, and the change became part of the story. A traveller could suddenly seem brave when his shadow grew, then small again when he stepped back.

On performance day, a loose stick fell from one figure. The reader paused for a moment, giving the rest of us time to repair it. We were so relieved (10) ____ we almost forgot to move the second traveller. The audience waited, then laughed when both figures finally reached the painted town.

## 作者核对

### 1：decision

make a decision派生名词。

### 2：actors

the number of后复数actors。

### 3：unusually

修饰practical需副词unusually，强调异常切实可行。

### 4：chosen

had后过去分词chosen。

### 5：themselves

travellers责备自身，用反身复数themselves。

### 6：but / yet

漂亮与运动不好转折，but/yet均可。

### 7：nor

neither...nor配对；任一人均看不全。

### 8：arguing

instead of后动名词arguing。

### 9：larger

much...than明确比较级larger。

### 10：that

so relieved that引出差点忘记后续动作的结果。
