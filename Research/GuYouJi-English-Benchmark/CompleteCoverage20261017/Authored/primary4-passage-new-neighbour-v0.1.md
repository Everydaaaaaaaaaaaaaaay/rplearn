### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: My name is Maya. I am nine years old. I live in a small town beside a lake. My favourite food is vegetable soup. On Sundays, I often read books with my brother. We go to the library after lunch. This winter holiday, I will visit my grandparents on their farm. I want to help them feed the hens. I am looking forward to the visit.
