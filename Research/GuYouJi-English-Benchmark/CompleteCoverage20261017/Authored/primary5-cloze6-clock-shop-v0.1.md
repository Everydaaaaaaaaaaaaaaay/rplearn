My aunt (1) ____ a small clock shop. I visit her on Saturday mornings. A clock has two long (2) ____ to show the time. Today a boy brings in a clock, but (3) ____ does not work. My aunt opens the back. I like (4) ____ the quiet tick-tock when she finishes a job. This clock makes no sound yet. “Put in a new battery (电池) (5) ____ you close the back,” she tells me. I do that, and it starts ticking. The boy smiles and gives her some (6) ____ to pay for the battery. My aunt lets me keep the old one for our class display.

### 全组回填

My aunt has a small clock shop. I visit her on Saturday mornings. A clock has two long hands to show the time. Today a boy brings in a clock, but it does not work. My aunt opens the back. I like listening to the quiet tick-tock when she finishes a job. This clock makes no sound yet. “Put in a new battery (电池) before you close the back,” she tells me. I do that, and it starts ticking. The boy smiles and gives her some money to pay for the battery. My aunt lets me keep the old one for our class display.
