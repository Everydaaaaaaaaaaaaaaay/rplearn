# 小四：音乐课、画笔与共同难题

## 学生题面

从每题的A、B、C三个选项中选择最合适的一项。两空或三空的题目须选一个完整组合，每题只填一个字母。 组合中的“/”表示相应空不填介词。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Here is some ____ for your tea. It is white and you can drink it.
   A. apples
   B. milk
   C. biscuits

2. The music lesson starts now. It's time ____ sing.
   A. to
   B. in
   C. for

3. —How many ____ are there? —Five drums beside the piano.
   A. drum
   B. balls
   C. drums

4. We draw ____ seven ____ the morning ____ every Saturday.
   A. at; on; in
   B. at; in; /
   C. in; in; at

5. Amy has new socks. Look at ____ socks! ____ green.
   A. her; They're
   B. she; They're
   C. her; It's

6. This cup has only clean brushes, not dirty ones. Take a ____ brush, not a ____ one.
   A. dirty; clean
   B. dirty; dirty
   C. clean; dirty

7. My aunt is in the music room. —____ your ____ there? —Yes, she is.
   A. Are; aunt
   B. Is; aunt
   C. Is; uncles

8. —____ ruler is that? —It's ____.
   A. Whose; Lily
   B. Who's; Lily's
   C. Whose; Lily's

9. —Our kite broke before the show. We feel sad. —____
   A. I'm sorry to hear that.
   B. Congratulations!
   C. You are welcome.

10. We both lost our homework and need to find it. We are in the same boat. Here, “in the same boat” means ____.
   A. 乘坐同一条船
   B. 有不同的难题
   C. 处于相同的困境

## 作者核对

### 1：B

A：apples为复数，与is、it不一致，也非饮品。；B：milk不可数，符合some、is、it及可饮用白色液体。；C：biscuits复数且不是可喝的液体。

### 2：A

A：time to接动词原形sing。；B：time in sing不成结构。；C：for后不直接接该动词原形。

### 3：C

A：many要求复数，不能用单数。；B：回答明确是drums，不是balls。；C：复数drums与many和Five drums一致。

### 4：B

A：一般时段morning用in，every Saturday前不加in。；B：at seven、in the morning正确，every Saturday前不加介词。；C：钟点不用in，every Saturday前不加at。

### 5：A

A：Amy用her修饰，socks复数用They are。；B：socks前不用主格she作物主限定。；C：socks复数，不能用It is。

### 6：C

A：把允许拿的干净笔与不拿的脏笔反过来。；B：第一空与only clean冲突。；C：与干净笔可拿、脏笔不拿的要求一致。

### 7：B

A：aunt单数不与Are搭配。；B：aunt单数用Is，she回答一致。；C：uncles复数并且不对应she。

### 8：C

A：后句表示所有关系，需所有格。；B：Who is不是这里的物主疑问词。；C：Whose询问所有关系，由Lily所有格回答。

### 9：A

A：对风筝坏掉及难过直接表示同情。；B：祝贺不适合这条坏消息。；C：没有感谢，不用不用谢回应。

### 10：C

A：题面在谈同样丢了作业，没有乘船。；B：与both lost our homework的共同困境不符。；C：两人同样丢了作业，支持共同困境的比喻。
