## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The new school lunch menu looks good, but why is there no hot soup on Fridays?

M: The kitchen uses that time to clean the large soup cooker. We can have baked potatoes instead.

W: I see. Can we still get soup on Thursdays?

M: Yes. Only Friday is different.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: I want to help with the animal shelter, but I cannot visit every weekend.

W: You could help update its website from home. They need someone to put up new photos of the cats.

M: I am good with computers. Do I need to take the photos too?

W: No, the staff will send them to you. I can introduce you to the manager after school.

M: Thanks. That sounds like a job I can manage.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: Students in our boat-building group have finished a small wooden boat. The project began in September, when a retired boat builder offered to teach us. We met after school every Wednesday. None of us had used a saw before, so our first lesson was about using tools safely. The wood came from an old garden shed that was being taken down. We checked every board and removed all the nails before using it. Next Saturday, we will test the boat on the lake. Only our adult teacher will be in the boat for the first test. Families may watch from the path, but the landing stage will be kept clear.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: How are you getting on with the walking tour for the exchange students?

M: I have chosen the places, but the route is too long. It takes nearly three hours.

W: They only have two hours before lunch. Could you leave out the castle?

M: Yes. It is the farthest stop. Then we can spend more time by the canal.

W: Good idea. The old lock is interesting.

M: I will draw a shorter route on the map tonight. Can you check the street names?

W: Of course. And mark a place where the group can fill their water bottles.

M: There is a public tap beside the canal cafe. I will add that.
