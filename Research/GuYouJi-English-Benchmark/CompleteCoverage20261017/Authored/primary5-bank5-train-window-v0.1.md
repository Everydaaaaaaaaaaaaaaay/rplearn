### 全组回填

On Saturday, we get up early. It is only five o’clock in the morning, and the streets are dark. Dad and I walk to the railway station. Our train leaves from there at six. Dad buys two tickets, one for him and one for me. I put my ticket in my pocket. The worker needs to check it before I get on the train. My seat is beside the window.
