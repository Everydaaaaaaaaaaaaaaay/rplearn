# 高二六选四：修过的椅子值多少钱

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从A—F六个句子中为四个空白各选出最合适的一句。每个句子只能使用一次，其中有两项多余。

At the back of a small furniture shop stood a chair with one pale leg and three dark ones. The label beside it listed the hours spent repairing the seat, cleaning the joints and replacing the missing leg. Most second-hand labels in the shop gave little more than a price. This one almost told a story.

A customer asked why the chair cost more than a new one she had seen in a catalogue. She had expected an old object to offer a way of spending less. (1) ____ The repairer did not pretend otherwise. He showed her the receipt for the replacement wood and explained how much of the price represented the time spent on work that a factory could have completed more quickly.

(2) ____ That record was unusually detailed. It included a small sketch of a joint that an earlier owner had fixed with a metal plate. The current repairer had left the plate in place after checking that it was sound. He had chosen to preserve a visible sign of another person's work rather than make every surface look as if it had come from the same workshop.

The customer pointed at the sketch and asked why he had not simply fitted a new joint. (3) ____ He opened a drawer and showed her a modern fitting that could have done the job. He was not claiming that an old method was always better, or that a repaired chair must suit every buyer. The plate mattered to this particular restoration because it showed how the chair had been kept in use before it reached his shop.

(4) ____ The customer was free to prefer the cheaper new chair. The label did not turn the repairer's choices into an obligation for her; it allowed her to see what she would be paying for. She left without buying anything, but returned the next week with a different question: could he examine a chair that had belonged to her grandfather?
