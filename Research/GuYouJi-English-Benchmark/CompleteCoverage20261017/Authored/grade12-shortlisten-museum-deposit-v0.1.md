A. Inside the library.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: The museum closes at six. Would you like me to book the last entry at five?

W: That would only give us an hour. Let us go at three, so we can look around without rushing.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Your designs use very little water, which is helpful in this climate.

M: I choose plants that suit the area and group them by their needs. My clients spend less time watering their gardens that way.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: The bicycle hire is twenty pounds a day, isn't it?

W: Yes, plus a refundable fifty-pound deposit. You pay both now and get the deposit back when you return it undamaged.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: Did you leave the lecture because you disagreed with the speaker?

M: Not at all. I had arranged to meet a visiting colleague at the station, and her train was earlier than expected.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Would you rather work alone on the report?

W: For the first draft, yes. But I want someone to challenge my argument afterwards. Otherwise I may miss a weakness I cannot see myself.

### 示例完整原创脚本

M: Shall we meet inside the library?

W: It closes early today. Meet me outside the cafe instead.
