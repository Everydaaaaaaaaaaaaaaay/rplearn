## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Are you still going to the pottery workshop on Sunday, Dan?

M: Yes. Last time I made a bowl, but this time I want to make a small jug.

W: Do we need to bring clay?

M: No, the teacher provides it. Bring an old shirt, though. Your sleeves may get dirty.

W: Good idea. I will put one in my bag tonight.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: You look tired, Rosa. Have you been studying late?

W: No. Our neighbour's dog barks early every morning and wakes me up.

M: Have you told the neighbour?

W: I do not want to sound unfriendly.

M: You can explain it politely. They may not know how much noise the dog makes.

W: You are right. I will speak to them this afternoon.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: This Saturday, the railway history room will display objects from the old North Line station. The station closed forty years ago. Most of the objects were found in its store room last winter. You can see lamps, tickets and a large station clock. A former railway worker will explain how the clock was set before every morning's first train. The display opens at ten, but the talk begins at eleven. Entry is free. Children can stamp a paper ticket to take home, but the metal signs must stay behind the ropes.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: You have invited your exchange partner to dinner, haven't you, Owen?

M: Yes, but I am worried about choosing the food. I do not know what she can eat.

W: Ask her directly. She will probably appreciate that.

M: I will send her a message. I thought of making fish, but perhaps there is something she avoids.

W: Also, let her know dinner is at six. Our family usually eats earlier than she does.

M: Should I plan games for afterwards?

W: One simple game would be nice, but give her time to talk. She may want to ask about your family.

M: Thanks. I was planning too much. A relaxed evening sounds better.
