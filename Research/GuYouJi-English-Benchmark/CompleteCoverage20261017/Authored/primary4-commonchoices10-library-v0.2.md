# 小四：图书室、午饭与小测验

## 学生题面

从每题的A、B、C三个选项中选择最合适的一项。两空或三空的题目须选一个完整组合，每题只填一个字母。 组合中的“/”表示相应空不填介词。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Here is some ____ in a bowl. You can eat it with a spoon.
   A. grapes
   B. sandwiches
   C. rice

2. It's twelve. It's time ____ have lunch.
   A. for
   B. to
   C. at

3. —How many ____ can you see? —Four tables in this room.
   A. tables
   B. table
   C. windows

4. We have lunch ____ one ____ the afternoon ____ every Friday.
   A. in; in; at
   B. at; on; on
   C. at; in; /

5. Ben has new shoes. Look at ____ shoes! ____ brown.
   A. his; It's
   B. his; They're
   C. he; They're

6. There is only milk, not juice, in the fridge. I can get ____, but not ____.
   A. milk; juice
   B. juice; milk
   C. juice; juice

7. My mum works in this library. —____ your ____ here? —Yes, she is.
   A. Are; mum
   B. Is; brothers
   C. Is; mum

8. —____ coat is this? —It's ____.
   A. Whose; Sam's
   B. Who's; Sam's
   C. Whose; Sam

9. —Our class trip is cancelled. I feel sad. —____
   A. Happy New Year!
   B. Good job!
   C. What a pity!

10. This puzzle is very easy for me. I finish it in a minute. It is a piece of cake! Here, “a piece of cake” means ____.
   A. 一块真正的蛋糕
   B. 很容易的事
   C. 一件很难的事

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：C

A：复数grapes与is及it不一致。；B：复数sandwiches与is和it不一致。；C：rice不可数，和some、is、it及碗中食物一致。

### 2：B

A：for后此处不能直接接动词have。；B：time to have lunch为正确动词结构。；C：time at have不构成该结构。

### 3：A

A：many后复数，与回答Four tables直接一致。；B：many后不用单数。；C：回答是tables，不是windows。

### 4：C

A：钟点不用in，every Friday前不加at。；B：一般时段the afternoon不用on，every Friday前不加on。；C：at one、in the afternoon正确，every Friday前不加介词。

### 5：B

A：shoes为复数，不用It is。；B：Ben用his修饰，shoes复数用They are。；C：名词前用his，不用主格he。

### 6：A

A：与only milk及not juice一致。；B：将有和没有的饮料反过来了。；C：第一空与只有milk冲突。

### 7：C

A：mum单数不与Are一致。；B：brothers复数不与Is及she一致。；C：mum单数配Is，并由she回答。

### 8：A

A：Whose问谁的，用Sam所有格回答。；B：Who is不在coat前表达所有关系。；C：后句需要所有格，不是Sam主格名词。

### 9：C

A：没有新年语境，不回应坏消息。；B：称赞完成任务不回应行程取消的难过。；C：直接表达对取消行程的遗憾。

### 10：B

A：上下文在谈解谜的容易程度，没有食用蛋糕。；B：前文very easy及很快完成支持此比喻。；C：与very easy冲突。
