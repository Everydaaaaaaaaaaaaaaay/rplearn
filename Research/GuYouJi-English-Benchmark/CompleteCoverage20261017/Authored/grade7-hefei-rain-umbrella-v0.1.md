## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: What are you carrying, Nick? Is it your kite?

M: No, that is at home. I am carrying an umbrella because it may rain. My cap is in my bag.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Do you mean the boy in the blue shirt?

W: No. I mean the boy in green. He is not wearing glasses. The woman with long hair is his teacher.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Is your full name Adam Fox?

M: Adam is my brother. I am Sam Fox. Sam is my first name, and Fox is our family name.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Are you and Ella in the same class, Grace?

W: No. Ella is my friend from the swimming club. My cousin Anna is in my class.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: How many kites did you draw on your card, Paul?

M: Six. I also drew three trees and two birds. The kites are above the trees.
