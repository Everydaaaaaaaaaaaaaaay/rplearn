### 答案与逐项依据

1. B。A：打印不以增加重量改善路线；B：与歪线及无名街对照，手机路线更精确，但父亲目的不同；C：家庭手绘本来私用，未涉及隐私发布；D：未比较地图售价，家里打印不是为了更贵

2. A。A：意外听到家史，一时不知道如何回应；B：未购物或付款，非不知道付费方式；C：不是睡眠场景；D：已到桥及墙边，不是不知道如何抵达这里

3. D。A：没要手机隐藏故事，而是常规地图缺家史；B：手机可以按路线导航，follow并非画中个人记忆所增加的信息；C：未说手机损坏地图，非损伤；D：当时屏幕街道图没有这些家族往事细节，不是声称所有手机不能记录故事

4. C。A：工作忙闲不决定旧店主名字是否准确；B：知名程度不是写对店主姓名的条件；C：写人名前核是否确定，父亲还需向兄弟确认；D：他就在旁边走，问距离无须想后找兄弟核实

5. B。A：图不是商品价目，不记录一条街每物售价；B：选择爷爷旧店和家庭生活相关地点，强调家族意义；C：不是只画售票地点，桥/广场亦在图上；D：未述选危险地点，所记是家族记忆

6. D。A：面包不能用来在纸上画鼓图；B：未有票，也不是画图工具；C：父亲要我在纸上画图，不是手机导航，原铅笔已出现；D：父亲先用铅笔加鞋图，现在递给我画鼓

7. B。A：没有手机上看配方的活动；B：手机显示行走距离，与纸上讲停步原因相对；C：下半句纸图提供why，手机导航没有个人停留原因；D：没有天气查询，手机原用于路线

8. C。A：maps waiting for same job不是本段两图替代的关系；B：未有为绘图付钱，maps paying不合；C：起初想以打印图替掉手绘图，认为两者竞做同一工作；D：图不为工作道歉，非比较

9. A。A：找街用手机，找故事也可使用父亲图，承认不同用途；B：出售图不等于读其中故事，未商业活动；C：擦除纸图不能获得故事；D：后文接受两种图，不怪罪个人记忆图

10. A。A：留空角给下次行走，有意保留未完成，不是事故；B：by mistake错误与有意给下次留空相反；C：by chance随机与not by accident相反；D：无人强迫留空，大家主动决定

### 全组回填

Dad drew a map before our family walk. He marked a bridge, a bakery and a little square. His lines were uneven, and he left several streets unnamed. I opened a map on my phone and offered to print something more accurate. He said his drawing would do. I assumed he simply did not like technology.

We reached the bridge easily. Then Dad stopped at a blank place on his map. I looked at my screen and said we should turn left. He was looking at a wall, not a road. "Your grandfather used to mend shoes here," he said. The shop was gone, and someone had painted the wall blue.

For a moment, I did not know how to respond. I had expected directions, not a family story. Dad took out a pencil and added a tiny shoe beside the wall. His map was recording details that the street map on my screen did not provide.

At the bakery, we bought three rolls. Dad told me he used to come there after school, but the owner was different now. I almost wrote down the old owner's name, then asked whether Dad was certain. He thought for a while and said he would ask his brother. We put a question mark beside the shop instead.

As we walked, I noticed Dad was not trying to record every street. He was choosing places with meaning for our family. The missing labels were not all mistakes. Some were spaces for details he still wanted to find.

At the square, Dad asked what I wanted to add. I remembered playing a small drum there with my class. It had seemed too ordinary to mention, but Dad handed me the pencil. I drew a drum beside his shoe and loaf of bread.

We returned home later than I had planned. My phone showed the distance we had walked, but Dad's paper showed why we had stopped. We kept both. The next day, my uncle sent the bakery owner's name, and we replaced the question mark.

I had thought the two maps were competing for the same job. Now I saw they served different purposes. Dad had trusted my screen when we needed a street; I could also use his drawing when I wanted a story. Before putting it away, we left a blank corner for our next walk. It was unfinished by choice, not by accident.
