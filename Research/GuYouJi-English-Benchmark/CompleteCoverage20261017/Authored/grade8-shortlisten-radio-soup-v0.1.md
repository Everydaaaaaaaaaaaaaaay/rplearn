1. What does Lily want to do?
   A. Write newspaper stories.
   B. Read news on the radio.
   C. Teach writing at school.

2. Why was Ben late?
   A. He took the wrong bus.
   B. The bus broke down.
   C. He woke up late.

3. How did Emma go to the pool yesterday?
   A. By bike.
   B. By car.
   C. On foot.

4. How often does Tom practise the piano?
   A. Every day.
   B. Three times a week.
   C. Once a week.

5. Where are the speakers probably?
   A. In a restaurant.
   B. At a bus stop.
   C. In a bookshop.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Will you write stories for a newspaper when you leave school, Lily?

W: I enjoy writing, but I want to read the news on the radio. My uncle works there, and I visited him last week.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Why did you miss the first lesson, Ben? Was the bus late?

M: No. I got on the bus to the sports centre by mistake. I had to get off and walk back.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Did you cycle to the pool yesterday, Emma?

W: Usually I do, but it was raining hard. Dad was using the car, so I went on foot with a big umbrella.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: Do you practise the piano every evening, Tom?

M: No. I practise on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday. On the other days I work on my school newspaper.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Can we sit at that table by the window?

W: Yes. I will bring you the menus. Today we have tomato soup and fresh bread, if you would like a quick lunch.
