# 高二语境词汇：报告与档案

## 学生题面

根据各句首字母与汉语注释完成单词拼写。填写完整单词。

以下各句彼此独立。首字母已印出的空，请在输入框填写完整单词（包括该首字母）。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The report was divided into three c____（章节）, each with a separate heading.

2. The technician compared the clocks to check their a____（准确性）.

3. They moved the meeting to avoid a c____（冲突） with the concert on Friday evening.

4. The archive keeps the o____（原始的） letters, rather than later photocopies.

5. We cannot draw a firm c____（结论） from such a small set of observations.

6. The tent ropes were still t____（绷紧的）, with no slack between the posts.

7. The witness remained s____（沉默的） throughout the interview and said nothing.

8. Keep the r____（收据） as proof that you have paid for the equipment.

9. The frame was r____（矩形的）, with four right angles and two longer sides.

10. In their report last year, the authors openly a____（承认） a limitation of the method.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：chapters

three后章节chapters。

### 2：accuracy

check their accuracy为准确性名词。

### 3：clash / conflict

a clash/conflict with活动日程冲突均成立。

### 4：original

original letters与later photocopies对照。

### 5：conclusion

draw a conclusion固定学术表达。

### 6：tight / taut

tight/taut均描写无松弛的绳子。

### 7：silent

silent与said nothing吻合；未列近义词不直接错。

### 8：receipt

支付证明收据receipt。

### 9：rectangular

rectangular形容矩形。

### 10：acknowledged / admitted / accepted

三个过去及物动词在承认局限语境均可成立；不只接受一个预期词。
