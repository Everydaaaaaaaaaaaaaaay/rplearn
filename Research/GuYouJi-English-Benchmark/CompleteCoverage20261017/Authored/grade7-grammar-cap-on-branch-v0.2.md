# 初一语法：树枝上的帽子

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词；给词空不额外限1词。

Our class (1) ____ (visit) a small park last Thursday. There were many (2) ____ (child) near the lake, but our group went to a quieter place behind the trees. Our teacher carried a bag of drawing paper. Ben thanked (3) ____ (she) for bringing extra pencils because he had left his own at home.

We walked (4) ____ (slow) along the path. One tree was much (5) ____ (big) than the others. We stopped beside it and began drawing. Then a strong wind took Lily's cap into one (6) ____ the branches. She could see it, but she could not reach it. Ben was interested in (7) ____ (take) pictures of the trees, so he still had his camera in his hands. He put it away and went to ask a park worker for help.

The worker looked (8) ____ a long stick in a nearby shed. Lily was the (9) ____ (small) person in our group, so nobody asked her to climb the tree. A few minutes later, the cap was back. We took a group photograph at (10) ____ same place before leaving.

## 作者核对

### 1：visited

last Thursday的当次到访叙述用过去式。

### 2：children

many后复数children。

### 3：her

thanked后人称代词宾格her。

### 4：slowly / slow

修饰walked用slowly。 词典有该词的平形副词用法，当前日常叙事不排除较口语的slow；不把常见ly形式等同唯一合法形式，也不冒称原校键均接受。

### 5：bigger

much...than比较级bigger。

### 6：of

one of the branches表示其中一根树枝。

### 7：taking

be interested in后动名词taking。

### 8：for

look for表示寻找棍子。

### 9：smallest

the...in our group构成组内最高级，smallest。

### 10：the

the same place固定限定。
