1. What helps Ella most?
   A. Watching films without sound.
   B. Writing new words.
   C. Comparing her recording with lesson audio.

2. Whose cup is blue?
   A. The boy's.
   B. The art teacher's.
   C. The girl's.

3. When does the coach leave now?
   A. At 8:10.
   B. At 8:30.
   C. At 8:50.

4. What does the boy advise?
   A. Buy a new bicycle immediately.
   B. Walk everywhere.
   C. Have the bicycle checked at a shop.

5. What does the girl still need?
   A. A new report title.
   B. An old photograph of the bridge.
   C. A bus ticket to the town.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: How do you practise French pronunciation, Ella? Do you repeat lines from films?

W: Sometimes. But recording my own voice and comparing it with the lesson audio helps me most.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: This blue cup was on your desk. Is it yours?

M: No, mine is white. The blue one belongs to our art teacher. I borrowed it to hold some paintbrushes.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: I thought the coach left at half past eight.

W: That was last month's timetable. From this week it leaves twenty minutes earlier.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: My bicycle chain keeps coming off. Shall I buy a new bicycle?

M: You probably only need a small repair. Take it to the shop by the post office first.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Have you chosen a title for your report on our town's old bridge?

W: Yes, but I still need a photograph from before it was repaired. The library may have one in its local history collection.
