On a small harbour island, a retired fisher started teaching visitors to mend nets. Each visitor received two wooden (4) ____ (needle) and a length of coloured cord. The colour made new stitches easier to distinguish from older ones. The aim was not to finish quickly, (7) ____ to make each new section strong enough to hold its shape.

Some sections were imperfect, and the teacher left them that way so visitors could compare different results. Since the festival, several participants (9) ____ (return) for further lessons. The teacher is delighted (10) ____ (welcome) them back. A skill that once seemed useful only on fishing boats has become a way for people of different ages to spend time together, talking while their hands learn a new rhythm.

## 作者核对

### 1：how

notice how...differed承接观察损坏与周边差异的方式。

### 2：practical

a practical demonstration实操展示，形容词practical；名词practice的合成用法待复核而非自动错。

### 3：folded

副词neatly修饰过去分词定语，example为被折叠物。

### 4：needles

two要求复数。

### 5：which

on which引导定语从句，介词后which为宾语。

### 6：checking / having checked

insist on后动名词，普通与完成形式可描述坚持检查/坚持已完成检查；具体未列时体待复核。

### 7：but

not...but...两个任务目标并列。

### 8：had produced

by the time festival began前已完成，过去完成。

### 9：have returned / have been returning

since festival后复数participants用完成/完成进行。

### 10：to welcome / to have welcomed

delighted后普通和完成不定式均可表已发生的迎回。
