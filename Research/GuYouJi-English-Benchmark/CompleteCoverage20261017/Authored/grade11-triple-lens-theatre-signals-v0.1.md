# 高二三篇式：镜头、剧场与信号训练

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，根据短文内容填空。在未给提示词的空白处仅填写1个适当的单词，在给出提示词的空白处用括号内所给词的正确形式填空。

### A

During a visit to a camera repair shop, we were asked to examine several lenses without (1) ____ (touch) the glass. The owner allowed us (2) ____ (record) the different marks we found on their metal cases. One of her rules says that every lens must (3) ____ (be) placed on a soft cloth. A small grain of sand on a hard table could damage it before the repair even began.

### B

A local theatre uses a noticeboard not only to announce performances, (4) ____ also to explain work that audiences rarely see. Several (5) ____ (photograph) show a costume being altered between two productions. Beside them is a note from the tailor, (6) ____ name appears on every finished piece. She describes how a seam can be moved without cutting away material, allowing the costume to be changed again for a different actor.

### C

Trainees at a small rowing club are interested (7) ____ learning signals they can recognise from a distance. The instructor wants a (8) ____ (rely) method that works when voices cannot be heard over the wind. Each movement is first practised beside the boats, which remain securely (9) ____ (tie) to the landing stage. The instructor has (10) ____ (teach) this sequence for years, but she still asks every new group to explain each signal before they leave the shore.

## 作者核对

### 1：touching / having touched

without后普通和完成动名词均能表未发生接触的条件；未列形式待复核。

### 2：to record

allow somebody to do，宾语后用to不定式。

### 3：be

情态must之后be用原形。

### 4：but

not only...but also成对连接目的。

### 5：photographs

several要求复数名词。

### 6：whose

whose name表裁缝的姓名，非限制性从句中的所属限定。

### 7：in

be interested in learning固定搭配。

### 8：reliable

method需形容词reliable，表示风中可靠的方法。

### 9：tied

remain系动词后描述船被拴牢的状态，tied为分词。

### 10：taught

has后过去分词taught。
