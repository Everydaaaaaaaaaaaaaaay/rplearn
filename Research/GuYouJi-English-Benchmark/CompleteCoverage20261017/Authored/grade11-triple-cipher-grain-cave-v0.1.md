# 高二三篇式：密码卡、粮食格与洞穴图

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，根据短文内容填空。在未给提示词的空白处仅填写1个适当的单词，在给出提示词的空白处用括号内所给词的正确形式填空。

### A

(1) ____ (write) by a pupil for a school puzzle trail, the instruction card used circles and triangles instead of ordinary letters. To avoid (2) ____ (confuse) two similar symbols, each team received a larger copy of the key. Anyone could (3) ____ (check) a symbol without asking for help. The challenge lay in finding the next location, not in guessing what a blurred mark was supposed to mean.

### B

The new storage trays hold more grain samples (4) ____ the old ones did, although they occupy the same shelf space. The samples are divided into several (5) ____ (category) for a classroom comparison. Each tray has a lid, (6) ____ prevents grains from spilling when it is carried. The teacher keeps empty labelled envelopes beside the trays so that small portions can be taken out for closer inspection without mixing the remaining samples.

### C

A visitor can learn a great deal (7) ____ a cave map without going underground. Our guide explained the marked routes as (8) ____ (careful) as she could before showing us a cross-section. On this second drawing, the cave was (9) ____ (divide) into levels according to height, rather than distance from the entrance. I had always (10) ____ (know) that maps left things out, but these two drawings made the reason clear: each one answered a different question.

## 作者核对

### 1：Written / Having been written

句首被动分词可用普通或完成被动形式，卡片被学生写成先于使用。

### 2：confusing

avoid后动名词confusing；不把不常见完成式直接判错，交待未列待复核。

### 3：check

could之后动词原形。

### 4：than

more...than作同一维度数量比较。

### 5：categories

several要求category复数categories。

### 6：which

逗号后非限制性从句缺主语，指lid，用which。

### 7：from

learn a great deal from a map，from标知识来源；未列1词表达继续复核。

### 8：carefully

as...as中的正级副词修饰explained。

### 9：divided

was之后被动过去分词，cave为划分对象。

### 10：known

had后过去分词known。

已列答案变体同等接受；去首尾空白、合并内部空白、忽略大小写，不按词根匹配。无提示空超过1词违反本组规则；给词空不另限1词。未列合法表达待复核，不据此自动判错；已列形式不证明答案穷尽。三篇须完整交付，不截取为三道小题。情境均为虚构，来源仅参照形式。旧暂停稿继续保留暂停。
