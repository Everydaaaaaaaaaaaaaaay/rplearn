N: Evan is the leader of our weekend activity camp. He is one point eight two metres tall. There is a picture of a cat on his name badge because cats are his favourite animals. He wears a red jacket during camp activities. At home on River Street, he keeps the camp equipment in a small shed. His home is quite far from the camp. Sometimes a colleague gives him a lift in a car, and sometimes he takes the bus. He does not cycle to the camp, because there is no safe cycle path on that route. Evan will meet our group at the main gate at nine on Saturday.
