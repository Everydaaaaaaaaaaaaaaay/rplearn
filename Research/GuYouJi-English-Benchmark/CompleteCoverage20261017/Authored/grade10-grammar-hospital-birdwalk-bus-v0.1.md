# 高一：医院、观鸟与公交

## 学生题面

阅读下列三个短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

### A

We visited a small hospital (1) ____ stands near the river. The entrance has two glass (2) ____ (door). One is (3) ____ (heavy) than the other, so a notice asks visitors to use the lighter one. My brother read the notice as (4) ____ (quiet) as possible while a nurse was speaking to a patient nearby.

### B

On our first bird walk, the guide carried (5) ____ (little) equipment than I expected. She asked us (6) ____ keep our phones in our bags and listen carefully. At first I was not familiar (7) ____ the sounds. After an hour, I could tell the calls of two birds apart without looking at them.

### C

On the way home, I noticed an elderly passenger (8) ____ (stand) near the bus door. He was the neighbour (9) ____ garden faces our kitchen window. I offered him my seat, and he smiled. Later he thanked me for (10) ____ (give) it up. We talked about the flowers in his garden until he reached his stop.

## 作者核对

### 1：which / that

限定从句指hospital并缺主语。

### 2：doors

two要求doors复数。

### 3：heavier

than the other明确比较。

### 4：quietly

as...as结构中修饰read，正级副词quietly。

### 5：less

equipment不可数，little比较级less。

### 6：to

asked us to keep不定式。

### 7：with

familiar with the sounds表示熟悉这些声音。

### 8：standing / who was standing / that was standing

notice后的宾语补足语可为standing；限定定语who/that was standing也构成完整名词短语，三者均合语境。

### 9：whose

whose garden表邻居与花园的所属关系。

### 10：giving / having given

for后的动名词及完成式均可，it up完整保留。
