### 一种完整修订

Our cycling club decided to keep a repair record for each bicycle that members lent to new riders. I designed the first page, but I forgot to include a space for the date of each repair. Two members tested the page by recording the work they had done on an old bicycle. They carefully compared the boxes with the length of their notes. The new writing area was wider than the old one, so longer notes could fit on the page. I then showed the page to the mechanic who fixed our club's bicycles, and she approved the change. She told us that every record should describe both the problem and the work carried out. During the next meeting, each rider was given a page, but one of the pages had two missing sections. We corrected the printing error before putting the pages into separate folders. Members have frequently checked the folders since then, so we know the extra space is useful.
