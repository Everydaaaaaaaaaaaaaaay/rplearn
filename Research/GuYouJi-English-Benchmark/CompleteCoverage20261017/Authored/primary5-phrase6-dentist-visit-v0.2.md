### 全组回填

Ben has a toothache this morning. His back tooth hurts when he tries to eat. Dad takes him to a dentist (牙医). The clinic is next to a flower shop; the two shops share a wall. Ben takes a bus to the clinic because it is too far to walk. Inside, the dentist asks to see his teeth. Ben opens his mouth, and she looks carefully. She helps him and gives him a new toothbrush (牙刷). After dinner that evening, Ben brushes his teeth with the new toothbrush. He wants to take better care of them.
