# 小六阅读：风中的种子袋

## 学生题面

阅读完整故事，完成全部五题，每题从A—D中选一个最佳答案。第4题两个空要选同时符合两项事实的一组答案，仍只选一个字母。

On the first Saturday of each month, Willow Street held a seed swap in the schoolyard. Nora usually brought beans because they were easy for her to pack. That morning she put six little bean bags in a wooden box. Her brother Max carried empty pots.

It was windy but dry. The ground was firm, and the trees kept shaking. Just as Nora was about to label her first pot, a gust blew an empty paper bag under the table. Max caught it before it reached the gate.

Nora's aunt took some labels from a green tin. Max found tape on the bottom shelf of the tool cupboard. They fixed a label to every pot and put a small stone on each empty bag.

The simple change worked. Before going home, they agreed to bring cloth bags to the next swap so the wind would not carry them away.

1. Nora usually brings ____ to the seed swap.
   A. peas
   B. flower seeds
   C. beans
   D. radishes

2. What was the weather like during the swap?
   A. Windy but dry.
   B. Rain kept falling.
   C. There was no wind at all.
   D. Snow covered the yard.

3. ____ just as Nora was about to label her first pot.
   A. Nora put the bean bags in the wooden box.
   B. Max arrived carrying empty pots.
   C. Everyone went home.
   D. An empty bag blew under the table.

4. ____ took labels from a green tin, and Max found the tape in ____.
   A. Nora / the tool cupboard
   B. Nora's aunt / the tool cupboard
   C. Nora's aunt / the wooden box
   D. Max / the green tin

5. What do Nora and Max plan to bring to the next swap?
   A. Cloth bags.
   B. More empty paper bags.
   C. A new gate.
   D. A box of matches.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

A：文中没有说她通常带豌豆，通常物品明列为beans。；B：通常带的是beans，不是flower seeds。；C：首段明说Nora usually brought beans。；D：通常带的是beans，未列radishes。

### 2：A

A：第二段直接说明windy but dry，并写树摇动。；B：dry与一直下雨不合。；C：windy及trees kept shaking排除完全无风。；D：文中未描述积雪，实际给出的天气是有风而干燥。

### 3：D

A：装好豆袋发生在首段准备阶段，非Just as所接事件。；B：Max提空盆是首段到场信息，非贴标签一刻。；C：回家在最后一段，时间更晚。；D：第二段Just as将贴标签前一刻与纸袋被吹到桌下明确连接。

### 4：B

A：胶带地点正确，但取标签的人是aunt而非Nora。；B：第三段两条事实分别为aunt取标签、Max从工具柜底层找胶带，两部分都对。；C：取标签的人对，胶带来自工具柜，非木箱。；D：两条事实都错：取标签为aunt，胶带在工具柜。

### 5：A

A：末段明确agreed to bring cloth bags，目的是不被风带走。；B：空纸袋是本次遇到的问题，没有增加空纸袋的计划。；C：gate只作纸袋差点吹到的地点，没有买新门计划。；D：全文无火柴或带火柴计划。
