## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The old photographs look good on the display board, but visitors keep asking when they were taken.

M: We know the year of most of them. For the two street scenes, we have only a likely decade.

W: Then let us say that. An uncertain date is better than a confident wrong one.

M: Should we remove those two pictures?

W: No, they still show how the street changed. Add a question mark to the date and explain why we are unsure.

M: I can put the explanation on a small card below them.

W: Good. And keep the original prints in their envelopes. Only the copies should go on the board, where light and fingers may damage them.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Can I borrow the club projector for Saturday afternoon?

W: It is available, but the small room has no screen. You would need the portable one from the cupboard.

M: I can carry it. I will return both items on Monday morning.

W: Please return the projector by Sunday evening instead. Another group is testing it first thing on Monday. The screen can wait until Monday.

M: All right. Do I leave the projector at the main office?

W: The office is closed on Sundays. Give it to the caretaker at the side entrance and ask him to mark it as returned.

M: I will put a reminder on my phone so I do not forget the earlier deadline.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: Our new seed library opens next week. Unlike a shop, it lets neighbours take small amounts of seed without paying. We hope people will later return seed from healthy plants, but a return is an invitation, not a condition of borrowing. If nothing grows, please tell us what happened instead of feeling that you owe us a packet.

Every envelope has a label with the plant name, the year the seed was collected and the space the plant needs. Keep that label with your pot until you are sure which plant it is. We cannot identify every young plant from a photograph.

The library will open on Wednesday afternoons in the community room. On the first day, volunteers will explain how the labels work. They will not give advice about eating wild plants. If you are unsure whether a plant is safe to eat, do not use this library as your source of advice. Our purpose is to share garden seed and records of growing it.
