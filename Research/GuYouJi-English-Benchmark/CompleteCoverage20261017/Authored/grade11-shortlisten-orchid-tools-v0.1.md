1. What will the woman do on Saturday?
   A. Watch a tennis match.
   B. Visit a flower show.
   C. Enter a gardening competition.

2. Why did the man miss the meeting?
   A. He was busy with a repair.
   B. He did not receive the message.
   C. He could not find the cafe.

3. What is the woman's opinion of the book?
   A. It is amusing.
   B. It is inspiring throughout.
   C. It is dull.

4. What are the speakers discussing?
   A. Choosing a bank account.
   B. Avoiding an online scam.
   C. Recovering a lost phone.

5. What is the man's attitude towards cycling to work?
   A. Interested but cautious.
   B. Completely opposed in all circumstances.
   C. Certain he will start tomorrow.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Are you still entering the tennis match on Saturday?

W: I have withdrawn. The match clashes with the orchid show, and I promised my grandmother I would take her to see it.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: We waited for you at the cafe. Did you forget our message?

M: I saw it, but I was repairing a chair and thought I could finish one last joint first. By the time I looked up, everyone had left.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: What did you think of the biography?

W: The subject led an extraordinary life, but the writer managed to make every chapter feel like a list of dates. I kept putting it down.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: This message looks like it is from the bank, but it asks for my password.

M: Do not reply. Open the bank's app yourself and check there, rather than following the link in the message.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: Cycling to work would save money, but that road has no cycle lane.

W: So are you going to try it?

M: Only if I can find a quieter route. I am not ready to share that narrow road with the morning traffic.
