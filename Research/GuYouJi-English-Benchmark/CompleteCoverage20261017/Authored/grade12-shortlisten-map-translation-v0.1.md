### 示例（已解，不计入五题）

Which platform will the train leave from?

A. Platform two.

B. Platform three.

C. Platform four.

答案：C。A：旧显示已被广播更改；B：未提三站台；C：最新广播为四站台

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: The tour was supposed to leave at a quarter to ten.

M: It has been delayed by half an hour. The guide is waiting for a replacement microphone.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: I can follow the footpaths on this map, but the river seems to cross a road twice.

W: The blue line is an old canal, now mostly dry. I should have made that clearer in the key before sending the design to the printer.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Your annual membership is ninety pounds. With the student discount, you pay two thirds of that.

M: Here is my student card. I would like to join today.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I thought the translation would be quick, but one paragraph took nearly an hour.

W: Was the vocabulary difficult?

M: The words were ordinary. It was a joke that depended on how two words sounded, and a literal translation made no sense.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: Will you publish the survey results as soon as the last form arrives?

M: I would rather check why so many answers came from one class first. The numbers may look precise without representing the whole school.

### 示例完整原创脚本

M: The board still says platform two.

W: There has just been an announcement. Our train now leaves from platform four.
