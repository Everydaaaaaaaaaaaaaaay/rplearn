## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: You left your bus card on the kitchen table again, Leo. I found it after you went to school.

M: Thanks, Mum. I borrowed some money from Max for the bus home.

W: Put the card in the front pocket of your bag now, before you forget.

M: I will. Can I help with dinner first?

W: The rice is cooking. Please wash the carrots, and then you can get your bag.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Are you free on Saturday, Jo? My cousin is showing us how to make shadow puppets at the arts room.

W: That sounds interesting. I thought they were made of wood.

M: Some are, but we will use thick paper. There will be scissors and sticks there.

W: What time does it start?

M: At two. We should arrive fifteen minutes early to choose a design.

W: All right. I will bring my little brother. He loves making things.
