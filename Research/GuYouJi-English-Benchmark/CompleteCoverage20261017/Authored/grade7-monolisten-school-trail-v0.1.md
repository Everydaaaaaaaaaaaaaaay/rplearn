### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Good morning. I am Mr Green, your English teacher. Today our class will do a school trail. You will work in groups of four. Each group will get a map and a yellow card. Start at the library, then follow the map to five places around the school. Read the English sign at each place and write one letter on your card. The five letters will make a word. Do not run, and please stay with your group. If it rains, come back to Room Nine. We have an indoor trail ready there. The activity ends at eleven. Give your card to me at the classroom door.
