## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: Have you finished the subtitles for our class film, Priya?

W: Almost. I checked the spelling yesterday. Today I need to shorten a few lines because they disappear before people can read them.

M: Should we slow the film down?

W: No, the pictures are fine. We can say the same thing in fewer words.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: My brother wants me to join his running club, but I would rather go swimming.

M: Have you told him?

W: Not yet. He bought me running shoes, so I feel I should agree.

M: Thank him for the shoes, but explain what you enjoy. You can still go for an occasional run together.

W: That is a good way to put it. I will talk to him tonight.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

N: Welcome to the seed library in West Hall. Unlike a normal library, we lend packets of seeds rather than books. Each packet has a picture and planting instructions. You may take up to three packets on your first visit. At the end of the growing season, bring back dry seeds from your own plants if you can. Please do not return fresh fruit or wet seeds, as they may spoil in storage. Our volunteers sort new donations on Tuesdays. On Thursday evenings they run a short class for beginners. All the classes are free, but please book a place because the room is small.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: I have to interview someone about an unusual job. Would your aunt be willing?

W: Possibly. She repairs clocks, but she is busy in her shop this week.

M: I could send my questions by email.

W: She prefers talking. Why not phone her on Sunday, when the shop is closed?

M: All right. I want to ask how she finds replacement parts.

W: Ask about her training too. She first studied jewellery, then learned clock repair from her grandfather.

M: I did not know that. May I record the call?

W: Ask her permission first. I will give you her number after I check that she is happy to be interviewed.
