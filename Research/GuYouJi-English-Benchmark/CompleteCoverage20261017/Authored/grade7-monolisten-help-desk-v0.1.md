### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: Hello, everyone. I am Rosa. This Saturday is our school sports day. My friends and I will work at a help desk near the main gate. Please come there if you lose something. We will put lost clothes in a blue box and other things in a green box. Write your name and class on a card before you take anything home. The desk opens at eight in the morning and closes at four in the afternoon. You do not need to pay. If you find a watch or a phone, please give it to our teacher, Mr Lee. He will keep these things in his office.
