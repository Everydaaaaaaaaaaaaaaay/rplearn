1. What did the woman do with the jacket?
   A. She bought it in a sale.
   B. She gave it to her father.
   C. She had an old one altered.

2. What will the man probably change?
   A. The date in the caption.
   B. The name of the photographer.
   C. The photograph's colour.

3. What does the woman ask the man to do now?
   A. Load the car.
   B. Check the box lids.
   C. Telephone the buyer.

4. Why did the man refuse the role?
   A. He disliked the script.
   B. He lacked acting experience.
   C. He had another regular commitment.

5. How does the woman feel about the proposal now?
   A. More positive, with one concern remaining.
   B. Entirely satisfied.
   C. Less positive than before.

## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: That jacket looks expensive. Was it in the sale?

W: No, it was my father's. I had the lining replaced and the sleeves shortened. It cost less than buying a new one.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: The caption says this is the 1928 flood. Are you sure?

M: The photographer's notebook dates it to 1931. I trusted the label on the envelope at first, but the notebook is clearer evidence.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: Do you want me to carry the boxes to the car?

W: Leave them here for now. The buyer has asked to collect them tomorrow instead. Could you check that the lids are secure?

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: I cannot believe you turned down the lead role.

M: The play is excellent, but rehearsals would take every evening. I have already committed to helping at the youth centre twice a week.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

M: You sounded unconvinced by the proposal at first.

W: I was. But seeing a working model answered most of my doubts. I would like to see the cost figures before I give it my full support.
