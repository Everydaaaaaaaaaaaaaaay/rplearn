### 答案与逐项依据

1. A。A：未先核路径而一律向右，后文发现方向错；B：没有先封路的要求，问题在未核方向；C：未要求改建路径，画路牌前应检查

2. A。A：Fox嫌走一遍慢，认为浪费时间；B：当时看不起核查，不视为礼物；C：散步核路不是允诺的含义

3. B。A：访客正迷路，不是已经能解释位置；B：有人到关门处，大家不知游戏在哪；C：游戏位置已定，访客不是决定设在哪

4. B。A：牌不是个人邀请函，发现的是错误方向；B：错方向牌直接说明作者制牌有误；C：将人引离游戏不是成功

5. B。A：核方向不是隐藏路径；B：每个转弯现场核访客需往哪走；C：正要查明并记住方向，不是忘记

6. C。A：水不能作为画箭头的固体板面；B：纸盒侧面提供的是板面，不是一片绳子；C：空箱的大块纸板可画牌，需屋檐下防湿

7. A。A：仍重速度，然而先查路，表保留偏好与改变流程；B：先查路不是喜欢快速做事的原因；C：核路是已执行安排，不是喜欢快的否定条件

8. C。A：指相反方向会再误导，与成功找到矛盾；B：各转角所需不同，指同一方向重犯错误；C：right way表示正确方向，与前面统一向右却出错形成对照

9. C。A：两人一起收牌，不是避开对方；B：Fox已认自己错且对方帮忙，非继续怪对方；C：Tortoise不嘲笑并帮助核路，事后表达感谢

10. A。A：盒上记提醒，为下一次记住先查后画；B：写提醒不是拒绝经验；C：已有教训不是下次再凭空发明

### 全组回填

In a village of talking animals, Fox was proud of making things quickly. When Rabbit asked for signs for the village fair, Fox offered to finish them before lunch. He painted every arrow pointing right. "People will know where to go," he said, without checking the paths.

Tortoise suggested walking around the fair first. Fox thought that would be a waste of time. The signs were already dry, so he carried them outside.

The first visitor followed an arrow and reached the cake stall. But another followed the next arrow straight towards a closed gate. Soon everyone was wondering where the games were.

Fox blamed the visitors. "They should read more carefully!" Tortoise quietly placed a sign beside him. Its arrow pointed away from the games. Fox finally noticed his mistake.

"I made each sign the same because it was faster," he admitted. Tortoise did not laugh. She asked him to walk from the entrance with her. At every turn, they stopped to check which direction a visitor would need.

They could reuse most of the boards by turning them round. One new sign was needed, but there was no wood left. Rabbit took an empty paper box and cut a large piece of cardboard from its side. Fox painted on it and kept it under the entrance roof so it would stay dry.

Fox put the corrected signs in place. This time, visitors found the games without asking. Fox still liked working quickly, but he checked the route first the next year. He had learned that a clear arrow was useful only when it pointed the right way.

Tortoise helped collect the signs afterwards. Fox thanked her and wrote a small note on the storage box: "Check before you paint." He wanted to remember the lesson when another fair came.
