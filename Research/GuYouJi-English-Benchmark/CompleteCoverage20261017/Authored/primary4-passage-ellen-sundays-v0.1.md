## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

N: I am Ellen, and I am eight years old. I live in a village near the mountains. I like fish best, especially when my grandfather cooks it. On Sundays, I often help my mother in our vegetable garden. I carry water for the plants. This winter holiday, I will visit my aunt in another town. She will teach me to make a kite. We will fly it together on a windy day.
