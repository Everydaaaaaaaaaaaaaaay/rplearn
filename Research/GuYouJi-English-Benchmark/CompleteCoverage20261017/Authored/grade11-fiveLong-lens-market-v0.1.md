## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Your camera photographs have a dark spot in the same place. Is the lens scratched?

M: I thought that too, but the spot disappeared after I cleaned the filter in front of it. The lens itself is fine.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: The market stall fee is thirty pounds for the day. If we share a stall, we can split that.

W: I would like to, but my prints need a flat table. Your hanging plants could drip on them.

M: I can put the plants on the ground beside the table, on a waterproof tray.

W: That might work. Let us ask the organiser whether we may use the space beside it.

M: I will call her this afternoon before we pay.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Have you finished editing the walking-tour recording?

M: Almost. I removed the traffic noise between stops, but left some footsteps in.

W: Why keep those?

M: They help listeners sense the movement through the town. If every pause is completely silent, the recording feels like separate lectures.

W: That makes sense. Did you keep the bell at the old crossing?

M: Yes, but made it quieter so it would not cover the guide's voice.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: Our school wants to compare energy use before and after the new windows were fitted. I have the monthly bills.

W: Be careful. Last winter was much colder than this one. A lower bill would not by itself prove the windows caused the change.

M: So I should compare the outdoor temperatures too?

W: Yes, and the number of days the building was used. It was closed for an extra week this year.

M: I can get temperature records from the local station. The caretaker has the opening dates.

W: Good. Also compare energy units, not just money. The price per unit changed in January.

M: Then the report should describe the limits rather than promise an exact saving.

W: Exactly. You can still show the evidence and explain what remains uncertain.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: The community language exchange will try a new arrangement next month. Previously, everyone joined one large conversation group. Confident speakers enjoyed it, but several beginners said they rarely had a chance to speak. We will now use pairs for the first twenty minutes, before returning to a larger group. Each pair will receive two simple topic cards. They are prompts, not a test, and you may change the topic if you wish. Please arrive ten minutes before the session starts so we can make the pairs. The exchange remains free. If you can help translate the welcome notice into another language, leave your name with the organiser. We are asking for volunteers for that task, not for paid lessons or formal examinations.
