### 错误1

### 错误3

### 错误4

### 错误5

### 错误9

### 一种完整修订

During the spring term, I helped a youth music group prepare its instrument cases for a weekend trip. The group had three large cases, but none of the labels showed which instrument belonged inside. The organiser asked me to make a new list while the players finished their rehearsal. I measured each case carefully and copied its number onto a paper tag. One player pointed out that I had written the same number on two tags. I corrected the list, and the organiser thanked the volunteers who stayed late to check the cases. We were tired, but everyone looked forward to hearing the group perform the next day. Before closing the cupboard, we checked that each small drum was protected by a soft cloth and that no strap was missing. The organiser gave us some useful advice, and we were very proud of our careful work. She also reminded us that we should check the labels again when the cases came back.
