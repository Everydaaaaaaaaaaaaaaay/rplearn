## 作者核对

### 完整原创对话脚本

T: I like these model boats.

N: Me too. What do you like about them, Tom?

T: The small painted windows. Liam, do you like them?

L: Yes. I want to make one myself.

N: Look at these paper kites. I do not like them very much. The strings fall off too easily.

T: I do like them. The children at this school made all the kites.

L: Can we look at the boats first, then come back here?

N: Sure. The boats are by the big window.
