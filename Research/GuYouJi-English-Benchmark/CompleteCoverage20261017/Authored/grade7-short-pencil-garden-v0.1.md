## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

W: Is your birthday in March, Leo?

M: Mine is in May. My brother’s birthday is in March.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

M: Is the woman in the red coat your mother?

W: No, she is my aunt. My mother is wearing a green coat.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

W: Do you usually read books after school, Dan?

M: I read before bed. After school, I usually water the plants in our garden.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

M: How much is this pencil, please?

W: Two yuan. The ruler beside it is three yuan.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

W: What is your favourite lesson, Tim?

M: I like art best. I like music too, but I find maths difficult.
