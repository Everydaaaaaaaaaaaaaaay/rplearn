## 作者核对

### 材料1完整原创脚本

M: The garden talk was advertised for the hall. Why are we meeting in the greenhouse?

W: The hall is available, but the speaker wants to show the plants as she discusses them. Pictures would not let us compare the leaves ourselves.

M: Will everyone be able to hear her?

W: She has asked us to split into two small groups. One group will listen while the other examines the labelled trays, then we will swap.

### 材料2完整原创脚本

W: Your room sounds different today. Have you bought a new speaker?

M: No. I hung those thick curtains to reduce the echo. I am recording a spoken history project, so clear speech matters more than powerful music.

W: The curtains seem to help. How will you know how much difference they make?

M: I recorded the same passage from the same chair before hanging them. I will compare it with a new recording using the same microphone settings.

W: Then do not move the rug until you have finished. Otherwise you will have changed two things at once.

M: Good point. I will leave everything else where it was.

### 材料3完整原创脚本

M: I have ordered fifty copies of the walking guide. If people like them, we can print more.

W: Are the opening times final? The mill closes on Mondays now, and your file still says it opens every day.

M: I checked the tourism page last month.

W: The mill's own notice was updated yesterday. We should use that, and put a checked-on date in the guide.

M: The printer has not started yet. I will stop the order and change the file.

W: Ask them to send a digital proof after the change. We can check that version rather than assume the correction carried through.

M: And I will put the guide online so we can update it between printings.

### 材料4完整原创脚本

W: You looked disappointed when the robot stopped at the edge of the mat.

M: It completed the route yesterday. Today I thought it had forgotten the program.

W: Was anything else different?

M: The mat was closer to the window. The code was unchanged.

W: Then we should test the light before rewriting the code. The sensor might respond differently in direct sun.

M: Should we cover the window and run the same route?

W: Yes, then uncover it and repeat the run. Record each result, including failures. One successful run will not settle the question.

M: If the pattern supports the light explanation, could we add a cover over the sensor?

W: That might help, but test that separately. A cover may also block what the sensor is meant to detect.

M: So we need to find a balance rather than just make the sensor darker.

### 材料5完整原创脚本

N: Our community repair afternoon is moving to the library next month. This is a change of venue, not a change in what volunteers can promise. They will examine an item and explain what they can attempt. They cannot guarantee that every item will work again, and some repairs require equipment that we do not have.

Please bring any removable parts and the instruction booklet if you still have it. A clear description of the fault is often more useful than saying that an item is simply broken. Tell the volunteer what happened just before it stopped working and whether the problem occurs every time.

There will be a demonstration table where visitors can watch a volunteer work on a practice item. You may ask questions there, but do not copy a procedure on an electrical item at home merely because it looked simple. The demonstration is meant to show how a problem is investigated, not to certify visitors to carry out repairs. We hope people leave with a better understanding of their belongings, whether or not a repair is possible that day.
