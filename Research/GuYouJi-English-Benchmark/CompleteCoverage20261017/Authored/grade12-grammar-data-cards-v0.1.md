# 高三：被贴错标签的数据卡

## 学生题面

阅读短文，在空白处填入1个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。无提示空限1词，给词空不另加1词限制。

At (1) ____ end of a classroom investigation, our group had two competing (2) ____ (hypothesis). We were using fictional data cards to practise evaluating an argument, rather than conducting a real experiment. Each card described a plant, a growing condition and a final height. The data (3) ____ (collect) on the cards had been invented by our teacher, and no one was expected to draw conclusions about real plants.

One student noticed that a card had been placed in the wrong group. If we had discarded it instead of correcting its label, our chart would have (4) ____ (look) much neater at yesterday's meeting. That appearance, however, would have depended on removing an inconvenient observation. We checked all the cards, none of (5) ____ could be traced to an actual plant, and corrected the grouping before discussing the two explanations.

One explanation seemed more (6) ____ (convince) than the other, but we had to say why. The question was whether it accounted for every card (7) ____ merely fitted the cards we preferred. Keeping a numbered list prevented the (8) ____ (lose) of any card during the discussion. We also made a (9) ____ (care) distinction between a description of the pattern and a claim about its cause. In our final report, we avoided (10) ____ (present) the exercise as scientific evidence. Its value lay in revealing how a small decision about a label could change an apparently persuasive argument.

## 作者核对

### 1：the

at the end of固定结构，特指本次活动的结束。

### 2：hypotheses

two competing后需hypothesis的不规则复数hypotheses。

### 3：collected

data与collect为被动关系，collected作后置分词定语；正文明确数据为虚构，不提升为真实实验事实。

### 4：looked

would have在空前已经给出，空内只填过去分词looked，完整回填would have looked。

### 5：which

逗号后的none of which引导非限制性从句，cards为物。

### 6：convincing

more已给；convincing描述解释有说服力，convinced描述被说服的人，不合explanation。

### 7：or

whether X or Y的两种可能：覆盖全部卡片，还是只符合偏爱的卡片。

### 8：loss

the...of any card中需名词loss，指卡片遗失。

### 9：careful

distinction前形容词careful，全文反复核查支持仔细区分；其他未核词形保留待复核。

### 10：presenting

avoid后接动名词；本句避免把练习呈现为科学证据，用presenting。
