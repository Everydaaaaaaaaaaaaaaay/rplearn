# 初二：陶艺馆日记

## 学生题面

从A—E中选择最合适的选项补全完整文章，有一个多余选项。保留题面已有标点；不另加每项仅一次限制。

### August 8

Today my cousin and I visited a pottery workshop. We arrived early, before the other visitors. (1) ____ The teacher opened it at ten and invited everyone in.

First, she gave each of us a piece of soft clay. I wanted to make a tall cup, but its sides kept falling down. (2) ____ This time I made a small bowl with a wide bottom. It stayed in shape.

Before lunch, we put our names under the bowls. We could not take them home that day because they still needed to be fired. (3) ____ I wrote the date in my diary so that I would not forget.

In the afternoon, we looked at finished pottery in the shop. I liked a blue plate, but I decided not to buy it. (4) ____ I wanted to leave enough money for the bus home. I was happy to have made something of my own, even though I would have to wait to collect it.

E. The lesson had ended before we left home.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

B对应arrived early和下句ten opened，未开门→开门连贯；A制作需进门后，C价格在末段，D返取日期未有制品，E已结束与之后上课反。

### 2：A

A从高杯塌转新泥/简单形状，下文This time smallbowl回扣；B门、C价、D取件、E课已结束均不解释再做小碗。

### 3：D

D给出followingSaturday取件，下句wrote date回扣；A/B/C/E均不提供未来领取日期，E反已经还需烧制的时间。

### 4：C

C解释不买蓝盘并保留车费，cost购物余额对应；A泥/B门/D领取时间/E课已结束均不解释购物预算。
