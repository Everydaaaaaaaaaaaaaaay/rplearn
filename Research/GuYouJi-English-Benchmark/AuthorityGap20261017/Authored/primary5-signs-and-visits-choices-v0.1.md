# 小五：标志与来访五情境

## 学生题面

读五个独立情境，每题选一个最佳答案。第2、3题各有两个空，每题仍只选一个组合选项，共五次回应。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. The room is dark. Please ____ the light so that we can read.
   A. turn off
   B. put away
   C. turn on

2. The train leaves at 10:20 and arrives at 11:00 on the same morning. It leaves ____ ten twenty and takes ____ minutes.
   A. at, forty
   B. in, forty
   C. at, eighty

3. I am Ben. This cap belongs to me. My brother has a different cap. This one is ____, not ____.
   A. my, his
   B. mine, him
   C. mine, his

4. Tomorrow is our class photo day. We ____ ready at nine tomorrow morning.
   A. were
   B. will be
   C. will have

5. —I cannot carry this big box. Can you help me, please?
—____.
   A. Of course. Let me carry it with you
   B. I am twelve years old
   C. It is under the desk

## 作者核对

### 1：C

为了在暗房读书需开灯，turn on为开；turn off关灯方向相反；put away收存不等于开启灯。

### 2：A

钟点用at；同日上午10:20至11:00为40分钟。B介词错；C计算错。两处条件共同决定一次选择，不拆为两回应。

### 3：C

Ben陈述帽子属于自己，独立表语mine；兄弟的独立所属his。A my缺名词；B him为宾格不表示所属。两空一响应且所有权明示。

### 4：B

明示明天九点，将来形容词表语用will be ready；were为过去，will have后不能直接以ready补成此谓语。

### 5：A

A明确同意并协助搬箱；B报年龄，C报位置都不回应求助。未借未用选项排除。
