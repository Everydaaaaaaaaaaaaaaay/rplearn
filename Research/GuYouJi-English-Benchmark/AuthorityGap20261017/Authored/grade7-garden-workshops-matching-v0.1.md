# 初一：四张课后活动卡

## 学生题面

读四张完整活动卡，为三名同学各选最符合完整需求的一项，有一项不用；不另加每项仅一次限制。

### A · Seed Pictures

Make a picture using dry seeds and glue. You will arrange shapes on card and take home a finished picture. We will not plant or cook the seeds.

Time: 15:30, April 8 · Place: Art Room

### B · Growing Notes

Plant a bean in a small pot and learn to keep a record of its changes. You can bring the pot home and add a short note each week.

Time: 15:30, April 9 · Place: Greenhouse

### C · Recipe Readers

Read a short English recipe with a teacher. Work in pairs to explain the steps aloud. We will talk about cooking but will not prepare food.

Time: 15:30, April 10 · Place: Room 204

### D · Quiet Drawing

Look at a bowl of fruit and draw it with pencils. The fruit will stay on the table; this is an art activity, not a cooking lesson.

Time: 15:30, April 11 · Place: Art Room
