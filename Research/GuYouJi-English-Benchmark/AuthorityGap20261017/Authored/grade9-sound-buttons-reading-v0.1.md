# 初三：发声按钮的提示

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，按各题所问范围选择一个最佳答案，保留段落划分及画线词。

At the fictional North Street Library, a group of pupils wanted to help visitors find different rooms. They recorded short messages for buttons beside the entrance. A visitor could press a button to hear directions. However, during a trial, some visitors pressed several buttons together. The messages then played at the same time, making all of them hard to understand.

The pupils first made the recordings louder. This did not separate the messages; it only made the mixture louder. Their next design allowed only one message to play at a time. When one recording was playing, the other buttons waited until <u>it</u> had finished. Visitors could also stop a message by pressing the same button again.

This solved one problem, but the pupils found another. The direction “turn left at the big painting” was clear only while that painting remained in its place. When the library moved the painting for an exhibition, the message became misleading. The pupils changed it to “turn left after the first doorway”, which referred to a fixed feature of the building.

Before adding more buttons, they asked visitors to try the new version. They also wrote the directions on a board for people who preferred to read. The pupils did not describe the buttons as a complete answer to every visitor's needs. They had made one route easier to follow and planned to listen for further problems.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

首段several buttons together导致messages同放，A因果；B距离未述，C录的是directions非音乐，D与按键听声事实反。

### 2：C

only one at a time/others wait支持C；A长度未改，B仍有按钮，D没有手机要求。

### 3：B

When one recording was playing后until it finished，先行是播放录音；A入口不能播放完，C按钮不说finished recording，D板到末段才引入。

### 4：D

画可移动导致指示失效，改固定门洞，D概括；A把不移画升级禁令，B/C均非段内活动。

### 5：B

全篇设计故障改进与补书面指引，B；A仍有board相反，C无历史，D只取局部画展背景。
