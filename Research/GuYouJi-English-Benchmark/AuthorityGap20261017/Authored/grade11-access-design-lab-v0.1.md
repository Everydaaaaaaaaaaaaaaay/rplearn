### Clear Instructions Lab

The fictional Clear Instructions Lab invites pupils to redesign everyday instructions. Teams will choose a simple classroom process, write a short guide and ask another team to try it. This is a learning activity, not certification to design safety-critical equipment.

### Who may apply?

Applicants must be in Year 10 or Year 11 and have a parent or guardian's permission. Attendance at all listed sessions is compulsory. Experience with design software is optional. It cannot replace attendance or permission.

### Application

Send a form, a short account of one confusing set of instructions, and a recommendation from an adult who is not a family member. The recommendation should give an example of working with others. Applicants do not need to submit a finished guide.

### Compulsory sessions

| Activity | Schedule | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design lab | 7—18 September | 30 hours in total |
| Trial workshop | 21 September, 13:00—16:00 | 3 hours |
| Revision review | 28 September, 13:00—16:00 | 3 hours |

The two workshops add to, rather than form part of, the 30 lab hours.

### What participants receive

A writing tutor will comment on draft guides after the trial. Teams then revise and explain what they changed. Completing the lab does not guarantee publication of a guide, admission to another course or a professional qualification.
