# 小五：社团开放日整表

## 学生题面

读整张活动表，包括表头和全部五行，从每题四个选项中选择一个最佳答案。

All activities are on the same day. The following timetable is fictional.

| Start | End | Activity | Place | Who can join |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8:40 | 9:00 | Welcome | Hall | Parents and pupils |
| 9:15 | 9:45 | Chess games | Room 2 | Parents and pupils |
| 10:00 | 10:20 | Story circle | Library | Parents only |
| 10:35 | 11:05 | Music show | Music room | Parents and pupils |
| 14:10 | 15:00 | Club talk | Room 5 | Parents and pupils |

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Where are the chess games?
   A. In Room 5.
   B. In the library.
   C. In Room 2.
   D. In the hall.

2. How long does the music show last?
   A. Twenty minutes.
   B. Thirty minutes.
   C. Forty minutes.
   D. Fifty minutes.

3. When does the club talk end?
   A. At 14:10.
   B. At 10:20.
   C. At 11:05.
   D. At 15:00.

4. A parent can come only after 13:00. Which activity can the parent join?
   A. Club talk.
   B. Welcome.
   C. Chess games.
   D. Music show.

5. Which activity is for parents only?
   A. Welcome.
   B. Chess games.
   C. Story circle.
   D. Club talk.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

Chess games行PlaceRoom2，C；Room5是Clubtalk、libraryStorycircle、hallWelcome。

### 2：B

10:35到11:05=30分，B；A20误作其他行/不跨时，C40和D50非该差值。

### 3：D

End15:00为D；14:10开始，10:20Storycircle结束，11:05Musicshow结束。

### 4：A

唯一列在13点后且可家长Clubtalk，A；B/C/D均上午，不能靠只看家长资格忽略时段。

### 5：C
