## 作者核对

### 第1段完整原创脚本

W: I would like to hire two bicycles for Sunday. Your website says the riverside branch opens at nine, but our train arrives at eight thirty.

M: That branch still opens at nine. The station branch opens at eight, though, so you could collect the bicycles there and ride down to the river.

W: Would that cost more? We had planned to return them beside the river, not at the station.

M: The daily hire price is the same at both branches. Returning to a different branch adds five pounds for each bicycle. If you bring them back here, there is no extra charge.

W: I see. My brother is arriving separately and will not reach the river until ten. Could he collect his bicycle there while I collect mine at the station?

M: Certainly, but each person must show identification when collecting. You cannot collect for him unless he comes with you.

W: That is fine. We are both adults. Do we need to bring helmets?

M: No, helmets and locks are included. Bring a small bag for your own things, because these bicycles do not have baskets.

W: What happens if the weather is terrible? I do not want to pay for a whole day and then be unable to ride.

M: You can cancel without charge until six on Saturday evening. After that, the booking payment is not refunded. We do not close simply because it rains.

W: Then I will make two separate bookings and check the forecast on Saturday. I can return my bicycle at the station before our train home.

M: Good. Put the collection and return branches on each form. That avoids any confusion when you arrive.

### 第2段完整原创脚本

M: I saw your advertisement for a photography course. I use a phone most of the time, but I would like to try making prints in a darkroom.

W: We have a Saturday digital editing class and a Wednesday darkroom course. For the Wednesday course, you work with black-and-white film. You do not need your own film camera; we can lend you one.

M: That sounds right. Do beginners start immediately with the darkroom chemicals?

W: No. The first meeting covers using the camera and loading film. At the second, you develop the film with the tutor. Printing begins in the third week. You must attend the safety introduction before using the darkroom.

M: I can come every Wednesday except the first one. Could I read the instructions at home and join the second meeting?

W: The introduction includes a demonstration and a check with the tutor, so reading it is not enough. But we repeat it on the Friday after the first meeting. You could attend that instead.

M: Friday is possible. I finish work at five. What time does that extra meeting begin?

W: At six thirty, and it lasts one hour. It is included in the course fee. Materials for your first roll of film are included too, but additional film must be bought separately.

M: One roll will be enough to start with. Can I keep the borrowed camera between lessons?

W: Yes, after completing a loan form. It must be returned at the final meeting. The prints you make are yours to keep.

M: Good. Please book the course and the Friday introduction for me. I will borrow a camera rather than buy one before I know whether I enjoy working with film.
