# 初一：手工围巾礼物

## 学生题面

读完整对话，第1—5空分别从本空四个候选中选择一个最佳答案；各空候选独立。

Lin: That scarf looks nice, Jo. (1) ____
Jo: My sister made it for me.
Lin: (2) ____
Jo: Last Sunday. She gave it to me in the evening.
Lin: I can see stars on it. (3) ____
Jo: Five. Two are big and three are small.
Lin: Would you like to show it at our class meeting?
Jo: Yes, of course.
Lin: (4) ____
Jo: In Room 6. The meeting is there every week.
Lin: Please bring the scarf tomorrow.
Jo: (5) ____ I will put it in my bag now.

1. 第1空
   A. Where is your sister?
   B. Who made it?
   C. How do you go to school?
   D. Why is the door open?

2. 第2空
   A. What colour are the stars?
   B. How much is the bag?
   C. When did she give it to you?
   D. Which room is yours?

3. 第3空
   A. Are they yellow?
   B. Where is the scarf?
   C. Do you like stars?
   D. How many stars are there?

4. 第4空
   A. Where do we have the meeting?
   B. When is your birthday?
   C. Who is your sister?
   D. How much are the stars?

5. 第5空
   A. I am sorry I lost it.
   B. OK, I will.
   C. No, I cannot bring it.
   D. It is on Sunday.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

答My sister made it明确制作者；A地点、C交通、D开门原因未被答，只有B。

### 2：C

Last Sunday/in evening回答赠送时间，C；A颜色、B价钱、D房间不能接此时间。

### 3：D

Five及two big/three small是数量明答，D；A/C是是非，B地点，不与数量答语衔接。

### 4：A

In Room 6直接答开会地点，A；B时间、C身份、D价格不符。

### 5：B

放包准备明天带来说明承诺照办，B；A丢失与现在放包冲突，C拒绝和准备行动冲突，D时间不答请求。
