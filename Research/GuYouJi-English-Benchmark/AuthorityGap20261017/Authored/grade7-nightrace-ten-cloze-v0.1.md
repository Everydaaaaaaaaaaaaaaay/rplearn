# 初一：夜里发亮的号码十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为第1—10空分别从3个选项中选择最恰当的一项。人物与经历均为虚构。

Our town had a short fun run on Friday evening. My sister and I went to watch. The runners had numbers pinned to the fronts of their shirts, on their (1) ____. We could read them as they came towards us. I wanted to see number 24. That was my father.

At first, there were many people beside the road. We could not see over their heads, so we stood on a small hill. From there, we had a (2) ____ view of the runners. A woman said the first runners would arrive soon. We waited and (3) ____ for number 24.

Soon the sky became dark. The numbers were hard to read, (4) ____ the lamps along this part of the road were off. My sister took a small light out of her bag. She did not shine it into anyone's (5) ____. She pointed it down at a card we were holding. The card said, “Go, Dad!”

A runner waved when he saw our card. It was Dad. Several other runners passed him as we watched. He was moving (6) ____ than those runners, but he was smiling. We shouted his name. At the finish, he thanked us for the (7) ____.

“Did you win?” I asked. “No,” he said, “but last time I stopped twice. Today I ran all the way without (8) ____.” He was happy about that. My sister and I gave him some (9) ____ because he was thirsty. Then we walked home together, carrying the little light and our (10) ____.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

fronts of shirts明示躯干正前的chests；backs在背后与front矛盾，shoes在脚上不在shirts。

### 2：A

从hill越过人头看跑者是clear view；heavy重量、sweet味觉不描述此可见性。

### 3：C

waited and looked for number24是寻找父亲号码；paid for是支付、cooked for是做饭，均非观看。

### 4：B

数字看不清的原因是lamps off，because；although使应相反的两事让步，before错误地用时间取代已给因果。

### 5：B

shine light into anyone’s eyes为避免照眼而改照卡，eyes；bags或hands不与向下照卡形成同一避免刺目目的。

### 6：B

Several runners passed him as we watched给同一时刻相对超越证据，more slowly符合；more quickly与被这些跑者超越冲突，earlier是时间而非本句速度比较。

### 7：C

saw our card而wave且加油，thanks for card；并未颁medal或买ticket，不能用比赛常识替代文内实际物。

### 8：C

last time stopped twice与today all the way构成连续跑对比，without stopping；without smiling与前文smiling冲突，without running与ran all way冲突。

### 9：A

because thirsty指向补水water；paper非饮食，bread是食物不直接解渴。

### 10：A

carrying little light and our card回收文内已持道具；全程步行，没有bicycle/umbrella在前文交付。
