# 四级：地图上两个不同的位置

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，从A—O中为全部十空各选一个最佳词项，填写字母。词形不变；每项最多使用一次，五项不用。材料为虚构情境。

Owen was walking towards a fictional coastal village when he reached a fork. One (1) ____ of the path led downhill; the other climbed a ridge. The wooden sign was still there, but its painted letters had (2) ____ until almost nothing could be read. He hoped to (3) ____ the village before five, when his sister would meet him at its only bus stop.

A stone hut nearby offered (4) ____ from a passing shower. Inside, Owen opened a photograph of an old map on his phone. Something seemed wrong: the river appeared on the opposite side of the road. He realised that the photograph had been (5) ____ from left to right when it was copied. The tiny words were mirror images too.

He found an unaltered copy in his notes. Its key helped him (6) ____ a black square as a former mill rather than a house. Beyond it, a lighthouse was (7) ____, appearing as a pale dot several kilometres away. The scale allowed him to (8) ____ the remaining distance without measuring it on the ground.

Owen decided to return another day to (9) ____ the side paths, spending time discovering where they went. Today he checked the mill's (10) ____ position carefully: a dotted square showed where a replacement had once been proposed, not where the old building stood.

共享词库：A. actual · B. branch · C. distant · D. estimate · E. exception · F. explore · G. faded · H. identify · I. inward · J. reach · K. reversed · L. shelter · M. slight · N. steadily · O. transit

## 作者核对

### 1：B

One branch of path与the other组成岔路分支对照。exception例外、shelter遮蔽处、transit运输不指路的分支；estimate虽可作名词，但路的一个估计不通；其他项不作此处路支名词。

### 2：G

letters had faded until almost nothing可读，指褪色。reversed若反转不会令颜料几乎不可见，后文反转另属手机照片；其余动词estimate/explore/identify/reach为原形，不能置于had后作完成式分词；其余项不表示褪色完成动作。

### 3：J

reach the village接到达地点与五点会面截止。identify识别村庄不实现去唯一车站会面；explore探索不等在截止前到达；estimate估计不以村庄作此数值对象；其他项不表示到达目的地。

### 4：L

offered shelter from shower表示提供避雨遮蔽。branch分支、exception例外、estimate估计、transit运输均不是抵御阵雨的保护；其余项不作此不可数保护名词。

### 5：K

river对边且tiny words mirror images明确reversed左右镜反。faded只褪色不能改变左右；其余形容词actual/distant/slight不表示影像翻转，其他项也非had been后的这个被动分词。

### 6：H

key帮助identify square as former mill，表示辨认图例。estimate不接as做身份判断；reach到达不表辨认；explore探索不提供X as Y认定结构；其他项不构成此原形识别动词。

### 7：C

several kilometres away及pale dot限定distant遥远。actual只表真实、slight微小不表距离；其余候选不在was后构成此表空间距离的形容词。

### 8：D

依据比例尺estimate distance估计距离。reach distance不等估算；identify可识别某距离而非按比例推算其长度，explore不表达数值估算；其余项不适合此原形计量动作。

### 9：F

spending time discovering where they went支持explore探索岔路。reach只是抵达，不能充分表述走访发现走向；estimate需要数值对象。identify在辨认路线语境有局部可行性，不能泛说语法不合法，但第6空图例辨认为identify X as Y，整组每项最多一次排除在第9空重复使用。其余候选不构成此原形探索动作。

### 10：A

actual position与只被proposed的点作事实/规划对比。distant只说明远近、slight不形容位置真伪、reversed为镜反照片非建筑位置；其余项不能作为此处真实位置的修饰。

完整向量B/G/J/L/K/H/C/D/F/A；十个不同词库项，未用五项为E exception/I inward/M slight/N steadily/O transit。逐槽位可合语法不自动等整组最佳：必须同时核对上下文和原印每项最多一次规则。词库仅十五项，控件逐空重复不当150个独立词项。正文/题干/候选/输出负荷分别记录。
