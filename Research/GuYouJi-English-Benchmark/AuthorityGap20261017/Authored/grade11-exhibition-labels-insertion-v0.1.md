从A—G中选择最合适的选项补全完整文章，有两个多余选项。保留题面已有标点；不另加每项仅一次限制。

A useful exhibition label does more than give an object a name. It helps a visitor notice something worth examining. Before writing, decide what this particular object can help a visitor understand.

#### Begin with a reason to look

(1) ____, so start with a detail that makes the object worth a closer look. A repaired seam in an old bag, for example, can raise a question about how it was used. You do not need to tell the whole history in the opening line.

#### (2) ____

After choosing that detail, check which statements are supported by the records. A date written on a repair receipt may establish when the work was paid for, but not exactly when the bag was first used. Keep those two claims separate when describing the seam.

#### Return to the example

A confident sentence can hide a weak link. (3) ____. The receipt can support the payment date, while the earlier history remains uncertain. Saying so gives visitors a way to understand the limit rather than asking them to accept an unexplained conclusion.

#### Check the reproduction plan

Some labels include a photograph that belongs to someone outside the exhibition team. (4) ____, obtain the required reproduction permission before sending the label to the printer. If the team owns the image, check the internal record instead. These are different routes for establishing what may be printed.

(5) ____. Put the draft beside the object and ask a reader to use them together. If the reader cannot find the seam mentioned in the text, revise the wording or placement before deciding that the label is finished.
