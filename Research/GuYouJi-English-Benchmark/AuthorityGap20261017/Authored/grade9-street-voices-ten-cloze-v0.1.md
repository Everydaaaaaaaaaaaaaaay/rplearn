# 初三：旧街名的声音十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，从每空所给的三个选项中选择最恰当的一项，完成第1—10空。人物、地点和经历均为虚构。

Our group recorded stories behind local street names. We expected a simple project: read the signs, ask questions and write a report. Our first interview made us (1) ____ that plan.

Mr Hu, a resident of Willow Street for sixty years, used a name absent from our map. I corrected him at once. He fell silent. My partner asked about the name's history. Mr Hu described a bridge that was no longer there. People had kept using its name after the bridge was (2) ____.

We found an old photograph of the bridge in the archive. The current map was not wrong, but it did not tell the (3) ____ story. We now wanted the memories as well as the printed names.

Mr Hu agreed to let us record his voice. This time, I listened without (4) ____. Afterwards, I asked about unfamiliar details. He spoke freely and laughed at a memory. The same words on paper could not fully (5) ____ his voice's warmth.

We included the recording in our report. Readers unable to hear it could read a (6) ____. Mr Hu checked the text and corrected one event's year. We kept his original words but marked that factual (7) ____ in a separate note.

At the presentation, I showed both photograph and map. The street had not moved; our (8) ____ had changed. Interviewing was no longer about making people (9) ____ what I already believed. It offered a chance to hear something I had not known to (10) ____ about.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

简单查牌问话计划被首访新信息改变，reconsider重想；complete完成与随后尚需档案/再访相反，protect保护原计划不合改变取向。

### 2：C

bridge that was no longer there明确已无桥，removed解释建筑消失而旧名保留；built表示建成、painted只改颜色，均不解释桥已不在。

### 3：A

currentmapnotwrong但只覆盖正式现称，nottellwhole story；false把地图内容当假故事与notwrong冲突，same无第二个已给故事作为相同对象。

### 4：A

前次corrected atonce使沉默，对照此次听完才问，withoutinterrupting；withoutrecording与获准录音和后文添加recording冲突，withoutlistening自相矛盾。

### 5：C

samewords onpaper不能完整再现声音温暖，capture；reduce减少、ignore忽视不是文字相对原声的表达保真能力。

### 6：C

给不能听者的书面逐语记录transcript，对照recording；invitation邀请、schedule日程不能提供同一采访的可读内容。

### 7：B

correctedyear后旁注标明事实correction，同时保留原话；prediction预测、question提问不表示确认后的年份改正。

### 8：A

streethadnotmoved而加旧照片/回忆，改变understanding；location位置被未移否定，ownership所有权无转移。

### 9：B

whatIalreadybelieved期待别人证实既有想法，confirm；discover发现意味着新知不是alreadybelieved，forget忘记不是访谈求证预设。

### 10：B

notknowntoaskabout表示原本未知该问的事；argue争论/complain抱怨不合倾听陌生信息的采访目的。
