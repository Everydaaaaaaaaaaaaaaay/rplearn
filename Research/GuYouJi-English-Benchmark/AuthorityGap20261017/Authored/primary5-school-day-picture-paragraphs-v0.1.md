# 小五：校园六图与短文

## 学生题面

看六幅图并读六段短文。第0幅已示范配D，不计作答；为第1—5幅各选一段A—F。每段是两句完整短文，不另加每项仅一次限制。

![六幅原创图，0为已答示例D，其余编号1至5](build/english-benchmark-authority-20261017/primary5-school-day-picture-paragraphs.svg)

共享文段：

A. A child is carrying a green bag in one hand. Two red apples are on the table beside the child.

B. Two children are reading the same big book together. They are sitting on a brown bench beside each other.

C. A teacher is writing HELLO on a board. A red marker is held near the letters on the board.

D. A child is drawing a blue boat on a sheet of paper. A yellow pencil is held in one hand.

E. A child is washing both hands at a sink. Water is coming from the tap above the hands.

F. A child is sitting on the floor with a white cat. The cat is beside a small blue ball.
