## 作者核对

### 第1段完整原创脚本

W: Hello, I'm interested in the pottery classes advertised in your window. I've never made anything from clay before. Would the Saturday workshop be suitable?

M: It is open to beginners, but that one is mainly for people who want to decorate a ready-made bowl. If you want to learn to shape clay yourself, the four-week evening course would be better.

W: I had assumed the evening course was for experienced students. The photograph showed some very impressive pots.

M: Those were made by the tutor. Everyone starts with a simple cup. We keep the group small so the tutor can show each person what to do.

W: That sounds closer to what I want. I work until six, though. What time does it begin?

M: At seven. The studio is open from six thirty if you want to arrive early, but teaching starts at seven.

W: And do I need to buy any equipment before the first class?

M: No. Tools and enough clay for the course are included. Bring an apron or wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty. If you want extra clay later, you can buy it here.

W: My sister might come too, but she can't manage the final evening. Would she lose the piece she makes?

M: Not at all. The last meeting is for discussing the finished work after it has been fired. She could collect hers the following week. She would miss that discussion, but the firing is included in her fee.

W: Good. I'll check with her tonight rather than book two places straight away.

M: That's fine. I can hold two places until tomorrow afternoon without payment.

### 第1题：B

录音问题：Why does the man suggest the evening course?

B：晚课教shape clay；A未说周六满；C初学可参加；D是周六装饰现成碗，不是晚课理由。

### 第2题：A

录音问题：What does the man say about the photograph?

A：those were made by tutor；B未给拍摄场次；C入门先做简单杯，非照片精美罐；D非购物清单。

### 第3题：D

录音问题：What must participants provide for the course?

D：自带围裙或可弄脏衣物；A工具材料含费；B从零做杯；C烧制已含费，不是逐件另付。

### 第4题：C

录音问题：What will the woman do next?

C：今晚先问姐姐/妹妹再订；A目前可无付款保留；B作品尚未制作；D只可之后取件，无改课请求。

### 第2段完整原创脚本

M: I bought this suitcase here last Friday, and one of the wheels has stopped turning. I'd like to exchange it before I travel tomorrow.

W: I'm sorry about that. Do you have the receipt with you?

M: Yes, here it is. I haven't actually taken it on a trip yet. I noticed the problem when I was packing this morning.

W: I can see that the wheel is stuck. We normally offer an exchange or a refund for a fault like this. Unfortunately, we don't have another one of this size in the same color.

M: Color isn't important, but I do need the same size. It has to fit the airline's cabin allowance.

W: There is a blue version at our branch across town. I could ask them to keep it for you. Or we have this larger case here at the same price.

M: The larger one won't help. I'm traveling with hand luggage only. Could the other branch send the blue one here today?

W: Our delivery van has already left, so we couldn't promise that. But I can arrange for you to exchange it there, with this receipt.

M: I won't be able to get across town before they close. I think I'd better take a refund and find another case nearer my office.

W: Certainly. Did you pay by card?

M: Yes. Will the money go back immediately?

W: We can process the refund now, but it may take a few working days to appear in your account. You'll receive a refund receipt today.

M: All right. I understand. I'll keep that receipt until the payment appears.
