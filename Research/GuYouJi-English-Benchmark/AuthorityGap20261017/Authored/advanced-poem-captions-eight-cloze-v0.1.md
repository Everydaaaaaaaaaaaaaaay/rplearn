# 高级：诗歌字幕的取舍八空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为第1—8空各选一个最佳选项。例0已在文中填写并给出四个候选，不计作答点。人物、机构与案例均为虚构。

A literary festival (0) commissioned new translations of poems for a public reading. The translators also had to supply short captions. These could not do (1) ____ to every feature of the originals, but they had to preserve the central images.

One translator initially fell (2) ____ on rhyming phrases whenever a literal version sounded awkward. The results were fluent, yet some introduced cheerful images into an otherwise bleak poem. Such additions (3) ____ its tone rather than merely changing its wording. The editor therefore asked her to explain the (4) ____ behind each departure from the original.

The team eventually reached a (5) ____: the spoken translation would retain more detail, while the captions would use shorter sentences. The organisers explicitly wanted the versions to complement each other, not for either to (6) ____ the other. Both would be available together. The editor rejected an explanation of the poet's entire career as beyond the (7) ____ of these brief captions. Instead, she required a note identifying where compression had removed a meaningful ambiguity. That would make the limitations (8) ____ to readers rather than leave them concealed.

例0（已答，不计作答）：A. commissioned　B. consented　C. committed　D. confirmed；答案 A。

## 作者核对

例0：A

commissioned...from/for表示委托创作；consented需to而非直接宾语，committed非委托翻译，confirmed确认既存内容不指派创作。

### 1：C

do justice to为充分体现/呈现；后文不能容纳全部features而保留centralimages给该搭配语义。do dignity/fairness/merit to不组成此惯用表达。

### 2：D

fall back on表示困难时求助于既有手段；fall away/over/out不能与on rhyming phrases构成这里退而依赖的短语。

### 3：D

为bleak诗添加cheerful images改变了情绪方向，distorted歪曲；condensed压缩不涉及反向加图，recorded记录和recovered恢复不说明被加内容扭转。

### 4：D

explain...behind eachdeparture要求说明改动的理由rationale；permission许可是是否获准非理由，chronology先后、location位置都非why改动。

### 5：B

口语保更多细节/字幕短句是在要求间达成compromise折中；diagnosis诊断、prediction预测、rehearsal排练不指此双方功能协调方案。

### 6：C

明确wanted versions to complement而不是替代，supplant为取代；accompany陪伴、complement互补、support支持均属共同存在/互助的相容方向，不是被对照排除的替代行为。

### 7：B

说明wholecareer超出briefcaptions的内容范围，beyondscope；schedule进度、outline提纲、caption字幕本身都不表达任务边界。

### 8：A

要求note指出被删ambiguity，与ratherthanconcealed相对是explicit明示；incidental附带、obsolete过时、exclusive排他的均不说明从隐藏到明确。
