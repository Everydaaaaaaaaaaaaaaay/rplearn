# 初一：家庭食谱册语法选择

## 学生题面

阅读完整语篇，为全部十空各选择一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，不需要也不允许改变候选词形。

Our class is making a little book about family meals. It is (1) ____ to learn how different families cook the same dish. We do not need (2) ____ expensive camera. We draw simple pictures beside the recipes instead.

Each pupil asks a family member about one meal. My grandmother (3) ____ me her favourite recipe every year, and this time I wrote it down. She uses two small (4) ____ to hold the ingredients. I put a number beside every step so readers can follow the order. This way is clearer (5) ____ writing everything in one long sentence.

We (6) ____ the first pages yesterday. Ms Lin said, “These lovely pictures are (7) ____, so write your names below them.” She then showed us how (8) ____ a simple contents page. There (9) ____ many useful ideas in our book already. We want more families to become interested (10) ____ trying one another's meals. We are sharing family stories, not saying that one family's way is the only right way.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

It is exciting to learn...评价学习这件事令人兴奋。excite为动词；excited形容人感受而此it为形式主语指学习活动；excitement为名词，此处非用冠词的名词结构。

### 2：B

空后是单数可数名词，结合其开头音素使用an。a与该词的开头音素不合；a few和many要求复数名词，不能直接接本题的单数名词。

### 3：C

every year为习惯，my grandmother单数，teaches作谓语。teach数不符；teaching/to teach不能独立作限定谓语。

### 4：B

two后需要复数名词bowls，且hold ingredients说明盛物容器。bowl/bowlful是单数形式，bowling表示保龄球活动不符容器与two。

### 5：C

clearer比较级后用than。then时间、that指示/连接、as同级比较结构均不合此比较形式。

### 6：D

yesterday明确过去，finished正确。finish/finishes为现在；finishing缺限定助动词。

### 7：C

名词性物主代词yours作表语表示“你们的画”，与write your names呼应。your缺所修饰名词；you不表示归属；yourself为反身单数而非画的归属。

### 8：A

how to make为疑问词+不定式结构。how making缺本句连接结构；made/makes不能在该showed us how后独立形成此补语。

### 9：C

There be中many useful ideas复数用are。is数不合；has/have不能作该存在句的be谓语。

### 10：D

become interested in doing表达对尝试饭菜产生兴趣。on/at/of不构成本句搭配。
