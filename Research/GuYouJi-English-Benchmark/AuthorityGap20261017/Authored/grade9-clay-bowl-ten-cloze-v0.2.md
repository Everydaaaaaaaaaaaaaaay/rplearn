# 初三：一只稍歪的碗十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，从每空所给的三个选项中选择最恰当的一项，完成第1—10空。人物、地点和经历均为虚构。

My first pottery bowl leaned to one side. The teacher's bowl was smooth and round. Mine looked worse whenever I (1) ____ them. I wanted to throw it away.

Ms Green asked what I had planned to do with it. “Give it to my brother,” I said. He had moved away to study, and I wanted him to have something from home. “Don't judge its value only by its (2) ____,” she replied.

A deep thumb mark remained where I had lifted the clay too early. Ms Green showed me how to smooth it. The clay was still (3) ____, so pressing hard would change its shape. I worked slowly until only a faint line remained.

After firing, the bowl was hard but still uneven. I painted it blue, my brother's (4) ____. He always chose blue clothes and bags. Inside, I drew our dog. That detail made it (5) ____ from the other bowls.

My brother sent me a photograph after receiving it. The bowl held paper clips on his desk. He had found a practical (6) ____ for it instead of merely displaying it.

I could see the uneven edge. The bowl had not suddenly become (7) ____. Yet I no longer wanted to hide it. By focusing on mistakes, I had almost (8) ____ the person it was for. I kept my next bowl, too, hoping to improve it without (9) ____ everything that made it mine. Learning could include (10) ____ work I was still learning from.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

开篇teacher bowl光滑圆、mine看着更差，compared them表示相互比较；separated分开、collected收集不能表达这里两碗外观的对照。

### 2：B

round/smooth/leaned为外观比较，appearance；weight重量从未测，price无交易报价，不能替代最初自卑维度。

### 3：A

原文The clay was still...与后句pressing hard would change its shape支持soft；之后After firing才hard。finished已完成与仍要smooth/firing冲突；frozen冻结不合这段可按变形的黏土状态。

### 4：A

后句He always chose blue clothes and bags支持favourite偏好；problem问题和surprise意外不能解释反复主动选择蓝色。

### 5：C

自己画our dog这一私人图样使different from其他碗；absent from表示未在其他碗中，不能说明此细节所形成的区别；free from表示摆脱其他碗，不合个人图样作用。

### 6：C

照片中盛paper clips并与merely displaying相对，practical use为实际用途；name名字和rule规则均不是盛放物件的功能。

### 7：A

前句uneven edge支持not suddenly perfect，与开篇教师圆碗相对；valuable价值未被不齐边缘否定，兄弟实际使用使价值可见；personal已有自家狗图样，不能由边缘不齐说它不具个人印记。

### 8：A

专盯缺点而忽略赠送对象，almost forgotten personitwasfor；met遇到、followed跟随不表达注意力被外观占据而忽略接受者。

### 9：C

improve without removing everythingmadweitmine，保留个人印记；noticing注意或describing描述不会损掉这些印记，不能承接保留个人特点的领悟。

### 10：B

Ikeptmynextbowl too直述保留未完美作品作学习，keeping；selling无出售，copying不是保留自己当次作品。
