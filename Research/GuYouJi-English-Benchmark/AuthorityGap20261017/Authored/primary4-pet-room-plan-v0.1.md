# 小四：照护分工对话阅读

## 学生题面

阅读完整对话，从每题三个选项中选择一个最佳答案。完成全部五题。

Ella: We need to look after the class rabbit this weekend. Can you come on Saturday, Toby?
Toby: I can't. I'm visiting my grandparents that day. I can come on Sunday morning.
Ella: Good. I'll give it food and clean water on Saturday. On Sunday, please check the water bowl first.
Toby: Is the bag of rabbit food on the shelf?
Ella: It was there yesterday. Miss Lin has put it in the cupboard beside the door. Please use that food, not your lunch.
Toby: I understand. Who has the room key?
Ella: Miss Lin. She will open the room at nine on both days.
Toby: I'll meet her then. Can my little brother come?
Ella: Ask Miss Lin first. We can't invite visitors without asking her.

1. Why can’t Toby come on Saturday?
   A. He is visiting his grandparents.
   B. He has lost the room key.
   C. He is cleaning the cupboard.

2. What should Toby check first on Sunday?
   A. The lunch box.
   B. The water bowl.
   C. The shelf.

3. Where is the rabbit food now?
   A. On the shelf.
   B. In Toby’s bag.
   C. In the cupboard.

4. Who will open the room?
   A. Ella’s brother.
   B. Miss Lin.
   C. Toby’s grandfather.

5. What must Toby do before bringing his brother?
   A. Ask Miss Lin.
   B. Give him his lunch.
   C. Move the rabbit outside.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

Toby明说看祖父母；B钥匙本就由老师保管非丢失；C橱柜是食物地点非冲突安排。

### 2：B

Ella明确check water bowl first；A不能喂个人午餐；C是昨日食物处不再是当前优先事项。

### 3：C

has put in cupboard支持C；A为昨天位置；B未给。

### 4：B

She回指Miss Lin且will open；A小弟未准来；C祖父未参与。

### 5：A

ask...first明确A；B错把午餐当许可条件；C并未提出且无必要。
