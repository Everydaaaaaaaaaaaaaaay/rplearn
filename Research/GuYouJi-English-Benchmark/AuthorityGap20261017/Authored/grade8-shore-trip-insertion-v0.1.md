## 学生题面

从A—E中选择最合适的选项补全完整文章，有一个多余选项。保留题面已有标点；不另加每项仅一次限制。

### September 3

Our family went to a beach today. I had packed a football because I wanted to play on the sand. When we arrived, however, the wind was strong. (1) ____ So we put the ball back in the car and went for a walk.

Near the rocks, I found a smooth stone shaped like a heart. Dad said we could look for different shapes without taking stones away from the beach. (2) ____ We placed them together, took a photograph and then left them where we had found them.

It began to rain just before noon. (3) ____ From our seats inside, we could still see the waves through a large window. We ate our sandwiches there.

When the rain stopped, we returned to the car. I was sorry not to have played football, but I had enjoyed looking closely at the beach. (4) ____ I will show it to my friend tomorrow and tell her about the heart-shaped stone.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

C强风吹沙导致不踢球回车，后So承因果；A把石带走与后leave相反，B照片末段已完成，D发现石还未到rocks，E雨棚因雨在第三段。

### 2：D

D新增圆石/扁石，与heart一起形成them拍照后放回；A带回与leave矛盾，B只是照片评价无新增石可数，C沙和E棚非石形线索。

### 3：E

E找到cafe边棚，inside/seats/window解释避雨午餐；A重袋带石反，B照片无座窗，C吹沙/D石形不能提供雨中inside处所。

### 4：B
