A date was written (5) ____ of the page, below the artist’s signature. Mina read the tiny figures as (6) ____ as she could. Although the edges had become brown, the date (7) ____ clear. It was the year her grandfather had stopped working in the village garden.

Her grandmother came into the room. For a moment, neither of them spoke; the room was filled with (8) ____. Then her grandmother explained that a neighbour had made the picture (9) ____ Mina’s grandfather after his death. The flower had come from his favourite plant.

Mina felt a surge of (10) ____ when her grandmother said she had noticed the family likeness in the drawing. She had never thought herself good at recognising faces. Together they found a clean folder for the picture, while the old gardening book returned to its place on the shelf.

### 1：A

### 2：D

unfolded：opening each corner untilflat明解unfolded主动过去；remained不及物不能带paper，faded不能表展开纸角；余项不是此主动过去谓语。 本空完整候选为A=faded；B=beneath；C=carefully；D=unfolded；E=remained；F=pride；G=silence；H=at the bottom；I=in memory of；J=portrait；K=narrow。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。
