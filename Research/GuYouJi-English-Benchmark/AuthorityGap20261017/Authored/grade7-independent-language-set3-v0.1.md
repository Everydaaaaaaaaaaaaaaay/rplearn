# 初1：独立语言选择第3稿

## 学生题面

根据每题完整语境，从本题三个候选中选择一个最佳答案，完成全部10题。

各题语境独立。

<!-- responses:start -->

1. These are my cousins. ____ live near the river.
   A. Their
   B. They
   C. Them

2. The family usually has a picnic ____ spring.
   A. in
   B. on
   C. at

3. The answer is not sixteen ____ sixty.
   A. so
   B. because
   C. but

4. —How often does Jo visit the library? —____.
   A. On the second floor
   B. Twice a week
   C. At three o’clock

5. ____ your sister play the piano after dinner?
   A. Does
   B. Do
   C. Are

6. There ____ a red umbrella behind the door.
   A. are
   B. have
   C. is

7. —____ is the school trip? —On Friday.
   A. Who
   B. When
   C. Where

8. Our teacher ____ us a story every Monday.
   A. tells
   B. tell
   C. telling

9. Please be quiet. The baby ____ now.
   A. sleep
   B. slept
   C. is sleeping

10. Next week I ____ take these books back.
   A. does
   B. will
   C. am

## 作者核对

### 1：B

They：主语复数They；Them宾格，Their需名词，不作此live主语。

### 2：A

in：spring季节搭配in；on具体日、at时点不合。

### 3：C

but：not sixteen but sixty纠正两个数；so/because后不接单独数字完成该并列。

### 4：B

Twice a week：Twiceaweek频率；atthree时点，secondfloor地点，不答howoften。

### 5：A

Does：sister单数实义play问句Does；Do数不合，Are不能接play且数不合。

### 6：C

is：aredumbrella单数用is；are数不符，have不构成该therebe句。

### 7：B

When：OnFriday时间答When；Where地点、Who人物不合。

### 8：A

tells：teacher单数习惯tells；tell数不合，telling缺限定助动词。

### 9：C

is sleeping：安静提示现在睡，is sleeping；sleep单数谓语不合，slept过去不合now。

### 10：B

will：will+take原形；am不能接原形take，does与I数不合且非本句将来陈述。
