# 高二：捐赠箱里的信封十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读一篇完整叙事语篇，为全部十空各选一个最佳答案。每空四个独立候选；根据上下文、事件顺序及人物态度判断。

Our community sale was nearly over when a visitor came back looking (1) ____. She explained that she had donated an old school bag by mistake. Inside its smallest pocket was an envelope containing a letter from her grandfather, who had died several years earlier. She did not care about the bag's price; she wanted the (2) ____ back.

My first thought was that the bag had already been sold. Then I remembered moving some late donations into the storeroom because there had been no time to (3) ____ them. Their sales labels were still blank. I asked the visitor to wait and checked the shelves. Several bags looked alike, and her description of a dark colour was not enough to (4) ____ hers.

I was about to bring them all out when she added one small detail: a green thread had been used to mend the strap. That clue (5) ____ the search. I found the repaired strap, opened the tiny pocket and saw the envelope still there. I carried the bag to her without opening the letter. Its contents were (6) ____; I did not need to read them to understand their value.

She checked the handwriting on the envelope and smiled with (7) ____. Before leaving, she thanked me for taking the extra time. I had done nothing dramatic, only gone back to a room I thought was already finished with.

Later, I suggested that we (8) ____ every pocket before accepting bags for future sales. The organiser agreed and added the check to our list. A small (9) ____ could save someone the worry we had seen that afternoon. The letter taught me that an ordinary object could carry a meaning far greater than its (10) ____.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

误捐含已故祖父信的包后回找，末段明确回指that worry，worried有直接态度依据。relaxed放松、sleepy困倦、proud自豪均无该忧虑及急找失物的支持。

### 2：A

smaller pocket中的祖父letter是想取回物，且不关心包价。money金钱、receipt收据、invitation邀请均未作为丢失物。

### 3：B

sale中晚到捐物仍在库房、sales labels空白，未完成的是price定价。return归还与捐售方向不同；repair修理不解释空价签，且所找包的补线已存在；borrow借用不是把捐物准备出售的步骤。

### 4：C

many similar dark bags，仅颜色不能identify识别其包。hide藏、copy仿、weigh称重均不是从相似包中认出目标。

### 5：D

绿色补线的独特细节把范围缩小narrowed，随即找到。abandoned放弃、repeated重复、confused混淆均与线索帮助定位相反。

### 6：B

信主人私人物件，without opening/无需读表private。public公开与不开信尊重私密冲突；amusing好笑、false虚假内容未读无依据。

### 7：C

认字确认找回后微笑，先前忧虑解除是relief。anger怒、doubt疑、shame羞愧均与确认取回后的情境不合。

### 8：A

教训是未来接包前逐口袋检查check。seal封口会藏遗物；remove every pocket拆口袋非目标；sell卖口袋而非检查遗物。

### 9：D

把逐口袋检查列入清单，是防止误捐遗物的precaution。discount折扣、argument争执、celebration庆祝均不是这项预先检查措施。

### 10：B

开头不在乎包价、后文祖父信情感价值，意义超price金钱价。colour颜色/size大小/weight重量均不是全文显式对照的经济与情感价值。
