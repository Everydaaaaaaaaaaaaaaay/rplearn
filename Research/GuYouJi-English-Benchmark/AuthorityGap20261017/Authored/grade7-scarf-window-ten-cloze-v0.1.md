# 初一：窗边的红围巾十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为第1—10空分别从3个选项中选择最恰当的一项。人物与经历均为虚构。

It was cold on Saturday. I put on my red scarf and went to the bakery. A sign on the door said CLOSED, so I could not (1) ____ any bread there. I walked towards home along the river.

On the way, I stopped to watch some ducks. I took off my scarf because I felt hot and placed it on a bench. A few minutes later, I left the bench (2) ____ it. When the cold wind touched my neck, I remembered the scarf and turned (3) ____.

The bench was empty. Then I saw something red in the window of a small shop. It was my scarf! The shopkeeper came to the door. “A boy brought it to me,” she said. “He found it on the (4) ____. I put it here so its owner could (5) ____ it from outside.”

I thanked her and put the scarf around my (6) ____. At that moment, a boy came out of the next shop. “You found your scarf!” he said. I knew he was the person who had (7) ____ me. I wanted to buy him a cake, (8) ____ he shook his head. “I'm happy you have it back,” he said.

Before leaving, I asked his name. It was Leo. We both liked watching the ducks, so we talked about (9) ____ for a while. The bakery was closed that morning, but I went home with my scarf and a new (10) ____.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

CLOSED且cannot进入营业购买，buy bread对应去面包店目的；cook是烹饪并非店内购物，carry搬运不说明关店阻止交易。

### 2：C

围巾留bench且后来回找，left without it表示未携；beside it表示一直在旁不合后来离开忘取，through it不构成穿围巾而行。

### 3：C

remembered后回先前bench，turned back；turned off关闭设备、turned on开启设备都不表示返回地点。

### 4：B

上段placed it on bench，现据男孩发现处回指bench；无乘bus或上boat经历，不能将河中鸭推成围巾在boat上。

### 5：C

window/from outside为失主看见，see；洗wash、穿戴wear不能只从外面通过展示窗完成找物目的。

### 6：B

前文cold wind touched my neck，scarf围neck；围hand/foot不回应颈部受冷及put on scarf常见明确场景。

### 7：C

男孩将遗失物交店主展示，行动帮助找回，helped；forgotten忘记叙述者、followed跟踪叙述者均非交失物功能。

### 8：A

想买cake而男孩摇头拒绝，but转折；because把拒绝当赠礼原因，so把拒绝当赠礼成功后果，均错连两件事。

### 9：B

两人都喜watching ducks，talked about them复数回指ducks；it单数不合，him指单一男性而不是共喜欢看的鸭。

### 10：A

互通姓名与共同爱好得新friend；无购票或新coat情节，物品不是人物交往结果。
