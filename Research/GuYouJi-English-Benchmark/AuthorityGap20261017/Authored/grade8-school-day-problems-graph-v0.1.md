1. Which problem was chosen by the largest number of students?
   A. Getting up early.
   B. Finishing homework on time.
   C. Carrying a heavy schoolbag.

2. How many students chose taking notes in class?
   A. 36.
   B. 18.
   C. 12.

3. Which statement must be true?
   A. Every student chose exactly three problems.
   B. Exactly 163 different students took part.
   C. The total number of problem choices exceeded the number of students.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：A

1号30/50=60%最大；4号50%=25，2号40%=20。

### 2：B

7号36%×50=18；36把百分数当人数，12为8号24%的计数。

### 3：C

11项总163选择>50人，所以C；A50×3=150不等163，B分母明确50，不能将类别选择当不同人。
