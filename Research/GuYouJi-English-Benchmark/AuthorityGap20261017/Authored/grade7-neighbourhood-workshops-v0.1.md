# 初一：四项社区体验活动

## 学生题面

读四张完整活动卡，为三名同学各选最符合完整需求的一项，有一项不用；不另加每项仅一次限制。

### A · Walking Map

Walk around the community centre with a teacher and draw a map of nearby places. Add names and directions to help first-time visitors.

Time: 16:00, May 6 · Place: Main Gate

### B · Sound Stories

Listen to a short recorded story and make sounds for its characters with simple objects. Tell the group why you chose each sound.

Time: 16:00, May 7 · Place: Music Room

### C · Paper Buildings

Fold and join paper to make a small model house. You will take the model home. We do not make maps in this workshop.

Time: 16:00, May 8 · Place: Room 105

### D · Sign Makers

Read examples of clear notices and design one sign for the centre. Use words and colours to explain one rule. We stay inside throughout.

Time: 16:00, May 9 · Place: Room 106
