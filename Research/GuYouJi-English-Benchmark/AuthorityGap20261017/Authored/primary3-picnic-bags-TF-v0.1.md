# 小三：野餐袋里的东西

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，判断五句话是否与短文相符，选T或F。人物、玩具和野餐情境均为虚构。

I am Tom. I go to the park with my sister, May. We have two bags. My bag is green. May's bag is yellow. I have four apples in my bag. May has some bread in her bag. We have no bananas. We sit under a big tree. May likes the bread. I like the apples.
