# 初一：班级演出准备五段标题

## 学生题面

为整篇文章的五个段落各选择一个最合适的小标题。五个标题共用；根据每段中心判断。

Read the five sections of the article and choose a heading for each one.

### (1) ____

Before you plan the show, find out when your classmates are free. Ask about lessons and clubs after school. Write two possible afternoons on the board. Pick the one that works for most helpers, then ask your teacher about the room.

### (2) ____

A show needs more than people on the stage. Someone can meet visitors at the door. Other helpers can move chairs or look after the music. Make a list with a name beside every task so everyone knows what to do.

### (3) ____

Do not wait until the visitors arrive to find out that nobody can hear the music. Play a short part through the speakers. Let someone sit at the back and listen. Check the lights too, and learn where the switches are.

### (4) ____

Make a simple notice for families. Say where the room is and when the doors will open. Explain how long the show will last. If visitors need to bring a ticket, put that on the notice too, so they can get ready.

### (5) ____

When the last visitor leaves, collect the paper from the floor. Put the chairs back and return anything you borrowed. Look under the seats for lost things. Your show is over, but another class will use the same space tomorrow.

共享标题：

C. Share the jobs
D. Choose the time together
E. Leave a clean room

## 作者核对

### 1：D

本段从调查空闲时间到选共同下午，核心是定时间。A宣传、B试器材、C分工、E清场均未发生。

### 2：C

列每个任务及负责人，核心为分工。A对外宣传、B器材测试、D择时、E结束清场不是本段中心。

### 3：B

音响试播、后排试听和查灯是设备测试。A不是宣传，C未分配全体工作，D不择日期，E不清场。

### 4：A

面向家庭告知地点、时间、时长、门票，核心是传达活动信息。B不试设备、C不分工、D不是讨论选日、E不做清场。

### 5：E

活动结束清地面、归椅还物，为下一班留好房间。A宣传、B测试、C安排分工、D定时间都不能概括该收尾段。
