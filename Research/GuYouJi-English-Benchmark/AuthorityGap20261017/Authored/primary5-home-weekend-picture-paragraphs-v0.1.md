# 小五：周末六图与短文

## 学生题面

看六幅图并读六段短文。第0幅已示范配D，不计作答；为第1—5幅各选一段A—F。每段是两句完整短文，不另加每项仅一次限制。

共享文段：

A. A person is cutting a cake into small pieces. Three white plates are beside the cake on the table.

B. A child is holding a white shirt above a table. A red shirt is hanging on the wall behind the child.

C. A child is cleaning a window with a yellow cloth. The child is standing on the floor beside the window.

D. A person is making soup in a big pot. A long spoon is held in one hand above the pot.

E. Two children are playing a board game on the floor. A small red box is between the two children.

F. A person is watering three plants beside a window. The water is coming from a blue watering can.
