# 小五：窗边画画的女孩

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，判断第1—5句话是否与短文相符，选T或F。人物和故事为虚构。

My name is May. I am eleven years old. I like drawing, and my favourite things to draw are animals. I go to the art room after school on Wednesday. My friend Leo goes with me. Last month, my father gave me a box of coloured pencils. Yesterday, my mother bought a new drawing book for me. I use it at home, not in the art room. There is a small table by my window, and I draw there after I finish my homework.

Next Friday, our class will have a little art show. I will take three pictures of cats. Leo will take a picture of his dog. We will put our names under our pictures. My little sister wants to come to the show. She likes the orange cat in my first picture. I hope she will enjoy the show.

## 作者核对

### 1：T

第一段明确Wednesday afterschool与friendLeo一起，T相符；F否定已印时间和同行者。

### 2：F

彩铅为father lastmonth；mother昨天买drawingbook。F有明确赠物/人物对照，不因文中未见就判假。

### 3：F

原文明说use it at home,not in artroom，F；T与地点否定冲突。

### 4：T

第二段I willtake three picturesofcats直接支持T，F与计划不符。

### 5：F

Leo willtake pictureofhis dog明确非cat，F。无需真实看画，全部信息在文内。
