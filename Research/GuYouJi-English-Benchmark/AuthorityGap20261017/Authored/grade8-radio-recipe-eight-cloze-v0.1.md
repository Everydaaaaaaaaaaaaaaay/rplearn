# 初二：电台里的食谱八空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为第1—8空分别从4个选项中选择最恰当的一项。人物与经历均为虚构。

My grandmother listened to the same local radio programme every Sunday. One week, a cook described a soup that Grandma wanted to try. She wrote down the ingredients, but the radio signal (1) ____ before the cook explained how long to heat the soup. All we could hear was a rough noise.

“I remember the name of the programme,” she said. “Perhaps we can find it online.” I searched and found a recording. We listened to the last part again. Grandma added the missing (2) ____ to her notes: fifteen minutes, not fifty.

The following morning, I helped her prepare the vegetables. She cut the carrots into small pieces and asked me to measure the water. I almost poured it straight into the pot. “Use the jug,” she said. “We need to know the (3) ____.” The marks on its side showed the number of millilitres.

While the soup was heating, Grandma told me that she used to follow recipes from magazines. Radio was useful, but written instructions were easier to (4) ____ while cooking. I suggested printing the online recipe. She agreed, provided that we used a large font so she could read it without her glasses.

When we tasted the soup, we both found it too salty. I felt the whole morning had been (5) ____, but Grandma laughed. “We have a meal, and we know what to change.” She crossed out “one spoon of salt” and wrote “half a spoon” in its (6) ____. Then she dated the page. The recipe was no longer an order we had to follow (7) ____. It was a starting point for a meal that would suit (8) ____.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

信号后只rough noise且missing后续内容，failed中断；improved改善、returned恢复、spread传播不解释听不清缺步骤。

### 2：A

fifteen minutes/notfifty明确时长time；price价格、address地址、weight重量不是分钟单位所标。

### 3：C

measure water+jug刻度millilitres用于amount；colour颜色、age年龄、cost成本不能用毫升刻度测。

### 4：D

做饭时可随时查阅written instructions，consult；erase擦除、postpone延后、announce宣告不是纸指令相较易听漏广播的使用优势。

### 5：C

尝太咸时沮丧，以为morning wasted，与祖母仍收获一餐及改法的回应相对；rewarded奖励、extended延长、planned已计划不表达此挫败判断。

### 6：B

in its place表示用新盐量替代已划旧量，place；colour颜色、price价格、voice声音不能组成这一次书面替换的位置表达。

### 7：A

由照方到改盐按口味，no longer follow exactly；publicly公开、recently最近、silently安静不是修改配方所解除的照本限制。

### 8：C

祖孙都嫌咸并一起改，suit us包含叙述者与祖母；them排除说话人且无已引入第三方，him无男性先行，her只祖母漏掉明确we both口味。
