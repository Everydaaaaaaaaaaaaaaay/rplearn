# 小五：蛋糕与空座六空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，每空从三个选项中选择最恰当的一项，完成全部6空。多词短语算一个候选、一次回应。人物与故事均为虚构。

On Sunday, Mina is taking a cake to her grandpa. The cake is in a big (1) ____. She sits down on the bus and puts the box on the seat next to her.

A few stops later, many people (2) ____ the bus. They come in through the front door. Mina sees an old woman (3) ____ beside her because there are no empty seats. “Please take this seat,” Mina says. She puts the box on her own knees and holds it with (4) ____ hands. The old woman thanks (5) ____.

At Grandpa's home, Mina opens the box. The cake is still in one piece. Grandpa smiles and is (6) ____ to hear about her trip. “You found a safe place for the cake and a seat for someone else,” he says.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

后文反复thebox及opensbox明确包装为box；bottle瓶子、plate盘子都不是这次后文回指容器。

### 2：A

Theycomein throughfrontdoor直接说明上车geton；getoff为下车方向相反，takeoff不表示进公交车。多词短语整体填一个空、一次选答。

### 3：C

公交内无空座、站在Mina旁使standing合场景；flying飞行和swimming游泳都与车内等座场景不符。

### 4：A

hands为复数，bothhands双手语法成立；every/another通常接单数可数名词，此处hands直接复数不合。

### 5：C

thanks后需宾语，her回指Mina；she/he是主格不能作此动词宾语。

### 6：B

Grandpasmiles并赞赏找到兼顾蛋糕和让座的方法，happy支持愉悦；angry愤怒/afraid害怕与笑和肯定的表态不符。
