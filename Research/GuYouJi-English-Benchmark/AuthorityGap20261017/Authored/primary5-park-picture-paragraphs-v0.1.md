# 小五：公园六图与短文

## 学生题面

看六幅图并读六段短文。第0幅已示范配D，不计作答；为第1—5幅各选一段A—F。每段是两句完整短文，不另加每项仅一次限制。

![六幅原创图，0为已答示例D，其余编号1至5](build/english-benchmark-authority-20261017/primary5-park-picture-paragraphs.svg)

共享文段：

A. Three people are having a picnic on a green mat. There are sandwiches and red apples in front of them.

B. Two children are riding their bikes along the path. Both children are wearing blue helmets on their heads.

C. A child is flying a red kite near a tree. The long kite string is held in one hand.

D. This is a pond in the park. Three yellow ducks are swimming in the water together.

E. A person is reading a newspaper on a brown bench. A brown bag is beside the person.

F. A person is walking a black dog beside a small lake. The person is holding the dog's lead.
