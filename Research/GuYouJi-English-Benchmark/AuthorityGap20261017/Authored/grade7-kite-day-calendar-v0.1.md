The school kite day will take place on the third Sunday of June 2027, from nine until eleven in the morning. Families should meet at the school gate. The group will walk to the sports field together. You do not need to own a kite: the school has some for visitors to borrow.

Each family will receive a small card at the gate. Please write the number of people in your family on **it**. A helper will use the card to decide how many chairs the family needs at the practice table. Keep any bags beside you, not on the table.

During the first hour, helpers will show families how to hold a kite safely. After a short break, the families can practise together. Children must stay with an adult. The event is free, but drinks are not provided. Please bring your own water.
