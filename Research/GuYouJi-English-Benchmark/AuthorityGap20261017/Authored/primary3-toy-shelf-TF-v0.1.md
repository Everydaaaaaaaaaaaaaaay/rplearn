# 小三：玩具架上的朋友

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，判断五句话是否与短文相符，选T或F。人物、玩具和野餐情境均为虚构。

Hello! I am Lily. I have a toy bear and a toy rabbit. The bear is brown. The rabbit is white. They are on my bed. My red ball is under the bed. I have three toy cars. They are in a blue box. The box is on the desk. I do not put my toys on the chair.

## 作者核对

### 1：T

I为Lily，bear后明确brown，T；white属于rabbit，不移给bear。

### 2：F

rabbit iswhite与black直接矛盾，F。

### 3：F

redball isunderthebed，与on相反位置，F；玩偶onbed不能代给ball。

### 4：T

three toy cars后They areinbluebox，数量与复数指代双重支持T。

### 5：F

I do not putmy toysonthechair明确否定，F非从未提chair推断。
