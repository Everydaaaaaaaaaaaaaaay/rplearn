# 高一：模型里的尺寸十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读一篇完整叙事语篇，为全部十空各选一个最佳答案。每空四个独立候选；根据上下文、事件顺序及人物态度判断。

Our group was making a model shelf for the school reading corner. I had drawn the plan and felt very (1) ____ about it. When Mina asked me to check the numbers again, I thought she was simply (2) ____ the work. We had only one afternoon left, and I wanted to start cutting.

Mina did not argue. She put two thick pieces of card on the table and placed the long piece between them. It would not (3) ____. I had measured the outside width of the model but had forgotten that the two side panels would take up some of the (4) ____.

For a moment, I tried to (5) ____ my drawing. Then I saw the model in front of me and stopped making excuses. Mina was not questioning how hard I had worked; she was checking whether the parts would go together. We (6) ____ the width before using any wood. That small change saved material we could not replace with our limited budget.

I offered Mina the pencil and asked her to add her measurements to mine. The final plan was a (7) ____ effort, with notes in two different styles of handwriting. Before making the real shelf, we built a second model to (8) ____ it. This time, every part fitted.

When classmates praised the finished shelf, I (9) ____ Mina's early question instead of taking all the credit. I now try to see a careful question as a (10) ____ to improve a plan, even when the plan is my own.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

急于开工、认为无需复核说明对计划confident。doubtful怀疑、ashamed羞愧、indifferent冷漠均与初始自信和护图态度不合。

### 2：C

only one afternoon/想立刻cut，叙述者误认为复核会拖延delaying。advertising宣传、finishing完成、funding资助都不是他最初嫌复核占时的判断。

### 3：A

长卡应放两侧板之间，后文解释宽度占用，故尺寸不fit。shine发光、bend弯曲、float漂浮均不解释侧板厚度导致的不能放入。

### 4：D

side panels有厚度，占用内部space。colour颜色、weight重量、time时间均不是外宽减去两侧板厚度所涉及的空间。

### 5：B

下一句stopped making excuses，且先不愿接受质疑，说明试图defend自己的图。copy复制、publish发布、erase擦除均不是作借口维护原图。

### 6：C

发现侧板占内宽，before using wood作尺寸调整adjusted。ignored忽略与修正冲突；doubled无倍增需要；guessed猜测不合Mina实际测量。

### 7：A

两人不同笔迹与add measurements to mine直接支持shared共同努力。secret秘密无据；single个人与两人矛盾；temporary暂时不是此处贡献归属主旨。

### 8：D

先做第二模型确认能否fit，是test实际验证。sell出售、decorate装饰、name命名均不解释随后every part fitted的检验结论。

### 9：B

instead of taking all credit，主动mentioned Mina的问题，承认贡献。avoided回避、rejected否定、forgot忘记均与分享功劳对比不合。

### 10：C

仔细提问帮助改进，chance为机会。promise保证不能由问题推出；command命令与询问不同；barrier障碍是原先误解，后文反省已改变。
