# 小六：提示与计划十句选择

## 学生题面

读十个独立句子或短对话，每题从三个选项中选择一个最佳答案。完成全部十题。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. The children ____ in the garden yesterday morning.
   A. are
   B. were
   C. is

2. —____ books are in your bag?
—Four.
   A. How many
   B. How much
   C. How old

3. My mother is a good driver. She drives ____.
   A. careful
   B. carefully
   C. care

4. Ben is 150 cm tall and Leo is 140 cm tall. Leo is ____ than Ben.
   A. short
   B. shortest
   C. shorter

5. —Did you ____ the letter last night?
—Yes, I did.
   A. wrote
   B. write
   C. writing

6. These two pencils belong to me. They are ____.
   A. mine
   B. my
   C. me

7. We plan to visit Grandma next Sunday. We ____ there by bus that day.
   A. went
   B. going
   C. will go

8. The meeting is ____ Friday afternoon.
   A. at
   B. on
   C. in

9. —Are there any eggs in the box?
—No, there ____.
   A. is not
   B. are not
   C. do not

10. My little sister can ____ the word without help.
   A. spells
   B. spelling
   C. spell

## 作者核对

### 1：B

children复数且yesterday morning过去，were同时满足；are时态不合，is数和时态都不合。

### 2：A

books可数复数且Four答数量，How many对应；How much不可数或价格、How old年龄不合。

### 3：B

修饰drives方式用副词carefully；careful形容词、care名词或动词，均不作此处方式修饰。

### 4：C

140<150，than后比较用shorter；short原级、shortest最高级不合。

### 5：B

did问句主语后的实义动词用原形write；wrote过去式重复标过去，writing非限定不合。

### 6：A

无后接名词的所属表语用mine；my需后接名词，me宾格不表此处所属。

### 7：C

next Sunday/that day指计划中的将来，will go符合；went过去，going缺助动词。

### 8：B

明确星期的下午用on；at钟点、in泛指下午但不用于这个带Friday的时间短语。

### 9：B

there are问句对应there are not，复数eggs；is not数不合，do not不能替代此存在句be。

### 10：C

can后原形spell；spells第三人称、spelling分词均不合情态补语。
