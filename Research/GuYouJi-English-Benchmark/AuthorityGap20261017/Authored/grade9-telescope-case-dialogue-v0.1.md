# 初三：整理望远镜盒

## 学生题面

读完整对话，从5个共享句子中选择合适的字母补全五处。不另加每项仅一次限制。

Owen: You have taken everything out of the telescope case, Gran.
Gran: Yes. (1) ____
Owen: First, put the long tube in the largest space.
Gran: Done. There are two little spaces on the left.
Owen: (2) ____
Gran: The round one with a silver edge. I use it most often.
Owen: OK. Put that eyepiece in the upper space.
Gran: What about the unused eyepiece?
Owen: (3) ____
Gran: All right. Both small spaces are full now.
Owen: We should label the outside next. (4) ____
Gran: “Garden telescope”, please. That will help me find it.
Owen: I've written it. Close the case and fasten both clips. (5) ____
Gran: Thanks. Now I can carry it safely.

## 作者核对

### 1：D

D请求教归位，与First put long tube构成指导开端；A直接下格但未指定余镜，E问上格镜、B问标签字、C结束均不能引出整体起手指导。

### 2：E

E询问上格用哪个目镜，对应round one/silver edge及后句Put that eyepiece upper；A下格指令、B标签、C完成、D整体求助均不是此处选择目镜问句。

### 3：A

A将unused eyepiece放下格，与上格已放及Both spaces full连续；E重问已选上格、D重启、B标签、C完成过早，均不答what about unused eyepiece。

### 4：B

B问标签写什么，Garden telescope和written it回扣；E目镜选择/A下格/D归位求助/C结束均不能引出该文字回答。

### 5：C

C在合盒扣两夹后说明整理完成，对应可安全携带；A/E镜位置、B标签、D整体求助都不合末句完成感谢的时序。
