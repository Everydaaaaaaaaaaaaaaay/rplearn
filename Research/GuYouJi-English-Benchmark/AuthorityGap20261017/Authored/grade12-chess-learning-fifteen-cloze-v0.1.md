# 高三：棋桌另一边十五空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整人物经历语篇，为全部十五空各选一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，结合跨句指代、时间线、限制条件及人物态度判断。

At seventy-one, Lena still spends Saturday mornings at a chessboard, but she no longer welcomes newcomers as an (1) ____ trying to beat them across the table. She pulls a chair beside them and asks what they notice about the position. The aim is to understand their thinking before offering her own.

As a teenager, Lena learnt from books she (2) ____ from the public library. Tournament entry fees and long train journeys were beyond her (3) ____, so she played at home instead. Years later, a free local club gave her the opportunity to meet stronger players. Her first results were disappointing, and for a while she considered (4) ____ altogether.

A club member suggested that she write down the moves from one lost game. At first, she wanted to skip the embarrassing parts, but the written record made them impossible to (5) ____. She began to examine not only her final mistake but the earlier decisions that had made it likely. A lost game became a source of (6) ____ rather than a reason to avoid the board.

Lena continued to work full time, setting aside a small amount each month for travel. She chose a few events she could (7) ____ instead of entering every competition. Her improvement was (8) ____, built through repeated practice and review rather than a sudden run of victories. She never became a famous champion, but she grew less afraid of discussing a move she did not understand.

After retirement, the club (9) ____ her to help with beginners: “Would you like to lead a group?” She initially prepared long explanations, then discovered that new players often needed time to try a position for themselves. She learnt to (10) ____ her teaching, using shorter questions and waiting for answers. A quiet pupil was not necessarily an uninterested one; some were considering several possibilities.

Lena also asked the club to keep a set of boards that anyone could borrow. She remembered how lack of money had once (11) ____ her own choices. The extra boards would not make everyone equally skilled, but they would make the activity more (12) ____.

Today, a visitor might mistake her patience for a gift she had always possessed. Her notebooks tell a different story: frustration, corrections and plans that had to be (13) ____. She shares those pages openly, so learners know that uncertainty is part of learning. Her greatest (14) ____ now is not a higher place in a competition. It is seeing someone return after a difficult game, ready to (15) ____ again.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

trying to beat them与across the table说明她作为opponent对手参加对弈，如今坐旁边了解思路。umpire裁判应保持中立而非击败学员；adviser顾问与assistant助手提供帮助而非以击败对方为目标。四个候选均可接an，须用棋局角色和前后对照判断。

### 2：C

from public library且预算有限，在家学棋的书为borrowed借阅。sold出售、ignored忽视、destroyed毁掉都不能说明从图书馆取得学习材料。

### 3：B

fees/train costs和后文lack of money限定经济means财力。patience耐心、memory记忆、interest兴趣不解释无钱参加与在家玩。

### 4：D

初次成绩差considered...altogether指完全放弃quitting。travelling出行不由失利直接说明退出学棋；celebrating庆祝与失望反；teaching那时尚未带初学者。

### 5：A

记录使尴尬错误无法忘却forget，承接原想跳过。publish发表无必须公开条件；repeat重复是以后可避免而不是记录让重复不可能；admire欣赏与embarrassing相反。

### 6：C

回顾早期决定及末步错误提供information信息。income收入未提；pride骄傲与失利尴尬反；entertainment娱乐不是该分析因果的主要所得。

### 7：B

每月存少量旅费，挑能afford的少数赛事而非全部。announce宣布、cancel取消、organise组织均不是按旅费筛选个人参赛的条件。

### 8：D

repeated practice/review而非sudden victories直接对gradual逐渐。instant瞬间相反；imaginary虚构与实际进步不合；accidental偶然不符合持续练习。

### 9：A

“Would you like to lead a group?”是询问意愿的邀请，故invited。forced强迫与自愿问句不符；forbade禁止方向相反；reminded提醒既定职责不等首次询问是否愿意带班。

### 10：C

从长解释改短问句并等待，自我调整adapt。advertise宣传、abandon放弃、repeat重复不概括改换教法。

### 11：B

回忆过去缺钱不能付参赛交通费，是restricted限制选择。expanded扩大相反；explained解释不是限制本身；ignored以lack money作忽略主体不合。

### 12：D

任何人可借棋盘，减少费用门槛，accessible更可及。profitable盈利没有收费；competitive竞争强度与供棋盘不等；mysterious神秘相反。

### 13：C

notebooks有frustration/corrections，计划需revised修订。concealed隐瞒与公开页反；financed出资不是这些认知纠错重点；celebrated庆祝不合困难修正。

### 14：A

不是竞赛排名而是看到学生失败后再回来，greatest reward内在回报。expense支出、risk风险、complaint抱怨均不适合这个积极目标对照。

### 15：B

return after difficult game,ready to try again表继续尝试，而不保证下次win。pay付费无活动收费证据；teach把新学习者角色变教师无支持。
