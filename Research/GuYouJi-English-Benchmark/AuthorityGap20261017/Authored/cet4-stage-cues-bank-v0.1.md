# 四级：演出中的空白

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，从A—O中为全部十空各选一个最佳词项，填写字母。词形不变；每项最多使用一次，五项不用。材料为虚构情境。

During a college play, the sound assistant started the closing music (1) ____, having mistaken one button for another. The actors were still halfway through their scene. Most of them were (2) ____ with the script because they had performed it twice before, but the unexpected music made them stop.

For several seconds there was complete (3) ____ on stage: nobody spoke. From behind the curtain, Eva could (4) ____ that one actor was waiting for another to begin. She held up the next line on a card. The actors read it and continued. The card was a (5) ____ solution, intended only to get them through that evening, not something to use in every future performance.

Afterwards, the group discussed the interruption without blaming the youngest member. His (6) ____ for help with the controls had been ignored during preparation. They agreed to (7) ____ with the sound equipment before their next show, practising every cue rather than only speaking the lines. Eva also helped (8) ____ the height of the control desk so that its operator could see the stage over it.

The assistant's (9) ____ returned when he completed the next rehearsal without an error. At the following performance, the (10) ____ watched the scene to its proper ending. Nobody needed a card, and the music began at the right moment.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

误认按钮解释无意按错，accidentally最合适。clearly表清楚程度，不表达误操作原因；其他十三项为名词、形容词或动词原形，不修饰started构成本处方式副词。

### 2：G

be familiar with script且演过两次说明熟悉。ordinary/permanent/temporary不以with script表达已有熟练知识；其余词不作此形容词补语。

### 3：N

complete silence由nobody spoke明确定义。benefit益处、audience观众、confidence信心、request请求、notice通知均不表示无人出声；其余项不在此构成这种名词状态。

### 4：H

could notice that引导观察到互相等待的情形。adjust/replace/rehearse不能以that从句表达察觉；request that为要求发生某行为，而此处看到已在发生的等待；其他项不提供此原形感知动词。

### 5：O

only that evening且not every future performance限定temporary临时措施。permanent永久相反；ordinary普通、familiar熟悉不概括只使用一晚的时限；其余项不作solution的这一形容词修饰。

### 6：M

request for help是此前提出、却被忽视的求助。notice for help不表达本人求助这一常规搭配；confidence/silence/benefit/audience均不是被忽视的请求；其他项不能作此his之后的中心名词。

### 7：K

practising every cue说明rehearse with equipment排练。adjust/replace设备是调整或更换，不是操练每个提示；notice观察、request请求不表示共同演练；其余项非本处动作原形。

### 8：B

adjust the height of desk表示改变高度以看见台上。replace the height不搭配；rehearse/notice/request不能表达落实这次高度修改；其余项不能担此动词原形。

### 9：E

成功排练使confidence信心恢复。silence安静、audience观众、benefit益处、request请求、notice通知不能概括助理经历失误后恢复的自信；其余项不是这个心理状态名词。

### 10：C

audience是观看演出的群体。其他名词benefit/confidence/request/silence/notice均不能作为集体观看的有生主体；其他词形也不提供此the之后的观看者。

完整向量A/G/N/H/O/M/K/B/E/C；十个不同词库项，未用五项为D clearly/F benefit/I ordinary/J permanent/L replace。逐槽位可合语法不自动等整组最佳：必须同时核对上下文和原印每项最多一次规则。词库仅十五项，控件逐空重复不当150个独立词项。正文/题干/候选/输出负荷分别记录。
