# 小五：教室小邮局

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，先为第1—2空从A—C三个共享句子中选择最合适的一句（有一句不用），再完成第3—5题，每题三个选项。完成全部五个响应；人物和活动为虚构。

On Monday morning, our classroom becomes a small post office. (1) ____ We read them together before we begin. Each note has the name of a class and a short message. Our teacher puts a box on the table, not beside the door. The extra paper stays in that box.

Ben has one note to deliver. It says, “Please bring your picture books to the hall after lunch.” He checks the classroom number before he leaves. (2) ____ When he arrives there, he gives the note to the teacher. The walk takes only two minutes. He does not leave the note on a desk without asking anyone.

Ben returns to our classroom after the short trip. Our teacher asks him which class received his message. Ben says, “Class Three.” He then writes a tick beside that class on our list. The tick helps us remember that the message has arrived. We do not take the same note there again.

前两空共享句子（有一项不用）：

A. There are short notes on the board.
B. The paper box stands beside the door.
C. He carries the note to Class Three.
