# 小五：图书分享下午

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，先为第1—2空从A—C三个共享句子中选择最合适的一句（有一句不用），再完成第3—5题，每题三个选项。完成全部五个响应；人物和活动为虚构。

On Friday afternoon, our class has Book Share in the library. We bring books that we can lend to friends. (1) ____ She looks at the books with us and helps us put them on a long table. We do not put them on the floor.

Ben brings two picture books. One is about a train, and the other is about a little dog. Amy wants to borrow the train book. She asks Ben first, and he says yes. She will return it next Friday. She is borrowing the book, not keeping it as a gift.

Before our friends choose books, we make small cards. (2) ____ These cards tell readers whom to ask before borrowing. We put a card beside each book. At the end, we write down which books our friends have borrowed. The list will help us find them when it is time to give them back.

前两空共享句子（有一项不用）：

A. Some pupils like playing basketball.
B. Our teacher, Miss Liu, is there to help.
C. We write each book owner’s name on a card.
