# 小五：午后的故事时间

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，判断第1—5句话是否与短文相符，选T或F。人物和故事为虚构。

I am Owen, and I am eleven. I like reading funny stories. On Monday and Thursday, I read a story to my classmates after lunch. We sit in our classroom. My friend Nina draws a picture on the board while I read. She does not read the story with me. Last week, my uncle gave me a book about a little bear. I read the first story on Monday. It was about the bear's red hat. My classmates laughed when I used a deep voice for the bear.

Tomorrow is Thursday. I will read the second story from the same book. In this story, the bear looks for his blue bag. Nina will draw the bag, not the hat. I will practise the story at home this evening. My younger brother wants to listen. He cannot read the book yet, but he likes hearing the bear's voice.
