# 初三：借用柜记录语法选择

## 学生题面

阅读完整语篇，为全部十空各选择一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，不需要也不允许改变候选词形。

Since the beginning of this term, our school (1) ____ a cupboard for borrowing small tools. Pupils can borrow a ruler, a pair of scissors or other classroom items. It is (2) ____ simple idea, but the rules need to be clear if everyone is to use it fairly.

At first, pupils wrote their names on loose pieces of paper. Several pieces (3) ____ during cleaning, so nobody knew which items were still out. The student helpers decided (4) ____ one record book instead. Each line now shows who borrowed an item and when it should be returned. The pupils (5) ____ return things check their own lines with a helper.

Yesterday, a helper thought I had not brought back two (6) ____. I showed her the return time beside my name. She had looked at the wrong line, and we corrected the mistake together. I did not blame (7) ____ for it; I understood how the similar names had caused confusion.

The helpers asked the teacher (8) ____ the cupboard needed a larger label. “Because some younger pupils cannot find it,” she replied. A new sign (9) ____ there tomorrow. With the sign and the record book, we hope the cupboard will work (10) ____ than before, but we will keep listening to pupils' suggestions.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

Since the beginning of this term表示延续到现在，school单数，has had符合时态与主谓一致。have had与单数主语不合；will have未来不合since；is having不表达本句持续拥有状态。

### 2：B

空后是单数可数名词，结合其开头音素使用a。an与该词的开头音素不合；a few和many要求复数名词，不能直接接本题的单数名词。

### 3：B

纸在清扫中被弄丢，pieces复数用were lost。lost主动会使纸丢失某物却无宾语；have losing结构错误；losing非限定谓语。

### 4：D

decide to do，用to use。use缺to；used和using均不能直接作该decided的本句补语。

### 5：A

pupils是人且在从句作return主语，用who。which指物；whose需接名词；what不能引导本句有先行词的定语从句。

### 6：B

two后为复数rulers。ruler单数，ruler’s单数所有格；ruling为裁决/统治或分词，不是本段借用的两把尺。

### 7：D

blame后宾格her指那位helper。she主格；hers物主代词；herself需要同主语反身，而主语是I。

### 8：C

后答句以Because解释原因，why引导“为何需要更大标签”的宾语从句。what与who在后面成分完整的从句无名词成分空位；that不表示此处询问原因。

### 9：B

sign作为被张贴物，tomorrow未来，用will be put up。will put up主动使标识贴物，方向不合；putting up无限定助动词；has putting up结构错误。

### 10：C

修饰work用副词，than要求比较级more smoothly。smoothly缺比较，smoothest形容词最高级，most smoothly副词最高级均不合than结构。
