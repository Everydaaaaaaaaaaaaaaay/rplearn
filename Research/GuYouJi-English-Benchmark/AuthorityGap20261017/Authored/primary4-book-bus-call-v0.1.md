# 小四：流动图书车对话阅读

## 学生题面

阅读完整对话，从每题三个选项中选择一个最佳答案。完成全部五题。

Nora: Hello, Dad. This is Nora. Can you collect me at the school gate today?
Dad: Yes. Your music lesson usually ends at four. Shall I come then?
Nora: Not today. A book bus is coming to school after the lesson. I want to choose a book, so please come at half past four.
Dad: All right. Is the bus selling books?
Nora: No. We can borrow one book for a week. We must bring our school cards.
Dad: Your card is in the small pocket of your bag.
Nora: Yes, I have it. The bus will be beside the playground. Only our class is visiting it today; other classes will go tomorrow.
Dad: Fine. I'll wait at the gate, not beside the bus.
Nora: Thank you. See you there.

1. Who is Nora talking to?
   A. Her music teacher.

2. When will Nora leave school today?
   A. At four.

   C. At half past four.

3. What must Nora take to borrow a book?
   A. Her school card.
   B. A ticket for tomorrow.
   C. Money for a new book.

4. Where will Dad wait?
   A. Beside the book bus.
   B. At the school gate.
   C. Inside the music room.

5. Which pupils can visit the bus today?
   A. All the classes.
   B. Only pupils with a music book.
   C. Nora’s class.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

Dad自称接她且Nora称Dad；A/C虽是场景可能人物均未加入对话。

### 2：C

Nora请父亲四点半来接；A是平常音乐课结束；B从未约定。

### 3：A

must bring school cards明确A；B不存在预约车票；C明确not selling故不收买书款。

### 4：B

末句明确at gate/not beside bus；A被否定；C无约定。

### 5：C

Only our class明确C；A其他班明天；B把music lesson误成借书资格。
