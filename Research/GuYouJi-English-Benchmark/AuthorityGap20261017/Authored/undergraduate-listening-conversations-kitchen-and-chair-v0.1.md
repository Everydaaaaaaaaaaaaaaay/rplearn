## 作者核对

### 第1段完整原创脚本

M: I would like to use the community kitchen for a family baking afternoon. The calendar shows a free space on Sunday.

W: The room is available from two until five. That includes cleaning time, so all food preparation has to finish early enough for you to leave it clean by five.

M: I thought we could bake until five and wash everything afterwards. We had better begin at two, then. There will be eight of us.

W: Eight is within the room limit of ten. Someone in your group must have attended our kitchen introduction. It explains the equipment and what to do if something goes wrong.

M: My cousin completed that last month. Does she have to stay for the whole session?

W: Yes. Being available by telephone is not enough. She will be the responsible person while the kitchen is in use.

M: That is possible. Do you supply baking trays?

W: We have six medium trays, measuring scales and mixing bowls. Bring ingredients and containers for taking your baking home. We do not keep leftover food overnight.

M: Can the children use the large mixer?

W: Only the trained adult may operate that one. The children can mix by hand with an adult supervising. We will put a reminder beside the machine.

M: Good. I will tell everyone to bring a container and check that my cousin can stay until we finish cleaning.

W: Then send the booking form with her name. You can pay after we confirm it.

### 第1题：C

录音问题：What must the group complete by five on Sunday?

C房间二到五含清洁；A只烤不全，B介绍已有人完，D无电话要求。

录音问题：Why is the cousin’s presence important?

A受训人在场全程；B食材任何人带，C无授课，D设备社区非她所有。

### 第3题：D

录音问题：What should participants bring?

D材料与带走盒；A/B设备有，C不留食过夜。

### 第4题：B

录音问题：Who may operate the large mixer?

Btrainedadult；A儿童只手拌，C付钱不代培训，D非限定office员工。

### 第2段完整原创脚本

W: I brought in a wooden chair last week. You said you would call with a repair estimate, and I received a message this morning.

M: Yes. One joint needs repairing, but the seat itself is sound. The estimate covers making the chair stable. It does not include changing the surface finish.

W: That is what I wanted to check. It belonged to my grandmother, and I would rather keep the worn paint than have it looking new.

M: We can leave the paint alone. There is a small mark beside the joint that may remain visible after the repair. Removing it would mean treating a larger part of the surface.

W: Please leave that too. I care more about using the chair safely than making the colour even. How long will the work take?

M: The repair can be done by Friday, but the material must be allowed to set before the chair is used. We ask you not to collect it until Monday.

W: I am away on Monday and Tuesday. Could a friend collect it for me?

M: Yes, if you give us the friend’s name in advance. They should bring the collection number from your receipt. We will not release it simply to someone who knows your name.

W: That seems sensible. I will send her name after I have asked her. Should I pay now?

M: A small deposit now, and the balance on collection. We will photograph the chair’s condition before starting and give you a copy with the receipt.

W: All right. Please go ahead with the joint repair and keep the old paint.

### 第5题：B

录音问题：What does the estimate include?

录音问题：Why is collection delayed until Monday?
