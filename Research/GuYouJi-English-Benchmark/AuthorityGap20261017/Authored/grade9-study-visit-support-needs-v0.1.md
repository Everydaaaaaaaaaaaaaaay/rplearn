# 初三：学习访问准备服务匹配

## 学生题面

阅读六则共享介绍，为全部五个人各选一个最合适的活动。须同时核对需求、时间和活动条件；不靠用剩候选决定。

All notices describe fictional activities.

A. Route Desk helps visitors plan a one-day trip using buses and trains. Staff print a route map and write down fares so you can use the plan without a phone. It is open on weekday afternoons. Staff do not book hotels or teach conversation.

B. Talk Circle is an evening meeting with volunteer partners. Practise everyday questions in pairs and change partners during the session. It is for speaking together, not receiving written corrections. No travel booking service is offered.

C. Kitchen Welcome is a Sunday morning class for beginners. Learn to prepare a simple lunch, with ingredients and equipment supplied. Tell the teacher about food allergies before the class. You cook and eat there rather than take a written language lesson.

D. Library Start offers a short indoor tour each weekday morning. Learn where different books are kept and how to borrow them. A large-print guide is available, and the whole route can be taken without stairs. There is no cooking or language practice.

E. Draft Clinic meets online on Wednesday evening. Send a short email draft beforehand. A tutor explains how its order and tone could be clearer, while you compare the original and suggested versions. It is not a live conversation club.

F. Repair Basket is a Saturday afternoon workshop. Learn to sew on a loose button and mend a small tear in a cloth bag. Needles, thread and practice cloth are provided. You may bring your own item, but electronic devices cannot be repaired here.

## 作者核对

### 1：E

E事先发邮件稿、周三晚在线解释顺序语气并比较。A路线非邮件；B口语不书面纠正；C烹饪；D借书导览；F缝补，均不符反馈对象。

### 2：A

A公交火车、票价、纸图及工作日下午全合。B无交通预订；C烹饪；D工作日上午图书馆导览非交通；E邮件；F缝补不合。

### 3：C

C周日早初学午餐、配设备食材且事前报过敏。A交通、B口语、D图书、E邮件、F缝补均不教备餐。

### 4：D

D工作日早馆导、借阅、提供大字与无楼梯路线全合。A下午交通，B口语，C烹饪，E邮件，F缝补不具要求组合。

### 5：F

F周六下午教缝钮/补布袋且配工具是本人学做。A交通、B口语、C备餐、D馆导、E邮件均不教衣物针线修补。
