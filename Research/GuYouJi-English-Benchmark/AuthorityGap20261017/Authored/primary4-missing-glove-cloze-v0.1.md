# 小四：失物处理完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为五个空各选一个最佳答案。每空两个选项，没有已答例题。

After the game, Finn cannot find one of his gloves. His left hand is warm, but his (1) ____ hand is cold. He looks under the seat. There is no glove there.

His friend May sees a red glove near the gate. “Is this (2) ____?” she asks. Finn looks at it and says, “No. Mine is blue.” They do not take the red glove (3) ____. They give it to their teacher. The teacher puts it in the lost-property box. Later, a girl comes to (4) ____ for it. She is pleased to get it back. Finn keeps looking and finds his own glove in his (5) ____. He had put it beside his lunch box.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. 第1空
   A. right
   B. left

2. 第2空
   A. you
   B. yours

3. 第3空
   A. home
   B. yesterday

4. 第4空
   A. ask
   B. sleep

5. 第5空
   A. sky
   B. bag

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A与left已戴手套的手相对；B会把同一左手同时说暖和冷，与but对照矛盾。

### 2：B

B为独立物主代词，问是否你的手套；A人称you不能指示这只手套归属。

### 3：A

A take...home表示带回家；B过去时间与do not一般现在时叙事所需地点不合。

### 4：A

A ask for表示来找回失物；B sleep for后需时长，不表寻找手套。

### 5：B

B与放在lunch box旁相容；A天空不可能是他此前放手套和饭盒的容器。
