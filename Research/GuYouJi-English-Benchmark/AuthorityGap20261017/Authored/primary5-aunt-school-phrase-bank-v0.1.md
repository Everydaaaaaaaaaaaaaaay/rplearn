# 小五：时间交通短语词库

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从六个短语中选择合适的一项，填入字母。词形不变，有一项不用。

My aunt lives far from her school. “Do you walk to work?” I ask. “No, I go (1) ____,” she says, pointing to the number 8 bus. Her lessons begin at eight (2) ____, not in the evening. At the school gate, there is a large tree (3) ____ the office building. The building is behind the tree.

My aunt finishes her last lesson at four. She stays for the painting club (4) ____, when all lessons have finished. At six, her friend drives her home. She has dinner, reads a book and goes to bed. She sleeps (5) ____ and works during the day.

词库：A. in the morning　B. by bus　C. in front of　D. after school　E. on foot　F. at night（有一项不用）

## 作者核对

### 1：B

B由pointing to the number 8 bus明确交通，且No回答是否步行。E on foot与No矛盾；A/D/F均表时间，不回答所问交通方式且不对应指车动作；C缺参照物。

### 2：A

A in the morning与not in the evening构成同一钟点的早晚对照。B by bus/E on foot是交通，lessons不能以这种方式开始；C in front of须有名词参照；D after school与课在放学之后才开始矛盾；F at night与题面的日常八点早课语境及not evening冲突。

### 3：C

C树在办公楼前，与下一句楼在树后完全对应；A/B/D/E/F均不能引出the office building作这一空间关系。

### 4：D

D由all lessons have finished明确放学后；A早晨与四点末课后矛盾；F夜间与六点回家之前的club不符；B/E是交通，不是此处俱乐部时间；C缺参照物。

### 5：F

F at night与during the day构成明确昼夜对照。A in the morning与work during the day冲突；D after school只表课后不能对应完整夜间且她先在club，未立即睡觉；B by bus/E on foot是交通，sleep on foot无此睡眠方式，sleep by bus也不构成正常搭配；C缺参照物。
