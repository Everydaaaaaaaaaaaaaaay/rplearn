# 初三：社区活动限制条件匹配

## 学生题面

阅读六则共享介绍，为全部五个人各选一个最合适的活动。须同时核对需求、时间和活动条件；不靠用剩候选决定。

All notices describe fictional activities.

A. Cycle Fix runs on Saturday from 10 to 12. Bring your bicycle and learn how to adjust its brakes with a trained helper. The group works in pairs inside a repair room. It is a practical repair session, not a ride around the city.

B. News Voices meets online on Sunday at 2 p.m. Bring one short report about your neighbourhood. Members discuss which details a listener needs and practise reading the report aloud. They do not make objects or use cameras during the session.

C. Town Memory is a Saturday afternoon visit inside the local museum. A guide explains old street photographs and stories of local families. There are lifts and a step-free route throughout. It is not an outdoor walk or a craft workshop.

D. Coast Watch meets on Sunday morning. Walk along an uneven coastal path with a bird guide and learn to photograph birds from a distance. Bring a camera and suitable shoes. This is an outdoor visit, with no indoor alternative in bad weather.

E. Tiny Movies starts at 9 on Saturday. Beginners use their own phones to photograph small models, moving them a little between pictures. The tutor helps turn the pictures into a short animation. Models are provided; no previous film-making experience is needed.

F. Second Life meets on Sunday afternoon. Use old cardboard to build small storage boxes and decorate them. All materials and hand tools are provided. No phone or computer is needed, and each visitor can take a finished object home.
