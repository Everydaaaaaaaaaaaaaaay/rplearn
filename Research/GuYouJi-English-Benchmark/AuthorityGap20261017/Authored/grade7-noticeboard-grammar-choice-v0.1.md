# 初一：班级公告栏语法选择

## 学生题面

阅读完整语篇，为全部十空各选择一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，不需要也不允许改变候选词形。

Our class has (1) ____ noticeboard near the library. We use (2) ____ to share school news. Every Monday, Li Hua (3) ____ a short message about our clubs. The words on the board are now larger (4) ____ before, so more pupils can read them easily.

Last week, the old board was too high for the youngest pupils. Our teacher (5) ____ it to a lower place on Friday. We decorated the new space together. The blue paper belongs to another class, but the yellow paper is (6) ____. We brought it from our own classroom.

Some pupils wanted (7) ____ with the notices. The teacher showed them where to put each one. There (8) ____ some spare pins in a box below the board. If you are interested (9) ____ our work, come and look after lunch. Tomorrow we (10) ____ a new notice about the school music show. I have already finished drawing its title.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

空后是单数可数名词，结合其开头音素使用a。an与该词的开头音素不合；a few和many要求复数名词，不能直接接本题的单数名词。

### 2：B

it作use的宾语，回指单数noticeboard。its为物主形式不独立作此宾语；they是主格复数，them是宾格复数，均不与单数板对应。

### 3：C

Every Monday说明习惯，单数Li Hua作主语，谓语writes。write数不符；writing和to write均不能独立作限定谓语。

### 4：C

larger为比较级，than引出before这一前后比较。then为时间副词；that非比较介词/连词；as需as large as结构而非larger as。

### 5：D

Last week和on Friday将事件限定过去，moved为过去式。move/moves为现在式；moving缺少限定助动词。

### 6：B

空格后无名词，ours独立表示our paper，与属于另一班的蓝纸对比。our需接名词；we为主格，us为宾格，均不表此处纸的归属。

### 7：C

want to do是所用结构，to help完整。help缺to；helped时态式/分词不能作want的本句补语；helps为三单限定形式。

### 8：D

There be句型中some spare pins为复数，are正确。has/have不是本句存在句be结构；is数不符。

### 9：D

be interested in是本句表达对工作感兴趣的搭配。at/on/of均不构成该搭配。

### 10：A

we后需限定谓语且Tomorrow指计划的未来，will put up成立。puts up与we数不符；putting up与to put up不能独立作限定谓语。
