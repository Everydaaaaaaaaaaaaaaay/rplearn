# 初三：社区声音档案语法选择

## 学生题面

阅读完整语篇，为全部十空各选择一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，不需要也不允许改变候选词形。

For the past three years, our neighbourhood library (1) ____ a project called Local Voices. Residents record short memories of everyday life. The purpose is not to decide whose memory is the most important. It is to give younger people (2) ____ chance to hear how the area has changed.

Before an interview begins, the speaker (3) ____ whether the recording may be shared publicly. Without permission, it stays in a private folder. Last month, I interviewed a woman (4) ____ once worked at the old railway station. She brought two (5) ____ showing the station before the new road was built. I recorded her story and sent a copy back to her.

I expected her to approve it immediately, (6) ____ she asked me to change one date. I checked the notes with her instead of guessing. She thanked me for (7) ____ her that chance. A memory can be valuable even when a detail needs checking.

Next week, several recordings (8) ____ at a meeting for local families. “Can you bring your own headphones?” the librarian asked. In other words, she asked (9) ____ we could bring our own headphones. We replied that we could. With clear labels and careful permission records, the collection is becoming (10) ____ to use than it was at the start. The project still has much to improve, especially when different people remember an event differently.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

For the past three years说明从过去延续至今，has run适合累计期间。ran一般过去未表达延续至今；will run未来与过去三年不符；is running现在进行不能承接本句for the past three years的完成期间。

### 2：A

空后是单数可数名词，结合其开头音素使用a。an与该词的开头音素不合；a few和many要求复数名词，不能直接接本题的单数名词。

### 3：B

speaker是被询问许可者，需被动is asked。asks会变为speaker询问别人，缺其宾语且与许可流程方向不合；has asking结构错误；asking非限定谓语。

### 4：C

先行词a woman在定语从句作worked的主语，用who。which指物；whose需接名词；what不能在该有先行词结构中替代who。

### 5：B

two后为复数photos。photo单数，photoes拼写不合常规复数，photo’s为单数所有格而非两张照片。

### 6：C

expected immediate approval与实际要求改日期构成转折，but对应。because改日期不是预期立即同意的原因；so会错误地把改日期写作预期同意的结果；or表示选择关系不合已经发生的两件事。

### 7：D

thank someone for后接名词或动名词，giving正确。give/gave限定形式不作for宾语；to give不定式不直接跟该介词for。

### 8：B

several recordings是被播放分享，next week限定未来，will be shared。will share主动主语方向不合录音被分享；sharing缺限定助动词；have sharing结构错误。

### 9：A

前面直接引语Can you...?为是非问，改为间接引语由whether引导。that不能引此是非疑问；what在完整句中无名词成分空位；how改为方式问，改变了原来Can you...?的问题含义。

### 10：C

becoming后用形容词，than提示比较级easier。easy无比较级；easiest最高级；easily副词不能作此表语。
