### River History Studio

The fictional River History Studio invites school pupils to prepare a small display about the history of a riverside street. Participants will compare photographs, select short captions and develop a paper display. The programme does not train professional historians or promise employment.

### Who may apply?

Applicants must be in Year 10 or Year 11, have permission from a parent or guardian, and attend every compulsory session below. Previous archive experience is not required. Someone who cannot attend the final review cannot join this run.

### Application

Submit the form and a short paragraph explaining a local-history question you want to explore. Attach a reference from an adult outside your family. The reference should describe how you collaborate, rather than give a professional judgement of your historical knowledge.

### Compulsory sessions

| Activity | Schedule | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main studio | 3—14 August | 24 hours in total |
| Caption workshop | 17 August, 10:00—12:00 | 2 hours |
| Final review | 24 August, 10:00—12:00 | 2 hours |

The two later sessions are additional to the 24 studio hours.

### What the studio offers

An archive educator will discuss the groups' selections and give feedback. Participants can revise captions between the workshop and final review. Completion provides neither a professional qualification nor a job guarantee.
