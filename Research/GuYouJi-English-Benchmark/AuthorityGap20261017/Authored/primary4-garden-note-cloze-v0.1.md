# 小四：种植观察完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为五个空各选一个最佳答案。每空两个选项，没有已答例题。

Our class has a small garden behind the school. On Monday, we put seeds in the ground. We make a (1) ____ with the date on it and put it beside the seeds.

On Friday, we see a little green leaf. “It is here!” says Bo. We are very (2) ____. The ground is dry, so we give the plant some (3) ____. We do not step on it. We look first and touch second. We look at the plant (4) ____ we touch it. At the end of the month, we draw its new leaves in our books. Our pictures help us see how the plant has (5) ____.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. 第1空
   A. noise
   B. sign

2. 第2空
   A. happy
   B. hungry

3. 第3空
   A. sand
   B. water

4. 第4空
   A. before
   B. after

5. 第5空
   A. grown
   B. flown

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：B

B可写日期并立种子旁；A声音不能承载书写日期并放在土旁。

### 2：A

A承接期待种子出叶的感受；B饥饿与见叶惊喜无因果且无食物语境。

### 3：B

B是干土需浇的物；A沙子不提供缓解干燥所需水分。此为虚构种植日常，不给现实植物照护处方。

### 4：A

前句look first、touch second明确先看后触，所以A before。B after会把顺序颠倒，并非仅凭一般园艺常识定键。

### 5：A

A为植物叶子增长，前文new leaves支持；B飞行不符合种在地里的该植物。
