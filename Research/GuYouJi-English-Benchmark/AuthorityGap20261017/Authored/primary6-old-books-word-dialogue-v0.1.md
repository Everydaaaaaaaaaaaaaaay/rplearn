# 小六：旧书阅读室词库对话

## 学生题面

阅读完整对话，从五个共享词项中选字母填入五空，词形不变；有的词项是短语，仍算一个候选。原参照未明示每项只用一次，本稿不加该限制，逐空按完整语境选择。

Mia: Is the reading room open today?

Leo: Yes. Everyone must keep (1) ____ in that room. Nobody is allowed to talk there.

Mia: Are the pictures on these two book covers the same?

Leo: No, they are (2) ____ from each other. One has a boat and the other has a train.

Mia: You read three stories yesterday. Which did you like (3) ____?

Leo: The last story. I liked it more than the other two.

Mia: How long did it take to read that story?

Leo: Only twelve minutes, (4) ____ fifteen minutes.

Mia: Can I turn the pages of that old book?

Leo: Yes, but be (5) ____ rather than careless. The old paper tears easily.

共享词库：

A. best
B. careful
C. quiet
D. different
E. less than

## 作者核对

### 1：C

C quiet形成keep quiet保持安静，与禁止说话一致。D different需对应差异而非保持安静，A best是最高级/副词不合keep后的该状态，E less than缺比较量，B careful不能构成这里keep careful的常规表达；不靠一次规则。

### 2：D

D different与from each other构成差异搭配，后面boat/train明示不同。C quiet from、A best from、B careful from不表达两幅画的差异；E less than from不成此句。

### 3：A

A best修饰like，问三故事最喜欢哪篇且答more than other two。C quiet/D different/B careful均非修饰like的此处副词或最喜程度；E less than缺比较对象，且不能构成Which did you like less than这一完整问句。

### 4：E

E less than十五分钟，12<15。C quiet/D different/A best/B careful均不能直接连接fifteen minutes表达这个大小比较。多词短语仍一选项一回应。

### 5：B

B careful与rather than careless直接形成小心/粗心对照，贴合易破旧纸。C quiet虽可用于安静，但不与careless形成此处反向操作要求；D different不说明谨慎，A best需the等且不对应careless，E less than缺比较量。
