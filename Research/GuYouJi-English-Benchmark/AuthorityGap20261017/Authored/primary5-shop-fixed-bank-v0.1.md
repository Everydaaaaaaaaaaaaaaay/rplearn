# 小五：商店准备词库

## 学生题面

阅读短文，从六个词中选择合适的一项，填入字母。词形不变，有一项不用。

On Saturday, Nia helps her father at a small shop. They (1) ____ the shop at nine. Before customers arrive, Nia puts paper bags on the shelf. The shelf is (2) ____ at first, with nothing on it.

A customer buys a glass bowl. Nia checks the bowl (3) ____ for cracks before she puts it down. She puts it in a bag and places the bag on the counter. Her father is happy: “You are never (4) ____ for work. You always arrive before nine.” At noon, there are no customers. There is no music and no one is talking. The shop is very (5) ____. Nia and her father can hear the clock. They eat lunch together.

词库：A. quiet　B. loudly　C. open　D. carefully　E. empty　F. late（有一项不用）

## 作者核对

### 1：C

C open是可接shop的此处开门营业动词。A quiet、E empty作动词虽可见于别义，但quiet the shop/empty the shop不等开门，且nine后customers arrive的流程支持开门；B/D/F为副词不能独立作此谓语。

### 2：E

E empty与nothing on it同义。A quiet描述声音不说明物件为零；B slowly/D carefully/F late均不描述空架；C open描述开闭而非数量。

### 3：D

D carefully表达仔细查裂缝。B loudly指发声响亮，目视检查碗裂缝没有发声行为；F late表迟到而不是检查方式且未有延迟情境；A quiet、C open、E empty不作此处方式副词。

### 4：F

F late与always arrive before nine及never构成守时说明；A quiet、C open、E empty不表迟到；B slowly、D carefully是方式副词，不作are后的迟到评价。

### 5：A

A quiet由no customers和hear clock提供安静场景。E empty会过度说明整店全空，父女仍在店里且有货；C open不说明可听时钟的安静；B/D/F不作此处描述声音的表语。
