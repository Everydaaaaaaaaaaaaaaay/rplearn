# 小四：叔叔的水果店

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，判断第1—5句是否与短文相符，选择T或F。人物与场景为虚构。

My uncle has a small fruit shop. It is near the bus stop. I help him on Saturday morning. There are red apples and yellow bananas in the shop. We sell fruit, not toys. My uncle puts the apples in blue boxes. I put the bananas on a long table. At ten, my aunt brings us some bread. We eat it in the back room. We do not eat in front of the shop. I go home before lunch.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. The fruit shop is near the bus stop.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

2. The writer helps at the shop on Sunday morning.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

3. The shop sells toys.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

4. The apples are in blue boxes.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

5. The writer and the uncle eat the bread in front of the shop.
   T. T：相符
   F. F：不符

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：T

第二句nearbusstop直接支持T，不需要地图。

### 2：F

help onSaturdaymorning明示日子不同，F。

### 3：F

sellfruit,nottoys明确否定，F非从只提fruit猜不卖其他。

### 4：T

uncleputsapples inblueboxes，T；bananas的longtable不借到苹果。

### 5：F

eat it inbackroom且do not eatinfront明确反证F。

三种来源具体模式分别完整绑定，不从边际数量拼组；共享库不新增未印的一次规则。所有判断F用明示反证，词库整句回填，情境角色明确。原转载年代/机构/逻辑键风险不升级，独立教研/儿童难度/学生/商用/生产未验证。
