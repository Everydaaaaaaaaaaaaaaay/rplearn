# 小四：图书馆指示完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为五个空各选一个最佳答案。每空两个选项，没有已答例题。

Eva goes to a new library with her brother. They want to read some (1) ____. At the door, they see a sign: “Please be quiet.” They speak very (2) ____.

Eva finds a book about birds. Her brother likes it too. “Can I read it (3) ____ you?” he asks. They sit together and look at the pictures. The book is not theirs, so they keep it (4) ____. Before they go home, they put it back on the right (5) ____. Then another child can find it.

<!-- responses:start -->

1. 第1空
   A. books
   B. boots

2. 第2空
   A. quietly
   B. quick

3. 第3空
   A. with
   B. under

4. 第4空
   A. dirty
   B. clean

5. 第5空
   A. shelf
   B. shoe

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A为图书馆可读之物；B靴子不可read，后文亦有book。

### 2：A

A为方式副词且对应Please be quiet；B形容词不能作本句speak的方式修饰，也不表安静。

### 3：A

A表示共同读，与sit together一致；B under you表示在你下面，不能表达共同阅读。

### 4：B

B对应借用公共图书保持整洁的关照；A与so引出的爱护行为矛盾。

### 5：A

A图书放回书架供他人找；B鞋子不是此处图书归还位置。
