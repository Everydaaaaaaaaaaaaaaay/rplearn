# 初二：幕布后的影子八空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，为第1—8空分别从4个选项中选择最恰当的一项。人物与经历均为虚构。

When I joined the school play, I hoped to stand in the bright light and speak the final lines. Instead, the director asked me to work (1) ____ the curtain. I would move the paper trees while the other pupils acted.

For the first two practices, I felt disappointed. I thought nobody would notice my work. Then our tree fell during a scene. The actors had to stop, and the director asked us to begin again. I suddenly understood that the scene (2) ____ on the work behind the curtain too.

I started to practise moving the trees without making a sound. To keep them steady, I held each one with (3) ____ hands instead of trying to carry two at once. Mina, who managed the lights, showed me the marks on the floor. I waited for her quiet signal before moving anything.

On the night of the play, one tree caught on a chair. I wanted to pull hard, but I (4) ____. Pulling would tear the paper. I lifted the tree slightly and moved the chair away with my foot. The next scene began on time. Nobody in the audience knew what had nearly happened.

Afterwards, Mina said, “That was a good (5) ____.” She had seen the problem and my response. I was pleased that someone had noticed, but I no longer needed everyone to (6) ____ me. From my place at the side, I had watched our work become one story. I asked the director if I could do the same (7) ____ in the next play. This time, it was my own (8) ____.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

move paper trees while others acted及结尾behind curtain明确幕后behind；above上方非站立位置，beyond泛在远处、across跨越都不指该幕后工作。

### 2：D

落树会使actors stop说明scene depended on幕后；insisted on坚持、reported on报道、knocked on敲击不表达成败关联。

### 3：C

instead of carrying two at once，改单树双手持，both hands；neither无手矛盾，empty不说明握持数量，dirty无稳定支撑关系。

### 4：B

想猛拉但先停再轻抬移椅，paused；cheered欢呼、escaped逃离、argued争论均非后续谨慎解卡的中间动作。

### 5：B

指不猛拉而抬树移椅的当场decision；accident意外并非赞扬的处理选择，invitation邀请、promise承诺未发生。

### 6：A

pleased someone noticed但no longer needed everyone to praise我，评价认可与开篇求舞台关注呼应；replace代替、interrupt打断、follow跟随不是所盼被看见的肯定。

### 7：D

same job是幕后移树职责；mistake错误非申请目标，speech台词不是所做职责，journey旅程未提。

### 8：D

先导演指定、后主动asked形成own choice；secret秘密、excuse借口、prize奖品不表达自主选择角色。
