# 小六：出行与任务十句选择

## 学生题面

读十个独立句子或短对话，每题从三个选项中选择一个最佳答案。完成全部十题。



<!-- responses:start -->

1. There are three ____ in the basket.
   A. potato
   B. potatoes
   C. potatos

2. —____ did you get home yesterday?
—At half past five.
   A. Where
   B. Who
   C. When

3. My desk is 80 cm wide. Yours is 60 cm wide. My desk is ____ than yours.
   A. wider
   B. wide
   C. widest

4. We ____ the windows yesterday afternoon.
   A. clean
   B. cleaned
   C. cleaning

5. Listen! Someone ____ at the door now.
   A. knocked
   B. knock
   C. is knocking

6. This notebook belongs to Lucy. Please give it to ____.
   A. she
   B. hers
   C. her

7. There is not ____ milk in the bottle. It is completely empty.
   A. any
   B. many
   C. a

8. My brother wants ____ a cook when he grows up.
   A. be
   B. to be
   C. being

9. The library closes ____ six o'clock.
   A. on
   B. in
   C. at

10. You must not ____ your bike inside the hall.
   A. ride
   B. rides
   C. riding

## 作者核对

### 1：B

three要求可数复数potatoes；A单数，C常规拼写错误，不采用source You dictionary错拼。

### 2：C

答句给时间，When对应；Where问地点、Who问人物，均不匹配。

### 3：A

明示80>60且than需比较级wider；wide原级、widest最高级不合该结构。数值为虚构设定，不用鲸寿命事实。

### 4：B

yesterday afternoon限定过去，cleaned合适；clean原形时态不合，cleaning缺助动词。

### 5：C

Listen及now指当前正在敲门；someone单数用is knocking。A过去、B原形均不构成此时正在发生的谓语。

### 6：C

介词to后指Lucy用宾格her。she主格；hers为独立所属，不能在此指收件人Lucy本人。

### 7：A

否定句不可数milk可用any；many用于可数复数，a用于可数单数。空瓶明示没有牛奶，不以图片猜。

### 8：B

want后用to be不定式补语；be缺to，being不作此处want的目标职业补语。

### 9：C

具体钟点前用at；on用于日期等，in用于时段，均不合six oclock这一位置。

### 10：A

情态must not后用原形ride；rides第三人称单数、riding分词不合。
