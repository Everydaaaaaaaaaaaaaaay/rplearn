# 小五：周末中心活动整表

## 学生题面

读整张活动表，包括表头和全部五行，从每题四个选项中选择一个最佳答案。

All activities are on the same day. The following timetable is fictional.

| Start | End | Activity | Place | Who can join |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9:10 | 9:25 | Welcome | Hall | Adults and children |
| 9:40 | 10:05 | Paper craft | Art room | Adults and children |
| 10:20 | 10:50 | Garden visit | Garden | Adults only |
| 11:00 | 11:20 | Puppet show | Room 3 | Adults and children |
| 13:30 | 14:15 | Photo talk | Room 6 | Adults and children |

<!-- responses:start -->

1. Where is the paper craft activity?
   A. In the garden.
   B. In the art room.
   C. In Room 3.
   D. In Room 6.

2. How long does the paper craft activity last?
   A. Fifteen minutes.
   B. Twenty minutes.
   C. Thirty minutes.
   D. Twenty-five minutes.

3. When does the photo talk finish?
   A. At 14:15.
   B. At 13:30.
   C. At 10:50.
   D. At 11:20.

4. An adult is busy all morning but free after lunch. Which activity can the adult join?
   A. Paper craft.
   B. Garden visit.
   C. Photo talk.
   D. Puppet show.

5. Which activity can children NOT join according to the table?
   A. Welcome.
   B. Garden visit.
   C. Puppet show.
   D. Photo talk.

<!-- responses:end -->

## 作者核对

### 1：B

Papercraft的Artroom，B；garden访园、Room3木偶、Room6摄影讲座。

### 2：D

9:40—10:05跨整点=25分，D；15/20/30均不合此差值，不把分钟数05当时长。

### 3：A

End14:15，A；13:30为Start，10:50花园终，11:20木偶终。

### 4：C

Photo13:30下午且Adultsandchildren可成人，C；其余上午，不因Gardenonlyadults误选。

### 5：B

GardenAdults only明禁儿童，B；A/C/D写Adultsandchildren。
