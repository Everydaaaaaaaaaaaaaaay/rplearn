The village theatre was almost ready for its evening play when Jo heard a faint cry. The old curtain was (1) ____ from top to bottom; a long rip let a line of light through. Behind it, a kitten was hiding (2) ____ the low platform, directly below the actors’ feet. Nobody knew how it had entered the building.

Jo (3) ____ a small wooden cover with both hands, raising it high enough to see inside. The frightened kitten did not move. Her mother brought a little food and placed it on a shallow dish. Jo then climbed (4) ____ the platform, moving from the floor to its upper surface, so that her mother could reach the opening below. The space between the wall and the platform was too (5) ____ for both of them to turn round side by side.

Her mother reached towards the kitten as (6) ____ as she could, keeping the dish level. Despite the noise outside, she (7) ____ calm and waited for the animal to come closer. The man who was (8) ____ of the stage lighting switched off the bright lamp beside the opening.

They put the cover back (9) ____ the kitten was safely outside. Jo let out an audible sigh of (10) ____, happy that the little animal had escaped injury. The damaged curtain could wait until the morning. For tonight, the actors simply began the play with it drawn to one side.

## 作者核对

### 1：C

torn：裂口rip与透光表撕裂；narrow仅窄，不能解释从顶到底裂开；relief/silence名词不用作此被动或形容状态，其余词性/所需补语不合was后此描述。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 2：A

beneath：directly below the actors feet明确在台下，beneath；onto表示往上表面方向，和hiding状态/下面定位不合；in charge/as soon as需不同补语，其余不作the low platform前本位置介词。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 3：F

lifted：主语Jo主动双手抬cover并raisedhigh，lifted及物过去谓语；remained为不及物不能带cover；torn是分词非tore过去，余项非此及物过去谓语。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 4：B

onto：floor→upper surface明确向上登台，onto；beneath是台下与明确终点反；其余词不构成climbed后接platform的该方向关系。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 5：D

narrow：too...for两人并排转身受空间宽度限制=narrow；torn撕裂不描述两者间净空，relief/silence名词不能作too后该程度形容词，余项词性不合。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 6：E

carefully：as...as she could修饰reach/level需方式副词carefully；narrow/torn为形容词，其他介词/名词/动词/短语不能单独进入此副词比较结构。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 7：G

remained：she...calm需要系动词过去remained；lifted及物需宾语而calm此为形容状态，torn非tore且不连calm，其余非本限定系动词。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 8：I

in charge：was in charge of固定责任搭配管灯；beneath/onto后若接of会错，其他形容/副词/名词/过去动词也不能构成此was...of责任谓语。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 9：J

as soon as：后the kitten was safely outside为完整时点从句，as soon as连接即安全后盖回；其余项不是连接两完整分句的从属连词。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。

### 10：H

relief：sigh of relief且happy escapedinjury明释宽慰；silence无声与audible sigh矛盾，不是该感受；其余项非of后的该情感名词。 本空完整候选为A=beneath；B=onto；C=torn；D=narrow；E=carefully；F=lifted；G=remained；H=relief；I=in charge；J=as soon as；K=silence。不靠先排除其他空已用项来解释本空。
