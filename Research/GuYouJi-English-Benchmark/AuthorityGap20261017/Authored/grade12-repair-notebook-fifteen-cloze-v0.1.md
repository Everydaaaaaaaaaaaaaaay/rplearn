# 高三：修补笔记十五空完形

## 学生题面

阅读完整人物经历语篇，为全部十五空各选一个最佳选项。每空四个独立候选，结合跨句指代、时间线、限制条件及人物态度判断。

At sixty-eight, Mara still begins a workshop by placing a damaged shopping bag on the table. She treats it as an opportunity to ask why something has failed. Visitors sometimes expect her to display prizes, but she opens a notebook containing decades of experiments instead.

Mara first wanted to study design when she was seventeen. Her family's budget could not (1) ____ the tuition, so she took a factory job and (2) ____ formal study. The decision changed her timetable, not her interest. In the evenings, she repaired neighbours' bags and kept careful (3) ____ of every alteration: the date, the material and the result.

Her first attempt at a new handle looked attractive, but it (4) ____ after a few minutes of carrying groceries. Rather than blame the owner, Mara (5) ____ the damaged seam to see where the stitches had pulled apart. She kept the failed handle beside the next model, so she could compare them without relying on memory.

When a later design was ready, she took it to market traders and asked for (6) ____, especially comments about what still did not work. Some found the handles awkward; others said the bag was difficult to store. Their suggestions (7) ____ the next version. Mara replaced a costly metal part with a cloth loop, (8) ____ the cost while keeping the feature the traders wanted.

After retiring from paid work, she (9) ____ a small repair class. Pupils brought objects they might otherwise have thrown away. Mara showed her failed experiments as well as her finished work, choosing to (10) ____ mistakes rather than pretend they had never happened. She provided spare tools because buying equipment would have been too (11) ____ for some beginners.

The purpose was to (12) ____ others how to repair with what was available, not to advertise a new brand. Looking through the notebook, visitors could see that her (13) ____ had been gradual. No single page explained everything. Different problems had required different responses.

Mara did not claim that every old object could be saved. Sometimes she marked an attempt “not successful” and explained the (14) ____ of her method. What she valued most was (15) ____: the wish to keep asking what might work better, even after many years of trying.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

budget不能cover tuition表示无法承担学费，与随后工厂工作推迟学习相连。hide隐藏、ignore忽略、exchange交换都不表示预算支付费用。

### 2：C

学费不足而先就业，是postponed正规学习。accelerated加快相反；repeated重复无先前课程；advertised宣传非学习安排改变。

### 3：D

后列date/material/result为records记录，与notebook呼应。promises承诺、complaints投诉、secrets秘密均不概括按实验项目记数据。

### 4：A

下一句damaged seam/stitches pulled apart，手柄试用失败failed。succeeded成功与破损反；improved改善非本次断裂；sparkled发光与结构故障无关。

### 5：C

to see where stitches pulled apart需examined检查缝。blamed指责与rather than blame重复且不能定位；shipped寄送、buried埋藏不提供观察原因。

### 6：B

especially comments about what still did not work，主动要批评性反馈criticism。permission许可不是请指出缺点；congratulations祝贺忽略未解决问题；silence沉默不能形成反馈。

### 7：A

商贩建议影响下一版并据其所需改件，shaped塑造。buried埋藏表示搁置；replaced把建议当实物替代版本不合；misunderstood以suggestions作理解主体也不合。

### 8：D

以布环替costly metal并保留功能，旨在降低reducing成本。raising提高与替贵件反；ignoring忽略与主动选替代件反；calculating仅算价不表达这次替换的成本变化。

### 9：C

退休后新办修补班，started符合接着介绍pupils学习。closed关闭、avoided避开均不合后文授课；inherited继承无之前创办者或转移证据。

### 10：A

展示失败尝试，不装作未发生，acknowledge承认。conceal隐瞒相反；finance资助、postpone推迟不描述对已发生错误的态度。

### 11：B

提供工具因部分初学者负担不起购买，expensive贵。distant远与设备购买费用无关；familiar熟悉、ordinary普通均不是供免费备用工具的理由。

### 12：D

repair class及pupils学习，purpose是teach修补。disguise掩饰、forbid禁止、delay推迟均不表示传授。

### 13：C

notebook逐次实验/不同问题不同回应显示gradual progress进步。departure离去、income收入、reputation声誉不由这些实验页直接体现。

### 14：B

并非所有物可救、有未成功试验，说明方法limits局限。rewards奖励、popularity人气、origin起源均不解释不能救所有物的保留判断。

### 15：D

冒号后the wish to keep asking定义curiosity好奇。certainty确定与持续追问不同；secrecy保密与分享笔记反；loyalty忠诚没有特定忠于对象。
