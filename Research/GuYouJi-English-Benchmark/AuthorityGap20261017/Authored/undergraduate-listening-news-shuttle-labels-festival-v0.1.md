## 作者核对

### 第1段完整原创脚本

R: Students at Lakeside College will be able to use a new evening shuttle between the library and the western residence halls from next week. The service is being introduced because the regular campus bus stops running at eight, while the library remains open until eleven. The college considered extending the regular route, but a smaller shuttle was found to be more practical for the number of passengers expected at night. It will depart every half hour from a stop beside the library entrance. Students will need to show a college card, but will not pay a fare. The shuttle will not call at the sports building, which already has a separate evening service. Transport staff will review passenger numbers at the end of the first six weeks. If demand is low on particular evenings, the timetable may change. The daytime campus bus schedule will remain as it is.

### 第1题：C

录音问题：Why is the college introducing an evening shuttle?

C：常规车八点停、图书馆十一点关的缺口；A体育楼已有另线；B白天未改；D关门时间未提早。

### 第2题：A

录音问题：What do students need to use the new shuttle?

A：college card；B不付费也未须图书馆买票；C六周是评估期非预约；D目的地不经体育楼，非体育会员。

### 第2段完整原创脚本

R: Visitors to the Marlow museum will soon see two kinds of label beside objects in its science gallery. One will give a brief explanation in everyday language. The other will provide technical details for visitors who want to explore the subject further. The museum began revising the labels after a visitor survey showed that many people stopped reading when they met an unfamiliar term in the first sentence. Staff initially tried removing all technical words, but teachers argued that students also needed opportunities to learn them. The two-label approach is intended to offer both an accessible starting point and a route to greater detail. The gallery will stay open while the labels are replaced, one display at a time. The museum has not purchased new objects for this project. It plans to ask visitors to compare the revised explanations with the earlier ones before changing labels in its other galleries.

### 第3题：B

录音问题：What did the visitor survey reveal?

B：首句陌生术语导致停止阅读；A无购买诉求；C教师反而希望学术语；D无到访动机统计。

### 第4题：D

录音问题：What will the museum do before extending the project to other galleries?

D：先让访客比较新旧解释；A保持开放逐展换；B双层说明保留技术细节；C无购新藏品。

### 第3段完整原创脚本

R: The Brookfield food festival is asking stallholders to use a shared collection point for reusable bowls this year. Visitors who choose a reusable bowl will pay a small deposit when they order food and receive the deposit back when they return the bowl. They can return it at the collection point even if they bought food from a stall at the other end of the festival. Last year's smaller trial required customers to find the original stall, and several said they kept the bowls because returning them was inconvenient. Festival staff will wash the returned bowls in a separate service area. Stallholders will still be responsible for serving food, but will not need to wash bowls themselves. Visitors may also bring their own clean containers. Organizers say the aim is to make returning shared bowls easier, rather than to ban every other type of container immediately. They will count how many bowls come back and compare the return rate with last year's trial before deciding on next year's arrangements.

### 第5题：D

录音问题：When will visitors get their bowl deposit back?

D：归还即退押金；A不须买第二餐或同摊；B比较率是后续管理；C自带不是借碗押金返还条件。

### 第6题：C

录音问题：What problem occurred during last year's trial?

C：旧试验须返原摊、不方便而留碗；A无拒供；B现可自带，未称去年禁；D共用点是今年方案，不能移去年。

### 第7题：A

录音问题：What will festival staff do in the new arrangement?
