# 初二：离线与在线学习工具匹配

## 学生题面

阅读四张应用介绍卡，为全部三个人各选最合适的一项。有一项多余；须同时满足人物的具体需求。

The four learning apps below are fictional.

A. PageTrail provides short illustrated stories. After downloading a story at home, you can read every page on a journey without an internet connection. Tap marked words to see simple meanings. There is no audio, and it does not connect readers to a speaking partner.

B. VoiceSwap arranges ten-minute online talks with a learning partner in the evening. You take turns asking questions in English. Both learners must be connected at the agreed time. It does not provide an offline library or check a written paragraph.

C. CaptionStep has short clips of everyday conversations. Watch first without words on the screen, then turn on captions and replay a difficult part. It needs an internet connection. You can hear the speakers but cannot record a reply or call a partner.

D. EditLens checks a short paragraph you have written. It marks possible spelling and punctuation mistakes and explains each suggestion. You choose what to change. It has no recorded conversations, story collection or partner calls.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

A下载后离线故事且有词义，满足全部条件。B需同时在线且是对话；C需联网且是视频；D改本人段落不供故事。

### 2：C

C先无字幕听后开字幕重放。A明确无声音，B真人对话不供字幕重放，D仅改书面段落。

### 3：B

B晚间约时真人伙伴轮问。A离线故事无伙伴；C只能听看不能回复呼叫；D书面修改无通话。
