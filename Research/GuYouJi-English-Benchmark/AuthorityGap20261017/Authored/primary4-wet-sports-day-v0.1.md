# 小四：雨天活动安排阅读

## 学生题面

阅读完整对话，从每题三个选项中选择一个最佳答案。完成全部五题。

Amy: Are we having Sports Day outside tomorrow, Mr Green?
Mr Green: No. The field is too wet. We will use the sports hall instead.
Amy: Can we still have the running race?
Mr Green: Not tomorrow. The hall is too small for that race. We will have it next Friday if the field is dry.
Amy: What will we do in the hall?
Mr Green: Play team games with soft balls. There will be four teams. Each team will have six pupils.
Amy: Do we need to bring balls?
Mr Green: No. The school has them. Wear clean sports shoes. Leave your umbrellas outside the hall so that the floor stays dry.
Amy: When should we arrive?
Mr Green: At nine. The games begin at quarter past nine. Parents may watch from the seats beside the door.

1. Why will the pupils use the hall tomorrow?
   A. There are no teams.
   B. The field is wet.
   C. The hall is bigger than the field.

2. What is planned for next Friday if the field is dry?
   A. The running race.
   B. A drawing class.
   C. A ball sale.

3. How many pupils will play in the four teams altogether?
   A. Six.
   B. Ten.
   C. Twenty-four.

4. What should pupils bring or wear?
   A. Their own balls.
   B. Clean sports shoes.
   C. Umbrellas inside the hall.

5. When should pupils arrive?
   A. At nine.
   B. At quarter past nine.
   C. At quarter to nine.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

首段明确field too wet；A有four teams；C不仅无据且hall太小不能跑赛。

### 2：A

推迟的it指running race且附干燥条件；B/C未安排。

### 3：C

four teams×six each=24；A单队数；B将4和6相加而非按每队相乘。

### 4：B

Wear clean sports shoes支持B；A学校提供球；C明确伞留场外。

### 5：A

At nine回答arrive；B为games begin；C未要求。
