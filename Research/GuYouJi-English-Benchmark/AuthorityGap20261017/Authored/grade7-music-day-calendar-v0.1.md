The school will hold a family music afternoon on the third Sunday of September 2027. It starts at two and ends at four. Families should arrive at the main hall ten minutes before the start. There is no need to play an instrument already: beginners are welcome.

At the entrance, a helper will give each visitor a coloured ticket. Please keep **it** until the end. The colour shows which practice room the visitor should use after the opening song. A blue ticket means Room 1 and a yellow ticket means Room 2. The visitor’s name will not be printed on the ticket.

The school has small drums for everyone, so visitors do not have to bring an instrument. During the final half-hour, the two groups will return to the hall to play together. Food is not allowed in the practice rooms. Families can eat in the garden after the afternoon ends.
