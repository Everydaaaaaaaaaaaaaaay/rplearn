# 初一：认识新社区五段标题

## 学生题面

为整篇文章的五个段落各选择一个最合适的小标题。五个标题共用；根据每段中心判断。

Read the five sections of the article and choose a heading for each one.

### (1) ____

Start with the main roads around your home. Draw them on paper and add your school, the nearest park and a shop you know. You do not need every small street. A few clear lines will help you see how the places are connected.

### (2) ____

Words and pictures on walls can tell you useful things. A small picture may show where bicycles can go. A notice outside a library may give its opening hours. Read before you enter, rather than finding a locked door and feeling surprised.

### (3) ____

A neighbour may know why an old building has an unusual name. A shop worker may know when the street market opens. Ask politely and choose a moment when people are not busy. You can learn things that are not written on your map.

### (4) ____

Ask an adult before you explore a new route. Stay on paths you are allowed to use and cross roads at proper places. Do not look at a screen while crossing. Knowing a place better should help you feel comfortable, but you still need to take care.

### (5) ____

After your walk, choose one interesting place to remember. Write a few words about it or draw a picture in a notebook. Add the date. Next month, look again and see what has changed. Your own notes can help you notice more each time.

共享标题：
A. Keep a record of what you find
B. Learn from people who live there
C. Begin with a simple map
D. Travel safely
E. Notice the signs

## 作者核对

### 1：C

画主要道路和熟悉地点形成简图，C概括目的。A虽也可含记录但本段明确是地图起步，B不访人、D非安全操作、E不读标识。

### 2：E

本段核心是读图示/公告获取路线和开放时间。A非保存个人发现、B无人访谈、C不制地图、D只泛安全不能概括图示和公告两例。

### 3：B

向邻居/店员礼貌询问，B统摄两种当地人。A不记日志、C现成地图仅对照、D非出行安全、E不是书面标识。

### 4：D

征求成人同意、守路、过街不看屏幕，中心为安全探索。A不记录、B非询问信息、C不画图、E无读公告主旨。

### 5：A

散步后写/画、记日期并回看变化，是留个人记录。B未访人、C非空间地图、D非途中安全、E非公共标识阅读。
