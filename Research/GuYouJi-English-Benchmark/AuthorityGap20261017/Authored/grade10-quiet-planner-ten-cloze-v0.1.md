# 高一：安静的策划者十空完形

## 学生题面

阅读一篇完整叙事语篇，为全部十空各选一个最佳答案。每空四个独立候选；根据上下文、事件顺序及人物态度判断。

At our first meeting for a school display, Finn said almost nothing. I (1) ____ his quietness as a lack of interest. When we shared the work, I kept him out of the planning group and gave him only a (2) ____ role: carrying spare chairs.

The following afternoon, Finn arrived holding a folded sheet of paper. It was a (3) ____ of the room, with the tables, doors and chairs carefully marked. He had noticed that our planned line of visitors would block the entrance. His explanation was (4) ____: he showed the exact place of every turn and the space each table needed.

Later, Finn told me that he found it difficult to join fast (5) ____. He preferred to think first and then put his ideas on paper. I felt (6) ____ about the decision I had made without asking him anything. I apologised and invited him to help arrange the whole display.

Before the next meeting, I (7) ____ everyone for ideas in writing. Finn's plan was (8) ____, and we put the tables in the positions he had drawn. The room itself was almost the same, but the way we worked had (9) ____. I had learnt that people needed an (10) ____ to show what they could contribute, rather than a role chosen for them after one quick impression.

## 作者核对

### 1：A

interpreted...as表示把安静解读为没兴趣，符合叙述者最初误判。supported支持、replaced替换、measured测量均不构成该对quietness的主观解释。

### 2：D

kept him out of planning且only/carry spare chairs表叙述者给次要角色，minor最佳。leading与排除策划矛盾；permanent长久性无证据；paid薪酬未提。

### 3：C

room中桌门椅位置和行进路线的纸是map。menu列餐食、receipt收据、diary日记均不承载此空间布局。

### 4：B

exact place of every turn及所需空间体现详尽detailed。angry无情绪证据；hurried匆促与carefully marked解释不符；uncertain与exact信息相反。

### 5：C

meeting中几乎不说话、先思考写纸说明难加入快速交谈。journeys旅程、repairs修理、competitions竞赛不是本文交流方式。

### 6：D

发现误判后apologised，对自己早先决定羞愧embarrassed。proud自豪、relieved如释重负、unconcerned不在意均与道歉及反省不符。

### 7：A

asked everyone for ideas是征求想法，与改为书面参与一致。blamed指责、charged收费、frightened吓唬均不表示征询意见。

### 8：B

之后按图摆桌说明采用adopted。cancelled取消、misplaced弄丢、concealed隐藏均与实际执行其位置方案冲突。

### 9：C

由先快谈排人改为征集书面意见，是合作方式changed。failed失败、disappeared消失、stopped停止都与继续成功合作不符。

### 10：D

完整故事反省给人展示贡献的机会opportunity。order命令、excuse借口、warning警告均不概括从预判分工转向参与的学习。
