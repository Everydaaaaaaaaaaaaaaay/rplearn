# 小五：课后活动信息卡

## 学生题面

阅读全部四张课后活动卡，判断第1—5句话是否与卡片信息相符，选T或F。卡片为虚构。

These are our after-school clubs.

| A. Green Hands | B. Puppet Pals |
| --- | --- |
| For pupils who enjoy growing plants. | For pupils who enjoy telling stories. |
| Monday and Thursday, 15:20—16:00 | Wednesday, 15:20—16:00 |
| School Garden | Classroom 3 |
| Water the plants. Draw one leaf in a notebook. | Make paper puppets. Tell a story in pairs. |

| C. Camera Club | D. Song Steps |
| --- | --- |
| For pupils who like taking pictures. | For pupils who enjoy singing. |
| Friday, 15:20—16:00 | Tuesday, 15:20—16:00 |
| Art Room | Music Room |
| Take pictures of small classroom objects. Choose one picture to show. | Learn a new song. Move to the music together. |

## 作者核对

### 1：F

A Monday andThursday，onlyMonday与双日明确冲突F。

### 2：T

B地点Classroom3支持T，F与印文反。

### 3：T

C活动takepictures ofsmallclassroomobjects支持T，不假定到户外摄影。

### 4：F

D学歌随音乐动，纸偶属B，F不同卡角色错配。

### 5：F

A Monday/Thursday，B Wednesday；bothWednesday不成立，有A明确时段反证F。
