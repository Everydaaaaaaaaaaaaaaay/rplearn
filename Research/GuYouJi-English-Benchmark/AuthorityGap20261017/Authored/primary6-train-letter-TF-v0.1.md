# 小六：车站里的等候

## 学生题面

阅读完整书信，判断第1—5句话与信中信息是否相符，选T或F。人物和情境均为虚构。

Dear Dad,
Mum and I are at the station. You are working in another city, so you cannot travel with us today. Our train leaves at ten twenty, and it is only nine fifty now. We have two bags. The blue bag has our clothes, and the small green bag has our lunch. Mum bought the tickets online yesterday. She is checking our seats on her phone. I wanted to buy a comic, but the bookshop is closed. I am writing this letter instead. A worker has just told us that our platform is number four, not number two as we first thought. We will find it after I finish writing. Grandma will meet us at the other station this afternoon.
Love,
Robin
