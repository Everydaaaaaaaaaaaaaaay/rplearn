# 六级：未建成桥梁的历史地位

## 学生题面

阅读完整短文，从A—O中为全部十空各选一个最佳词项，填写字母。词形不变；每项最多使用一次，五项不用。材料为虚构情境。

In an imaginary town archive, historian Clara finds a proposal for a bridge that was never built. Its designer had received a (1) ____ from a local company to prepare the plans, but the existence of a formal instruction does not tell Clara whether the scheme could have succeeded.

She first examines the engineer's (2) ____: letters sent to suppliers and replies discussing the strength of available materials. These suggest that he regarded the design as technically (3) ____, capable of being constructed rather than merely imagined. No bridge of this kind had yet been built locally. However, the letters do not establish that the company could afford the labour or obtain all the materials at the prices quoted.

The public presentation used (4) ____ building methods familiar to its audience as a point of comparison with the unusual proposal. The engineer wanted to (5) ____ why his design required less material, making the reasoning clear through calculations rather than simply asserting an advantage. Those calculations were never tested in a completed structure. The project was (6) ____ after its financial supporter withdrew, and work did not resume.

An earlier history had focused (7) ____ on the designer, discussing no other contributor. Clara instead examines the workers and suppliers whose letters survive. In (8) ____, their questions reveal uncertainties absent from the polished public presentation. She resists treating those uncertainties as proof that failure was certain; questions raised before construction are not the same as observed defects.

The proposal produced no bridge; (9) ____, it changed the discussion of what local builders might attempt. Clara considers that influence historically significant. Calling it proof of superior engineering would (10) ____ the distinction between promise and performance, making the two seem indistinguishable.

## 作者核对

### 1：B

a commission from company to prepare plans为正式委托，后文formal instruction释义。correspondence信函为不可数且不由a引入单一委托；retrospect回顾不表正式指令；其余项非此委托名词。

### 2：D

冒号后letters/replies定义correspondence往来信函。commission是一次任务非这些信件总体；retrospect回顾不是被逐封检查的档案材料；其他词不作此信函名词。

### 3：G

capable of being constructed rather than imagined直接解释feasible可行；conventional常规不等可建造，不能仅以本地未建过就证明其他地区无常规做法，且第4空familiar methods需要该词。inferior较差没有比较结论，transparent透明不表达能否建成；其余候选非此处技术可行性的补语。

### 4：C

familiar methods相对unusual proposal，conventional常规符合。feasible可行不等被观众熟知；inferior较差没有被贬低证据；transparent透明不表示传统做法；其余项非修饰building methods的此形容词。

### 5：E

用计算让推理clear，要demonstrate why展示原因。obscure遮蔽与clear相反；reconcile why不表示展示论证；hesitate犹豫不以why从句实现说明；其他候选非此动词原形。

### 6：A

资金支持退出、never built/work not resume说明abandoned放弃。conventional/feasible/inferior/transparent不表达因退出而停弃的状态；其余项不能置于was后表达已被放弃项目。

### 7：F

no other contributor明确exclusively只关注设计者。substantially主要/很大程度不能表达排除其他所有人；nevertheless为转折不修饰focus的范围；其余项不是此范围副词。

### 8：M

In retrospect为后来回顾，适合历史学者重看早期疑问的视角。correspondence可在别的句子组成in correspondence（在通信中），不能单凭词性排除；但本整组第2空letters/replies明确要求correspondence，依原印每项最多一次规则不能再用。commission不构成回顾短语；其他项不提供in之后的这个回顾名词。

### 9：J

no bridge与仍产生影响之间是nevertheless尽管如此。exclusively/substantially只表排他范围/程度，不构成两分句的让步转折；其他候选不作此句间副词。

### 10：K

making two indistinguishable说明obscure遮蔽区别。demonstrate会凸显而非抹去；reconcile把相冲突对象调和，不是使承诺与实绩看不出区别；hesitate不及物，abandoned为过去式不接would；其余项不提供此隐匿区别的动词。

完整向量B/D/G/C/E/A/F/M/J/K；十个不同词库项，未用五项为H hesitate/I inferior/L reconcile/N substantially/O transparent。逐槽位可合语法不自动等整组最佳：必须同时核对上下文和原印每项最多一次规则。词库仅十五项，控件逐空重复不当150个独立词项。正文/题干/候选/输出负荷分别记录。
